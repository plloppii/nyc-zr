# 145-32 Public Use of Accessory Parking

All required or permitted **_accessory_** off-street parking spaces may be made available for public use. However, any such space shall be made available to the occupant of a **_residence_** to which it is **_accessory_** within 30 days of a written request for such space made to the landlord.
