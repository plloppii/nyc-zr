# 145-31 Accessory Off-street Parking Spaces for Non-residential Uses

In all **_Commercial Districts_**, the **_accessory_** off-street parking requirements for **_manufacturing_**, **_commercial_** or **_community facility_** **_uses_**, applicable to a C4-4 District, shall apply.
