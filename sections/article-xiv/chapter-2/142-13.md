# 142-13 Enclosure Requirements in Subdistrict E

In Subdistrict E, **_commercial_** and **_manufacturing_** activities and storage **_uses_** shall not be subject to the provisions of Section [42-51](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iv/chapter-2/42-51) (Enclosure of Commercial and Manufacturing Activities) or Section [42-52](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iv/chapter-2/42-52) (Enclosure or Screening of Storage).
