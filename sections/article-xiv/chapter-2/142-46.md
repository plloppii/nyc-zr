# 142-46 Height and Setback in Subarea B3

In Subarea B3, as shown on Map 1 (Special Inwood District – Subdistricts and Subareas) in the Appendix to this Chapter, the maximum **_building_** height shall be 125 feet within 10 feet of a **_street line_**. Beyond 10 feet of a **_street line_**, the maximum **_building_** height shall be 265 feet.
