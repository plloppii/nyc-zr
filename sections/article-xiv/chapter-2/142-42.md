# 142-42 Height and Setback for Non-Shoreline Adjacent Lots in Subareas A2, A3 and B1

In Subareas A2, A3 and B1, as shown on Map 1 (Special Inwood District – Subdistricts and Subareas) in the Appendix to this Chapter, for **_zoning lots_** other than **_shoreline adjacent lots_**, the height and setback regulations for R7A Districts set forth in the applicable provisions of Section [23-43](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-3/23-43), inclusive, shall apply.

The **_street wall_** location provisions of paragraph (a) of Section [35-631](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-5/35-631) shall apply to **_street_** frontages along and within 50 feet of Ninth Avenue, and the **_street wall_** requirements of paragraph (b) of Section [35-631](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-5/35-631) shall apply along all other **_street_** frontages of the **_zoning lot_**.
