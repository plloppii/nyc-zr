# 142-04 Definitions

For purposes of this Chapter, matter in italics is defined in Section [12-10](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-i/chapter-2/12-10) (DEFINITIONS), Section [32-301](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-2/32-301) (Definitions) and in this Section.

Shoreline adjacent lot

For the purposes of this Chapter, a “shoreline adjacent lot” shall refer to a **_waterfront zoning lot_** with a **_shoreline_** length of more than 100 feet, or any **_zoning lot_** that has entered into a binding agreement to improve and maintain a **_waterfront public access area_** for an adjoining **_waterfront zoning lot_**.
