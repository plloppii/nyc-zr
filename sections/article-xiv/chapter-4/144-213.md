# 144-213 Floor area limitations on additional uses

The additional **_uses_** allowed pursuant to Section [144-11](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-xiv/chapter-4/144-11) (Additional Uses Allowed in M2 Districts) shall be limited to 300,000 square feet of **_floor area_** within each Subdistrict where such additional **_uses_** are allowed, and the total **_floor area_** allocated to such additional **_commercial uses_** shall not exceed 100,000 square feet in each Subdistrict where the additional **_uses_** are allowed.
