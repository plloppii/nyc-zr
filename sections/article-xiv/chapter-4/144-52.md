# 144-52 Required Accessory Off-street Parking

In all districts, the requirements of Section [44-21](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iv/chapter-4/44-21) (General Provisions) shall apply, except that, in the event that a Transportation Management Plan has been prepared in accordance with Section [144-56](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-xiv/chapter-4/144-56) (Transportation Management Planning), no parking shall be required.
