# 144-01 Definitions

Definitions specifically applicable to this Chapter are set forth in this Section and may modify definitions set forth in Section [12-10](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-i/chapter-2/12-10) (DEFINITIONS). Where a term in italics is defined in both Section [12-10](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-i/chapter-2/12-10) and in this Chapter, the definitions in this Chapter shall govern. The terms **_pier_**, **_platform_**, and **_floating structure_** shall have the meaning set forth in Section [62-11](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-2/62-11) (Definitions).

Barge Basin

“Barge Basin” shall mean that inlet from the East River identified on Map 2 in the Appendix to this Chapter as the Barge Basin.

Shoreline

The definition of **_shoreline_** set forth in Section [12-10](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-i/chapter-2/12-10) is modified for the purposes of this Chapter to mean the shoreline on a survey available on the Department of City Planning website.
