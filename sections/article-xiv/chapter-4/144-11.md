# 144-11 Additional Uses Permitted in All Districts

In all districts, the underlying **_use_** regulations are modified as follows:

The provisions of Section [62-21](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-2/62-21) (Classification of Uses in the Waterfront Area), Section [62-22](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-2/62-22) (Commercial Docking Facilities), Section [62-24](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-2/62-24) (Uses on Piers and Platforms), and Section [62-25](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-2/62-25) (Uses on Floating Structures) shall apply, provided that all **_uses_** existing on **_piers_** and **_platforms_** as of December 15, 2021, shall be deemed conforming **_uses_**.
