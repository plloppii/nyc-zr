# 144-55 Bicycle Parking

The requirements of Section [44-60](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iv/chapter-4/44-60) (BICYCLE PARKING) shall apply, provided that up to half of the required spaces may be provided as unenclosed bicycle parking spaces, and may be located anywhere on the **_zoning lot_**.
