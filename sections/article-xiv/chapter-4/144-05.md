# 144-05 Applicability of Special Regulations Applying in the Waterfront Area

The provisions of Article VI, Chapter 2 (Special Regulations Applying in the Waterfront Area) shall not apply in the **_Special Brooklyn Navy Yard District_** except as expressly provided in this Chapter.
