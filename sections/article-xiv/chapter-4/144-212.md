# 144-212 Floor area ratio calculations

For the purposes of calculating **_floor area_** on **_waterfront zoning lots_**, the provisions of Section [62-31](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-2/62-31) (Bulk Computations on Waterfront Zoning Lots) shall apply.
