# 144-143 Special provisions near certain parks

The provisions of Section [42-65](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iv/chapter-2/42-65) (Additional Regulations for Signs Near Certain Parks and Designated Arterial Highways) shall not apply to any **_signs_** provided in accordance with Section [144-141](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-xiv/chapter-4/144-141) (Illuminated non-flashing signage) within the Barge Basin East Subarea.
