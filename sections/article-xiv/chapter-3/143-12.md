# 143-12 Home Occupation

For the purposes of this Chapter, the **_home occupation_** provisions of Section [12-10](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-i/chapter-2/12-10) shall apply, except that a **_home occupation_** may occupy more than 1,000 square feet of **_floor area_** and up to three persons not residing in the **_dwelling unit_** or **_rooming unit_** may be employed. In addition to the **_uses_** listed in Section [12-10](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-i/chapter-2/12-10), a **_home occupation_** may include any permitted **_commercial use_**.
