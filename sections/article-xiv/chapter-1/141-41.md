# 141-41 Location of Curb Cuts

In all districts, for **_zoning lots_** existing on March 22, 2018, with frontage along Edward L. Grant Highway, West 170th Street, or the portions of Jerome or River Avenues with an elevated rail structure, and fronting along other **_streets_**, no curb cut accessing off-street parking spaces or loading spaces shall be permitted along such **_streets_**, as applicable.
