# 141-25 Special Height and Setback Regulations in R8A Districts

For **_zoning lots_** in R8A Districts, or in **_Commercial Districts_** mapped within, or with a **_residential equivalent_** of an R8A District, within 100 feet of a **_wide street_** located outside of **_Mandatory Inclusionary Housing areas_**, the underlying height and setback provisions applicable to an R8 District within 100 feet of a **_wide street_** shall apply. However, **_sky exposure plane buildings_** shall not be permitted, and the additional height allowances for eligible sites set forth in Section [23-434](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-3/23-434) (Height and setback modifications for eligible sites) shall not apply.
