# 125-041 Modification of use and bulk regulations for zoning lots bounding parks

Where the **_lot line_** of a **_zoning lot_** coincides with the boundary of a **_public park_**, such **_lot line_** shall be considered to be a **_street line_** for the purposes of applying all **_use_** and **_bulk_** regulations of this Resolution.
