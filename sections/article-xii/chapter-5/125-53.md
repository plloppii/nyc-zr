# 125-53 Maximum Size of Permitted Accessory Group Parking Facilities

In the East River Subdistrict, Section [16-13](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-i/chapter-6/16-13) (Permitted Parking for Zoning Lots With Multiple Uses) shall apply, except that the maximum number of spaces shall be 780. Additionally, the restrictions for off-site parking pursuant to Section [16-21](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-i/chapter-6/16-21) (Public Use and Off-site Parking) shall not apply.

In the Newtown Creek Subdistrict, Section [16-13](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-i/chapter-6/16-13) shall apply except that the maximum number of spaces shall not exceed 40 percent of the number of **_dwelling units_** within the **_development_** or **_enlargement_**.
