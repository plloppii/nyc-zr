# 125-51 General Regulations

For the purposes of Section [125-50](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-xii/chapter-5/125-50) (PARKING REGULATIONS), inclusive, the **_floor area_** of a **_building_** shall not include floor space used for **_accessory_** off-street parking spaces provided in any **_story_** located not more than 33 feet above **_curb level_**.

No **_public parking garages_** or **_public parking lots_** shall be permitted.
