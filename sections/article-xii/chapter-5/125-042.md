# 125-042 Applicability of Article II, Chapter 5

The provisions of Section [25-86](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-5/25-86) (Waiver or Reduction of Spaces for Subsidized Housing) shall not apply in the **_Special Southern Hunters Point District_**.
