# 125-21 East River Subdistrict

In the East River Subdistrict, the basic maximum **_residential_** **_floor area ratio_** for **_zoning lots_** containing standard **_residences_** shall be as set forth in the following table. On Parcels B, C, and F, the maximum **_residential_** **_floor area ratio_** for **_zoning lots_** containing **_qualifying affordable housing_** or **_qualifying senior housing_** shall be as set forth in Section [23-22](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-3/23-22) (Floor Area Regulations for R6 Through R12 Districts) for R10 Districts. For **_developments_** or **_enlargements_** on **_qualifying transit improvement sites_**, a **_floor area_** bonus for **_mass transit station_** improvements may be granted by the City Planning Commission pursuant to the provisions of Section [66-51](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-6/66-51) (Additional Floor Area for Mass Transit Station Improvements). No other **_floor area_** bonuses shall apply. For the purposes of this paragraph, defined terms additionally include those in Section [66-11](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-6/66-11) (Definitions).

| Parcel | Maximum **_Floor Area_** |
| --- | --- |
| A | 12.0 |
| B | 10.0 |
| C | 10.5 |
| D | 12.0 |
| E | 12.0 |
| F | 10.0 |
| G | 12.0 |
