# 125-31 Permitted Obstructions

The provisions of Section [33-42](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-3/33-42) (Permitted Obstructions) shall apply to all **_buildings_** within the **_Special Southern Hunters Point District_**, except that dormers provided in accordance with paragraph (b) of Section [23-413](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-3/23-413) (Permitted obstructions in certain districts) shall be a permitted obstruction in a required setback.
