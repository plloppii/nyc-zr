# 125-32 Balconies

Balconies shall not be permitted below a height of 70 feet. Above a height of 70 feet, balconies are permitted in accordance with the provisions of Section [23-62](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-3/23-62) (Balconies).
