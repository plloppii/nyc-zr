# 125-043 Modification of Article VI, Chapter 2

The provisions of Sections [62-52](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-2/62-52) (Applicability of Waterfront Public Access Area Requirements) and [62-60](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-2/62-60) (DESIGN REQUIREMENTS FOR WATERFRONT PUBLIC ACCESS AREAS) are modified as set forth in Section [125-46](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-xii/chapter-5/125-46) (Newtown Creek Waterfront Access Plan).
