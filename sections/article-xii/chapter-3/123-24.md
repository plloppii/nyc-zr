# 123-24 Modification of Special Permit Requirements

In the event that provisions of this Resolution permit a **_use_** by special permit, authorization or certification in both designated M1 and **_Residence Districts_**, no more than one special permit, authorization or certification is required to permit any such **_use_** in the **_Special Mixed Use District_**.

In the event that a provision of this Resolution permits a **_use_** by special permit, authorization or certification in either a designated M1 or **_Residence District_** and another provision permits such **_use_** without a special permit, authorization or certification in the other designated district, no special permit, certification or authorization shall be required in the **_Special Mixed Use District_**. In such case, the **_bulk_** regulations of the district allowing the **_use_** as-of-right shall control.
