# 123-20 SPECIAL USE REGULATIONS

In **_Special Mixed Use Districts_**, all **_uses_** permitted in the designated **_Residence District_** and all **_uses_** permitted in the designated M1 District, as set forth in any other provision of this Resolution other than Special Purpose Districts, shall be permitted, except as superseded, modified or supplemented by this Section, inclusive, and Section [123-40](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-xii/chapter-3/123-40) (SIGN REGULATIONS).
