# 123-12 Special Provisions for Applying Certain Underlying Regulations to MX Districts

For the purposes of determining the applicability of underlying adjacency regulations to **_zoning lots_** adjoining or within close proximity to district boundaries, M1 Districts paired with **_Residence Districts_** shall be considered C6 Districts.
