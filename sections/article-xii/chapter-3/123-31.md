# 123-31 Provisions Regulating Location of Uses in Mixed Use Buildings

In **_Special Mixed Use Districts_**, in any **_building_** or portion of a **_building_** occupied by **_residential uses_**, **_commercial_** or **_manufacturing_** **_uses_** may be located only on a **_story_** below the lowest **_story_** occupied by **_dwelling units_**, except that this limitation shall not preclude the:

(a) extension of a permitted business **_sign_**, **_accessory_** to such non-**_residential use_**, to a maximum height of two feet above the level of a finished floor of the second **_story_**, but in no event higher than six inches below the lowest window sill on the second **_story_**;
(b) location of **_commercial_** or **_manufacturing_** **_uses_** on the same **_story_**, or on a **_story_** higher than that occupied by **_dwelling units_**, in **_buildings_** in existence on or prior to December 10, 1997, that are partially **_converted_** to **_residential use_** pursuant to Article I, Chapter 5 (Residential Conversion Within Existing Buildings), as modified in Section [123-67](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-xii/chapter-3/123-67) (Residential Conversion); or
(c) location of **_commercial_** or **_manufacturing_** **_uses_** on the same **_story_**, or on a **_story_** higher than that occupied by **_dwelling units_**, provided that:
    (1) no access exists between such **_uses_** at any level containing **_dwelling units_**; and
    (2) the environmental requirements set forth in Section [32-423](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-2/32-423) shall be met where **_commercial_** or **_manufacturing_** **_uses_** are located above any **_story_** containing **_dwelling units_**.
