# 123-65 Special Height and Setback Regulations in Special Mixed Use Districts with R6 Through R12 District Designations

In **_Special Mixed Use Districts_** where the designated **_Residence District_** is an R6 through R12 District, the **_street wall_** location of a **_building_** shall be as set forth in [123-651](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-xii/chapter-3/123-651) (Street wall location for all buildings), and the height and setback regulations of a **_building or other structure_** shall be as set forth in Section [123-652](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-xii/chapter-3/123-652) (Special base and building heights). Additional height and setback provisions are set forth in Section [123-653](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-xii/chapter-3/123-653) and Section [123-66](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-xii/chapter-3/123-66), inclusive.

Permitted obstructions shall be permitted pursuant to Sections [23-41](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-3/23-41), [24-51](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-4/24-51) or [43-42](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iv/chapter-3/43-42). In addition, for all **_buildings or other structures_**, a dormer may be provided in accordance with the provisions of paragraph (b)(1) of Section [23-413](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-3/23-413) (Permitted obstructions in certain districts).

The height of all **_buildings or other structures_** shall be measured from the **_base plane_**. For the purposes of this Section, where **_base planes_** of different elevations apply to different portions of a **_building or other structure_**, each such portion of the **_building_** may be considered to be a separate **_building_**.
