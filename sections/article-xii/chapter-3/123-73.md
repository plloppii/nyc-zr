# 123-73 On Waterfront Blocks

For **_uses_** on **_waterfront blocks_**, as defined in Section [62-11](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-2/62-11), the special **_accessory_** off-street parking and loading regulations set forth in Section [62-40](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-2/62-40), inclusive, shall apply. When any **_use_** is permitted in both the designated **_Residence District_** and the designated M1 District, the **_accessory_** off-street parking and loading requirements applicable to the designated M1 District shall apply to such **_use_**.
