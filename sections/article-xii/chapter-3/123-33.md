# 123-33 Health and Fitness Establishments

In M1 Districts paired with a **_Residence District_**, high-intensity **_uses_**, as listed in the definition of **_health and fitness establishments_**, shall be subject to the enclosure and environmental conditions for C7 Districts set forth in Section [32-16](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-2/32-16) (Use Group VI – Retail and Services), inclusive.
