# 123-40 SIGN REGULATIONS

In **_Special Mixed Use Districts_**, the provisions regulating **_signs_** in C6-1 Districts, as set forth in Section [32-60](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-2/32-60) (SIGN REGULATIONS), shall apply for any **_sign_**. For the purposes of applying such regulations in **_Special Mixed Use Districts_**, all references to **_mixed buildings_** shall include **_mixed use buildings_**.
