# 121-332 Base height regulations

Except for **_zoning lots_** located south of West 31st Street and east of Avenue of the Americas, the maximum base height of a **_street wall_** may vary between the applicable maximum set forth in the underlying regulations, inclusive, and the height of the **_street wall_** of adjacent **_building_** before setback, if such height is higher than the maximum base height.
