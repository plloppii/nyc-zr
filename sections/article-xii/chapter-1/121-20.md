# 121-20 SIGN REGULATIONS

All **_signs_** shall be subject to the regulations applicable in C6-4 Districts, as set forth in Section [32-60](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-2/32-60) (SIGN REGULATIONS). However, **_flashing signs_** shall not be permitted.
