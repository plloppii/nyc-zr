# 121-321 Rear yard equivalent

Where the designated **_Residence District_** is an R6 through R12 District without a letter suffix, for **_buildings_**, or portions thereof, containing **_residential uses_**, the alternative location allowances set forth in paragraph (c)(2) of Section [23-343](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-3/23-343) (Rear yard equivalent requirements) shall not apply.
