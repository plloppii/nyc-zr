# 121-31 Floor Area Exemption for Public Schools

On **_zoning lots_** above 20,000 square feet in **_lot area_**, up to 150,000 square feet of **_floor area_** within a public **_school_**, constructed in whole or in part pursuant to agreement with the New York City School Construction Authority and subject to the jurisdiction of the New York City Department of Education, shall be exempt from the definition of **_floor area_** for the purposes of calculating the permitted **_floor area ratio_** for **_community facility_** **_uses_** and the maximum **_floor area ratio_** of the **_zoning lot_**.
