# 121-12 Regulations for Use Group VI

In addition to all **_uses_** permitted in the designated M1 District with an A suffix, all **_uses_** listed under Use Group VI shall be permitted, and where such **_uses_** have a size limitation, as denoted with an “S” in the use group tables set forth in Section [42-16](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iv/chapter-2/42-16) (Use Group VI – Retail and Services), such size limitation shall not apply.
