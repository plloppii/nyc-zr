# 122-70 SPECIAL PROVISIONS FOR ZONING LOTS DIVIDED BY DISTRICT BOUNDARIES

Notwithstanding any other provisions of this Resolution, whenever a **_zoning lot_** existing on September 28, 1989, is divided by a boundary between a district to which R8X District regulations apply and one to which R8 District regulations apply, the provisions of an R8X District shall apply to the entire **_zoning lot_**.
