# 122-01 Definitions

Display window

A "display window" is a window or opening in the exterior wall of any portion of a **_building_** which is glazed with tinted or transparent material and which is used to display merchandise, services or business.

Sign band

A "sign band" is a horizontal band which extends the full length of the **_street wall_** of a **_building_**, and is located between 8 feet and 14 feet above **_curb level_**.
