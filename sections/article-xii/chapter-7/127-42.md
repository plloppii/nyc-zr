# 127-42 Publicly Accessible Private Streets

The provisions of this Section, inclusive, shall apply to any **_development_**, as defined in Section [62-11](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-2/62-11) (Definitions), on a **_zoning lot_** that contains any portion of a required **_publicly accessible private street_**.
