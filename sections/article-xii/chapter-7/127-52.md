# 127-52 Special Requirements for Visual Corridors

For **_developments_** within Parcels 3, 5 and 7, **_visual corridors_** shall be provided in the locations designated on Map 5 in the Appendix to this Chapter and pursuant to the requirements of Sections [62-51](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-2/62-51) (Applicability of Visual Corridor Requirements) and [62-65](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-2/62-65) (Public Access Design Reference Standards).
