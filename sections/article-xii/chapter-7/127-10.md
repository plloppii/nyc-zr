# 127-10 SPECIAL USE REGULATIONS

Within the **_Special Flushing Waterfront District_**, the **_use_** regulations of the underlying zoning districts and of Article VI, Chapter 2 (Special Regulations Applying in the Waterfront Area), and Article XII, Chapter 3 (Special Mixed Use District), shall apply, except as modified by the provisions of this Section, inclusive.
