# 127-53 Special Requirements for Waterfront Public Access Areas

**_Waterfront public access areas_** shall be provided pursuant to Sections [62-52](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-2/62-52) (Applicability of Waterfront Public Access Area Requirements), [62-60](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-2/62-60) (DESIGN REQUIREMENTS FOR WATERFRONT PUBLIC ACCESS AREAS), and [62-70](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-2/62-70) (MAINTENANCE AND OPERATION REQUIREMENTS FOR WATERFRONT PUBLIC ACCESS AREAS), inclusive, except as modified in this Section, inclusive.

For all such **_waterfront public access areas_**, as designated on Map 6 (Waterfront Access Plan: Public Access Areas) in the Appendix to this Chapter, the minimum seat depth requirement of paragraph (b) of Section [62-652](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-2/62-652) (Seating) shall be modified to 16 inches.
