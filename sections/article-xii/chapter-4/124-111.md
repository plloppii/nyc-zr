# 124-111 Location of residential use within buildings

The provisions of Section [32-422](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-2/32-422) (Location of floors occupied by commercial uses) shall be modified such that the limitations set forth in paragraph (a) of such Section need not apply, and the requirements in paragraph (b) of such Section shall apply only where **_commercial uses_** are located above any **_story_** containing **_dwelling units_**.
