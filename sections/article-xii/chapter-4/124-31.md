# 124-31 Standards for Streets and Blocks

**_Developments_** or **_enlargements_** that result in a total of at least 100,000 square feet of **_floor area_** on individual **_zoning lots_** of at least 200,000 square feet or groups of **_zoning lots_** subject to the provisions of Section [124-60](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-xii/chapter-4/124-60) (SPECIAL PERMIT TO MODIFY USE OR BULK REGULATIONS) that are, in aggregate, at least 200,000 square feet, shall front upon **_streets_** that comply with the requirements of this Section, inclusive, unless modified by special permit pursuant to Section [124-60](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-xii/chapter-4/124-60).
