# 124-40 PUBLICLY ACCESSIBLE OPEN SPACE REQUIREMENTS

Publicly accessible open spaces shall be provided as specified in Section [124-40](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-xii/chapter-4/124-40), inclusive. In the event of a conflict between the provisions of this Section, inclusive, and any underlying regulation, the provisions of this Section shall govern.
