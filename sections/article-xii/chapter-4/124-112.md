# 124-112 Residential entrances on residential streets

Each ground floor **_dwelling unit_** on a **_residential street_** shall be directly accessible from such **_residential street_**. No more than one entrance to a **_residential street_** shall be required for any one ground floor **_dwelling unit_**.
