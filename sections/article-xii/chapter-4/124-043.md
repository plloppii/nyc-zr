# 124-043 Applicability of Article VII, Chapter 4

The following special permit by the City Planning Commission shall not be applicable:

Section [74-142](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-4/74-142) (Electric utility substations). In lieu thereof, such **_use_** shall be allowed within the **_Special Willets Point District_** upon authorization of the City Planning Commission, pursuant to Section [124-16](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-xii/chapter-4/124-16).
