# 128-31 Permitted Obstructions

The provisions of Section [33-42](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-3/33-42) (Permitted Obstructions) shall apply to all **_buildings_**, except that dormers may penetrate a maximum base height in accordance with the provisions of paragraph (b)(1) of Section [23-413](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-3/23-413) (Permitted obstructions in certain districts). However, where towers are provided in accordance with Section [128-35](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-xii/chapter-8/128-35) (Towers), the allowance in paragraph (f) of such Section shall supersede the underlying dormer provisions.
