# 128-11 Streetscape Regulations

The underlying **_ground floor level_** streetscape provisions set forth in Section [32-30](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-2/32-30) (STREETSCAPE REGULATIONS), inclusive, shall apply, except that:

(a) **_ground floor level_** **_street_** frontages along **_streets_**, or portions thereof, designated on Map 2 (Designated Street Frontages) in the Appendix of this Chapter shall be considered **_Tier C street frontages_**, and
(b) remaining **_ground floor level_** **_street_** frontages shall be considered **_Tier B street frontages_**. However, in lieu of the parking wrap and enclosure provisions of Section [32-32](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-2/32-32), inclusive, the provisions for **_Tier A street frontages_** set forth in Section [32-312](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-2/32-312) (Ground floor level parking restrictions) may be applied.
