# 128-055 Applicability of Article VII, Chapter 5

Within the North Waterfront Subdistrict of the **_Special St. George District_**, the following authorization by the City Planning Commission and certifications by the Chairperson of the City Planning Commission, shall not apply:

Section [75-411](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-5/75-411) (Developments on or over railroad rights-of-way)

Section [75-412](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-5/75-412) (Developments on lots under one and a half acres that include railroad right-of-ways)
