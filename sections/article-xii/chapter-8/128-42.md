# 128-42 Visual Corridors in the North Waterfront Subdistrict

The designated locations for **_visual corridors_**, as defined in Article VI, Chapter 2, are shown on Map 5 in the Appendix to this Chapter. Such **_visual corridors_** shall be provided in accordance with the standards of Sections [62-512](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-2/62-512) (Dimensions of visual corridors) and [62-513](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-2/62-513) (Permitted obstructions in visual corridors), except that:

(a) lighting fixtures in **_visual corridors_** shall be considered permitted obstructions; and
(b) within the **_visual corridor_** provided through Parcel 2 to the Pierhead line within the flexible location zone indicated on Map 5, a portion of a **_building_** shall be a permitted obstruction, provided that such obstruction is located no more than 14 feet above the reference plane of the **_visual corridor_**, and that such obstruction occupies no more than 185,000 cubic feet in total above the reference plane of the **_visual corridor_**.
