# 128-04 Subdistricts

In order to carry out the purposes and provisions of this Chapter, the **_Special St. George District_** shall include three subdistricts: the Upland Subdistrict, the North Waterfront District and the South Waterfront Subdistrict, as shown on Map 1 (Special St. George District and Subdistricts) in the Appendix to this Chapter.
