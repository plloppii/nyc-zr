# 73-41 General Provisions

Subject to the general findings required by Section [73-03](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-3/73-03) and in accordance with the provisions contained in Sections [73-42](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-3/73-42) to [73-53](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-3/73-53), the Board of Standards and Appeals shall have the power to permit modification of **_use_** or parking regulations of this Resolution, and shall have the power to impose appropriate conditions and safeguards thereon.
