# 74-944 Modifications in conjunction with a floor area increase

In Industrial Business Incentive Areas, the City Planning Commission may modify the following in conjunction with an application for a **_floor area_** increase pursuant to Section [74-943](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-4/74-943) (Permitted floor area increase).

(a) Bulk modifications

(1) Yard regulations

In all Industrial Business Incentive Areas, the **_rear yard_** regulations set forth in Section [43-20](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iv/chapter-3/43-20) (YARD REGULATIONS), inclusive, shall be modified pursuant to the provisions of paragraph (c) of Section [74-945](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-4/74-945) (Conditions). In addition, the Commission may modify any other **_yard_** regulations set forth in Section [43-20](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iv/chapter-3/43-20), inclusive.

(2) Height and setback regulations

(i) In Industrial Business Incentive Area 1, the height and setback regulations of Section [43-40](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iv/chapter-3/43-40) (HEIGHT AND SETBACK REGULATIONS), inclusive, shall be modified pursuant to the conditions of paragraph (d) of Section [74-945](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-4/74-945).

(ii) In Industrial Business Incentive Area 2, the Commission may modify the height and setback regulations of Section [43-40](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iv/chapter-3/43-40), inclusive.

(b) Modification for publicly accessible open space

In Industrial Business Incentive Area 1, where a publicly accessible open space is provided pursuant to paragraph (f) of Section [74-945](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-4/74-945), the Commission may modify the provisions of Section [37-70](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-7/37-70) (PUBLIC PLAZAS), inclusive.

(c) Parking and loading modifications

In all Industrial Business Incentive Areas, the Commission may reduce or waive the off-street parking requirements set forth in Section [44-20](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iv/chapter-4/44-20) (REQUIRED ACCESSORY OFF-STREET PARKING SPACES), inclusive, not including bicycle parking, and may also reduce or waive the loading berth requirements as set forth in Section [44-50](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iv/chapter-4/44-50) (OFF-STREET LOADING REGULATIONS), inclusive.
