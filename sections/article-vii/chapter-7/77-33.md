# 77-33 Districts of Different General Use Class

When such boundary is between a **_Residence District_** and a **_Commercial District_**, or between a **_Commercial District_** and a **_Manufacturing District_**, or between a **_Residence District_** and a **_Manufacturing District_**, the provisions of this Section shall apply.
