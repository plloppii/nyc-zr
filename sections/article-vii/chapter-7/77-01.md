# 77-01 Applicability of This Chapter

Whenever any **_zoning lot_** is located in two or more districts in which different **_uses_** are permitted, or in which different **_use_**, **_bulk_**, **_accessory_** off-street parking and loading, or other regulations apply, the provisions of this Chapter shall apply.
