# 77-03 Zoning Lots Existing Prior to Effective Date or Amendment of Resolution

Whenever a **_zoning lot_** is divided by a boundary between two or more districts and such **_zoning lot_** existed on December 15, 1961, or any applicable subsequent amendment thereto, the provisions of this Resolution may be applied to such **_zoning lot_** as set forth in subsequent Sections of this Chapter. Except as specifically provided in this Chapter, each portion of such **_zoning lot_** shall be regulated by all the provisions applicable to the district in which such portion of the **_zoning lot_** is located.
