# 77-21 General Provisions

Whenever a **_zoning lot_** existing on December 15, 1961, or on any applicable subsequent amendment thereto, is divided by a boundary between districts with different **_bulk_** regulations, and the provisions of Sections [77-11](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-7/77-11) (Conditions for Application of Use Regulations to Entire Zoning Lot) or [77-211](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-7/77-211) (Conditions for application of bulk regulations to entire zoning lot) do not apply, the **_bulk_** regulations may apply as set forth in Sections [77-22](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-7/77-22) to [77-29](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-7/77-29), inclusive, relating to Bulk Regulations.
