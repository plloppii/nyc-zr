# 77-331 Use permitted in both districts

For any **_use_** which is permitted in both such districts, the applicable requirements for **_accessory_** off-street parking and loading of that district in which more than 50 percent of the **_zoning lot_** is located shall apply to the entire **_zoning lot_**. The parking spaces or loading berths may be located anywhere on the **_zoning lot_** without regard to district boundaries, provided that such spaces or berths shall conform to all other applicable regulations of this Resolution.
