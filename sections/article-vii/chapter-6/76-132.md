# 76-132 Boundary line parallel to the long dimension of block between parallel streets

In case of parallel **_streets_**, where a boundary line extends parallel to the long dimension of the **_block_** and no dimension is shown, such boundary line shall be considered to coincide with the center line of the **_block_**.
