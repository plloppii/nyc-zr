# 76-141 When distance from street line shown

In cases where the boundary line is shown by a dimension as being located a specific distance from a **_street line_**, this distance shall be considered to be measured from the nearest **_street line_** of the **_street_** from which dimensioned.

In cases where a **_Limited Height District_** boundary line appears to be identical or approximately identical with the boundary line of another district whose location is shown by a dimension, the boundary line of the **_Limited Height District_** shall be considered to be identical with the boundary line of the other district.
