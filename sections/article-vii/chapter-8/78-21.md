# 78-21 Permitted Uses

A **_large-scale residential development_** may include within its area any **_residential uses_**, **_commercial_** uses or **_community facility_** uses permitted in the district or districts in which it is located. The **_commercial_** uses in these **_Commercial Districts_** shall be restricted to **_uses_** permitted in C1, C2 or C4 Districts.
