# 78-44 Modification of Curb Cut Regulations

The City Planning Commission may, upon application, authorize modification of the requirements set forth in Section [25-631](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-5/25-631) (Location and width of curb cuts in certain districts), provided the Commission finds that the proposed location and width of curb cuts:

(a) results in a more efficient traffic circulation system;

(b) permits better site planning; and

(c) does not unduly increase the amount of traffic on nearby local **_streets_** so as to adversely affect **_zoning lots_** outside the **_large-scale residential development_**.
