# 78-241 Location of commercial uses

For any **_large-scale residential development_**, the City Planning Commission, by special permit, may allow **_residential_** and non-**_residential uses_** to be arranged within a **_building_** without regard for the regulations set forth in Section [32-42](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-2/32-42) (Location Within Buildings) when terracing is required because of unusual topographic conditions in a **_large-scale residential development_** having a minimum area of 20 acres.
