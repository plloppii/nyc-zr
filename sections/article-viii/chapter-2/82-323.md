# 82-323 Permitted obstructions within required setback areas

Above a height of 85 feet above **_curb level_**, a dormer shall be allowed as a permitted obstruction pursuant to paragraph (b) of Section [23-413](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-3/23-413) (Permitted obstruction in certain districts) provided that, along a **_wide street_**, or on a **_narrow street_** within 50 feet of a **_wide street_** the **_building_** shall contain special architectural expression lines at a height of 85 feet above **_curb level_**.
