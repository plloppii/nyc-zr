# 82-21 Streetscape Regulations

The underlying **_ground floor level_** streetscape provisions set forth in Section [32-30](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-2/32-30) (STREETSCAPE REGULATIONS) shall apply, except that **_ground floor level_** **_street_** frontages facing Broadway, Columbus Avenue or Amsterdam Avenue, or portions thereof, shall be considered **_Tier C street frontages_**.
