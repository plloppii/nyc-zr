# 88-311 Special floor area regulations in Subdistrict A

Within Subdistrict A, any floor space designated for **_use_** as a **_school_** shall be exempted from the definition of **_floor area_** for the purposes of calculating the permitted **_floor area ratio_** for **_community facility uses_** and the total maximum **_floor area ratio_** of the **_zoning lot_**, provided that such school is either:

(a) a public school, subject to the jurisdiction of the New York City Department of Education, pursuant to an agreement accepted by the School Construction Authority; or
(b) a charter school, subject to the New York State Education Law, pursuant to an agreement with a charter school organization.
