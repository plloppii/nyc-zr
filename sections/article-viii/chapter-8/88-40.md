# 88-40 PARKING AND LOADING REGULATIONS AND CURB CUT LOCATIONS

In the **_Special Hudson Square District_**, the parking regulations applicable in C6-4 Districts, as set forth in Article III, Chapter 6, and as modified, pursuant to Article I, Chapter 3 (Comprehensive Off-street Parking and Loading Regulations in the Manhattan Core) shall apply.
