# 88-051 Applicability of Article I, Chapter 5

The conversion to **_dwelling units_** of non-**_residential buildings_** erected prior to December 31, 1990, or portions thereof, shall be permitted subject to the provisions applicable to **_Commercial Districts_** in Article I, Chapter 5 (Residential Conversion within Existing Buildings), except as superseded or modified by the provisions of this Chapter.
