# 88-20 SIGN REGULATIONS

In the **_Special Hudson Square District_**, **_signs_** are subject to the regulations applicable in C6-4 Districts, as set forth in Section [32-60](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-2/32-60), inclusive.
