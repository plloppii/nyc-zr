# 88-10 SUPPLEMENTAL USE REGULATIONS

All permitted **_uses_** in the underlying districts, as set forth in Section [42-10](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iv/chapter-2/42-10) (USE ALLOWANCES), inclusive, shall comply with the provisions set forth in this Section, inclusive.
