# 87-211 Special floor area regulations

The maximum **_floor area ratio_** for **_zoning lots_** containing only standard **_residences_**, or such **_residential uses_** with **_community facility_** or **_commercial_** **_uses_**, shall be 3.33. The maximum **_floor area ratio_** for **_zoning lots_** containing **_qualifying affordable housing_** or **_qualifying senior housing_** shall be 4.0.
