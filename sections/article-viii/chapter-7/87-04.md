# 87-04 Applicability of District Regulations

Parcels 1, 2, 3 and 4, as shown on Map 1 (Special Harlem River Waterfront District and Parcels) in the Appendix to this Chapter, shall be considered **_waterfront zoning lots_**, notwithstanding the mapping of any **_streets_** on such parcels after June 30, 2009.
