# 87-113 Location of underground uses

Notwithstanding the provisions of Section [62-332](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-2/62-332) (Rear yards and waterfront yards), underground **_uses_**, such as parking garages, shall not be allowed in **_waterfront yards_**.
