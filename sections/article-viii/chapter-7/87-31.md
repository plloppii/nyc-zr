# 87-31 Permitted Obstructions

In the Core and North Subdistricts, the provisions of Section [33-42](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-3/33-42) (Permitted Obstructions) shall apply to all **_buildings_**. In addition, dormers may penetrate a maximum base height in accordance with the provisions of paragraph (b)(1) of Section [23-413](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-3/23-413) (Permitted obstructions in certain districts).
