# 87-20 SPECIAL FLOOR AREA REGULATIONS

The applicable **_floor area_** regulations of the underlying districts and of Article VI, Chapter 2 (Special Regulations Applying in the Waterfront Area), or of Article XII, Chapter 3 (Special Mixed Use District), are modified by the provisions of this Section, inclusive.
