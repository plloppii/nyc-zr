# 87-111 Vehicle storage establishments

Commercial or public utility vehicle storage, including **_accessory_** motor fuel pumps, listed under Use Group IX(C), open or enclosed, shall be a permitted **_use_** on Parcel 5, as shown on Map 1 in the Appendix to this Chapter, provided that:

(a) such **_use_** is the primary **_use_** on Parcel 5;
(b) no more than 10,000 square feet of **_floor area_** shall be provided on Parcel 5; and
(c) a **_shore public walkway_** is provided as set forth in paragraph (a) of Section [87-71](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-viii/chapter-7/87-71) (Special Public Access Provisions).
    The streetscape provisions of Section [87-41](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-viii/chapter-7/87-41), inclusive and the special height and setback regulations of Section [87-32](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-viii/chapter-7/87-32), inclusive, shall not apply to such **_use_**. In lieu thereof, the applicable height and setback provisions of Article VI, Chapter 2 shall apply.
