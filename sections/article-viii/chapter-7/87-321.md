# 87-321 Street wall location

In the Core Subdistrict, the following **_street wall_** location rules shall apply.

Along the following frontages, the **_street wall_** location provisions of paragraph (b) of Section [23-431](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-3/23-431) shall apply, except that the **_street walls_** shall extend to the minimum base heights as specified in Section [87-322](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-viii/chapter-7/87-322) (Base heights and transition heights):

(a) On Parcels 1, 2, 3 and 4, as shown on Map 1 in the Appendix to this Chapter, for frontages along the **_shore public walkway_**, **_supplemental public access areas_**, **_upland connections_**, mapped parkland or **_visual corridors_**, as shown on Map 2; and
(b) On Parcels 5, 6, 7, 8 and 9, as shown on Map 1, for frontages along **_visual corridors_**, or **_upland connections_**, as shown on Map 2.

Along other frontages, no **_street wall_** location provisions shall apply.
