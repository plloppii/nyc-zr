# 87-22 Floor Area Regulations in the North Subdistrict

Within the North Subdistrict, as shown on Map 1 in the Appendix to this Chapter, for all permitted **_uses_**, the **_floor area_** provisions of Article VI, Chapter 2 (Special Regulations Applying in the Waterfront Area) shall apply. However, in no event shall the maximum **_floor area ratio_** for any **_zoning lot_** exceed 4.6.
