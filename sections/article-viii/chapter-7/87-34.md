# 87-34 Special Height and Setback Provision in the South Subdistrict

For **_zoning lots_** that coincide with, or are completely within, the boundaries of Parcel 19 in the South Subdistrict, as shown on Map 1 in the Appendix to this Chapter, the setback provisions of Section [62-34](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-2/62-34) (Height and Setback Regulations on Waterfront Blocks) shall be modified to require an **_initial setback distance_** with a depth of 15 feet from the boundary of a **_shore public walkway_**.
