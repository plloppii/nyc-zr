# 84-143 Off-street loading

Enclosed **_accessory_** off-street loading berths shall be provided in conformity with the requirements set forth in the following table and under rules and regulations promulgated by the Commissioner of Buildings for the **_uses_** listed in the table.

REQUIRED OFF-STREET LOADING BERTHS

| Type of **_Use_** | For **_Floor Area_** (in square feet) | Required Berths |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Food and beverage retailers | First 8,000 | None |
| Food and beverage retailers | Next 17,000 | 1 |
| Food and beverage retailers | Next 15,000 | 1 |
| Food and beverage retailers | Each additional 15,000 or fraction thereof | 1 |
| **_Transient hotels_** | First 100,000 | None |
| **_Transient hotels_** | Next 200,000 | 1 |
| **_Transient hotels_** | Each additional 300,000 or fraction thereof | 1 |
