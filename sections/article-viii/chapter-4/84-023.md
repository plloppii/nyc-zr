# 84-023 Subdistricts

In order to carry out the purposes and provisions of this Chapter, the **_Special Battery Park City District_** is divided into three subdistricts: Zone A, Zone B and Zone C. The location and boundaries of the subdistricts are shown on the District Plan in Appendix 1.
