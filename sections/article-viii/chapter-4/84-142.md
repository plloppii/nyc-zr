# 84-142 Accessory off-street parking spaces for buildings containing hotel uses

For the **_zoning lot_** south of First Place and east of Battery Place, **_accessory_** off-street parking spaces for hotel **_uses_** may be provided at the rate established for **_transient hotels_** in Section [13-12](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-i/chapter-3/13-12) (Permitted Parking for Non-residential Uses) or [13-13](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-i/chapter-3/13-13) (Permitted Parking for Zoning Lots With Multiple Uses), as applicable.
