# 84-342 Off-street loading

The number of required **_accessory_** loading berths in subzone C-2 may be reduced by up to 50 percent of the number required pursuant to Section [36-62](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-6/36-62) (Required Accessory Off-street Loading Berths).
