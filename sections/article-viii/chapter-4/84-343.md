# 84-343 Curb cuts

Curb cuts are permitted only in locations indicated in Appendix 3.5 and along any **_street_** or right-of-way not shown on Appendix 3.5. The aggregate width of all curb cuts for subzone C-1 shall not exceed 50 feet. The aggregate width of all curb cuts for subzone C-2 shall not exceed 60 feet.
