# 84-121 Uses along Esplanade

**_Uses_** fronting on the **_Esplanade_** shall be limited to the **_uses_** listed under Use Groups I, II and III, except that in the areas indicated as permitted **_commercial_** locations in Appendix 2.3, in the lowest **_story_** other than a **_basement_** in any **_building_**, eating or drinking establishments listed under Use Group VI shall be permitted.
