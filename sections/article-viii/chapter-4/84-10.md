# 84-10 ZONE A GENERAL DISTRICT REGULATIONS

Zone A is designed generally to provide for residential development with ancillary retail and service **_uses_**, and **_transient hotels_** as permitted pursuant to Section [84-12](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-viii/chapter-4/84-12). Zone A is divided into six subzones: A-1, A-2, A-3, A-4, A-5 and A-6. The location and boundaries of the subzones are shown in Appendices 2 and 3 of this Chapter.
