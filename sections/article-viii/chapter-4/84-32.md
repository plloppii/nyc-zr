# 84-32 Use Regulations

Use regulations applicable in C6-6 Districts shall apply. In addition, public parking facilities, subject to the provisions of Section [84-341](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-viii/chapter-4/84-341) (Off-street parking), shall be permitted.

The underlying **_ground floor level_** streetscape provisions set forth in Section [32-30](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-2/32-30) (STREETSCAPE REGULATIONS), inclusive, for a C6 District shall apply.
