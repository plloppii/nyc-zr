# 84-13 Bulk Regulations

**_Residential_** and **_community facility_** **_bulk_** regulations otherwise applicable in R10 Districts are modified to the extent set forth in this Section and Sections [84-131](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-viii/chapter-4/84-131) through [84-135](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-viii/chapter-4/84-135), inclusive.

The regulations otherwise applicable in R10 Districts are superseded by the regulations set forth in Sections [84-131](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-viii/chapter-4/84-131) (Floor area regulations), [84-132](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-viii/chapter-4/84-132) (Mandatory front building walls), [84-133](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-viii/chapter-4/84-133) (Front wall recess), [84-134](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-viii/chapter-4/84-134) (Mandatory arcades) and [84-135](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-viii/chapter-4/84-135) (Limited height of buildings).

The provisions of Sections [23-343](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-3/23-343) (Rear yard equivalent requirements) and [24-11](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-4/24-11) (Maximum Floor Area Ratio and Percentage of Lot Coverage), and Article VII, Chapter 8 (Special Regulations Applying to Large-scale Residential Developments) and Chapter 9 (Special Regulations Applying to Large-scale Community Facility Developments), are not applicable.

The provisions of Section [23-371](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-3/23-371) (Distance in between buildings) may be modified by the Battery Park City Authority. Prior to the granting of any such modification, the Authority shall make the following findings:

(a) that such modification will aid in achieving the general purpose and intent of the **_Special Battery Park City District_**, as set forth in Section [84-01](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-viii/chapter-4/84-01);

(b) that such modification will not unduly increase the **_bulk_** of **_buildings_**, the density of population or the intensity of **_use_** on any **_zoning lot_** to the detriment of the occupants of **_buildings_** on such **_zoning lot_** or nearby **_zoning lots_** ;

(c) that such modification will not adversely affect the **_buildings_** on the **_zoning lot_** or nearby **_zoning lots_** by restricting access to light and air; and

(d) that if an open area is provided, at any level, between two **_buildings_**, it shall have a width of not less than eight feet.
