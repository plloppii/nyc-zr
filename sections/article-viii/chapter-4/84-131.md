# 84-131 Floor area regulations

Notwithstanding any other provisions of this Resolution, the permitted **_floor area ratio_** for any **_zoning lot_**:

(a) in subzones A-1 and A-5 shall not exceed 12.0; and
(b) in subzones A-2, A-3 and A-6 shall not exceed 8.0, except that for **_qualifying affordable housing_** or **_qualifying senior housing_**, the permitted **_floor area ratio_** shall be 9.60. In addition, within subzone A-6, the **_floor area ratio_** for the **_zoning lot_** on the southeast corner of Chambers Street and North End Avenue may be increased from 8.0 to a maximum of 12.0, provided that such additional **_floor area_** is occupied by a **_school_**.

The **_floor area_** bonus provisions with respect to R10 Districts shall not apply.
