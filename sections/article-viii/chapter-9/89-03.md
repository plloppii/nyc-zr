# 89-03 District Plan and Maps

The regulations of this Chapter are designed to implement the **_Special Hudson River Park District_** Plan. The District Plan includes the map in the Appendix to this Chapter which is hereby incorporated and made part of this Resolution for the purpose of specifying locations where special regulations and requirements set forth in this Chapter apply.
