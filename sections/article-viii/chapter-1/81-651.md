# 81-651 Floor area bonus for public plazas

For **_non-qualifying sites_** in subareas outside the Grand Central Core Area, as shown on Map 2 (East Midtown Subdistrict and Subareas) in Appendix A of this Chapter, the basic maximum **_floor area ratio_** permitted on such **_zoning lots_** shall be increased, up to the amount specified in Row B of the table in Section [81-65](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-viii/chapter-1/81-65) (Special Floor Area Provisions for All Non-qualifying Sites), where a **_public plaza_** is provided in accordance with the provisions of Section [81-23](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-viii/chapter-1/81-23) (Floor Area Bonus for Public Plazas).
