# 81-67 Special Mandatory District Plan Element Requirements

For **_buildings_** which are **_developed_** or **_enlarged_** within the East Midtown Subdistrict, the applicable provisions of Section [81-40](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-viii/chapter-1/81-40) (MANDATORY DISTRICT PLAN ELEMENTS) shall be modified in accordance with the provisions of this Section, inclusive.
