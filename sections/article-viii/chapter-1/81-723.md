# 81-723 Entertainment-related uses

In the Theater Subdistrict, references to entertainment-related **_uses_** shall include the following **_uses_**, as applicable:

From Use Group III

Museums

From Use Group VI

Eating or drinking establishments

From Use Group VIII

Art galleries

Art, music, dancing or theatrical studios

Auditoriums

Historical exhibits

Production or entertainment studios

Theaters.
