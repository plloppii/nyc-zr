# 81-621 Location of uses in mixed buildings

For **_mixed buildings_** **_developed_** on **_qualifying sites_**, the provisions of Section [32-422](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-2/32-422) (Location of floors occupied by commercial uses) shall be modified such that only the limitations set forth in paragraph (c) of such Section need apply.
