# 81-53 Special Bulk and Urban Design Requirements

In addition to the requirements set forth in Sections [81-25](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-viii/chapter-1/81-25) (General Provisions Relating to Height and Setback of Buildings) and [81-40](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-viii/chapter-1/81-40) (MANDATORY DISTRICT PLAN ELEMENTS), the provisions of this Section shall apply to a **_zoning lot_** having 50 percent or more of its **_lot area_** within the Penn Center Subdistrict. For the purposes of this Section, all such **_zoning lots_** shall be deemed to be entirely within the Subdistrict. If any of the provisions of Sections [81-25](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-viii/chapter-1/81-25), [81-40](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-viii/chapter-1/81-40) and [81-53](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-viii/chapter-1/81-53) are in conflict, the regulations of this Section shall govern.
