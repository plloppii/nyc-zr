# 81-232 Special provisions for zoning lots divided by district boundaries

Where a **_public plaza_** is located on a **_zoning lot_** divided by a district boundary, the **_floor area_** bonus for such **_public plaza_** may be credited to either portion of the **_zoning lot_** regardless of the **_public plaza's_** location or the date when the **_zoning lot_** was created, provided that the amount of such bonus permitted on either portion of the **_zoning lot_** shall not exceed the maximum amount that would be permitted on such portion if it were a separate **_zoning lot_** and subject to all other applicable provisions of Article VII, Chapter 7.
