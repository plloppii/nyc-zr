# 81-21 Floor Area Ratio Regulations

The **_floor area ratio_** regulations of the underlying districts are modified in accordance with the provisions of Section [81-21](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-viii/chapter-1/81-21), inclusive, or Section [81-241](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-viii/chapter-1/81-241) (Maximum floor area ratios for a residential building or the residential portion of a mixed building). However, the provisions of Section [81-211](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-viii/chapter-1/81-211) (Maximum floor area ratio for non-residential or mixed buildings), shall not apply to **_non-residential buildings_** or **_mixed buildings_** in the East Midtown Subdistrict, where the special **_floor area_** provisions of Sections [81-63](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-viii/chapter-1/81-63) (Special Floor Area Provisions for the Vanderbilt Corridor Subarea), [81-64](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-viii/chapter-1/81-64) (Special Floor Area Provisions for Qualifying Sites) or [81-65](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-viii/chapter-1/81-65) (Special Floor Area Provisions for Non-qualifying Sites) shall apply.
