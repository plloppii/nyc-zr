# 81-12 Special Retail Continuity Requirements

Special requirements for allocation of frontage to retail and service **_uses_** along designated **_streets_** are set forth in Section [81-42](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-viii/chapter-1/81-42) (Retail Continuity Along Designated Streets).
