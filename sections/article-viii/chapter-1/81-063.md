# 81-063 Applicability of Article VII, Chapter 5

Within the **_Special Midtown District_**, the following certification by the Chair of the City Planning Commission or authorization by the Commission shall only be applicable as modified below:

Section [75-24](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-5/75-24) (Bulk Modifications Associated with a Transfer of Development Rights from Landmarks)

Section [75-42](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-5/75-42) (Transfer of Development Rights from Landmarks) shall be applicable within the **_Special Midtown District_**, except in the East Midtown Subdistrict and Theater Subdistrict. Such provisions shall be subject to modifications of Section 81-212.
