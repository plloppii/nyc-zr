# 86-41 Parking Regulations for Commercial Uses

For all **_commercial_** **_uses_** located within the **_Special Forest Hills District_**, the parking requirements of Section [36-21](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-6/36-21) (General Provisions) pertaining to the number of **_accessory_** off-street parking spaces required for each type of **_use_** shall be modified as follows:

(a) **_uses_** in parking requirement category (PRC) A shall be required to provide one parking space per 400 square feet of **_floor area_** for all such **_uses_**;
(b) **_uses_** in PRC-B shall be required to provide one parking space per 12 persons rated capacity; and
(c) for **_transient hotels_**, the **_floor area_** used for sleeping accommodations (PRC-F2) shall be required to provide one parking space per 12 guest rooms or suites.
