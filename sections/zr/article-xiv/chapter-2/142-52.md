# 142-52 Accessory Parking on a Roof in Subarea A1

In Subarea A1, the underlying off-street parking regulations of Section [44-11](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iv/chapter-4/44-11) (General Provisions) are modified to permit **_accessory_** parking to be located on the roof of any **_story_** of a **_building_**.
