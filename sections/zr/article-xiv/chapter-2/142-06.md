# 142-06 Modification of Use and Bulk Regulations for Zoning Lots Fronting on Former West 208th Street

Where the **_lot line_** of a **_zoning lot_** coincides with the former boundary of West 208th Street, as shown on Map 1 in the Appendix to this Chapter, such **_lot line_** shall be considered a **_street line_** for the purpose of applying all **_use_** and **_bulk_** regulations of this Resolution.
