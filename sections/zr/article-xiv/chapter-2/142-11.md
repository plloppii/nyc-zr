# 142-11 Permitted Uses

In Subarea B1, as shown on Map 1 (Special Inwood District – Subdistricts and Subareas) in the Appendix to this Chapter, commercial or public utility vehicle storage, open or enclosed, including **_accessory_** motor fuel pumps, listed under Use Group IX(C), shall be a permitted **_use_**, open or enclosed.

In Subareas B2 and B3, as shown on Map 1, **_uses_** listed under Use Group III, shall be permitted, and **_uses_** listed under Food and Beverage Retailers in Use Group VI shall not be limited to **_floor area_** per establishment.

In Subdistrict D, as shown on Map 1, **_self-service storage facilities_** listed under Use Group IX(A), shall be permitted as-of-right in C6-2A Districts.
