# 142-111 Regulations for manufacturing uses in Subareas B2 and B3

In Subareas B2 and B3, as shown on Map 1 (Special Inwood District – Subdistricts and Subareas) in the Appendix to this Chapter, the **_manufacturing uses_** permitted in M1 Districts shall be subject to the modifications set forth in Section [123-20](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-xii/chapter-3/123-20) (SPECIAL USE REGULATIONS), inclusive.
