# 142-24 Floor Area Regulations in Subdistrict D

For **_zoning lots_** that are located partially in a **_Commercial District_** mapped within an R8A District and partially in a **_Commercial District_** mapped within an R9A District, **_residential_** **_floor area_** may transfer across the zoning district boundary from the **_Commercial District_** mapped within an R8A District to the **_Commercial District_** mapped within an R9A District.
