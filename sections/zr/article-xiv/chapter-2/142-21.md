# 142-21 Floor Area Regulations on Waterfront Blocks

On **_waterfront blocks_**, the provisions of Section [62-31](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-2/62-31) (Bulk Computations on Waterfront Zoning Lots) shall be modified so that **_lot area_** that is seaward of the **_shoreline_** shall not be included for the purpose of determining allowable **_floor area_** or to satisfy any other **_bulk_** regulation.
