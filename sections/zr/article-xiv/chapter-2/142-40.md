# 142-40 SPECIAL HEIGHT AND SETBACK REGULATIONS

In Subareas A1 and B2, and in Subdistrict E, as shown on Map 1 (Special Inwood District – Subdistricts and Subareas) in the Appendix to this Chapter, the height and setback regulations of the underlying districts shall apply.

In Subareas A2, A3, B1, B3 and B4, the height and setback regulations of the underlying district regulations are modified by Sections [142-41](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-xiv/chapter-2/142-41) through [142-47](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-xiv/chapter-2/142-47) shall apply, and all heights shall be measured from the **_base plane_**.

In Subdistricts C, D and F, the height and setback regulations of the underlying district regulations are modified by Sections [142-48](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-xiv/chapter-2/142-48) (Special Regulations for Certain Sites in Subdistricts C and F) and [142-49](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-xiv/chapter-2/142-49) (Height and Setback for Certain Zoning Lots in Subdistricts C and D), as applicable.
