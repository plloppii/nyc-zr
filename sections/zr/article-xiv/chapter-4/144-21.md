# 144-21 Floor Area Regulations

The underlying **_floor area_** regulations of Section [43-10](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iv/chapter-3/43-10) (FLOOR AREA REGULATIONS), inclusive, are modified by the provisions of Sections [144-211](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-xiv/chapter-4/144-211) (Floor area ratio) through [144-213](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-xiv/chapter-4/144-213) (Floor area limitations on additional uses).
