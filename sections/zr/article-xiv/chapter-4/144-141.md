# 144-141 Illuminated non-flashing signage

The provisions of Section [42-633](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iv/chapter-2/42-633) (Illuminated or flashing signs) shall be modified to allow one **_accessory_** non-**_flashing_** **_illuminated sign_** to have a **_surface area_** of 750 square feet in each of the following Subareas: the Navy Street Central Subarea, the Flushing East Subarea, between Clinton Avenue and Washington Avenue, the Barge Basin East Subarea, and the Barge Basin West Subarea. All other **_illuminated signs_** shall comply with the requirements of Section [42-633](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iv/chapter-2/42-633).
