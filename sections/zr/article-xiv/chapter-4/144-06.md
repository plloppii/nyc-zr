# 144-06 Single Zoning Lot

For all purposes of this Chapter, the **_Special Brooklyn Navy Yard District_** shall be deemed to be a single **_zoning lot_** except where expressly provided otherwise in this Chapter. **_Floor area_** attributable to the **_zoning lot_** may be located anywhere on the **_zoning lot_** without regard to Subdistrict boundary lines, and all **_floor area_** located within a Subdistrict shall be subject to the **_use_** and **_bulk_** requirements of such Subdistrict.
