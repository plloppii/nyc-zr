# 144-142 Special provisions along district boundaries

The provisions of Section [42-66](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iv/chapter-2/42-66) (Special Provisions Applying Along District Boundaries) shall be modified as follows:

(a) within the Flushing East Subarea, the provisions of Section [42-661](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iv/chapter-2/42-661) (Restrictions along the district boundary located in a street) shall not apply to any **_sign_** provided in accordance with Section [144-141](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-xiv/chapter-4/144-141) (Illuminated non-flashing signage); and
(b) within the Navy Street Central Subarea, the Flushing East Subarea, and the Barge Basin Subdistrict, the orientation provisions of Section [42-662](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iv/chapter-2/42-662) (Restriction on angle and height above curb level) shall not apply to **_signs_** provided in accordance with Section [144-141](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-xiv/chapter-4/144-141).
