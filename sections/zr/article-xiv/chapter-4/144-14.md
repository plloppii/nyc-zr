# 144-14 Special Sign Regulations

In the **_Special Brooklyn Navy Yard District_**, the underlying **_sign_** regulations of Section [42-60](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iv/chapter-2/42-60) (SIGN REGULATIONS) shall apply, except as modified by the provisions of Section [144-141](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-xiv/chapter-4/144-141) (Illuminated non-flashing signage) through Section [144-143](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-xiv/chapter-4/144-143) (Special provisions near certain parks).
