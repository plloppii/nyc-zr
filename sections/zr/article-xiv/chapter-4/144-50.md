# 144-50 SPECIAL PARKING AND LOADING REGULATIONS

Within the **_Special Brooklyn Navy Yard District_**, the **_accessory_** off-street parking and loading regulations of Article IV, Chapter 4 shall not apply, except as specifically set for the in this Section.
