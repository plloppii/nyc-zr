# 146-01 General Provisions

The provisions of this Chapter shall apply within the **_Special Atlantic Avenue Mixed Use District_**. The regulations of all other Chapters of this Resolution are applicable, except as superseded, supplemented or modified by the provisions of this Chapter. In the event of a conflict between the provisions of this Chapter and other regulations of this Resolution, the provisions of this Chapter shall control, except where explicitly stated otherwise in this Chapter.
