# 143-05 Applicability of Article VI, Chapters 4 and 6

In **_flood zones_**, or for **_transit-adjacent sites_** or **_qualifying transit improvement sites_**, as defined in Section [66-11](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-6/66-11) (Definitions), in the event of a conflict between the provisions of this Chapter and the provisions of Article VI, Chapter 4 (Special Regulations Applying in Flood Zones), or Article VI, Chapter 6 (Special Regulations Applying Around Mass Transit Stations), the provisions of Article VI shall control.
