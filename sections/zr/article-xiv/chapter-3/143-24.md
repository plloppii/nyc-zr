# 143-24 Additional Bulk Modifications

For **_buildings_** containing **_residences_**, or portions thereof, the following additional **_bulk_** modifications shall apply.

(a) **_Outer court_** modifications

For **_outer courts_** containing **_legally required windows_**, if an **_outer court_** is less than 20 feet wide, the width of such **_outer court_** shall be at least equal to the depth of such **_outer court_**. If an **_outer court_** is 20 feet or more in width, it may extend to any depth.

(b) **_Inner court_** modifications

For **_inner courts_** containing **_legally required windows_**, the area shall not be less than 600 square feet, and the minimum dimension of such **_inner court_** shall not be less than 20 feet.

The minimum horizontal distance between a **_legally required window_** opening on an **_inner court_** and any wall opposite such window on the same **_zoning lot_** shall not be less than 20 feet.
