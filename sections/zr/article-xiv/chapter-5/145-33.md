# 145-33 Loading Regulations

For the purposes of applying the provisions of Section [36-60](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-6/36-60) (OFF-STREET LOADING REGULATIONS), the **_accessory_** off-street loading berth requirements of C4-4 Districts shall apply in all **_Commercial Districts_**.
