# 145-40 SPECIAL APPROVALS

In Subdistrict A, a **_floor area_** bonus for public realm improvements shall be granted pursuant to the provisions of Section [145-41](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-xiv/chapter-5/145-41) (Special Approvals in Subdistrict A).

In portions of Subdistrict B, special approvals for a transfer of **_floor area_** or **_floor area_** bonus for public realm improvements may be granted pursuant to the provisions of Section [145-42](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-xiv/chapter-5/145-42) (Special Approvals in Subdistrict B).
