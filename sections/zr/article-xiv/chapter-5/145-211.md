# 145-211 Basic floor area regulations

(a) **_Commercial_** **_floor area ratio_**
    In C4-3 or C4-4 Districts, the maximum **_floor area ratio_** for **_commercial_** **_uses_** shall be 4.0.
(b) **_Community facility_** **_floor area ratio_**
    In M1 Districts with an A suffix paired with an R7-3 District, the maximum **_floor area ratio_** for **_community facility_** **_uses_** shall be 6.5.
