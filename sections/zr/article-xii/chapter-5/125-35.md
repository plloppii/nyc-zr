# 125-35 Authorization for Height and Setback Modifications

Within the **_Special Southern Hunters Point District_**, for any **_development_** or **_enlargement_**, the City Planning Commission may modify the regulations set forth in Section [125-30](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-xii/chapter-5/125-30), inclusive, provided the Commission finds that such modifications:

(a) will result in a better distribution of **_bulk_** on the **_zoning lot_** and will not adversely affect access to light and air for surrounding public access areas, **_streets_** and properties;

(b) are consistent with the goals of the Special District to provide flexibility of architectural design and encourage more attractive **_building_** forms; and

(c) will result in a **_development_** or **_enlargement_** that enhances the streetscape and is compatible with development in the surrounding area.

The Commission may prescribe additional conditions and safeguards to minimize adverse effects of the **_development_** or **_enlargement_** on the character of the surrounding area.
