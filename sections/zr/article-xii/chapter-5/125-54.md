# 125-54 Off-site Facilities in the East River Subdistrict

In the East River Subdistrict, all **_accessory_** off-street parking spaces may be provided within parking facilities on **_zoning lots_** other than the same **_zoning lot_** as the **_uses_** to which they are **_accessory_**, provided such parking facilities are located within the **_Special Southern Hunters Point District_**, and the number of parking spaces within such facility shall not exceed the combined maximum number of spaces permitted on each off-site **_zoning lot_** using such facility, less the number of any spaces provided on such **_zoning lots_**.
