# 125-55 Location of Curb Cuts

Curb cuts are permitted only in the locations indicated on Map 10 (Permitted Curb Cut Locations) in Appendix A of this Chapter. The aggregate width of all curb cuts provided for any **_building_** shall not exceed 50 feet.
