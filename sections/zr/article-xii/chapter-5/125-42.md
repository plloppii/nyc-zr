# 125-42 Publicly Accessible Open Area Requirements on

Parcels B, D, E and F

Map 7 (Mandatory Sidewalk Widenings and Publicly Accessible Open Area), in Appendix A of this Chapter, shows locations where open areas are permitted or required on Parcels B, D, E and F in the East River Subdistrict. Where any such area is provided, it shall be publicly accessible and comply with the standards of Sections [37-741](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-7/37-741) (Seating), [37-743](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-7/37-743) (Lighting and electrical power), [37-744](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-7/37-744) (Litter receptacles), [37-745](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-7/37-745) (Bicycle parking), [37-746](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-7/37-746) (Drinking fountains), [37-747](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-7/37-747) (Public space signage), [37-76](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-7/37-76) (Mandatory Allocation of Frontages for Permitted Uses) and [37-77](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-7/37-77) (Maintenance).

In addition, the provisions of Section [37-742](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-7/37-742) (Planting and trees) shall apply to such open areas, and shall be modified to require that:

(a) at least 30 percent of each open area be comprised of planting beds;

(b) at least two four-inch caliper trees or three ornamental trees be provided within such open areas on Parcels D and F; and

(c) no trees shall be required within the open areas on Parcels B and E.
