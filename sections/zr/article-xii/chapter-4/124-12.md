# 124-12 Regulation of Commercial Uses in Area B

(a) Within Area B, as shown on Map 1 in the Appendix to this Chapter, **_commercial_** and special permit **_uses_** shall be limited to those **_uses_** permitted in a C1-4 District, provided that **_commercial_** **_uses_** shall be located no more than 100 feet from a **_connector street_**, the present or former 34th Avenue or Willets Point Boulevard, or Area A, as shown on Map 1 in the Appendix to this Chapter.

(b) Within Area B, **_uses_** permitted in the underlying district shall be permitted within an area east and south of the present or former Willets Point Boulevard and contiguous with Area A, provided that such area shall be limited to 115,000 square feet in land area.
