# 124-193 Location of office uses in towers

**_Uses_** listed under Offices in Use Group VII located on any **_story_** entirely above 120 feet in height shall be located entirely within 600 feet of the intersection of Roosevelt Avenue and 126th Street, as depicted on Map 2 (Location Requirements for Convention Center, Cinema and Office Tower) in the Appendix to this Chapter.
