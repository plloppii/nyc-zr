# 123-50 MODIFICATION OF ARTICLE V, CHAPTER 2 (NON-CONFORMING USES)

In **_Special Mixed Use Districts_**, a **_non-conforming use_** may be changed only to a conforming **_use_**.

The following Sections of Article V, Chapter 2 (Non-conforming Uses), shall not apply: Sections [52-32](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-v/chapter-2/52-32) through [52-37](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-v/chapter-2/52-37), inclusive; Sections [52-43](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-v/chapter-2/52-43) through [52-45](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-v/chapter-2/52-45), inclusive; Sections [52-54](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-v/chapter-2/52-54), [52-56](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-v/chapter-2/52-56), [52-62](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-v/chapter-2/52-62), [52-72](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-v/chapter-2/52-72), [52-731](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-v/chapter-2/52-731), [52-732](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-v/chapter-2/52-732), [52-74](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-v/chapter-2/52-74), and [52-75](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-v/chapter-2/52-75).
