# 123-653 Additional height and setback provisions

The additional height and setback regulations set forth in Section [23-436](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-3/23-436) shall apply, except as follows:

(a) for the purposes of applying such provisions, references to the **_street wall_** location provisions of Section [23-431](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-3/23-431) shall be superseded by those of Section [123-651](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-xii/chapter-3/123-651) and base heights by the provisions of Section [123-652](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-xii/chapter-3/123-652); and
(b) for the purposes of applying the **_street wall_** modifications on **_corner lots_**, where a **_zoning lot_** is bounded by only one **_street line_** along a **_street_** frontage where a **_Commercial District_** or **_Special Mixed Use District_** is mapped along the entire **_block_** frontage, the **_street wall_** shall be applied along such frontage.
