# 123-80 MODIFICATION OF ARTICLE V, CHAPTER 4 (NON-COMPLYING BUILDINGS)

In Article V, Chapter 4 (Non-complying Buildings), Section [54-311](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-v/chapter-4/54-311) (Buildings containing rooming units), shall not apply.
