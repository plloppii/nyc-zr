# 123-71 Manufacturing and Commercial Uses

For **_manufacturing_** and **_commercial_** **_uses_**, the **_accessory_** off-street parking and loading regulations of the designated M1 District, as set forth in Article IV, Chapter 4 (Accessory Off-street Parking and Loading Regulations), shall apply.
