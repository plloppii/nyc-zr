# 123-21 Modifications to M1 Use Regulations

In **_Special Mixed Use Districts_**, the regulations for M1 Districts are modified as follows:

(a) Use Group IV
    The provisions of M1 Districts set forth in Section [42-14](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iv/chapter-2/42-14) (Use Group IV – Public Service Facilities and Infrastructure), inclusive, shall apply, except that for **_uses_** listed under Use Group IV(B), the provisions of C7 Districts set forth in Section [32-14](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-2/32-14) (Use Group IV – Public Service Facilities and Infrastructure), inclusive, shall apply.
(b) Use Group VI
    The provisions of M1 Districts set forth in Section [42-16](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iv/chapter-2/42-16) (Use Group VI – Retail and Services), inclusive, shall apply, except that for **_uses_** listed in paragraph (c) of Section [42-163](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iv/chapter-2/42-163) (Use Group VI – uses subject to additional conditions), the provisions of Section [123-22](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-xii/chapter-3/123-22) (Additional Conditions for Certain Uses) shall apply.
(c) Use Group IX
    The provisions of M1 Districts set forth in Section [42-19](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iv/chapter-2/42-19) (Use Group IX – Storage), inclusive, shall apply, except that all **_uses_** listed under Use Group IX(B) shall not be permitted. In addition, the storage of substances in quantities required to file a Risk Management Plan for Extremely Hazardous Substances shall be subject to the provisions of Section [123-22](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-xii/chapter-3/123-22) (Additional Conditions for Certain Uses).
(d) Use Group X
    The provisions of C7 Districts set forth in Section [32-20](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-2/32-20) (Use Group X – Production Uses), inclusive, shall apply, except that:
    (1) the size limitations on the ground floor, as set forth in paragraph (c) of Section [32-202](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-2/32-202) (Use Group X – uses subject to size limitations) shall not apply; and
    (2) the additional conditions of Section [32-203](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-2/32-203) (Use Group X – additional conditions) shall be superseded by the provisions of Section [123-22](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-xii/chapter-3/123-22) (Additional Conditions for Certain Uses).
