# 126-231 Minimum required front yards

**_Front yards_** shall be provided with a depth of 15 feet, except for:

(a) **_zoning lots_** with frontage along the Whitestone Expressway, where **_front yards_** shall be provided with a depth of 20 feet; and

(b) **_corner lots_**, where one **_front yard_** may have a depth of 10 feet.
