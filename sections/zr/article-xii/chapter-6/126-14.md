# 126-14 Sign Regulations

Within the **_Special College Point District_**, no **_advertising signs_** shall be permitted. **_Signs_** may be **_illuminated_** but not **_flashing_**. The provisions of Section [32-671](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-2/32-671) (Special provisions applying along district boundaries) shall not apply for **_zoning lots_** with frontage on the Whitestone Expressway.

No **_sign_** displayed from the wall of a **_building or other structure_** shall extend above the parapet wall or roof of such **_building or other structure_**, and no **_signs_** shall be permitted on the roof of any **_building_**.
