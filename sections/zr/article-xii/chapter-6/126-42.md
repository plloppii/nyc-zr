# 126-42 Authorization for Reduction of Required Parking

Within the commercial areas identified on the **_Special College Point District_** Map, the City Planning Commission may authorize a reduction of the parking requirement of Section [44-21](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iv/chapter-4/44-21) (General Provisions) and paragraphs (b) and (c) of Section [126-31](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-xii/chapter-6/126-31) (Parking Regulations) by an amount not to exceed 50 percent, provided that the Commission finds that the proposed parking is sufficient for the **_use_** proposed.
