# 126-32 Loading Regulations

(a) Off-street loading berths shall not be permitted between a **_street wall_** and a **_street line_**. However, the City Planning Commission may authorize off-street loading berths located between a **_street wall_** and a **_street line_**, provided that the Commission finds that such loading berths can not be located elsewhere on the **_zoning lot_** and that such loading berths shall be enclosed or screened to the greatest extent practicable.
(b) All open off-street loading berths shall be screened from all adjoining **_zoning lots_** pursuant to the standards of Section [44-565](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iv/chapter-4/44-565) (Screening).
