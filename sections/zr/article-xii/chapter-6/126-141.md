# 126-141 Special sign regulations in M2-1 Districts

In M2-1 Districts, only non-**_illuminated signs_** and **_signs with indirect illumination_** are permitted. The surface area of all **_signs_** on a **_zoning lot_** shall be limited to:

(a) one **_sign_** displayed from the wall of a **_building_** limited to 1.5 square feet of **_surface area_** for each linear foot of **_street wall_** or 150 square feet, whichever is less, and further provided that such **_signs_** shall not exceed a height of eight feet, and shall not extend to a height greater than 20 feet above **_curb level_** ; and

(b) one **_sign_** not affixed to a **_building_** limited to one square foot of **_surface area_** for each 200 square feet of **_street wall_** or 30 square feet, whichever is less, and further provided that such **_signs_** shall not exceed a height of five feet above **_curb level_**.
