# 126-12 Performance Standards

Section [42-40](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iv/chapter-2/42-40) (PERFORMANCE STANDARDS) shall be modified so that the performance standards of an M1 District apply throughout the **_Special College Point District_**.
