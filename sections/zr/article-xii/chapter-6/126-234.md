# 126-234 Planting requirement in front yards

**_Front yards_** shall be planted, except at entrances to and exits from the **_building_** and except for access driveways to **_accessory_** parking and loading areas. **_Front yards_** shall not be used for **_accessory_** off-street parking, **_accessory_** off-street loading, or for storage or processing of any kind.
