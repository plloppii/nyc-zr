# 126-21 Street Tree Planting

Within the **_Special College Point District_**, all **_developments_**, or **_enlargements_** of 20 percent or more in **_floor area_**, shall provide **_street_** trees in accordance with Section [26-41](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-6/26-41) (Street Tree Planting). In addition, any **_building_** where 20 percent or more of the **_floor area_** is **_converted_** shall provide **_street_** trees in accordance with Section [26-41](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-6/26-41). The **_street_** frontage used to calculate the number of required trees may exclude the **_street_** frontage occupied by curb cuts serving **_uses_** listed in Use Groups 16B, 16C, 16D, 17 and 18.
