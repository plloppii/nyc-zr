# 126-11 Recreational Uses

The regulations of Section [42-10](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iv/chapter-2/42-10) (USE ALLOWANCES), shall be modified to allow the following **_uses_** as-of-right within the **_Special College Point District_**:

From Use Group I

Golf courses

Outdoor racket courts

Outdoor skating rinks

**_Public parks_**, playgrounds or private parks

From Use Group III

Non-commercial recreation centers.
