# 121-322 Minimum distance between buildings

On In M1 Districts paired with a **_Residence District_**, on any single **_zoning lot_**, if two or more **_buildings_** or portions of **_buildings_** are detached from one another at any level, such **_buildings_** or portions of **_buildings_** shall at no point be less than eight feet apart.
