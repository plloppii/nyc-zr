# 121-11 Regulations for M1-9A Districts

In M1 Districts with an A suffix without a paired **_Residence District_**, **_uses_** listed under Use Groups IV(B), IX and X shall be limited to those permitted within M1 Districts paired with **_Residence Districts_** pursuant to the provisions of Section [123-21](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-xii/chapter-3/123-21) (Modifications to M1 Use Regulations), inclusive.
