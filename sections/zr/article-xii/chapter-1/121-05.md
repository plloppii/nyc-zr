# 121-05 Applicability of Article XII, Chapter 3

In the **_Special Midtown South Mixed Use District_**, M1 Districts are paired with a **_Residence District_**. In paired districts, the special **_use_**, **_bulk_**, and parking and loading provisions of Article XII, Chapter 3 (Special Mixed Use District) shall apply, except where modified by the provisions of this Chapter.

Notwithstanding the provisions of Section [123-10](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-xii/chapter-3/123-10) (GENERAL PROVISIONS), in the event of a conflict between the provisions of this Chapter and the provisions of Article XII, Chapter 3, the provisions of this Chapter shall control.
