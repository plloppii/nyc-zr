# 127-232 Street wall location regulations

Along **_street_** frontages where a sidewalk widening is required pursuant to Map 3 in the Appendix to this Chapter, the **_street wall_** location requirements of this Section shall apply from the interior boundary of such sidewalk widening.

(a) Along primary **_street_** frontages
    The **_street wall_** location provisions of paragraph (b) of Section [35-631](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-5/35-631) shall apply along primary **_street_** frontages, as shown on Map 3, except that the **_street wall_** shall rise to at least the minimum base height as specified in Section [127-233](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-xii/chapter-7/127-233) (Base heights and setback regulations), or the height of the building, whichever is lower. The requirements of this paragraph shall not apply to the portion of a **_building_** adjacent to the major portion of a publicly accessible area, as specified in Section [127-43](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-xii/chapter-7/127-43) (Publicly Accessible Area).
(b) Along secondary **_street_** frontages
    Along **_street_** frontages that are not designated as primary **_street_** frontages, **_street walls_** may be located at any distance from the **_street line_**.
