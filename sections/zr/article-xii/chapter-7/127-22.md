# 127-22 Special Yard Regulations

On **_waterfront zoning lots_**, the **_waterfront yard_** provisions of Section [62-33](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-2/62-33) (Special Yard and Lot Coverage Regulations on Waterfront Blocks) shall apply, except as modified as follows:

(a) for all **_waterfront zoning lots_**, as defined in Section [62-11](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-2/62-11) (Definitions), whose **_developments_** are comprised **_predominantly_**, as defined in Section [62-11](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-2/62-11), of **_uses_** in listed under Use Groups IV(B), IX or X, a **_waterfront yard_**, as also defined in Section [62-11](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-2/62-11), shall be provided in accordance with the provisions of Section [62-332](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-2/62-332) (Rear yards and waterfront yards); and
(b) the grading requirements of paragraph (b) of Section [62-332](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-2/62-332) (Rear yards and waterfront yards) may be modified pursuant to a certification by the Chairperson of the City Planning Commission as set forth in Section [127-61](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-xii/chapter-7/127-61) (Certification for Interim Grading Conditions).

On **_zoning lots_** that are not **_waterfront zoning lots_**, no **_yard_** regulations shall apply.
