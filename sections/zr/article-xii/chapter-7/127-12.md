# 127-12 Sign Regulations

For M1 Districts paired with a **_Residence District_**, the provisions regulating **_signs_** in C4 Districts, as set forth in Section [32-60](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-2/32-60) (SIGN REGULATIONS), inclusive, shall apply for any **_signs_**.
