# 127-231 Permitted obstructions

The permitted obstruction provisions of paragraph (c) of Section [62-34](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-2/62-34) (Height and Setback Regulations on Waterfront Blocks) shall be modified as follows:

(a) the applicable dormer provisions shall be modified pursuant to the provisions of paragraph (c)(1) of Section [127-233](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-xii/chapter-7/127-233) (Base heights and setback regulations);
(b) the penthouse regulations of paragraph (c)(1) of Section [62-34](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-2/62-34) shall not apply; and
(c) the maximum height of any permitted obstructions shall be determined in accordance with the provisions of Article VI, Chapter 1 (Special Regulations Applying Around Major Airports), except where modified by certification of the Chairperson of the City Planning Commission pursuant to Section [127-236](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-xii/chapter-7/127-236) (Certification to modify maximum height of building or other structure).
