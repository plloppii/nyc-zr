# 127-51 Modified Applicability for Visual Corridors and Waterfront Public Access Areas

The applicability provisions for **_visual corridors_** pursuant to Section [62-51](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-2/62-51) (Applicability of Visual Corridor Requirements) and **_waterfront public access areas_** pursuant to Section [62-52](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-2/62-52) (Applicability of Waterfront Public Access Area Requirements) shall apply, except as modified as follows:

(a) **_developments_** comprised predominantly of **_uses_** listed under Use Groups IV(B), IX or X, except for docks, are subject to the special requirements for **_visual corridors_** set forth in Section [127-52](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-xii/chapter-7/127-52); and
(b) **_developments_** comprised predominantly of **_uses_** listed under Use Groups IV(B), IX or X shall provide a minimum amount of **_waterfront public access area_** in accordance with the provisions of Section [62-58](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-2/62-58) (Requirements for Water-Dependent Uses and Other Developments). Within such **_waterfront public access area_**, a circulation path shall be provided with a minimum clear width of at least 10 feet and shall connect with either an adjoining **_shore public walkway_** or additional circulation paths on adjoining **_zoning lots_**.
