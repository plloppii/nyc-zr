# 128-051 Applicability of Article I, Chapter 2

The definition of “lower density growth management area” in Section [12-10](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-i/chapter-2/12-10) (DEFINITIONS) shall be modified to exclude all districts within the **_Special St. George District_**.
