# 128-054 Applicability of Article VII, Chapter 4

Within the North Waterfront Subdistrict of the **_Special St. George District_**, the following special permits shall not apply:

Section [74-194](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-4/74-194) (Public parking garages or public parking lots outside high density areas)

Section [74-61](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-4/74-61) (Development on Lots that Include Railroad Right-of-Ways).

In addition, the provisions of the following special permits, as applicable, shall be deemed to be modified when an application pursuant to Section [128-61](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-xii/chapter-8/128-61) (Special Permit for North Waterfront Sites) for Parcel 1 or Parcel 2, as shown on the maps in the Appendix to this Chapter, under application numbers C130317ZSR or C130318ZSR, as applicable, has been approved:
