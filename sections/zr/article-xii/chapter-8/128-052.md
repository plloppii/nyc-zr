# 128-052 Applicability of Article III, Chapter 6

The provisions of Section [36-76](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-6/36-76) (Waiver or Reduction of Spaces for Subsidized Housing) shall not apply in the **_Special St. George District_**.
