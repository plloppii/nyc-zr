# 128-52 Special Floor Area Regulations

The **_floor area_** of a **_building_** shall not include floor space used for off-street parking spaces provided in any **_story_** that complies with the provisions of Section [128-54](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-xii/chapter-8/128-54) (Location of Accessory Off-street Parking Spaces).
