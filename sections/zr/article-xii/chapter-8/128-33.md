# 128-33 Maximum Base Height

The maximum height of a **_building or other structure_** before setback shall be as specified on Map 3 (Minimum and Maximum Base Heights) in the Appendix to this Chapter. When a **_building_** fronts on two intersecting **_streets_** for which different maximum base heights apply, the higher base height may wrap around to the **_street_** with the lower base height for a distance of up to 100 feet. At a height not lower than the minimum base height, and not higher than the maximum base height, a setback shall be provided in accordance with the provisions of Section [23-433](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-3/23-433) (Standard setback regulations).
