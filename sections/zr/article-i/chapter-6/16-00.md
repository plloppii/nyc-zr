# 16-00 GENERAL PURPOSES

The provisions of this Chapter establish special comprehensive regulations for off-street parking in the Long Island City area, as defined in Section [16-02](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-i/chapter-6/16-02) (Definitions).

These regulations will allow parking to be provided in a manner that supports a mass transit and pedestrian-oriented central mixed use district.
