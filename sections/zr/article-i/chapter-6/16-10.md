# 16-10 PERMITTED OFF-STREET PARKING IN THE LONG ISLAND CITY AREA

No parking shall be required for the **_Long Island City area_**. Off-street parking spaces located within **_accessory_** off-street parking facilities, **_public parking lots_** and **_public parking garages_** in the **_Long Island City area_** shall be allowed only as set forth in this Section, inclusive.

All such parking facilities shall be subject to the applicable regulations set forth in Section [16-20](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-i/chapter-6/16-20).
