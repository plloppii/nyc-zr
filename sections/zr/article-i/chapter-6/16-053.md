# 16-053 Applicability of Special Purpose Districts within the Long Island City area

In addition to the provisions of this Chapter, further requirements relating to the **_Long Island City area_** can be found in the following Special Purpose Districts:

(a) the **_Special Long Island City Mixed Use District_**, as set forth in Section [117-54](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-xi/chapter-7/117-54) (Off-street Parking and Loading Regulations); and

(b) the **_Special Southern Hunters Point District_**, as set forth in Section [125-50](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-xii/chapter-5/125-50) (PARKING REGULATIONS), inclusive.
