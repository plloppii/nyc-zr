# 16-352 Public parking garages and public parking lots

The City Planning Commission may, by special permit, allow **_public parking garages_** and **_public parking lots_** not otherwise permitted, pursuant to the applicable provisions of Section [74-195](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-4/74-195) (Public parking garages or public parking lots in high density central areas).
