# 16-15 Permitted Public Parking Garages

Within Areas A or B, as shown on the map in Section [16-03](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-i/chapter-6/16-03), **_public parking garages_** shall be permitted only in accordance with the special permit provisions of Section 16-352. However, notwithstanding any underlying district regulations, within Subarea C, as shown on the map, **_public parking garages_** with a maximum capacity of 150 spaces shall be permitted as-of-right within any zoning district.
