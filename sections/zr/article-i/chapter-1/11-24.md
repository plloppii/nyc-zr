# 11-24 Equivalence of Use Group Classifications

**_Uses_** existing prior to June 6, 2024, within a Use Group that on June 6, 2024, were recategorized into another Use Group shall not be considered a change of **_use_** for the purposes of applying the provisions of this Resolution.
