# 11-14 Incorporation of Maps

The location and boundaries of the districts established by this Resolution are shown upon the **_zoning maps_**, which are hereby incorporated into the provisions of this Resolution. The said **_zoning maps_** in their entirety, including all amendments thereto, shall be as much a part of this Resolution as if fully set forth and described herein.
