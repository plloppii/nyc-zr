# 11-16 Pierhead Lines, Bulkhead Lines and Marginal Streets

The pierhead and bulkhead lines shown on the **_zoning maps_** shall be the lines adopted by the United States Army Corps of Engineers, except where a New York City pierhead or bulkhead line is adopted, in which case the New York City line shall be the governing line for the purposes of this Resolution. In the event a provision of this Resolution refers to a pierhead or bulkhead line and no such line is shown on the **_zoning map_**, then the **_shoreline_** shall control.

Marginal streets, ways, places or wharves shown on the **_zoning maps_** shall not be deemed to be **_streets_** for the purposes of this Resolution, unless expressly stated otherwise.
