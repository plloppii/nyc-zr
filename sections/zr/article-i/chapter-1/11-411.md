# 11-411 Renewals

Where no limitation as to duration of the **_use_** was imposed at the time of authorization, such **_use_** may be continued. Where such **_use_** was authorized subject to a term of years, such **_use_** may be continued until the expiration of the term, and thereafter, the agency which originally authorized such **_use_** may, in appropriate cases, extend the period of continuance for one or more terms of not more than 10 years each. The agency may prescribe appropriate conditions and safeguards to minimize adverse effects of such **_use_** on the character of the neighborhood.
