# 13-34 Modification of Minimum Size of Loading Berth

For all permitted or required **_accessory_** loading berths, the minimum length requirements for uses in LRC-A, set forth in Sections [36-661](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-6/36-661) (Size of required berths) and [44-561](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iv/chapter-4/44-561) (Size of required berths), shall be 37 feet.
