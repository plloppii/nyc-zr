# 13-30 OFF-STREET LOADING REGULATIONS IN THE MANHATTAN CORE

All **_accessory_** off-street loading berths provided in the **_Manhattan Core_** after May 8, 2013, shall comply with the applicable provisions of this Section, inclusive.
