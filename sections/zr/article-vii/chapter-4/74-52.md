# 74-52 Special Permit to Remove Required Parking

Outside the **_Inner Transit Zone_**, the City Planning Commission may permit the reduction or removal of **_accessory_** off-street parking spaces required pursuant to Section [25-20](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-5/25-20) (REQUIRED ACCESSORY OFF-STREET PARKING SPACES FOR RESIDENCES), provided the Commission finds that such reduction or removal:

(a) will not impede access to existing **_accessory_** off-street parking spaces on adjoining **_zoning lots_**; and

(b) will not have undue adverse effects on residents, businesses or **_community facilities_** in the surrounding area;

The Commission may prescribe appropriate conditions and safeguards to minimize adverse effects on the character of the surrounding area.
