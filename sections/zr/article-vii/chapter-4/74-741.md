# 74-741 Requirements for application

An application to the City Planning Commission for the grant of a special permit pursuant to Section [74-74](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-4/74-74) for a **_large-scale general development_** shall include a site plan showing the boundaries of the **_large-scale general development_** and the proposed location and **_use_** of all **_buildings or other structures_** on each **_zoning lot_** comprising the **_large-scale general development_**.

However, for applications proceeding pursuant to the ownership provisions of paragraph (e) of Section [74-742](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-4/74-742), such site plan need only show the applicable portion of the **_large-scale general development_** as set forth in paragraph (e)(1) or (e)(2) of Section [74-742](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-4/74-742).
