# 74-85 Covered Pedestrian Space

C4-7 C5-2 C5-3 C5-4 C5-5 C6-4 C6-5 C6-6 C6-7 C6-8 C6-9 C6-11 C6-12 M1-6A M1-7A M1-8A M1-9A

In the districts indicated and in the **_Special Midtown South Mixed Use District_**, the City Planning Commission may permit **_floor area_** bonuses for **_covered pedestrian space_** in accordance with the provisions of Sections [74-851](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-4/74-851) through [74-853](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-4/74-853), inclusive.
