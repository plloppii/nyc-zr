# 74-821 Court houses

In all **_Commercial Districts_**, the City Planning Commission may permit modifications of the applicable **_bulk_** regulations so as to allow the same **_bulk_** regulations as would apply for a **_community facility building_** in the applicable **_Commercial District_** and may permit modifications of the applicable regulations in Sections [33-26](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-3/33-26) to [33-30](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-3/33-30), inclusive, relating to Yard Regulations or Sections [33-41](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-3/33-41) to [33-45](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-3/33-45), inclusive, relating to Height and Setback Regulations. The Commission shall find that because of site limitations such modifications are necessary for the proper design and operation of the court house.

The Commission may prescribe appropriate conditions and safeguards to minimize adverse effects on the character of the surrounding area.
