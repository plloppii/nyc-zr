# 74-54 Rear Yard Modifications

In C4, C6, C7, C8, M1, M2 or M3 Districts, for **_public parking garages_** with more than 150 spaces, the City Planning Commission may permit modifications of the applicable regulations in Sections [33-26](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-3/33-26) to [33-30](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-3/33-30), inclusive, and Sections [43-26](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iv/chapter-3/43-26) to [43-31](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iv/chapter-3/43-31), inclusive, relative to **_rear yard_** regulations, provided the following findings are made:

(a) that the **_public parking garage_** will alleviate excessive on-street parking demand and thereby relieve traffic congestion in the area; and

(b) that because of site limitations such modification is necessary for the proper design and operation of the **_public parking garage_**.

The Commission shall consider the characteristics of surrounding development and may prescribe appropriate conditions and safeguards to minimize adverse effects on the character of adjacent areas.
