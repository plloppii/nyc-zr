# 77-12 Application of Use Regulations Under All Other Conditions

Whenever a **_zoning lot_** is divided by a boundary between districts in which different **_uses_** are permitted and the provisions of Section [77-11](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-7/77-11) (Conditions for Application of Use Regulations to Entire Zoning Lot) do not apply, the applicable **_use_** regulations for each district shall apply to that portion of the **_zoning lot_** located within such district, except as provided in Section [73-42](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-3/73-42) (Enlargement of Uses Across District Boundaries) or [73-52](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-3/73-52) (Modifications for Zoning Lots Divided by District Boundaries).

The regulations governing **_use_** are set forth in Article II, Chapter 2; Article III, Chapter 2; and Article IV, Chapter 2.
