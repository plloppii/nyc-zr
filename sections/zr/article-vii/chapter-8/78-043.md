# 78-043 Requirements for findings

The requirements for findings as set forth in this Chapter shall constitute a condition precedent to the grant of any such modification by special permit or otherwise. The decision or determination of the City Planning Commission shall set forth each required finding in each grant of modifications for a **_large-scale residential development_**. Each finding shall be supported by substantial evidence or data considered by the Commission in reaching its final decision.
