# 78-041 Authorization by Commission

For any **_large-scale residential development_** for which proposed modifications of the applicable district regulations are limited to those which may be granted in accordance with the provisions of Sections [78-21](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-8/78-21) to [78-23](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-8/78-23), inclusive, relating to Use Regulations, Sections [78-311](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-8/78-311) and [78-313](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-8/78-313) relating to Bulk Regulations, Section [78-41](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-8/78-41) (Location of Accessory Parking Spaces), or Sections [78-51](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-8/78-51) to [78-53](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-8/78-53), inclusive, relating to Subdivision of Large-Scale Residential Developments, the City Planning Commission may grant such modifications in accordance with the provisions of such specified Sections and may prescribe appropriate conditions and safeguards thereon.
