# 78-111 Public facilities report

Within one month after a request from the Chairperson of the City Planning Commission, the Department of City Planning shall make a report, based upon information from the Department of Education and other appropriate City Departments, on the anticipated effect of the proposed **_large-scale residential development_** on the existing capacity of public **_schools_** or other public facilities serving the area in which the proposed **_large-scale residential development_** is to be located.
