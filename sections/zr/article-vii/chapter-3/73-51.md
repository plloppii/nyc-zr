# 73-51 Modification of Supplementary Use Regulations

In C1, C2, C3, C4, C5, C6 or C7 Districts, the Board of Standards and Appeals may permit public utility radio or television facilities which do not comply with the applicable provisions of Section [32-42](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-2/32-42) (Location Within Buildings) to be located on the top **_story_** or the roof of a **_building_** and may prescribe appropriate conditions and safeguards to minimize adverse effects on **_uses_** occupying lower **_stories_** or on the character of the surrounding area.

In addition, the Board may permit public utility antennas, microwave platforms and dishes or other radio or television equipment to penetrate the maximum height limit or the **_sky exposure plane_** set forth in Section [33-43](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-3/33-43) (Maximum Height of Walls and Required Setbacks) or [33-44](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-3/33-44) (Alternate Front Setbacks) provided that such equipment shall not exceed 20 feet in height.
