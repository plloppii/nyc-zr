# 73-141 Radio or Television Towers

In all districts, the Board of Standards and Appeals may permit non-**_accessory_** radio or television towers listed under Use Group IV(B), provided that it finds that the proposed location, design, and method of operation of such tower will not have a detrimental effect on the privacy, quiet, light and air of the neighborhood.

The Board may prescribe appropriate conditions and safeguards to minimize adverse effects on the character of the surrounding area.
