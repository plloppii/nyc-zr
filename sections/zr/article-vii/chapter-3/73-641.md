# 73-641 Integration of new buildings or enlargements with existing buildings

For any such new **_building_** or **_enlargement_**, subject to the required findings set forth in this Section, the Board of Standards and Appeals may permit modifications of the applicable regulations in Sections [24-38](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-4/24-38), [33-28](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-3/33-28) or [43-28](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iv/chapter-3/43-28) (Special Provisions for Through Lots), or in Sections [24-50](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-4/24-50) through [24-55](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-4/24-55), inclusive, paragraphs (b) through (d) of Section [24-56](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-4/24-56), Sections [33-40](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-3/33-40) through [33-45](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-3/33-45), inclusive, or Sections [43-41](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iv/chapter-3/43-41) through [43-45](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iv/chapter-3/43-45), inclusive, relating to Height and Setback Regulations, or in Sections [24-61](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-4/24-61) through [24-65](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-4/24-65), inclusive, Section [33-51](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-3/33-51), or Section [43-51](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iv/chapter-3/43-51), relating to Court Regulations and Minimum Distance between Windows and Walls or Lot Lines, provided that on December 15, 1961, the applicant owned the **_zoning lot_** or any portion thereof, and continuously occupied and used one or more **_buildings_** located thereon for a specified **_community facility_** **_use_**, from December 15, 1961, until the time of application. However, for **_Quality Housing buildings_** utilizing the height and setback regulations of Article II, Chapter 3, as required by Sections [24-50](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-4/24-50) and [33-40](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-3/33-40), the Board shall not permit modification to the provisions of Sections [23-67](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-3/23-67) through [23-69](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-3/23-69), inclusive.

As a condition of granting such modification, the Board shall find:

(a) that such modification is required in order to enable such **_use_** to provide an essential service to the community;

(b) that without such modification there is no way to design and construct the new **_buildings_** or **_enlargements_** in satisfactory physical relationships to the existing **_buildings_** which are to remain upon the site, so as to produce an integrated development; and

(c) that such modification is the minimum modification necessary to permit the development of such integrated community facility, and thereby creates the least detriment to the character of the neighborhood and the use of nearby **_zoning lots_**.
