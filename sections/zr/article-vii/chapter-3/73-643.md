# 73-643 Community centers

In any such **_development_** or **_enlargement_** consisting of a community center serving primarily the residents of the **_zoning lot_**, the Board of Standards and Appeals may permit the density regulations set forth in Sections [24-20](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-4/24-20) (APPLICABILITY OF DENSITY REGULATIONS TO ZONING LOTS CONTAINING BOTH RESIDENTIAL AND COMMUNITY FACUILITY USES) or [35-40](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-5/35-40) (APPLICABILITY OF DENSITY REGULATIONS) to be modified, provided that the total number of **_dwelling units_** permitted by these Sections and all other applicable **_bulk_** regulations set forth in Articles II and III of this Resolution shall not be increased by more than 10 percent.
