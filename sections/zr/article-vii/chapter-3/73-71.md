# 73-71 Bulk modifications for community facilities in lower density growth management areas

In addition, for **_buildings_** in R3, R4 and R5 Districts in **_lower density growth management areas_** subject to the provisions of Section [24-05](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-4/24-05) (Buildings containing certain community facility uses) the Board may permit the **_development_** of a **_building_** pursuant to the **_bulk_** regulations of Article II, Chapter 4 (Bulk Regulations for Community Facilities in Residence Districts).

In order to grant such special permit, the Board shall find that:

(a) the amount and type of open area and its distribution on the **_zoning lot_** is compatible with the character of the neighborhood;
(b) the distribution of **_bulk_** on the **_zoning lot_** will not unduly obstruct access of light and air to adjoining properties or **_streets_**; and
(c) the scale and placement of the **_building_** on the **_zoning lot_** relates harmoniously with surrounding **_buildings_**.

The Board may prescribe appropriate conditions and safeguards to minimize adverse effects on the character of the surrounding area.
