# 73-142 Telephone exchanges or other communications equipment structures

In all **_Residence Districts_**, the Board of Standards and Appeals may permit telephone exchanges or other communications equipment structures listed under Use Group IV(B), provided that the Board finds that:

(a) such **_use_** will serve the residential area within which it is proposed to be located; and
(b) there are serious difficulties in locating it in a district wherein it is permitted as of right and from which it could serve the residential area, which make it necessary to locate such **_use_** within a **_Residence District_**.

The Board may prescribe appropriate conditions or safeguards to minimize adverse effects on the character of the surrounding area, including requirements that such **_use_** shall be landscaped.
