# 81-253 Special provisions for the East Midtown, Theater, Fifth Avenue, Penn Center and Preservation Subdistricts

The provisions of Sections [81-26](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-viii/chapter-1/81-26) (Height and Setback Regulations-- Daylight Compensation) and [81-27](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-viii/chapter-1/81-27) (Alternate Height and Setback Regulations--Daylight Evaluation) are supplemented and modified by special provisions applying in the Fifth Avenue Subdistrict, as set forth in Sections [81-81](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-viii/chapter-1/81-81) (General Provisions) and [81-83](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-viii/chapter-1/81-83) (Special Street Wall Requirements) or in the Theater Subdistrict as set forth in Sections [81-71](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-viii/chapter-1/81-71) (General Provisions) and [81-75](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-viii/chapter-1/81-75) (Special Street Wall and Setback Requirements) or in the East Midtown Subdistrict as set forth in Sections [81-61](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-viii/chapter-1/81-61) (General Provisions), [81-66](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-viii/chapter-1/81-66) (Special Height and Setback Requirements), inclusive, or [81-671](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-viii/chapter-1/81-671) (Special street wall requirements), or in the Penn Center Subdistrict as set forth in Section [81-532](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-viii/chapter-1/81-532) (Special street wall requirements).

The provisions of Sections [81-26](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-viii/chapter-1/81-26) and [81-27](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-viii/chapter-1/81-27) are not applicable in the Preservation Subdistrict, where height and setback is regulated by the provisions of Section [81-90](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-viii/chapter-1/81-90) (SPECIAL REGULATIONS FOR PRESERVATION SUBDISTRICT).
