# 81-733 Special signage regulations for portions of the west side of Eighth Avenue

For a **_corner lot_**, or portions thereof, bounded by two **_wide streets_** on the west side of Eighth Avenue within the Eighth Avenue Corridor of the Theater Subdistrict, the **_sign_** provisions for C6-7 Districts pursuant to Section [32-60](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-2/32-60) (SIGN REGULATIONS) shall apply only to such **_corner lot_** portion, with the following modifications:

(a) no **_sign_** shall function with sound; and

(b) **_illuminated signs_** may face both **_wide streets_**, or be parallel to the **_street line_** of one **_wide street_**.
