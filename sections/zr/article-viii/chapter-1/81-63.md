# 81-63 Special Floor Area Provisions for the Vanderbilt Corridor Subarea

For **_non-residential buildings_** or **_mixed buildings_** in the Vanderbilt Corridor Subarea of the East Midtown Subdistrict, as shown on Map 2 (East Midtown Subdistrict and Subareas) in Appendix A of this Chapter, the basic maximum **_floor area ratios_** of the underlying districts shall apply as set forth in this Section. Such basic maximum **_floor area ratio_** on any **_zoning lot_** may be increased by bonuses or other **_floor area_** allowances only in accordance with the provisions of this Chapter, and the maximum **_floor area ratio_** with such additional **_floor area_** allowances shall in no event exceed the amount set forth for each underlying district in the following table:

| Means for Achieving Permitted FAR Levels on a **_Zoning Lot_** in the Vanderbilt Corridor Subarea | Maximum **_Floor Area Ratio_** (FAR) |
| --- | --- |
| A.Basic Maximum FAR | 15 |
| B. Maximum **_Floor Area_** Allowances: (District-wide Incentives), **_Mass transit station_** improvements (Section [66-51](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-6/66-51)) | 3.0 |
| C. Maximum FAR of Lots Involving Landmarks: |  |
| Maximum FAR of a lot containing non bonusable landmark (Section [74-711](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-4/74-711) or as-of-right) | 15.0 |
| Development rights (FAR) of a landmark lot for transfer purposes (Section [81-213](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-viii/chapter-1/81-213)) | 15.0 |
| Maximum amount of transferable development rights (FAR) from a landmark **_zoning lot_** that may be utilized on: |  |
| (a) a **_receiving lot_** within a **_surrounding area_** (Section [81-213](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-viii/chapter-1/81-213)) | No Limit |
| (b) a **_receiving lot_** from **_a granting lot_** in the Grand Central Core Area (Section [81-632](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-viii/chapter-1/81-632)) | 15.0 |
| D. Maximum **_Floor Area_** Allowances by Special Permit for Grand Central public realm improvements (Section [81-633](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-viii/chapter-1/81-633)) | 15.0 |
| E. Maximum Total FAR of a Lot with Transferred Development Rights on a **_receiving lot_** from a **_granting lot_** in the Grand Central Core Area (Section [81-632](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-viii/chapter-1/81-632)) or District-wide Incentives (including Section [81-633](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-viii/chapter-1/81-633)) | 30.0 |
| F. Maximum Total FAR of a Lot with Transferred Development Rights on a **_receiving lot_** within a **_surrounding area_** (Section [81-213](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-viii/chapter-1/81-213)) or District-wide Incentives (other than Section [81-633](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-viii/chapter-1/81-633)) | No Limit |

Any transfer of development rights from a landmark site may be made pursuant to either Section [81-213](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-viii/chapter-1/81-213) (Special provisions for transfer of development rights from landmark sites in certain areas) or Section [81-632](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-viii/chapter-1/81-632) (Special permit for transfer of development rights from landmarks to the Vanderbilt Corridor Subarea), but not both.
