# 81-81 General Provisions

The regulations of Sections [81-82](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-viii/chapter-1/81-82) to [81-85](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-viii/chapter-1/81-85), inclusive, relating to Special Regulations for the Fifth Avenue Subdistrict are applicable only in the Fifth Avenue Subdistrict, whose boundaries are shown on Map 1 in Appendix A of this Chapter. They supplement or modify the regulations of this Chapter applying generally to the **_Special Midtown District_**, of which this Subdistrict is a part.
