# 81-66 Special Height and Setback Requirements

For **_buildings_** which are **_developed_** or **_enlarged_** within the East Midtown Subdistrict, the applicable height and setback regulations of Sections [81-26](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-viii/chapter-1/81-26) (Height and Setback Regulations – Daylight Compensation), inclusive, and [81-27](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-viii/chapter-1/81-27) (Alternate Height and Setback Regulations – Daylight Evaluation), inclusive, are modified by the provisions of this Section, inclusive.
