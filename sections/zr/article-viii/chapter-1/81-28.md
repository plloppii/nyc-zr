# 81-28 Minimum Distance Between Buildings

On any single **_zoning lot_** within the **_Special Midtown District_**, if two or more **_buildings_** or portions of **_buildings_** are detached from one another at any level, such **_buildings_** or portions of **_buildings_** shall at no point be less than eight feet apart.
