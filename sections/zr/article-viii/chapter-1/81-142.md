# 81-142 Special frontage regulations

(a) Transparency requirement

On **_wide street_** frontages in underlying C5 Districts, at least 50 percent of a **_building's_** **_street wall_** surface shall be glazed and transparent at the ground floor level, and not more than 50 percent of such transparent surface shall be painted. For the purpose of the glazing requirements, the **_building’s_** **_street wall_** surface at the ground floor level shall be measured from the floor to the height of the ceiling or 14 feet above grade, whichever is less.

(b) Banners or pennants

The display of banners or pennants from the exterior of any **_building_** in an underlying C5 District is prohibited.
