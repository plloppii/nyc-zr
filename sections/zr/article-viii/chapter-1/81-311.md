# 81-311 Prohibitions of off-street parking or off-street loading facilities

Notwithstanding the provisions of Article I, Chapter 3, prohibitions of off-street parking facilities or **_accessory_** off-street loading berths or restrictions as to their location or access, as provided in Sections [81-44](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-viii/chapter-1/81-44) (Curb Cut Restrictions) or [81-84](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-viii/chapter-1/81-84) (Mandatory Regulations and Prohibitions), may be waived only in accordance with the applicable provisions of Sections [81-44](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-viii/chapter-1/81-44) or [81-84](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-viii/chapter-1/81-84).
