# 81-11 Modifications of Use Regulations in Subdistricts

The **_use_** regulations of the underlying districts are modified in:

(a) the East Midtown Subdistrict in accordance with the provisions of Section [81-62](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-viii/chapter-1/81-62) (Special Use Provisions), inclusive;

(b) the Theater Subdistrict in accordance with the provisions of Sections [81-72](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-viii/chapter-1/81-72) (Use Regulations Modified) and [81-73](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-viii/chapter-1/81-73) (Special Sign and Frontage Regulations); and

(c) the Fifth Avenue Subdistrict in accordance with the provisions of Section [81-82](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-viii/chapter-1/81-82) (Special Regulations on Permitted and Required Uses).
