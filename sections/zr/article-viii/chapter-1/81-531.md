# 81-531 Special retail frontage requirements

The provisions of Section [81-42](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-viii/chapter-1/81-42) (Retail Continuity Along Designated Streets) shall apply within the Penn Center Subdistrict, except that any underlying transparency requirement shall not apply to any informational **_signs_** or marquees provided to identify rail mass transit or subway entrances in accordance with Section [81-52](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-viii/chapter-1/81-52) (Sign Regulations).
