# 81-062 Applicability of Article VII, Chapter 4

Within the **_Special Midtown District_**, the following provisions regarding special permits by the City Planning Commission shall not be applicable:

Section [74-72](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-4/74-72) (Bulk Modification)

Section [74-74](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-4/74-74) (General Large-scale Development)

Section [74-75](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-4/74-75) (Educational Construction Fund Projects)

Section [74-81](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-4/74-81) (Through Block Arcades)

Section [74-821](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-4/74-821) (Court houses)

Section [74-831](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-4/74-831) (Developments in certain Commercial Districts)

Section [74-85](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-4/74-85) (Covered Pedestrian Space)

Section [74-91](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-4/74-91) (Modification of Public Plazas)

Within the **_Special Midtown District_**, the following special permit by the City Planning Commission shall only be applicable as modified below:

Section [74-71](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-4/74-71) (Landmark Preservation) shall be applicable subject to the height and setback modifications of Sections [81-068](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-viii/chapter-1/81-068), [81-254](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-viii/chapter-1/81-254), [81-266](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-viii/chapter-1/81-266) and 81-277.
