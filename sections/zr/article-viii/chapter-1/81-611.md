# 81-611 Applicability of regulations

The provisions of Section [81-60](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-viii/chapter-1/81-60), inclusive, shall apply in the East Midtown Subdistrict as follows:

(a) Section [81-61](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-viii/chapter-1/81-61), inclusive, sets forth general provisions, applicability and definitions for the East Midtown Subdistrict;

(b) Section [81-62](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-viii/chapter-1/81-62), inclusive, sets forth special use provisions;

(c) Section [81-63](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-viii/chapter-1/81-63), inclusive, sets forth special **_floor area_** provisions for the Vanderbilt Corridor Subarea;

(d) Section [81-64](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-viii/chapter-1/81-64), inclusive, sets forth special **_floor area_** provisions for **_qualifying sites_**;

(e) Section [81-65](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-viii/chapter-1/81-65), inclusive, sets forth special **_floor area_** provisions for all other **_zoning lots_**;

(f) Section [81-66](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-viii/chapter-1/81-66), inclusive, sets forth certain height and setback modifications to the provisions of Sections [81-26](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-viii/chapter-1/81-26) and [81-27](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-viii/chapter-1/81-27);

(g) Section [81-67](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-viii/chapter-1/81-67), inclusive, sets forth certain modifications to the mandatory district plan elements of Section [81-40](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-viii/chapter-1/81-40), inclusive; and

(h) Section [81-68](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-viii/chapter-1/81-68), inclusive, sets forth additional provisions pertaining to **_qualifying sites_**.
