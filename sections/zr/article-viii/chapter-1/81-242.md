# 81-242 Regulations on minimum spacing between buildings

When a **_building_** containing **_residential_** **_uses_** is located on the same **_zoning lot_** as another **_building_**, the provisions of Section [23-371](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-3/23-371) (Distance between buildings) shall not apply.
