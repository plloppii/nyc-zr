# 81-254 Modifications for height and setback

In the **_Special Midtown District_**, the City Planning Commission may modify the special height and setback regulations set forth in this Chapter only in accordance with the following provisions:

Section [74-711](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-4/74-711) (Landmark preservation in all districts) as modified by the provisions of Sections [81-266](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-viii/chapter-1/81-266) or [81-277](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-viii/chapter-1/81-277) (Special permit for height and setback modifications)

Section [75-24](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-5/75-24) (Bulk Modifications Associated With a Transfer of Development Rights From Landmark Sites)

Section [81-067](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-viii/chapter-1/81-067) (Special permit modifications of Section [81-254](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-viii/chapter-1/81-254), Section [81-40](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-viii/chapter-1/81-40) and certain Sections of Article VII, Chapter 7)

Section [81-632](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-viii/chapter-1/81-632) (Special permit for transfer of development rights from landmarks to the Vanderbilt Corridor Subarea)

Section [81-633](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-viii/chapter-1/81-633) (Special permit for Grand Central public realm improvements)

Section [81-685](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-viii/chapter-1/81-685) (Special permit to modify qualifying site provisions)
