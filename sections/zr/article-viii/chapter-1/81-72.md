# 81-72 Use Regulations Modified

The **_use_** regulations of this Section, inclusive, shall apply within that portion of the Theater Subdistrict bounded by West 40th Street, a line through the midblock between 40th Street and 41st Street, a line 100 feet east of Eighth Avenue, West 51st Street and a line 200 feet west of Avenue of the Americas, to **_buildings_** **_developed_** after May 13, 1982, to portions of **_buildings_** **_enlarged_** on the ground floor level after May 13, 1982, and to **_extensions_**.
