# 81-85 Transfer of Development Rights From Landmark Sites

For **_developments_** or **_enlargements_**, in addition to the provisions of Sections [75-42](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-5/75-42) (Transfer of Development Rights from Landmark Sites) and [81-212](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-viii/chapter-1/81-212) (Special provisions for transfer of development rights from landmark sites), the City Planning Commission may modify or waive the requirements of Section [81-40](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-viii/chapter-1/81-40) (MANDATORY DISTRICT PLAN ELEMENTS), inclusive, and the requirements of Section [81-84](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-viii/chapter-1/81-84) (Mandatory Regulations and Prohibitions).

In granting such special permit, the Commission shall find that the permitted transfer of **_floor area_** and modification or waiver of mandatory plan elements will result in a distribution of **_bulk_** and arrangement of **_uses_** on the **_zoning lot_** that relate more harmoniously with surrounding landmark **_buildings or other structures_**.
