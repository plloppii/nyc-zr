# 81-41 General Provisions

The provisions of Section [81-40](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-viii/chapter-1/81-40) (MANDATORY DISTRICT PLAN ELEMENTS) specify mandatory planning and urban design features. Requirements which apply generally or with minor specified exceptions throughout the **_Special Midtown District_** are fully set forth in the provisions of Section [81-40](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-viii/chapter-1/81-40). For requirements which are not generally applicable but tied to specific locations within the District, the locations where these requirements apply are shown on Map 3 (Retail and Street Wall Continuity) or Map 4 (Subway Station and Rail Mass Transit Facility Improvement Areas) in Appendix A of this Chapter.

The provisions of Section [81-40](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-viii/chapter-1/81-40) are all primarily oriented toward the accommodation and well-being of pedestrians. The requirements pertain to a number of elements which are interrelated and complement one another but are set forth in different sections because they can be treated separately. Sections [81-42](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-viii/chapter-1/81-42) (Retail Continuity Along Designated Streets), [81-43](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-viii/chapter-1/81-43) (Street Wall Continuity Along Designated Streets) and [81-44](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-viii/chapter-1/81-44) (Curb Cut Restrictions) are a group of sections with closely related purposes concerned with amenity and the well-being and safety of pedestrians. Sections [81-45](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-viii/chapter-1/81-45) to [81-48](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-viii/chapter-1/81-48), inclusive, are all concerned primarily with pedestrian traffic circulation. Major **_building_** entrances are focal points of heavy pedestrian traffic, so that controls on the locations of these entrances, as set forth in Section [81-48](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-viii/chapter-1/81-48), are closely related to the pedestrian circulation space requirements.

Special district plan requirements for the Penn Center Subdistrict are set forth in Section [81-50](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-viii/chapter-1/81-50), for the East Midtown Subdistrict are set forth in Section [81-60](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-viii/chapter-1/81-60), for the Theater Subdistrict are set forth in Section [81-70](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-viii/chapter-1/81-70), for the Fifth Avenue Subdistrict are set forth in Section [81-80](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-viii/chapter-1/81-80) and for the Preservation Subdistrict are set forth in Section [81-90](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-viii/chapter-1/81-90).
