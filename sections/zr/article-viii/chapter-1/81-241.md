# 81-241 Maximum floor area ratios for a residential building or the residential portion of a mixed building

For **_residential buildings_** or **_residential_** portions of **_mixed buildings_** in the **_Special Midtown District_**, the underlying **_floor area_** regulations shall apply except that in the C5-P District, the maximum **_residential_** **_floor area ratio_** shall be 8.0 for **_zoning lots_** containing standard **_residences_**, and 9.6 for **_zoning lots_** containing **_qualifying affordable housing_** or **_qualifying senior housing_**.
