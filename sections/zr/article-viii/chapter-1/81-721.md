# 81-721 Special ground floor street frontage restrictions

Each establishment along a designated **_street_** frontage shall be located within 10 feet of the **_lot line_** on which it is required to front for the full length of the frontage of that establishment, except that, where a **_street wall_** recess at **_curb level_** permitted under Section [81-43](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-viii/chapter-1/81-43) (Street Wall Continuity Along Designated Streets) extends a greater distance from the **_lot line_**, the length of frontage of any establishment adjoining that recess may be located at an equal, but not greater, distance from the **_lot line_**.
