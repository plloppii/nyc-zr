# 82-20 SPECIAL USE AND SIGN REGULATIONS

In order to provide for the special cultural needs, convenience, enjoyment, education and recreation of the residents of the area and of the many visitors who are attracted to the Lincoln Center for the Performing Arts, special **_ground floor level_** streetscape and **_sign_** regulations apply.
