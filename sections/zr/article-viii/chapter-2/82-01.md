# 82-01 Definitions

For the purposes of this Chapter, matter in italics shall include those defined in Sections [12-10](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-i/chapter-2/12-10) (DEFINITIONS), [32-301](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-2/32-301) (Definitions) or in this Section.

Development

For purposes of this Chapter, a "development" includes both **_development_** and **_enlargement_**, as defined in Section [12-10](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-i/chapter-2/12-10) (DEFINITIONS).
