# 87-52 Curb Cut Restrictions

On Parcels 1, 2, 3 and 4, as shown on Map 1 in the Appendix to this Chapter, no curb cuts shall be provided facing a **_shore public walkway_**, and further, on Parcels 1 and 2, no curb cuts shall be provided facing a mapped parkland.
