# 87-05 Modification of Use and Bulk Regulations for Parcels Containing Newly Mapped Streets

In the event that **_streets_** are mapped on Parcels 1, 2, 3 and 4 in the Core Subdistrict after June 30, 2009, and on any Parcel in the South Subdistrict after December 11, 2017, as shown on Map 1 in the Appendix to this Chapter, the area within such **_streets_** may continue to be considered part of the **_zoning lot_** for the purposes of applying **_floor area_** regulations of this Zoning Resolution.
