# 87-43 Open Parking Areas

Open parking areas shall be permitted only in the following locations:

(a) on Parcel 1, as shown on Map 1 in the Appendix to this Chapter, a parking lot shall be permitted east of the **_Major Deegan Expressway street line_**;
(b) on Parcel 5, as shown on Map 1, a parking lot shall be permitted anywhere within the parcel only if a commercial or public utility vehicle storage **_use_**, including **_accessory_** motor fuel pumps listed under Use Group IX(C), is **_developed_** or **_enlarged_** as the primary **_use_** on the parcel;
(c) on Parcel 6, a parking lot shall be permitted within 130 feet of the southern boundary of the parcel with East 138th Street; and
(d) on all parcels, open, unscreened, in tandem (one behind the other), **_accessory_** off-street parking spaces shall be permitted on private roads, including fire apparatus access roads provided pursuant to the provisions of Section [87-61](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-viii/chapter-7/87-61) (Special Provisions for Certain Fire Apparatus Access Roads), provided that all parking spaces comply with the Department of Transportation standards for on-street parking.

For such open parking lots, the provisions of paragraph (c) of Section [25-621](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-5/25-621) (Location of parking spaces in certain districts) shall not apply. In addition, on Parcel 1, for parking lots located east of the **_Major Deegan Expressway street line_**, or, on Parcel 5, for parking lots used solely as a commercial or public utility vehicle storage **_use_**, including **_accessory_** motor fuel pumps listed under Use Group IX(C), the provisions of Sections [37-90](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-7/37-90) (PARKING LOTS) and [62-655](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-2/62-655) (Planting and trees) shall be modified to permit fencing, excluding chain link fencing, in lieu of all planting requirements, provided that the surface area of such fencing is not more than 50 percent opaque and provided that the height does not exceed six feet. The provisions of Sections [37-90](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-7/37-90) and [62-555](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-2/62-555) shall not apply to any parking lots provided within private roads, including fire apparatus access roads provided pursuant to the provisions of Section [87-61](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-viii/chapter-7/87-61).
