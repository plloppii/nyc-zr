# 86-21 Special Floor Area Regulations in C4-5X Districts

In C4-5X Districts within the **_Special District_**, the underlying **_floor area ratio_** for **_commercial uses_** shall not apply. In lieu thereof, the provisions of Section [33-122](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-3/33-122) (Commercial buildings in all other Commercial Districts) shall be modified to permit a maximum **_floor area ratio_** of 5.0.
