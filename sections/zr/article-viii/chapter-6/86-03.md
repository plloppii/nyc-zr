# 86-03 District Plan and Maps

The regulations of this Chapter are designed to implement the District Plan for the **_Special Forest Hills District_**. The District Plan includes the map of the **_Special Forest Hills District_**, which is set forth in the Appendix to this Chapter and is hereby incorporated as part of this Resolution for the purpose of specifying locations where the special regulations and requirements set forth in this Chapter apply.
