# 86-11 Use Modifications Along Austin Street

Within C2 Districts fronting on Austin Street in the **_Special Forest Hills District_**, the **_use_** regulations shall be modified to permit **_uses_** listed under Use Groups VI and VIII, pursuant to the regulations applicable to C4 Districts.
