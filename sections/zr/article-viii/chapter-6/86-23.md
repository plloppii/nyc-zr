# 86-23 Height and Setback Regulations

**_Buildings or other structures_** within the Special District shall comply with the height and setback regulations of Section [35-63](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-5/35-63) (Height and Setback Requirements in Commercial Districts with R6 Through 12 Equivalency) except that within the C4-5X District west of 70th Road, the minimum base height of the **_street wall_** shall be 40 feet.
