# 88-14 Manufacturing Use

In the **_Special Hudson Square District_**, **_manufacturing uses_** permitted in M1 Districts shall be subject to the modifications set forth in Section [123-22](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-xii/chapter-3/123-22) (Additional conditions for certain uses), inclusive.
