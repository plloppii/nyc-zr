# 84-123 Streetscape regulations

In the areas indicated as permitted **_commercial_** locations in Appendices 2.3 and 3.3 of this Chapter, the underlying **_ground floor level_** streetscape provisions for a C2 District, set forth in Section [32-30](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-2/32-30) (STREETSCAPE REGULATIONS), inclusive, shall apply.
