# 84-33 Bulk Regulations

**_Bulk_** regulations otherwise applicable in C6-6 Districts and equivalent **_Residential Districts_** are hereby modified to the extent set forth in this Section and Sections [84-331](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-viii/chapter-4/84-331) through [84-333](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-viii/chapter-4/84-333), inclusive.

The height and setback regulations otherwise applicable in C6-6 Districts, and equivalent **_Residential Districts_**, are superseded by the regulations set forth in Section [84-332](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-viii/chapter-4/84-332) (Mandatory front building walls) and [84-333](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-viii/chapter-4/84-333) (Limited height of buildings) and Appendices 3.1 and 3.2.
