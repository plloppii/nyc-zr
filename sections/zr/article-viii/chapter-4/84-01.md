# 84-01 Definitions

For purposes of this Chapter, matter in italics is defined in Section [12-10](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-i/chapter-2/12-10) (DEFINITIONS), in this Section or Section [84-021](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-viii/chapter-4/84-021) (District Plan). Where matter in italics is defined both in Section [12-10](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-i/chapter-2/12-10) and in this Chapter, the definitions in this Chapter shall govern.

Esplanade

The "Esplanade" is a **_public park_** extending along all waterfront edges of the **_Special Battery Park City District_**. The **_Esplanade_** is shown in the District Plan in Appendix 1.

Mandatory front building wall lines

"Mandatory front building wall lines" are imaginary lines extending through Zone A and Zone C of the **_Special Battery Park City District_** which, except as shown in Appendices 2.1 and 3.1 of this Chapter, coincide with **_street lines_** and with which **_building_** walls must generally coincide, as provided in Sections [84-132](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-viii/chapter-4/84-132) and [84-332](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-viii/chapter-4/84-332) (Mandatory front building walls).

Special height locations

"Special height locations" are designated areas in Zone A and Zone C of the **_Special Battery Park City District_** subject to the regulations in Appendices 2.2 and 3.2 of this Chapter, in accordance with Sections [84-135](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-viii/chapter-4/84-135) and [84-333](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-viii/chapter-4/84-333) (Limited height of buildings).
