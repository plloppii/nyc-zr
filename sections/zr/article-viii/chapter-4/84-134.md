# 84-134 Mandatory arcades

Appendix 2.4 (Mandatory Arcades) specifies those **_zoning lots_** where an **_arcade_**, as defined in Section [12-10](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-i/chapter-2/12-10) (DEFINITIONS), shall be provided; and:

(a) the **_arcade_** shall extend the full width of the **_zoning lot_** along the indicated **_street lines_** or other lines, except where otherwise indicated in Appendix 2.4; and

(b) the minimum unobstructed depth of the **_arcade_** shall be 12 feet, and the minimum height of the **_arcade_** shall be 20 feet above **_curb level_**.
