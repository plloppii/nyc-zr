# 92-01 Definitions

Development

For purposes of this Chapter a "development" includes the construction of a new **_building or other structure_** on a **_zoning lot_**, the relocation of an existing **_building_** on another **_zoning lot_**, and an **_enlargement_** involving an increase in **_lot coverage_**.
