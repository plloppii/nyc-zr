# 96-81 C6-3X Districts

In C6-3X Districts in Excluded Areas, the underlying regulations shall apply except that the maximum **_residential_** **_floor area ratio_** shall be 7.5 for **_zoning lots_** containing standard **_residences_**, or 9.0 for **_zoning lots_** containing **_qualifying affordable housing_** or **_qualifying senior housing_**.
