# 96-20 PERIMETER AREA

Within the Perimeter Area, the underlying provisions shall apply except as modified in this Section, inclusive. Such **_developments_** or **_enlargements_** within the Perimeter Area shall be eligible for increased **_floor area_** only pursuant to Sections [96-21](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ix/chapter-6/96-21) (Special Regulations for 42nd Street Perimeter Area) or [96-22](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ix/chapter-6/96-22) (Special Regulations for Eighth Avenue Perimeter Area). Because of increased pressures for **_development_**, the relocation and demolition provisions of Section [96-24](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ix/chapter-6/96-24) (Relocation and Demolition of Buildings in the Perimeter Area) shall apply therein for all demolition, **_development_**, **_enlargement_** or **_extensions_** on lots containing **_residential uses_**. All existing legal **_uses_** in enclosed **_buildings_** shall be considered conforming **_uses_**. Except as otherwise provided in this Chapter, any existing **_commercial_** or **_manufacturing_** **_uses_** may be changed to another **_non-conforming_** **_use_** only in accordance with the provisions of Sections [52-31](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-v/chapter-2/52-31) (General Provisions), [52-33](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-v/chapter-2/52-33) (Manufacturing or Related Uses in Residence Districts), [52-34](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-v/chapter-2/52-34) (Commercial Uses in Residence Districts), [52-35](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-v/chapter-2/52-35) (Manufacturing or Related Uses in Commercial Districts) and [52-36](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-v/chapter-2/52-36) (Non-conforming Commercial Uses in Commercial Districts).
