# 94-092 Maximum floor area ratio

The permitted basic **_floor area ratio_** for **_community facility_** **_use_** is 1.25. The underlying district **_floor area ratio_** may be increased on any **_zoning lot_** by the amount set forth in Section [94-08](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ix/chapter-4/94-08) (Special Floor Area Bonus Provisions) or through transfer provisions pursuant to Section [94-094](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ix/chapter-4/94-094) (Authorization provisions for transfer of development rights to receiving lots) or by special permit pursuant to Section [94-095](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ix/chapter-4/94-095) (Special permit for floor area, location within buildings, building height and related parking modifications within Area G).
