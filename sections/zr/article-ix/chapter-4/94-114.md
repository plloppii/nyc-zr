# 94-114 Exceptions to application of waiver provisions

In areas A, B, C, D, E and F, the provisions of Section [36-23](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-6/36-23) (Waiver of Requirements for Spaces Below Minimum Number) do not apply.

The provisions relating to modifications of parking requirements of Article VII, Chapter 3 (Special Permits by the Board of Standards and Appeals) in Sections [73-10](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-3/73-10) through [73-52](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-3/73-52), shall not apply in the Special District.
