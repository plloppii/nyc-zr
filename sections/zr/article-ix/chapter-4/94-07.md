# 94-07 Mandatory Provisions

All **_developments_** within the Special District shall comply with the mandatory provisions made applicable by this Section and such mandatory improvements, when developed for a **_floor area_** bonus pursuant to Section [94-08](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ix/chapter-4/94-08) (Special Floor Area Bonus Provisions), shall require certification by the City Planning Commission, pursuant to Section [94-13](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ix/chapter-4/94-13).
