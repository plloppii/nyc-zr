# 93-11 ERY Culture, Festival and Exhibit Facility

For purposes of this Chapter, all references to **_community facility_**, **_community facility use_** or **_uses_** listed under Use Group III, in connection with Eastern Rail Yard Subarea A1 shall be deemed to include an **_ERY Culture, Festival and Exhibit Facility_**.
