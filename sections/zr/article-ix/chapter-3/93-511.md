# 93-511 Tower lot coverage

The tower **_lot coverage_** requirements of paragraph (c) of Section [93-42](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ix/chapter-3/93-42) shall not apply within the Large-Scale Plan Subdistrict A.
