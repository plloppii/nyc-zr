# 93-52 Special Height and Setback Regulations in the Farley Corridor Subdistrict B

The tower **_lot coverage_** requirements of paragraph (c) of Section [93-42](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ix/chapter-3/93-42) shall not apply within the Farley Corridor Subdistrict B.
