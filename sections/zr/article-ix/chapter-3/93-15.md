# 93-15 Public Parking Facilities

In C2-5, C2-8 and C6 Districts, the provisions of Section [32-19](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-2/32-19) (Use Group IX - Storage), inclusive, with respect to **_public parking garages_** and **_public parking lots_** are inapplicable and are superseded by the provisions of Section [93-80](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ix/chapter-3/93-80) (OFF-STREET PARKING REGULATIONS).
