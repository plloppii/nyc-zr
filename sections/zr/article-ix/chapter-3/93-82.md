# 93-82 Permitted Parking

**_Developments_** or **_enlargements_** in the **_Hudson Yards parking regulations applicability area_** may provide **_accessory_** parking spaces in accordance with the provisions of this Section. The provisions of Sections [36-52](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-6/36-52) (Size, Location and Identification of Spaces) and [36-53](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-6/36-53) (Width of Curb Cuts and Location of Access to the Street) shall apply to all permitted **_accessory_** off-street parking spaces.
