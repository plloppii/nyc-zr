# 91-30 HEIGHT AND SETBACK AND LOT COVERAGE REGULATIONS

For all **_buildings or other structures_** in the **_Special Lower Manhattan District_**, the height and setback regulations of the underlying districts are superseded by the regulations of this Section.

The height of all **_buildings or other structures_** shall be measured from **_curb level_**.
