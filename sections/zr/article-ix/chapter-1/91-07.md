# 91-07 Modification of Use and Bulk Regulations for Zoning Lots Fronting Upon DeLury Square Park

Where the **_lot line_** of a **_zoning lot_** coincides with, or is within 20 feet of, the boundary of DeLury Square Park, such **_lot line_** shall be considered to be a **_street line_** for the purposes of applying all **_use_** and **_bulk_** regulations of this Resolution.
