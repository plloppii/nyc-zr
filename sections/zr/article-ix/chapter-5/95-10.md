# 95-10 MISCELLANEOUS PROVISIONS

The pavement on a public sidewalk fronting a **_development_** within the **_Special Transit Land Use District_** shall be consistent in color and material with all pavements located within the transit easement volume and in any adjoining **_public plaza_**.
