# 95-055 Knockout panel

Any underground walls constructed along the **_front lot line_** of a **_zoning lot_** in which transit easement volume is required by the Metropolitan Transportation Authority shall contain a knockout panel, not less than 12 feet wide, below **_curb level_** down to the bottom of the easement. The actual location and size of such knockout panel shall be indicated by the Metropolitan Transportation Authority.
