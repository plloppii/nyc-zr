# 95-08 Special Use Regulations

The underlying **_use_** regulations shall apply, except that for **_zoning lots_** with transit easements in **_Residence Districts_**, the special allowances of Section [66-222](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-6/66-222) (Special use allowances around easement volumes) may be applied around such easement volume.
