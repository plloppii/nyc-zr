# 97-56 Public Parking Facilities

Notwithstanding the special permit regulations of Section [74-194](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-4/74-194) (Public parking garages or public parking lots in high density central areas), **_public parking garages_** with 150 spaces or less shall be permitted as-of-right in C4-7 and C6 Districts, subject to the requirements of Section [36-50](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-6/36-50), inclusive, pertaining to surfacing and screening, and Section [97-53](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ix/chapter-7/97-53) (Location of Access to the Street). **_Public parking garages_** with more than 150 spaces shall be subject to the requirements of Sections [74-193](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-4/74-193) (Public parking garages or public parking lots outside high density central areas) or [74-194](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-4/74-194), as applicable.

**_Public parking lots_** are not permitted on zoning lots with 125th Street frontage within the Special District.
