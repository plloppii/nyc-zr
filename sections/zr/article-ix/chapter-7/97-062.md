# 97-062 Applicability of Mandatory Inclusionary Housing Program

For the purposes of applying the Mandatory Inclusionary Housing Program provisions set forth in Section [27-10](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-7/27-10) (ADMINISTRATION OF AFFORDABLE HOUSING), inclusive, **_Mandatory Inclusionary Housing areas_** within the **_Special 125th Street District_** are shown on the maps in APPENDIX F of this Resolution.
