# 97-11 Special Arts and Entertainment Uses

In order to sustain the arts and entertainment character of the 125th Street corridor, the provisions of this Section shall apply.

(a) The following **_uses_** shall be designated as entertainment **_uses_**:
    From Use Group VI
    Eating or drinking establishments
    From Use Group VIII
    Auditoriums
    Production or entertainment studios.
(b) The following **_uses_** shall be designated as visual or performing arts **_uses_**:
    From Use Group III
    Museums
    From Use Group VIII
    Art galleries
    Art, music, dancing or theatrical studios
    Theaters
    Historical exhibits.
