# 97-41 Special Floor Area Regulations

The maximum **_floor area ratio_** requirements of the applicable underlying district shall apply within the **_Special 125th Street District_**, unless modified by the following regulations.

For **_developments_** or **_enlargements_** on **_qualifying transit improvement sites_**, a **_floor area_** bonus for **_mass transit station_** improvements may be granted by the City Planning Commission pursuant to the provisions of Section [66-51](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-6/66-51) (Additional Floor Area for Mass Transit Station Improvements). Bonuses pursuant to Sections [66-51](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-6/66-51) or this Section, inclusive, may be applied separately or in combination. For the purposes of this paragraph, defined terms additionally include those in Section [66-11](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-6/66-11) (Definitions).
