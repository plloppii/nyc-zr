# 97-44 Special Provisions for Zoning Lots Divided by District Boundaries

The regulations of Article VII, Chapter 7 (Special Provisions for Zoning Lots Divided by District Boundaries) shall apply within the **_Special 125th Street District_**, except that for any **_zoning lot_** that is completely within the Core Subdistrict, **_floor area_** may be located anywhere on such **_zoning lot_** without regard to the requirements of Section [77-22](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-7/77-22) (Floor Area Ratio), subject to the applicable height and setback regulations.
