# 97-31 Definitions

Marquee

A “marquee” is a permanent structure or canopy located above the primary entrance to an arts **_use_** fronting on 125th Street or Fifth Avenue, that projects over the sidewalk and is attached to, and entirely supported from, the **_street wall_** of the **_building_**. The location and dimensions of the **_marquee_** shall be determined by the requirements of Section [97-32](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ix/chapter-7/97-32).

All **_marquees_** shall comply with the construction and maintenance requirements of Title 27, Subchapter 4, Article 9, of the New York City Building Code, or its successor, pertaining to projecting **_signs_**.

Marquee sign

A “marquee sign” is a **_sign_**, other than an **_advertising sign_**, mounted on a **_marquee_** that identifies the arts **_use_** and provides informational displays about such **_use_**.
