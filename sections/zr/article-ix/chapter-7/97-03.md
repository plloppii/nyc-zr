# 97-03 District Plan and Maps

The regulations of this Chapter are designed to implement the **_Special 125th Street District_** Plan. The District Plan, including Map 1 (Special 125th Street District and Subdistricts), is set forth in Appendix A of this Chapter and is hereby incorporated as part of this Resolution for the purpose of specifying locations where the special regulations and requirements set forth in this Chapter apply.
