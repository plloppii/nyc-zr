# 97-33 Vertical Distance Above Sidewalk of Marquees and Marquee Signs

The minimum vertical distance from the sidewalk for a **_marquee_** shall be 12 feet; the maximum vertical distance above the sidewalk for such **_marquee_** shall be 20 feet.

Notwithstanding the provisions of paragraph (b) of Section [32-653](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-2/32-653) (Additional regulations for projecting signs), additional **_signs_** may be displayed on a **_marquee_**, provided such **_sign_** is no more than two feet above the **_marquee_**.

No **_marquee_** or **_marquee sign_** shall be located at a height higher than three feet below any floor containing a **_residential use_**.
