# 97-21 Supplemental Use Regulations Along 125th Street

Within the **_Special 125th Street District_**, for any **_zoning lot_** that fronts upon 125th Street, the **_use_** regulations of the underlying districts shall be modified by the requirements of this Section, inclusive. However, on **_through lots_** or **_corner lots_** with frontage along 125th Street, such requirements shall apply within the first 100 feet of the 125th Street **_street line_**.
