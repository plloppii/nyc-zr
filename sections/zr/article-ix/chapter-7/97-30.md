# 97-30 SPECIAL SIGN REGULATIONS

**_Signs_** for all **_uses_** within the **_Special 125th Street District_** shall be subject to the applicable **_sign_** requirements in Section [32-60](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-2/32-60), inclusive, subject to the modifications of Sections [97-31](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ix/chapter-7/97-31) through [97-34](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ix/chapter-7/97-34), inclusive.

**_Marquee signs_** for an arts **_use_** may be combined, subject to the requirements of Section [32-641](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-2/32-641) (Total surface area of signs).

In the event of a conflict between the provisions of this Section, [97-30](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ix/chapter-7/97-30), inclusive, and other regulations of the Administrative Code, the provisions of this Chapter shall apply.
