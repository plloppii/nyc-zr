# 97-061 Applicability of Special Transit Land Use District Regulations

Wherever the **_Special 125th Street District_** includes an area which also lies within the **_Special Transit Land Use District_**, the requirements of the **_Special Transit Land Use District_**, as set forth in Article IX, Chapter 5, shall apply, except as modified by the provisions of the Chapter.
