# 98-243 Located partially within Subarea D and partially within C6-3A Districts

For a **_zoning lot_** fronting on West 23rd Street and Eleventh Avenue, located partially within Subarea D and partially within a C6-3A District, **_floor area_** may be transferred from the portion of the **_zoning lot_** in the C6-3A District to the portion in Subarea D.
