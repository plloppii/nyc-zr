# 98-65 Transit Facilities

The provisions of paragraph (b) of Section [93-65](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ix/chapter-3/93-65) (Transit Facilities) shall apply to any subway-related **_use_** on a **_zoning lot_** that includes the volume bounded by a line 37 feet east of and parallel to Eleventh Avenue, West 26th Street, a line 100 feet east of and parallel to Eleventh Avenue, and a line 95 feet south of and parallel to West 26th Street, up to a height of 60 feet, as illustrated on the District Map in Appendix A of this Chapter.
