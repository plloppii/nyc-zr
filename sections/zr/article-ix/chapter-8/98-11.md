# 98-11 Special Regulations for Developments and Enlargements Above, Beneath or Adjacent to the High Line

The Commissioner of Buildings shall not issue any building permit for demolition, excavation or foundation work to be performed above or beneath the **_High Line_** or within 25 feet of support structures of the **_High Line_**, except by determination by such Commissioner that such work would not adversely affect the structural integrity of the **_High Line_** and by determination by the Commissioner of Parks that such work would not adversely affect the City's ability to inspect and maintain as necessary to ensure the structural integrity of the **_High Line_**.
