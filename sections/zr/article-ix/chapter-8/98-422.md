# 98-422 Special rooftop regulations

The provisions of Section [33-42](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-3/33-42) (Permitted Obstructions) shall apply to all **_buildings or other structures_** within the **_Special West Chelsea District_**, except that dormers may penetrate a maximum base height in accordance with the provisions of paragraph (b)(1) of Section [23-413](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-3/23-413) (Permitted obstructions in certain districts).

All mechanical equipment located within 15 feet of the level of the **_High Line bed_** that is within 25 feet of the **_High Line_**, measured horizontally, or within the **_High Line frontage_**, as applicable, shall be screened and buffered with no intake or exhaust fans or vents facing directly onto the **_High Line_**.
