# 98-22 Maximum Floor Area Ratio and Lot Coverage in Subareas

For all **_zoning lots_**, or portions thereof, located in Subareas A through K, the maximum **_floor area ratios_** of the applicable underlying district shall not apply. In lieu thereof, the maximum **_floor area ratio_** permitted for **_commercial_**, **_community facility_** and **_residential uses_**, separately or in combination, shall be as specified in the tables in this Section.

For **_zoning lots_** not using **_qualifying affordable housing_** and **_qualifying senior housing_**, the provisions set forth in Table 1 shall apply. For **_zoning lots_** using **_qualifying affordable housing_** and **_qualifying senior housing_**, the provisions set forth in Table 2 shall apply.
