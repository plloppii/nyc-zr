# 98-32 General Provisions

The location of the **_High Line Transfer Corridor_** is specified in Appendix B of this Chapter.

In the **_High Line Transfer Corridor_**, special regulations relating to the transfer of **_floor area_** are set forth in Sections [98-33](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ix/chapter-8/98-33) through [98-35](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ix/chapter-8/98-35), inclusive.
