# 98-151 Modification of sign regulations in Subarea K

Within Subarea K, the **_sign_** regulations of the underlying district shall apply. However, within 15 feet of the intersection of two **_streets_**, the provisions of Section [42-662](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iv/chapter-2/42-662) (Restriction on angle and height above curb level) shall not apply.
