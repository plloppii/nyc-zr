# 52-36 Non-Conforming Commercial Uses in Commercial Districts

In **_Commercial Districts_**, any **_non-conforming_** **_commercial_** **_use_** is listed in Use Groups IV, VI, VII, VIII, IX or X and permitted as-of-right within C7 Districts, may be changed, initially or in any subsequent change, only to a conforming **_use_** or to any **_commercial_** **_use_** permitted as-of-right in C2 Districts, other than those listed in Use Group V.
