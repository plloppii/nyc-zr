# 52-44 Non-Conforming Residential Uses in M1 Districts

In an M1 District, a **_non-conforming_** **_residential use_** occupying at least 50 percent of the **_floor area_** of a **_building_** which was **_designed for residential use_** and erected prior to December 15, 1961, may be **_extended_** on the ground floor level provided that no **_dwelling unit_** or **_rooming unit_** may be located on or below a **_story_** occupied by a **_commercial_** or **_manufacturing_** **_use_**. The total number of **_dwelling units_** shall be that which would be permitted through the provisions of Section [23-50](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-3/23-50) (DENSITY REGULATIONS), inclusive, in accordance with the applicable geography.
