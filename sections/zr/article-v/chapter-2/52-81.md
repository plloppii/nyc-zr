# 52-81 General Provisions

A **_non-conforming_** **_sign_** shall be subject to all the provisions of this Chapter relating to **_non-conforming_** **_uses_**, except as modified by the provisions of Sections [52-82](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-v/chapter-2/52-82) (Non-Conforming Signs other than Advertising Signs) and [52-83](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-v/chapter-2/52-83) (Non-Conforming Advertising Signs).

A change in the subject matter represented on a **_sign_** shall not be considered a change of **_use_**.
