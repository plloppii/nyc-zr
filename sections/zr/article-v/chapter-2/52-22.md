# 52-22 Structural Alterations

No structural alterations shall be made in a **_building or other structure_** substantially occupied by a **_non-conforming_** **_use_**, except when made:

(a) in order to comply with requirements of law; or
(b) in order to accommodate a conforming **_use_**; or
(c) in order to conform to the applicable district regulations on performance standards; or
(d) in the course of an **_enlargement_** permitted under the provisions of Section [52-40](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-v/chapter-2/52-40) (ENLARGEMENTS OR EXTENSIONS), inclusive; or
(e) in the course of an alteration to improve the energy performance of the **_building or other structure_**, including, but not limited to, the addition of solar energy systems, energy storage systems, or **_qualifying exterior wall thickness_**;
(f) or except as set forth in Section [52-80](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-v/chapter-2/52-80) (REGULATIONS APPLYING TO NON-CONFORMING SIGNS), inclusive.
