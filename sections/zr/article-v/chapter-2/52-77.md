# 52-77 Termination of Adult Establishments

In all districts, a **_non-conforming_** **_adult establishment_** shall terminate within one year from October 25, 1995, or from such later date that the **_adult establishment_** becomes **_non-conforming_**, except that such establishment may be continued for a limited period of time by the Board of Standards and Appeals pursuant to Section [72-40](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-2/72-40) (AMORTIZATION OF CERTAIN ADULT ESTABLISHMENTS AND SIGNS FOR ADULT ESTABLISHMENTS). However, the provisions of this Section shall not apply to an **_adult establishment_** subject to the provisions of paragraph (f) of Section [32-01](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-2/32-01) or [42-01](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iv/chapter-2/42-01) (Special Provisions for Adult Establishments).
