# 54-21 General Provisions

Repairs, **_incidental alterations_**, or structural alterations may be made in a **_non-complying_** building or other structure, except that such alterations made in the course of an **_enlargement_** shall be subject to the provisions of Section [54-31](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-v/chapter-4/54-31) (General Provisions).
