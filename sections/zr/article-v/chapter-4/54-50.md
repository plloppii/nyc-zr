# 54-50 MODIFICATIONS TO THE PROVISIONS OF THIS CHAPTER

The provisions of this Section may be combined, except that individual **_bulk_** allowances to increase the height of a **_building_** or diminish the amount of an open area through an **_enlargement_** shall not be aggregated.
