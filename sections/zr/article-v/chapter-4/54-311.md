# 54-311 Buildings containing rooming units

If a **_building_** or portion of a **_building_** contains **_rooming units_**, such **_rooming units_** may be **_converted_** to **_dwelling units_** in accordance with the provisions of Section [23-50](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-3/23-50) (DENSITY REGULATIONS), inclusive.
