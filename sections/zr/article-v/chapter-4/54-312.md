# 54-312 Modification of provisions

The Board of Standards and Appeals may permit **_enlargements_** or **_conversions_** that create a new **_non-compliance_**, or increase an existing **_non-compliance_** with applicable **_bulk_** regulations in accordance with the provisions of Section [73-60](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-3/73-60) (MODIFICATIONS OF BULK REGULATIONS), inclusive.
