# 53-00 GENERAL PROVISIONS

The provisions of this Chapter shall apply to all conforming **_uses_** which are in violation of the provisions of Sections [32-40](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-2/32-40) and [42-50](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iv/chapter-2/42-50), inclusive, relating to Supplementary Use Regulations.
