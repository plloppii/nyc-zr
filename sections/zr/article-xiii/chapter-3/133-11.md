# 133-11 Additional Uses

Within the **_development parcel_**, the provisions of Section [32-10](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-2/32-10) (USE ALLOWANCES) are modified to permit listed under Use Group VII, subject to the provisions of an M1 District.
