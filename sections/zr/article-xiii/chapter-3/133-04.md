# 133-04 Applicability of Article III, Chapter 6

The provisions of Article III, Chapter 6 (Accessory Off-Street Parking and Loading Regulations) shall not apply in the **_Special Southern Roosevelt Island District_**. In lieu thereof, a maximum of 500 **_accessory_** parking spaces shall be permitted, which may be made available for public use.

However, bicycle parking shall be provided in accordance with the provisions of Section [36-70](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-6/36-70).
