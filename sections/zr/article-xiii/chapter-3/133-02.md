# 133-02 General Provisions

The provisions of this Chapter shall apply within the **_Special Southern Roosevelt Island District_**. The regulations of all other Chapters of this Resolution are applicable, except as superseded, supplemented or modified by the provisions of this Chapter. In the event of a conflict between the provisions of this Chapter and other regulations of this Resolution, the provisions of this Chapter shall control.
