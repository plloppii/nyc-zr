# 135-041 Applicability of Article I, Chapter 2

The definition of “lower density growth management area” in Section [12-10](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-i/chapter-2/12-10) shall exclude all districts within the **_Special Bay Street Corridor District_**.
