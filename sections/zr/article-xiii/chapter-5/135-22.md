# 135-22 Special Yard Regulations

The underlying **_yard_** regulations are modified by the provisions of this Section.

In Subdistrict A, no **_rear yard_** or **_rear yard equivalent_** need be provided for **_commercial buildings_**, **_community facility buildings_**, or the portion of a **_mixed building_** containing **_commercial_** or **_community facility uses_**.
