# 135-045 Applicability of this Chapter to certain zoning lots in Subdistrict D

In Subdistrict D, for **_uses_** listed under Miscellaneous Manufacturing in Use Group X that support the operation of a public service or public transportation facility and were existing on June 26, 2019, the provisions applicable to an M1-1 District shall apply.
