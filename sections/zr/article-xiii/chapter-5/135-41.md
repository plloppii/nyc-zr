# 135-41 Commercial Parking Requirements

In **_mixed buildings_**, the underlying parking requirements shall apply, except that for the purposes of determining the parking requirement for **_commercial_** **_uses_** other than **_uses_** listed under Offices in Use Group VII, the equivalent of 0.5 **_floor area ratio_**, or the amount of non-office **_commercial_** **_floor area_** in the **_building_**, whichever is less, may be deducted from the **_floor area_** used to determine such **_commercial_** parking calculation.
