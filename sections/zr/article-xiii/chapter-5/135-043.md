# 135-043 Applicability of Article VI, Chapter 4

Notwithstanding the general provisions of Section [135-01](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-xiii/chapter-5/135-01), in **_flood zones_**, in the event of a conflict between the provisions of this Chapter and the provisions of Article VI, Chapter 4 (Special Regulations Applying in Flood Zones), the provisions of Article VI, Chapter 4 shall control.
