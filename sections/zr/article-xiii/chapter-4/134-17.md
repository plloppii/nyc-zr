# 134-17 Special Signage Regulations

In the **_Special Governors Island District_**, the **_sign_** regulations shall apply as follows:

(a) In the Northern Subdistrict, the **_sign_** regulations of a C1 District mapped within an R3-2 District shall apply.

(b) In the Southern Subdistrict, the **_sign_** regulations of a C4 District shall apply, except that no **_flashing signs_** shall be permitted.

(c) No **_signs_** shall be permitted on any pier within the Southern Subdistrict. However, such restriction shall not apply to informational or wayfinding signs not exceeding 25 square feet each, or to signage identifying “Governors Island” or a pier from the waterway.
