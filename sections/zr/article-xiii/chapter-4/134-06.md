# 134-06 Applicability of Article VII, Chapter 6

The provisions of Section [76-145](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-6/76-145) (Boundary Line Coinciding With Parks, Cemeteries or Navigable Waters) shall be modified such that where the zoning district boundaries shown on the applicable zoning map coincide with the **_shoreline_**, such zoning district boundaries shall be deemed to extend beyond the **_shoreline_** to also include any piers contained within the **_Special Governors Island District_**.
