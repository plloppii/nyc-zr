# 138-02 District Plan and Maps

The regulations of this Chapter are designed to implement the **_Special East Harlem Corridors District_** Plan. The District Plan includes the map, “Special East Harlem Corridors District and Subdistrict,” in the Appendix to this Chapter which is hereby incorporated and made part of this Resolution for the purpose of specifying locations where the special regulations and requirements set forth in this Chapter apply.
