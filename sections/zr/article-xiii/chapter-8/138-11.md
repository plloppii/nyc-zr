# 138-11 Location of Residential Use Within Buildings

In C4 or C6 Districts, the provisions of Section [32-422](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-2/32-422) (Location of floors occupied by commercial uses) shall be modified such that the limitations set forth in paragraph (a) of such Section need not apply, and the requirements in paragraph (b) of such Section shall apply only where **_commercial_** **_uses_** are located above any **_story_** containing **_dwelling units_**.
