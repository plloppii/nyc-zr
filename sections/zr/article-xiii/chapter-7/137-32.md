# 137-32 Height and Setback Regulations

In **_Special Coastal Risk District_** 4, all **_detached_** or **_semi-detached_** **_single-_** or **_two-family residences_** shall be subject to the height and setback provisions set forth in Section [64-332](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-4/64-332) (Height and setback regulations for cottage envelope buildings).
