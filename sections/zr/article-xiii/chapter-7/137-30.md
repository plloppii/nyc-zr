# 137-30 SPECIAL BULK REGULATIONS

The special **_bulk_** regulations of this Section [137-30](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-xiii/chapter-7/137-30), inclusive, shall apply to **_buildings_** in the **_Special Coastal Risk Districts_** as set forth in the table in Section [137-12](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-xiii/chapter-7/137-12) (Applicability of Special Regulations).
