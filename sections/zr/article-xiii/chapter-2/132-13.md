# 132-13 Streetscape Regulations

The underlying **_ground floor level_** streetscape provisions set forth in Section [32-30](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-2/32-30) (STREETSCAPE REGULATIONS), inclusive, shall apply, except that the **_ground floor level_** **_street_** frontages **_along those portions of_** streets **_mapped in the_** Special Enhanced Commercial Districts**_, as listed in Section [132-11](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-xiii/chapter-2/132-11) (Special Enhanced Commercial Districts Specified), shall be considered_** Tier C street frontages#. Additional regulations are set forth in Section [132-20](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-xiii/chapter-2/132-20), inclusive.
