# 132-211 Non-conforming uses

In **_Special Enhanced Commercial Districts_** 2 and 3, the regulations of Article V, Chapter 2, shall be modified so that the discontinuance provisions of Section [52-60](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-v/chapter-2/52-60) shall not apply to such change of **_use_** within establishments with **_non-conforming_** **_street wall_** widths.
