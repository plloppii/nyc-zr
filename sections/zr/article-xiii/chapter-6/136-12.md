# 136-12 Certain Uses in C2 Districts

Within the locations shown on Map 2 (Commercial Core) in the Appendix to this Chapter, the **_use_** regulations shall be modified within C2 Districts to permit **_uses_** listed under Use Groups VI and VIII, pursuant to the regulations for a C4 District.
