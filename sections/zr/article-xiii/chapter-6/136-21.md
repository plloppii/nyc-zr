# 136-21 Street Wall Location

In C2 Districts, the **_street wall_** location regulations of the underlying district shall apply except as modified in this Section.

(a) In C2 Districts mapped within R6 and R7-1 Districts, the **_street wall_** location provisions of paragraph (b) Section [35-631](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-5/35-631) shall apply except that the **_street wall_** shall extend to at least the minimum base height specified in Section [136-222](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-xiii/chapter-6/136-222) (Minimum and maximum base height), or the height of the **_building_**, whichever is less.
(b) In C2 Districts mapped within R5 Districts, the **_street wall_** location provisions of paragraph (b) Section [35-631](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-5/35-631) shall apply except that the **_street wall_** shall extend to a height of 30 feet, or the height of the **_building_**, whichever is less.
(c) Below a height of 15 feet or the height of the second **_story_** floor, whichever is lower, no recesses shall be permitted within 30 feet of the intersection of two **_street lines_** except recesses that do not exceed a depth of 12 inches.
