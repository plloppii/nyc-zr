# 139-111 Permitted uses in Manufacturing Districts

In all **_Manufacturing Districts_**, in addition to the **_uses_** specified in Article IV, Chapter 2, the following **_uses_** shall also be permitted:

(a) **_uses_** listed under Use Group III(B); and
(b) **_uses_** listed under Food and Beverage Retailers in Use Group VI, without limitation as to **_floor area_** per establishment.
