# 139-142 Enclosure of uses

In **_Manufacturing Districts_**, the underlying regulations of Section [42-50](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iv/chapter-2/42-50) (SUPPLEMENTARY USE REGULATIONS) shall apply, except that all storage of materials or products shall be located within **_completely enclosed buildings_** regardless of distance from a **_Residence District_**.
