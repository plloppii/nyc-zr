# 139-521 Parcels 4, 5, 13, 14 and 15

On Parcels 4, 5, 13, 14 and 15, where a **_shore public walkway_** is required, such **_shore public walkway_** shall have a minimum width of 30 feet. The required minimum depth of a **_waterfront yard_** shall be 30 feet.
