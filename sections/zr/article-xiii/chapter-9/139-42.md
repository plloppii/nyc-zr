# 139-42 Street Tree Requirements

In all districts, all **_developments_**, or **_enlargements_** that increase the **_floor area_** on a **_zoning lot_** by 20 percent or more, shall provide **_street_** trees in accordance with Section [26-41](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-6/26-41) (Street Tree Planting).
