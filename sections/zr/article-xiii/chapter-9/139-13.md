# 139-13 Gowanus Retail and Entertainment Uses

For the purposes of applying the basic **_floor area ratio_** regulations of Section [139-21](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-xiii/chapter-9/139-21), **_Gowanus retail and entertainment uses_** shall be **_uses_** listed under Use Groups VI or VIII, other than those included in **_referenced commercial and manufacturing uses_**.
