# 139-02 General Provisions

In harmony with the general purposes and content of this Resolution and the general purposes of the **_Special Gowanus Mixed Use District_**, the regulations of this Chapter shall apply within the **_Special Gowanus Mixed Use District_**. The regulations of all other Chapters of this Resolution are applicable, except as modified, supplemented or superseded by the provisions of this Chapter. In the event of a conflict between the provisions of this Chapter and other regulations of this Resolution, the provisions of this Chapter shall control.
