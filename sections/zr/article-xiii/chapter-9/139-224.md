# 139-224 Waterfront yards

The provisions of Section [62-33](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-2/62-33) (Special Yard and Lot Coverage Regulations on Waterfront Blocks) shall be modified such that a **_waterfront yard_** shall be provided in accordance with the provisions of Section [62-332](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-2/62-332) (Rear yards and waterfront yards) on all **_waterfront zoning lots_**, as that term is defined in Section [62-11](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-2/62-11), regardless of **_use_**.

The depth of the **_waterfront yard_** shall be measured from the **_zoning lot line_** adjoining the Gowanus Canal, or where the provisions of paragraph (f) of Section [139-51](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-xiii/chapter-9/139-51) (Area-wide Modifications) are utilized, from the bulkhead. The depth of the **_waterfront yard_** may be reduced as set forth in Section [62-332](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-2/62-332).
