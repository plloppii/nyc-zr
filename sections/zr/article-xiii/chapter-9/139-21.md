# 139-21 Floor Area Regulations

Basic **_floor area_** regulations are established in Section 139-211. Such regulations may be modified by the provisions of this Section, inclusive.

The basic maximum **_floor area ratios_** may be increased for certain **_zoning lots_** pursuant to Sections [139-212](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-xiii/chapter-9/139-212) (Gowanus mix) and [139-213](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-xiii/chapter-9/139-213) (Special floor area provisions for transit improvements).

Special regulations for **_community facility floor area_** on **_zoning lots_** containing **_schools_** are set forth in Section [139-214](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-xiii/chapter-9/139-214) (Special floor area provisions for zoning lots containing schools).

Special regulations for certain **_zoning lots_** are set forth in Section [139-215](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-xiii/chapter-9/139-215) (Special floor area provisions for zoning lots containing comfort stations) and [139-216](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-xiii/chapter-9/139-216) (Special floor area provisions for street improvements).

For the purposes of applying the provisions of Section [64-322](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-4/64-322) (Special floor area modifications for flood-resistant buildings), **_primary frontages_** shall be the locations designated on Map 3 in the Appendix to this Chapter.
