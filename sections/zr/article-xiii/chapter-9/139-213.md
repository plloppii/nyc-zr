# 139-213 Special floor area provisions for transit improvements

In **_Commercial Districts_**, the **_floor area ratios_** set forth in Section [139-211](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-xiii/chapter-9/139-211) (Basic floor area regulations) may be increased by up to 20 percent, pursuant to the provisions of Section [139-46](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-xiii/chapter-9/139-46) (Certification for Transit Improvements). Where the **_residential_** **_floor area ratio_** is increased, such additional **_floor area_** shall be exempt from the requirements of Section [27-131](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-7/27-131) (Mandatory Inclusionary Housing).
