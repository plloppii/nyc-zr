# 139-541 Special design standards for guardrails

In addition to the provisions of paragraph (a) of Section [62-651](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-2/62-651) (Guardrails, gates and other protective barriers), guardrails shall comply with the illustrations provided in either paragraph (a) or (b) of this Section, or shall be of a comparable design which is the minimum modification needed. Where modification is sought, it shall be deemed suitable by the Chairperson of the City Planning Commission in consultation with the Department of Parks and Recreation (DPR).
