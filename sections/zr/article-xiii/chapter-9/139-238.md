# 139-238 Special height regulations for zoning lots containing schools

In Subdistrict C, and in Subareas D1, D2, and D3, for **_zoning lots_** containing **_schools_** regulated by Section [139-214](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-xiii/chapter-9/139-214) (Special floor area provisions for zoning lots containing schools), the maximum tower height specified by the regulations in this Section, inclusive, may be increased as-of-right by 40 feet. This allowance may be further modified by the provisions of Section [139-47](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-xiii/chapter-9/139-47) (Authorization for sites containing schools).
