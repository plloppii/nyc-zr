# 139-55 Special Signage Regulations

Any **_accessory sign_** that is provided adjacent to any **_waterfront public access area_** shall be limited to a single non-illuminated **_sign_**, indicating only the name or address of the **_building_** or commercial establishment to which it is **_accessory_**, not exceeding 16 inches in height.
