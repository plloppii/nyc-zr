# 131-14 Location of Uses Within Buildings

The provisions of Section [32-421](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-2/32-421) (Limitation on floors occupied by commercial uses) shall be modified such that the limitations set forth in paragraphs (a), (b) and (e) of such Section shall not apply.

In the Coney North and Coney West Subdistricts, a **_public parking garage_** may occupy any **_story_** of a **_mixed building_** provided such garage complies with the provisions of Section [131-52](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-xiii/chapter-1/131-52) (Use and Location of Parking Facilities).
