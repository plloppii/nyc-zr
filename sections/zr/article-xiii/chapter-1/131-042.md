# 131-042 Applicability of Article VII, Chapter 4

The provisions of Section [74-194](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-4/74-194) (Public parking garages or public parking lots in high density central areas) shall not apply in the **_Special Coney Island District_**. In lieu thereof, **_public parking lots_** shall not be permitted, and **_public parking garages_** of any size shall be permitted as-of-right, provided such garages comply with the provisions of Section [131-52](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-xiii/chapter-1/131-52) (Use and Location of Parking Facilities).
