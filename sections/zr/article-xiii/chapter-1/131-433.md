# 131-433 Riegelmann Boardwalk and building line of Parcel F

A **_street wall_** shall be located on Riegelmann Boardwalk **_street line_** and extend along the entire Riegelmann Boardwalk frontage of the **_zoning lot_** to a minimum height of 20 feet, as shown on Map 5 (Minimum and Maximum Base Heights). Any **_building or other structure_** within 70 feet of Riegelmann Boardwalk shall not exceed a height of 40 feet above the level of Riegelmann Boardwalk.

In addition, on Parcel F, a **_street wall_** shall be located on the Parachute Way building line and the portion of the Ocean Way building line that is within 100 feet of the Parachute Way building line, as shown on Map 4 (Street Wall Location). Such **_street walls_** shall extend along such entire frontages of Parcel F to a minimum height of 20 feet.
