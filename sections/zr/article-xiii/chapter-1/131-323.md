# 131-323 Special floor area ratio regulations for hotel uses

In the Coney North Subdistrict, for **_transient hotels_** located within 200 feet of Surf Avenue between Stillwell Avenue and West 16th Street, the maximum permitted **_floor area ratio_** shall be 3.75.
