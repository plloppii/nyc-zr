# 131-44 Coney North Subdistrict

The regulations of this Section shall apply to all **_buildings or other structures_** in the Coney North Subdistrict. Maps 4 (Street Wall Location) and 5 (Minimum and Maximum Base Heights), in the Appendix to this Chapter, illustrate the **_street wall_** location provisions, minimum and maximum base height provisions and maximum **_building_** height provisions of this Section, inclusive.
