# 131-16 Special Permit for Certain Arenas

On Parcels B and G in the Coney West Subdistrict of the **_Special Coney Island District_**, the City Planning Commission may permit an arena in accordance with the provisions of paragraph (d) of Section [74-182](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-4/74-182) (Arenas, auditoriums, stadiums or trade expositions).
