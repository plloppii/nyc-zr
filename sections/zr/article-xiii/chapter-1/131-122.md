# 131-122 Use Group B: Amusement and entertainment-enhancing uses

Use Group B consists of a group of **_uses_**, selected from Use Groups VI, VIII and X, as modified in this Section:

From Use Group VI

Eating or drinking establishments, without size limitations

From Use Group VIII

All **_uses_** listed under Art Galleries and Studios

All **_uses_** listed under Other Assembly Spaces

From Use Group X

Breweries, included under all other beverage manufacturing.
