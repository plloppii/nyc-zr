# 131-05 Definitions

For purposes of this Chapter, matter in italics is defined in Sections [12-10](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-i/chapter-2/12-10) (DEFINITIONS), [32-301](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-2/32-301) (Definitions), or in this Section.

Outdoor amusement parks

For the purposes of this Chapter, the definition of “outdoor amusement park” set forth in Section [12-10](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-i/chapter-2/12-10) shall be modified to also include recreational attractions, rides, games and other forms of entertainment for visitors of all ages, as found in North American Industry Classification System (NAICS) industry code 71311 (Amusement and Theme Parks) that are enclosed within a **_building or other structure_**.
