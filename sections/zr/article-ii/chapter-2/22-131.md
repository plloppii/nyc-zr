# 22-131 Use Group III – general use allowances

The following tables include **_uses_** classified as Use Group III and set forth their allowances by **_Residence District_**. Such **_uses_** are categorized as **_community facilities_** with and without sleeping accommodations, as provided in paragraphs A and B of this Section. Notations found in the tables are further described in Section [22-10](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-2/22-10) (USE ALLOWANCES).
