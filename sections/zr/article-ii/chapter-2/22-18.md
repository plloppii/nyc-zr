# 22-18 Use Group VIII – Recreation, Entertainment and Assembly Spaces

R1 R2 R3 R4 R5 R6 R7 R8 R9 R10 R11 R12

Use Group VIII consists of **_uses_** that provide recreation and entertainment opportunities, as well as other places of assembly. In **_Residence Districts_**, such **_uses_** shall include:

(a) outdoor day camps, where allowed by special permit of the Board of Standards and Appeals, in accordance with Section [73-184](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-3/73-184) (Outdoor day camps); and
(b) riding academies or stables, where allowed by special permit of the Board of Standards and Appeals, in accordance with Section [73-185](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-3/73-185) (Riding Academies or Stables).
