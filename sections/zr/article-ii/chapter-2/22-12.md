# 22-12 Use Group II – Residences

R1 R2 R3 R4 R5 R6 R7 R8 R9 R10 R11 R12

Use Group II consists of **_residences_** of various types. The provisions regulating **_uses_**
classified in this Use Group are set forth as follows:

(a) Section [22-121](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-2/22-121) (Use Group II – general use allowances) which includes the compilation of **_uses_** in the Use Group table; and
(b) Section [22-122](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-2/22-122) (Use Group II – uses subject to additional conditions) for additional conditions that apply to certain **_uses_**, as denoted with a “P” in the Use Group table.
