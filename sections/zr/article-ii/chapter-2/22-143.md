# 22-143 Use Group IV – uses subject to additional conditions

For **_uses_** denoted with a “P” in Section [22-141](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-2/22-141) (Use Group IV – general use allowances), the provisions of this Section shall apply. In all **_Residence Districts_**, **_energy infrastructure equipment_** shall comply with the enclosure and screening provisions of Section [26-50](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-6/26-50) (SPECIAL SCREENING AND ENCLOSURE PROVISIONS).
