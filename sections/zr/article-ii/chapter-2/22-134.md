# 22-134 Use Group III – uses permitted by special permit

For **_uses_** denoted with “○” in Section [22-131](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-2/22-131) (Use Group III – general use allowances), the provisions of this Section shall apply.

(a) The following **_uses_** may be permitted in R1 or R2 Districts by special permit of the Board of Standards and Appeals:
    (1) College or school student dormitories and fraternity or sorority student houses may be permitted in accordance with Section [73-131](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-3/73-131) (College or school student dormitories or fraternity or sorority student houses).
    (2) Colleges or universities, including professional schools but excluding business colleges or trade schools may be permitted in accordance with Section [73-132](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-3/73-132) (Colleges or universities).
    (3) Non-commercial clubs may be permitted in accordance with Section [73-135](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-3/73-135) (Non-commercial clubs).
    (4) Welfare centers may be permitted in accordance with Section [73-136](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-3/73-136) (Welfare centers).
(b) The following **_uses_** may be permitted in R1 or R2 Districts by special permit of the City Planning Commission: **_long-term care facilities_** in accordance with Section [74-131](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-4/74-131) (Long-term care facilities). However, **_long-term care facilities_** shall be permitted as-of-right on **_qualifying residential sites_**.
