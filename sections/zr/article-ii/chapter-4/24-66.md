# 24-66 Modifications of Court Regulations or Distance Requirements

R1 R2 R3 R4 R5 R6 R7 R8 R9 R10 R11 R12

In all districts, as indicated, for certain **_community facility_** **_uses_** in specified situations, the Board of Standards and Appeals may modify the regulations set forth in Sections [24-61](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-4/24-61) to [24-65](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-4/24-65), inclusive, relating to Court Regulations and Minimum Distance between Windows and Walls or Lot Lines, in accordance with the provisions of Section [73-64](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-3/73-64) (Modifications for Community Facility Uses).
