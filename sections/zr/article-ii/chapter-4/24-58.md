# 24-58 Special Provisions for Zoning Lots Divided by District Boundaries

R1 R2 R3 R4 R5 R6 R7 R8 R9 R10 R11 R12

In all districts, as indicated, whenever a **_zoning lot_** is divided by a boundary between districts, or is subject to other regulations resulting in different height and setback regulations, or whenever a **_zoning lot_** is divided by a boundary between a district to which the provisions of Section [24-54](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-4/24-54) (Tower Regulations) apply and a district to which such provisions do not apply, the provisions set forth in Article VII, Chapter 7, shall apply.
