# 24-592 Height limitations for narrow buildings or enlargements

R7-2 R8 R9 R10

In the districts indicated, the provisions of Section [23-738](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-3/23-738) (Height limitations for narrow buildings) shall apply to portions of **_buildings_** with **_street walls_** less than 45 feet in width.
