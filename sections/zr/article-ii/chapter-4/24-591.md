# 24-591 Limited Height Districts

R1 R2 R3 R4 R5 R6 R7 R8 R9 R10

In the districts indicated, within a **_Limited Height District_**, the maximum height of a **_building or other structure_**, or portion thereof, shall be as shown in the following table:

| **_Limited Height District_** | Maximum Height above **_Curb Level_** or **_Base Plane_**, as applicable |
| --- | --- |
| LH-1 | 50 feet |
| LH-lA | 60 feet |
| LH-2 | 70 feet |
| LH-3 | 100 feet |
