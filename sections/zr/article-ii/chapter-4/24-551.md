# 24-551 Required side setbacks for tall buildings in low bulk districts

R1 R2 R3 R4 R5

In the districts indicated, except R5D Districts, no **_community facility_** portion of any **_building_** that is more than 35 feet or more than three **_stories_** above the level of a **_side yard_**, whichever is lower, shall be nearer to a **_side lot line_** bounding such **_yard_** than a distance equal to one-half the height above yard level of such portion of the **_building_**.

The following are permitted to project into any open area required under the provisions of this Section:

(a) parapet walls, not more than four feet high; and

(b) chimneys or flues, with a total width not exceeding 10 percent of the width of the **_building's_** walls facing such open area.
