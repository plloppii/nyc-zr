# 24-31 Applicability of Yard Regulations

R1 R2 R3 R4 R5 R6 R7 R8 R9 R10 R11 R12

In all districts, as indicated, **_yards_** shall be provided as set forth in Sections [24-30](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-4/24-30) (YARD REGULATIONS) and [24-40](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-4/24-40) (SPECIAL PROVISIONS FOR ZONING LOTS DIVIDED BY DISTRICT BOUNDARIES), inclusive.

For **_zoning lots_** with **_residential_** and **_community facility_** **_uses_**, **_front yards_** shall be provided pursuant to Article II, Chapter 3, where applicable, and **_side yards_** and **_rear yards_** shall be provided in accordance with this Chapter. The **_building_** articulation provisions of paragraph (b) of Section [23-426](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-3/23-426) (Additional height and setback provisions) shall apply to **_zoning lots_** with **_residential_** and **_community facility_** **_uses_**.

For the **_residential_** portion of a **_building_** with both **_residential_** and **_community facility_** **_uses_**, the required **_residential rear yard_** shall be provided at the floor level of the lowest **_story_** used for **_dwelling units_**, where any window of such **_dwelling units_** faces onto such **_rear yard_**.
