# 24-55 Required Side and Rear Setbacks

R1 R2 R3 R4 R5 R6 R7 R8 R9 R10 R11 R12

In all districts, as indicated, side and rear setbacks shall be provided as specified in this Section. Unenclosed balconies, subject to the provisions of Section [24-164](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-4/24-164) (Balconies); and awnings and other sun control devices, decks, **_qualifying exterior wall thickness_**, parapet walls, roof thickness, solar energy systems up to four feet high, vegetated roofs, and weirs, as set forth in Section [24-51](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-4/24-51) (Permitted Obstructions), are permitted to project into or over any open areas required by the provisions of this Section.
