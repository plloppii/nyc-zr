# 24-38 Special Provisions for Through Lots

R1 R2 R3 R4 R5 R6 R7 R8 R9 R10 R11 R12

In all districts, as indicated, the regulations of this Section shall apply to all **_through lots_**. In the case of a **_zoning lot_** occupying an entire **_block_**, no **_rear yard_** or **_rear yard equivalent_** shall be required.
