# 24-41 Modifications of Rear Yard Regulations

R1 R2 R3 R4 R5 R6 R7 R8 R9 R10 R11 R12

In all districts, as indicated, the regulations set forth in Section [24-393](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-4/24-393) (For zoning lots with multiple rear lot lines) may be modified in accordance with the provisions of Section [73-69](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-3/73-69) (Rear Yard Modifications).
