# 24-50 HEIGHT AND SETBACK REGULATIONS

R1 R2 R3 R4 R5 R6 R7 R8 R9 R10 R11 R12

In all districts, as indicated, the height and setback regulations of this Section [24-50](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-4/24-50), inclusive, shall apply as follows.

Height and setback regulations applicable to R1 through R5 Districts, except R5D Districts, are set forth in Section [24-521](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-4/24-521) (Front setbacks in districts where front yards are required). In R5D Districts, all **_buildings or other structures_** shall comply with the applicable height and setback requirements set forth in Section [23-40](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-3/23-40) (HEIGHT AND SETBACK REGULATIONS), inclusive.

In R6 through R12 Districts with a letter suffix, the height and setback regulations of Section [23-43](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-3/23-43) (Height and Setback Requirements in R6 Through R12 Districts), inclusive, shall be applied to all **_buildings_** in accordance with the applicable **_Residence District_** regulations, pursuant to Section [24-04](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-4/24-04) (Applicability of Article II, Chapter 3 Regulations). For other **_buildings_** in R6 through R12 Districts without a letter suffix utilizing the provisions of this Chapter, height and setback regulations are set forth in Sections [24-522](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-4/24-522) (Front setbacks in districts where front yards are not required), [24-53](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-4/24-53) (Alternate Front Setbacks) and [24-54](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-4/24-54) (Tower Regulations), as applicable.

In all districts, supplemental provisions are set forth in Sections [24-55](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-4/24-55) (Required Side and Rear Setbacks), [24-56](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-4/24-56) (Special Height and Setback Provisions for Certain Areas), [24-57](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-4/24-57) (Modifications of Height and Setback Regulations), [24-58](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-4/24-58) (Special Provisions for Zoning Lots Divided by District Boundaries) and [24-59](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-4/24-59) (Special Height Limitations), respectively.
