# 24-651 General provisions

R1 R2 R3 R4 R5 R6 R7 R8 R9 R10 R11 R12

In all districts, as indicated, except as otherwise provided in Section [24-652](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-4/24-652) (Minimum distance between required windows and certain walls), the minimum distance between any required windows and:

(a) any wall;

(b) a **_rear lot line_**, or vertical projection thereof; or

(c) a **_side lot line_**, or vertical projection thereof;

shall be 20 feet, measured in a horizontal plane at the sill level of, and perpendicular to, such window; provided, however, that a required window may open on any **_outer court_** meeting the requirements of Section [24-63](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-4/24-63) (Outer Court Regulations).
