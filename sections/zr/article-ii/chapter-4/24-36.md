# 24-36 Minimum Required Rear Yards

R1 R2 R3 R4 R5 R6 R7 R8 R9 R10 R11 R12

In all districts, as indicated, a **_rear yard_** with a depth of not less than 30 feet shall be provided at every **_rear lot line_** on any **_zoning lot_** except as otherwise provided in Sections [24-37](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-4/24-37) (Special Provisions for Shallow Interior Lots), [24-38](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-4/24-38) (Special Provisions for Through Lots) or [24-39](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-4/24-39) (Other Special Provisions for Rear Yards). **_Rear yards_** shall also be provided along portions of **_side lot lines_** as set forth in Section [24-361](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-4/24-361) (Beyond one hundred feet of a street line).
