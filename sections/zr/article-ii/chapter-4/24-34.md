# 24-34 Minimum Required Front Yards

R1 R2 R3 R4 R5

In the districts indicated, **_front yards_** shall be provided as set forth in the following table, except that for a **_corner lot_** in an R1-2 District, one **_front yard_** may have a depth of 15 feet.

| District | **_Front Yard_** (in feet) |
| --- | --- |
| R1 | 20 |
| R2 R3 R4 | 15 |
| R5 | 10 |
| R5D | 5\* |

^\* In R5D Districts, the provisions set forth in Section [23-321](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-3/23-321) (Basic front yard requirements) shall apply.
