# 24-351 Special provisions applying along district boundaries

R6 R7 R8 R9 R10 R11 R12

In the districts indicated, if the boundary of an adjoining R1, R2, R3, R4 or R5 District coincides with a **_side lot line_** of a **_zoning lot_**, a **_side yard_** at least eight feet wide shall be provided along such boundary within the districts indicated.
