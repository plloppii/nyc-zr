# 24-17 Special Provisions for Zoning Lots Divided by District Boundaries or Subject to Different Bulk Regulations

R1 R2 R3 R4 R5 R6 R7 R8 R9 R10 R11 R12

In the districts, as indicated, whenever a **_zoning lot_** is divided by a boundary between districts or is subject to **_bulk_** regulations resulting in different maximum **_floor area ratios_** or different maximum percentages of **_lot coverage_**, on portions of the **_zoning lot_**, the provisions set forth in Article VII, Chapter 7, shall apply.
