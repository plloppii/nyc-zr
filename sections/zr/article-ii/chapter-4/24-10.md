# 24-10 FLOOR AREA AND LOT COVERAGE REGULATIONS

R1 R2 R3 R4 R5 R6 R7 R8 R9 R10 R11 R12

In all districts, as indicated, the **_floor area_** and **_lot coverage_** regulations of this Section [24-10](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-4/24-10), inclusive, shall apply as follows.

For any **_zoning lot_**, the maximum **_floor area ratio_** and maximum percent of **_lot coverage_** for a **_community facility_** **_use_** shall not exceed the **_floor area ratio_** and **_lot coverage_** set forth in Section [24-11](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-4/24-11) (Maximum Floor Area Ratio and Percentage of Lot Coverage), except as otherwise provided in the following Sections:

Section [24-111](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-4/24-111) (Maximum floor area ratio for certain community facility uses)

Section [24-112](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-4/24-112) (Special floor area ratio provisions for certain areas)

Section [24-13](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-4/24-13) (Floor Area Bonus for Deep Front and Wide Side Yards)

Section [24-14](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-4/24-14) (Floor Area Bonus for a Public Plaza)

Section [24-15](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-4/24-15) (Floor Area Bonus for Arcades)

Section [24-16](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-4/24-16) (Special Provisions for Zoning Lots Containing Both Community Facility and Residential Uses)

Section [24-17](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-4/24-17) (Special Provisions for Zoning Lots Divided by District Boundaries or Subject to Different Bulk Regulations).

Where **_floor area_** in a **_building_** is shared by multiple **_uses_**, the **_floor area_** for such shared portion shall be attributed to each **_use_** proportionately, based on the percentage each **_use_** occupies of the total **_floor area_** of the **_zoning lot_** less any shared **_floor area_**.
