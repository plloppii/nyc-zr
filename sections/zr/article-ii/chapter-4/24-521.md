# 24-521 Front setbacks in districts where front yards are required

R1 R2 R3 R4 R5

In the districts indicated, except R5D Districts, where **_front yards_** are required, the front wall or any other portion of a **_building or other structure_** shall not penetrate the **_sky exposure plane_** set forth in the following table:

MAXIMUM HEIGHT OF FRONT WALL AND REQUIRED FRONT SETBACKS

| Height above **_Front Yard Line_** (in feet) | **_Sky Exposure Plane_** |  |  |  | District |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Height above **_Front Yard Line_** (in feet) | Slope over **_Zoning Lot_** (expressed as a ratio of vertical distance to horizontal distance) |  |  |  | District |
| Height above **_Front Yard Line_** (in feet) | On **_Narrow Street_** |  | On **_Wide Street_** |  | District |
| Height above **_Front Yard Line_** (in feet) | Vertical Distance | Horizontal Distance | Vertical Distance | Horizontal Distance | District |
| 25 | 1 to | 1 | 1 to | 1 | R1 R2 R3 |
| 35 | 1 to | 1 | 1 to | 1 | R4 R5 |
