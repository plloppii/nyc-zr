# 24-522 Front setbacks in districts where front yards are not required

R6 R7 R8 R9 R10 R11 R12

In the districts indicated without a letter suffix,if the front wall or other portion of a **_building or other structure_** is located at the **_street line_** or within the **_initial setback distance_** set forth in the table in this Section, the height of such front wall or other portion of a **_building or other structure_** shall not exceed the maximum height above **_curb level_** set forth in the table. Above such specified maximum height and beyond the **_initial setback distance_**, the **_building or other structure_** shall not penetrate the **_sky exposure plane_** set forth in the table:
