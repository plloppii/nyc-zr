# 23-712 Floor area modifications

For **_zoning lots_** in R4 or R5 Districts, without a letter or number suffix, utilizing the **_predominantly built-up area_** provisions of Section [23-71](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-3/23-71), inclusive, the maximum **_floor area ratio_** shall be as set forth in the following table:

| **District** | Maximum **_Floor Area Ratio_** |
| --- | --- |
| R4 | 1.35 |
| R5 | 1.65 |
