# 23-04 Zoning Lots Divided by District Boundaries

In all districts, whenever a **_zoning lot_** is divided by a boundary between districts or is subject to different **_bulk_** regulations for different portions of the **_zoning lot_**, the provisions set forth in Article VII, Chapter 7, shall apply.
