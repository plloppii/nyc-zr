# 23-12 Lot Area and Lot Width Regulations in R6 through R12 Districts

R6 R7 R8 R9 R10 R11 R12

In the districts indicated, no **_residence_** shall be permitted on a **_zoning lot_** with a total **_lot area_** or **_lot width_** less than as set forth in the following table:

REQUIRED MINIMUM LOT AREA AND LOT WIDTH

| Type of **_Residence_** | **District** | Minimum **_Lot Area_** (in sq. ft) | Minimum **_Lot Width_** (in ft) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **_Single-_** or **_two-family_** **_detached_** or **_zero lot line_** | R6 - R12 | 2,375 | 25 |
| Other **_residences_** | R6 - R12 | 1,700 | 18 |
