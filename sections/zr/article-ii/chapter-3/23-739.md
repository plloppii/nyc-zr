# 23-739 Limited Height Districts

In the **_Limited Height Districts_**, for **_zoning lots_** that do not contain **_qualifying affordable housing_** or **_qualifying senior housing_**, the maximum height of **_buildings or other structures_** shall be as shown in the following table:

| **_Limited Height District_** | Maximum height above **_curb level_** or **_base plane_**, as applicable |
| --- | --- |
| LH-1 | 50 feet |
| LH-1A | 60 feet |
| LH-2 | 70 feet |
| LH-3 | 100 feet |
