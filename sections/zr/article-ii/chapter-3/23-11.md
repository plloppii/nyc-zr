# 23-11 Lot Area and Lot Width Regulations in R1 Through R5 Districts

R1 R2 R3 R4 R5

In the districts indicated, no **_residence_** shall be permitted on a **_zoning lot_** with a total **_lot area_** or **_lot width_** less than as set forth in the following table:

REQUIRED MINIMUM LOT AREA AND LOT WIDTH

| Type of **_Residence_** | **District** | Minimum **_Lot Area_** (in sq. ft) | Minimum **_Lot Width_** (in ft) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **_Single-_** or **_two-family_** **_detached_**, or **_zero lot line_**, where permitted | R1-1 | 7,125 | 75 |
| **_Single-_** or **_two-family_** **_detached_**, or **_zero lot line_**, where permitted | R1-2 R1-2A | 4,750 | 50 |
| **_Single-_** or **_two-family_** **_detached_**, or **_zero lot line_**, where permitted | R2 R2A R2X R3X R3-1 R3-2 R4 R5 | 2,850 | 30 |
| **_Single-_** or **_two-family_** **_detached_**, or **_zero lot line_**, where permitted | R3A R4-1 R4A R4B R5A R5B R5D | 2,375 | 25 |
| Other **_residences_**, where permitted | R1 – R5 | 1,700 | 18 |
