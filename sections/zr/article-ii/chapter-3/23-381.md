# 23-381 Special provisions in other geographies

In all districts, for **_buildings_** containing **_multiple dwelling residences_** on **_zoning lots_** that adjoin a **_public park_**, the minimum distance between a **_legally required window_** and a **_lot line_** need not apply to portions of **_buildings_** facing such **_public park_** where the Commissioner of the Department of Parks and Recreation determines that the **_public park_** will provide sufficient access to light and air for such **_building’s_** **_legally required windows_**. Where the Commissioner makes such determination, the **_public park_** shall be considered a **_street_** for the purposes of applying other applicable laws or statutes to such **_legally required window_**.
