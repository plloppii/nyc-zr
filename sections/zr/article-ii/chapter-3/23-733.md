# 23-733 Floor area ratios in R9 and R10 Districts

(a) **_Floor area ratios_** in R10 Districts
    In R10 Districts, the maximum **_floor area ratio_** on a **_zoning lot_** shall be 12.0 for **_qualifying affordable housing_** or **_qualifying senior housing_** and 10.0 for other **_residences_**.
(b) Additional **_floor area_** regulations in R9 and R10 Districts for **_buildings_** with towers
    In R9 and R10 Districts, for **_zoning lots_** containing a **_building_** that is **_developed_** or **_enlarged_** pursuant to the applicable tower regulations of Section [23-737](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-3/23-737) (Tower regulations), the **_floor area_** provisions of Section [23-241](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-3/23-241) (Special tower provisions) shall apply.
