# 23-233 Floor area provisions for refuse storage and disposal

Floor space in a **_building_** allocated to refuse storage and disposal may be exempted from the definition of **_floor area_** in an amount not to exceed a maximum of three square feet per **_dwelling unit_** in the **_building_**.
