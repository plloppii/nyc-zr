# 25-18 Maximum Spaces for Permitted Community Facility or Commercial Uses

R1 R2 R3 R4 R5 R6 R7 R8 R9 R10 R11 R12

In all districts, as indicated, on a **_zoning lot_** used for permitted **_community facility_** or **_commercial_** **_uses_**, not more than one off-street parking space shall be provided for every 400 square feet of **_lot area_**, except as provided in Section [25-19](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-5/25-19) (Modification of Maximum Spaces for Permitted Community Facility or Commercial Uses).
