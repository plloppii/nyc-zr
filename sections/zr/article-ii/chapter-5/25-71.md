# 25-71 Permitted Accessory Off-street Loading Berths

R1 R2 R3 R4 R5 R6 R7 R8 R9 R10 R11 R12

In all districts, as indicated, **_accessory_** off-street loading berths, open or enclosed, may be provided for **_residences_**, for permitted **_community facility_** **_uses_**, for **_commercial_** **_uses_** permitted as **_accessory_** **_uses_** in **_large-scale residential developments_**, or for **_uses_** permitted by special permit, under rules and regulations promulgated by the Commissioner of Buildings, and subject to the provisions set forth in Sections [25-75](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-5/25-75) (Location of Access to the Street), [25-76](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-5/25-76) (Surfacing), and [25-77](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-5/25-77) (Screening).
