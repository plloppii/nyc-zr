# 25-635 Maximum driveway grade

R1 R2 R3 R4 R5

In the districts indicated, the maximum grade of a driveway shall not exceed 11 percent in any **_front yard_**. Driveways existing on April 30, 2008, which exceed a grade of 11 percent, may be used to access parking spaces provided for **_residences_** constructed after April 30, 2008.
