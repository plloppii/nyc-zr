# 25-02 Applicability

Except as otherwise provided in this Section, the regulations of this Chapter on permitted or required **_accessory_** off-street parking spaces and **_accessory_** bicycle parking spaces apply to **_residences_**, **_community facility_** **_uses_** or **_commercial_** **_uses_**, as set forth in the provisions of the various Sections.
