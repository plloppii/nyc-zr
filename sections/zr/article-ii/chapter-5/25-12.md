# 25-12 Maximum Size of Accessory Group Parking Facilities

R1 R2 R3 R4 R5 R6 R7 R8 R9 R10 R11 R12

In all districts, as indicated, no **_group parking facility_** **_accessory_** to **_residences_** shall contain more than 200 off-street parking spaces, and no such facility **_accessory_** to permitted **_community facility_** or **_commercial_** **_uses_** shall contain more than 150 off-street parking spaces, except as provided in Section [25-13](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-5/25-13) (Modification of Maximum Size of Accessory Group Parking Facilities).
