# 26-30 SPECIAL REQUIREMENTS FOR LOTS WITH PRIVATE ROADS IN LOWER DENSITY GROWTH MANAGEMENT AREAS

For all **_zoning lots_** with **_buildings_** accessed by **_private roads_** in **_lower density growth management areas_**, the provisions of Sections [26-20](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-6/26-20) through [26-27](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-6/26-27), inclusive, shall apply. In addition, such regulations are supplemented or superseded in accordance with the provisions of this Section.
