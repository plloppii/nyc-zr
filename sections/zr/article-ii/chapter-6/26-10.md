# 26-10 SPECIAL REQUIREMENTS FOR DEVELOPMENTS IN R9 AND R10 DISTRICTS

For applicable **_buildings_** in R9 and R10 Districts, an application to the Department of Buildings for a permit respecting any **_development_** shall include a plan and an elevation, drawn to a scale of at least one-sixteenth inch to a foot, of the new **_building_** and **_buildings_** on **_contiguous lots_** or **_contiguous blocks_** showing **_arcades_**, **_street wall_** articulation, **_street_** trees, sidewalk paving and such other necessary information as may be required by the Commissioner of Buildings.
