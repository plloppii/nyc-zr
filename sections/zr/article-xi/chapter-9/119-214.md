# 119-214 Tier II requirements for driveways and private roads

The provisions set forth in this Section and Section [119-213](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-xi/chapter-9/119-213) (Grading controls) shall apply to driveways and to **_private roads_** that provide access to **_buildings_** **_developed_** after December 11, 1999. The provisions for **_private roads_** set forth in Section [26-20](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-6/26-20), inclusive, shall not apply. However, the provisions of Sections [26-23](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-6/26-23) (Yards), [26-31](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-6/26-31) (Entrances, Parking Location and Curb Cuts) and [26-32](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-6/26-32) (Lighting, Signage and Crosswalks) shall apply for **_private roads_** in **_lower density growth management areas_**.

(a) Driveways

(1) the maximum grade of a driveway shall not exceed 10 percent;

(2) the paved width of a driveway shall not exceed 18 feet; and

(3) the maximum length of a driveway from a **_private road_** or **_street_** to an **_accessory_** parking space shall not exceed 80 feet.

(b) **_Private roads_**

(1) the maximum grade of a **_private road_** shall not exceed 10 percent;

(2) the width of the graded section beyond the curb back or edge of pavement of a **_private road_** shall extend no more than three feet beyond the curb back or edge of pavement on both the cut and the fill sides of the roadway. If a sidewalk is to be installed parallel to the roadway, the graded section shall be increased by the width of the sidewalk plus no more than one foot beyond the curb back;

(3) the paved width of a **_private road_** shall not exceed 30 feet;

(4) curbs shall be provided along each side of the entire length of a **_private road_** and **_accessory_** parking spaces may be located between the required roadbed and curb;

(5) a curb cut, excluding splays, from a **_street_** to a **_private road_** may be as wide as such **_private road_**;

(6) curb cuts providing access from **_private roads_** to parking spaces shall not exceed the width of the driveway served and in no event shall exceed a width of 18 feet, including splays;

(7) a minimum distance of 16 feet of uninterrupted curb space shall be maintained between all curb cuts;

(8) no building permit shall be issued by the Department of Buildings without approval by the Fire Department regarding the adequacy of vehicular access to and within the development for fire safety. Such approval may include the modification of **_private road_** width as set forth in paragraph (b)(3) of this Section; and

(9) for the purposes of applying the **_yard_** regulations of Section [26-23](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-6/26-23), the curb of the **_private road_** shall be considered to be the **_street line_**.

The City Planning Commission may by authorization or special permit, as applicable, pursuant to Section [119-30](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-xi/chapter-9/119-30) (SPECIAL REVIEW PROVISIONS), allow modifications to, or waivers of, the requirements of this Section. The approval of the Fire Department regarding the adequacy of vehicular access to and within the development for fire safety shall be a condition precedent for any modification or waiver.
