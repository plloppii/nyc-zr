# 112-25 Location of Parking Spaces Along City Island Avenue

In districts not otherwise governed by the streetscape regulations of Section [112-073](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-xi/chapter-2/112-073), no parking shall be permitted between the **_street line_** of City Island Avenue and the **_street wall_** of any **_building_** or its prolongation facing City Island Avenue. However, this provision shall not apply to **_waterfront zoning lots_**.
