# 117-20 QUEENS PLAZA WEST SUBDISTRICT

In the **_Special Long Island City Mixed Use District_**, the special regulations of Section [117-20](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-xi/chapter-7/117-20), inclusive, shall apply in the Queens Plaza West Subdistrict.
