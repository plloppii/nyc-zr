# 117-524 Special yard regulations

The **_yard_** regulations of the underlying districts shall apply, except as specified in this Section.

(a) **_Manufacturing_** or **_commercial buildings_**

The **_rear yard_** provisions of the designated M1 District shall not apply to **_manufacturing_** or **_commercial buildings_** on **_through lots_**.

(b) **_Zoning lots_** adjacent to the Sunnyside Yard

On a **_zoning lot_** sharing a **_lot line_** with the Sunnyside Yard, no **_yards_** are required for any **_building_** within a distance of 100 feet from the shared **_lot line_**.

(c) For **_zoning lots_** occupying an entire **_block_**

No **_rear yard_** or **_rear yard equivalent_** shall be required for a **_zoning lot_** occupying an entire **_block_**.
