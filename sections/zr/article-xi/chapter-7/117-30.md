# 117-30 NORTHERN HUNTERS POINT WATERFRONT SUBDISTRICT

In the **_Special Long Island City Mixed Use District_**, the special regulations of Section [117-30](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-xi/chapter-7/117-30), inclusive, shall apply in the Northern Hunters Point Waterfront Subdistrict.
