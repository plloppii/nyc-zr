# 117-511 Large retail establishments

The underlying M1 District **_use_** regulations shall be modified such that **_uses_** listed under Use Group VI with a size limitation, as denoted with an “S” in the Use Group tables set forth in Section [42-16](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iv/chapter-2/42-16) (Use Group VI – Retail and Services), shall be permitted without a size limitation.
