# 117-11 General Provisions

The **_use_**, **_bulk_** and parking regulations of the underlying districts shall apply, except as modified by the provisions of Section [117-10](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-xi/chapter-7/117-10) (HUNTERS POINT SUBDISTRICT), inclusive.
