# 117-10 HUNTERS POINT SUBDISTRICT

In the **_Special Long Island City Mixed Use District_**, the special regulations of Section [117-10](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-xi/chapter-7/117-10), shall apply within the Hunters Point Subdistrict.
