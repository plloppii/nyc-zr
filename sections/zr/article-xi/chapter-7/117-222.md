# 117-222 Special floor area provisions for zoning lots containing schools

For **_zoning lots_** with a **_lot area_** of at least 20,000 square feet, up to 150,000 square feet of floor space within a public **_school_** constructed in whole or in part pursuant to a written agreement with the New York City School Construction Authority and subject to the jurisdiction of the Department of Education shall be exempt from the definition of **_floor area_**.
