# 117-302 Northern Hunters Point Waterfront Subdistrict Plan

The regulations of Section [117-30](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-xi/chapter-7/117-30), inclusive, are designed to implement the Northern Hunters Point Waterfront Subdistrict Plan as set forth in Appendix C to this Chapter.
