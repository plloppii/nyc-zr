# 117-33 Special Yard Regulations

The underlying **_yard_** regulations of Section [62-33](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-2/62-33) (Special Yard and Lot Regulations on Waterfront Blocks), inclusive, shall apply. In addition, where a **_waterfront yard_** is not required pursuant to Section [62-33](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-2/62-33), **_yards_** meeting the dimensional requirements of Section [62-332](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-2/62-332) (Rear yards and waterfront yards) shall be provided in connection with any **_development_**, in accordance with the provisions of paragraph (f) of Section [62-912](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-2/62-912) (Elements of a Waterfront Access Plan).
