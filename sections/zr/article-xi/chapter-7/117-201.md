# 117-201 General provisions

In the Queens Plaza West Subdistrict, the **_use_**, **_bulk_**, and parking regulations of the underlying districts shall apply, except as modified by the provisions of Section [117-20](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-xi/chapter-7/117-20) (QUEENS PLAZA WEST SUBDISTRICT), inclusive.
