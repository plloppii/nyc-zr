# 117-42 Special Bulk and Use Regulations in the Court Square Subdistrict

**_Zoning lots_** of at least 10,000 square feet with **_buildings_** containing at least 70,000 square feet of **_floor area_** are subject to the provisions of the underlying C5-3 District, as modified by Sections [117-40](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-xi/chapter-7/117-40) through [117-45](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-xi/chapter-7/117-45), inclusive.

All other **_zoning lots_** are subject to the **_use_** provisions of the underlying C5-3 District and the **_bulk_** provisions of an M1-4/R6B District.
