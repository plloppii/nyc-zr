# 111-11 Residential Use Modification

A **_home occupation_** may occupy a **_loft dwelling_**, or a **_dwelling unit_** converted pursuant to Article I, Chapter 5 (Residential Conversion Within Existing Buildings), as an **_accessory_** **_use_** pursuant to Section [15-13](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-i/chapter-5/15-13) (Special Home Occupation Provision), except that:

(a) businesses operated as **_home occupations_** may have up to three persons not residing in the **_dwelling unit_** or **_rooming unit_** may be employed; and

(b) a **_home occupation_** may include any permitted **_commercial_** or **_manufacturing_** **_use_**.
