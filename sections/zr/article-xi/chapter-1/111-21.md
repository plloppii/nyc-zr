# 111-21 Bulk Regulations for Area A1

The regulations applicable to a C6-2A District shall apply to **_developments_** and **_enlargements_**, except that the maximum **_floor area ratio_** permitted on a **_zoning lot_** shall be 5.0. However, for **_zoning lots_** containing **_qualifying affordable housing_** or **_qualifying senior housing_**, the maximum **_residential_** **_floor area ratio_** shall be 6.0.
