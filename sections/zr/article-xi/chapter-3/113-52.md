# 113-52 Special Bulk Regulations

For **_residential buildings_**, or the portion of **_building_** containing **_residences_**, the underlying **_bulk_** regulations applicable to an R4-1 District shall apply, except as set forth in this Section, inclusive.

For **_community facility_** **_buildings_**, or the portion of **_buildings_** allocated to **_community facility uses_**, the **_bulk_** regulations of Article II, Chapter 3 shall apply, except as modified by the provisions of this Section, inclusive.
