# 113-30 TREE PLANTING REQUIREMENTS

In addition to the applicable underlying **_street_** tree planting requirements, all changes of **_use_** on **_zoning lots_** having frontage on Ocean Parkway, shall provide **_street_** trees in accordance with the provisions of Section [23-611](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-3/23-611) (Street tree planting).
