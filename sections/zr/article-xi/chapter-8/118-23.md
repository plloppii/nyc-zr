# 118-23 Height and Setback Regulations

A **_sky exposure plane_** of 2.5 to 1 shall begin at a height above **_curb level_** of 125 feet on all **_streets_** within the Special District, except that on a **_narrow street_** beyond 100 feet from any **_street line_** opposite a **_public park_** or from the intersection of such **_narrow street_** with a **_wide street_**, the **_sky exposure plane_** shall begin at a height above **_curb level_** of 85 feet. No **_development_** or **_enlargement_** shall penetrate such **_sky exposure plane_** except pursuant to Section [33-45](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-3/33-45) (Tower Regulations). However, Section [33-45](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-3/33-45) shall not be applicable to any portion of a **_building_** located within 100 feet of a **_street line_** opposite a **_public park_**. In addition, **_energy infrastructure equipment_** and accessory# mechanical equipment shall be permitted obstructions above such height limits, subject to the provisions of Section [33-42](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-3/33-42) (Permitted Obstructions).

For **_qualifying affordable housing_** or **_qualifying senior housing_**, the height and setback provisions of Section [35-632](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-5/35-632) (Maximum height of buildings and setback regulations) may be applied as an alternative as follows:

(a) above a height of 125 feet above **_curb level_** or 85 feet on a **_narrow street_** beyond 100 feet from any **_street line_** opposite a **_public park_** or from the intersection of such **_narrow street_**, a setback pursuant to the provisions of Section [23-433](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-3/23-433) (Standard setback regulations) shall apply;
(b) the maximum **_building_** height provisions of a C6-3A District shall apply to **_zoning lots_** subject to the provisions of paragraph (a) of Section [118-21](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-xi/chapter-8/118-21) (Floor Area Regulations);
(c) the maximum **_building_** height provisions of a C6-4A District shall apply to **_zoning lots_** subject to the provisions of paragraph (b) of Section [118-21](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-xi/chapter-8/118-21); and
(d) permitted obstructions pursuant to Section [35-61](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-5/35-61) shall apply.
