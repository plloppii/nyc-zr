# 115-31 Sidewalk Widenings

The provisions of this Section shall apply to all **_developments_** fronting upon locations requiring sidewalk widenings as shown on Map 3 (Sidewalk Widening) in Appendix A of this Chapter. A sidewalk widening is a continuous, paved open area along the **_street line_** of a **_zoning lot_** having a depth of two feet or five feet, as set forth on Map 3. Such depth shall be measured perpendicular to the **_street line_**. Sidewalk widenings shall be improved as sidewalks to Department of Transportation standards, at the same level as the adjoining public sidewalk and directly accessible to the public at all times. No **_enlargement_** shall be permitted to decrease the depth of such sidewalk to less than such minimum required total sidewalk depth.
