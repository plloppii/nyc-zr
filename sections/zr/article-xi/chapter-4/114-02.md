# 114-02 Applicability of Certain Provisions

Within the **_Special Bay Ridge District_**, the definition of **_qualifying residential site_** shall be modified to exclude **_zoning lots_** existing on December 5, 2024, with a **_lot area_** that exceeds five acres.

In addition, no **_backyard ancillary dwelling units_** shall be permitted within the portion of the **_Special Bay Ridge District_** west of Ridge Boulevard or south of Marine Avenue.
