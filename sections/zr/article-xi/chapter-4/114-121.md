# 114-121 Special rooftop regulations

The provisions of Section [33-42](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-3/33-42) (Permitted Obstructions) shall apply to all **_buildings or other structures_** in R6A, R6B, R7A, R7B, C4-2A and C8-2 Districts in the **_Special Bay Ridge District_**, except that dormers may penetrate a maximum base height in accordance with the provisions of paragraph (b)(1) of Section [23-413](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-3/23-413) (Permitted obstructions in certain districts).
