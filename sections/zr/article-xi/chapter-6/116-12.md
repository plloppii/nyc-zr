# 116-12 Location of Uses

In addition, the provisions of paragraph (d) of Section [32-422](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-2/32-422) (Location of floors occupied by commercial uses) shall be modified such that eating or drinking establishments listed under Use Group VI shall be permitted on a **_story_** above **_dwelling units_**.
