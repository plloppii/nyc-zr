# 116-03 District Plan

The District Plan for the **_Special Stapleton Waterfront District_** identifies specific areas comprising the Special District in which special zoning regulations are established in order to carry out the general purposes of the **_Special Stapleton Waterfront District_**.

These areas shall include Subareas A, B1, B2, B3, B4, B5, C, D and E, the **_Esplanade_** and two designated public open spaces: **_Pier Place_** and the **_Cove_**. In addition, Subareas B and E shall include **_upland connections_** and Subarea E shall include a **_shore public walkway_**.

The District Plan includes the following maps in Appendix A of this Chapter.
