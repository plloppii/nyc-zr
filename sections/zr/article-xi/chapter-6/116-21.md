# 116-21 Residential Bulk Regulations in C4-2A Districts

The provisions of Sections [34-112](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-4/34-112) and [35-23](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-5/35-23) (Residential Bulk Regulations in other C1 or C2 Districts or in C3, C4, C5 or C6 Districts) are modified as follows:

The applicable **_bulk_** regulations for **_residential uses_** in C4-2A Districts shall be those for R6B Districts.
