# 34-232 Modification of side yard requirements

C1 C2 C3 C4 C5 C6

In the districts indicated, no **_side yard_** shall be required for any **_residential building_**. However, if any open area extending along a **_side lot line_** is provided at any level, it shall have a minimum width of five feet, measured perpendicular to the **_side lot line_**. The allowances for permitted obstructions in any **_yard_** or **_rear yard equivalent_** set forth in Sections [23-311](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-3/23-311) and [23-312](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-3/23-312) shall be permitted in such open areas.
