# 34-222 Change of use

C1 C2 C3 C4 C5 C6

A non-**_residential use_** occupying a **_building_**, or portion thereof, that was in existence on December 15, 1961, may be changed to a **_residential use_** and the regulations pertaining to maximum **_floor area ratio_** shall not apply to such change of **_use_**.
