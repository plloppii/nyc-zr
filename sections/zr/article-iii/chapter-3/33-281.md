# 33-281 Excepted districts

C4-2 C4-3 C4-4 C4-5 C4-6 C4-7 C4-8 C4-9 C4-11 C4-12 C5 C6 C7 C8-4

In the districts indicated, no **_rear yard_** regulations shall apply to any **_through lot_**, except as otherwise provided in Section [33-303](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-3/33-303) (For zoning lots with multiple rear lot lines).
