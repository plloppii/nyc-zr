# 33-26 Minimum Required Rear Yards

C1 C2 C3 C4 C5 C6 C7 C8

In all districts, other than C7 Districts, a **_rear yard_** with a depth of not less than 20 feet shall be provided at every **_rear lot line_** on any **_zoning lot_** except as otherwise provided in Sections [33-27](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-3/33-27) (Special Provisions for Shallow Interior Lots), [33-28](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-3/33-28) (Special Provisions for Through Lots) or [33-30](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-3/33-30) (OTHER SPECIAL PROVISIONS FOR REAR YARDS). **_Rear yards_** shall also be provided along portions of **_side lot lines_** as set forth in Section [33-261](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-3/33-261) (Beyond one hundred feet of a street line).

For C7 Districts, the provisions of Section [33-262](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-3/33-262) shall apply. However, such provisions shall be modified by the provisions of [33-27](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-3/33-27), [33-28](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-3/33-28) or [33-30](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-3/33-30).
