# 33-452 Community facility buildings in C1 or C2 Districts when mapped within R7-2 through R12 Districts

C1-1 C1-2 C1-3 C1-4 C1-5 C2-1 C2-2 C2-3 C2-4 C2-5

In the districts indicated, when mapped within an R7-2, R7-3, R8, R9, R10, R11 or R12 District, the provisions set forth in Section [33-451](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-3/33-451) (In certain specified Commercial Districts) shall apply to any **_community facility building_**. If a **_building_** is used for both **_community facility_** and **_commercial_** **_uses_**, no portion of such **_building_** occupied by **_commercial_** **_use_** shall penetrate the **_sky exposure plane_** as set forth in Sections [33-43](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-3/33-43) (Maximum Height of Walls and Required Setbacks) or [33-44](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-3/33-44) (Alternate Front Setbacks).
