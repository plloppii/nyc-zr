# 33-302 Along short dimension of block

C1 C2 C3 C4 C5 C6 C7 C8

In all districts, as indicated, whenever a **_front lot line_** of a **_zoning lot_** coincides with the **_street line_** of the **_short dimension of a block_**, no **_rear yard_** shall be required within 100 feet of such **_front lot line_**.
