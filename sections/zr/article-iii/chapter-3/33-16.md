# 33-16 Special Provisions for Zoning Lots Divided by District Boundaries

C1 C2 C3 C4 C5 C6 C7 C8

In all districts, as indicated, whenever a **_zoning lot_** is divided by a boundary between districts or is subject to other regulations resulting in different maximum **_floor area ratios_** on portions of the **_zoning lot_**, the provisions set forth in Article VII, Chapter 7, shall apply.
