# 33-293 Required yards along district boundary coincident with side lot line of zoning lot in a Commercial District

C1 C2 C3 C4 C5 C6 C7 C8

In all districts, as indicated, along such portion of a **_side lot line_** of a **_zoning lot_** in a **_Commercial District_** that coincides with a **_rear lot line_** of a **_zoning lot_** in an adjoining **_Residence District_**, an open area at **_curb level_** of at least the width set forth in the following table shall be provided along such boundary within the **_Commercial District_**. Such an open area shall not be used for **_accessory_** off-street loading, or for storage or processing of any kind.

| Districts | Required Width of **_Yard_** (in feet) |
| --- | --- |
| C1 C2 C3 C4 C5 C6 C7 | 8 |
| C8 | 15 |
