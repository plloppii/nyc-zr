# 33-44 Alternate Front Setbacks

C1 C2 C3 C4 C5 C6 C8

In the districts indicated, if an open area is provided along the full length of the **_front lot line_** with the minimum depth set forth in this Section, the provisions of Section [33-43](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-3/33-43) (Maximum Height of Walls and Required Setbacks) shall not apply. The minimum depth of such open area shall be measured perpendicular to the **_front lot line_**. However, in such instances, except as otherwise provided in Sections [33-42](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-3/33-42) (Permitted Obstructions), or [33-45](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-3/33-45) (Tower Regulations), inclusive, no **_building or other structure_** shall penetrate the alternate **_sky exposure plane_** set forth in this Section, and the **_sky exposure plane_** shall be measured from a point above the **_street line_**.

If the open area provided under the terms of this Section is a **_public plaza_**, such open area may be counted for the bonus provided for a **_public plaza_** in the districts indicated in Section [33-13](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-3/33-13) (Floor Area Bonus for a Public Plaza).
