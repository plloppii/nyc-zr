# 33-29 Special Provisions Applying Along District Boundaries

C1 C2 C3 C4 C5 C6 C7 C8

In all districts, as indicated, open areas shall be provided in accordance with the provisions of this Section along the boundaries of **_Residence Districts_** except where such district boundaries are also the boundaries of railroad rights-of-way or cemeteries.
