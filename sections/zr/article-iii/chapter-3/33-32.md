# 33-32 Modification of Yard Regulations

C5-5 C6-8 C6-9 C6-11 C6-12

(a) In the districts indicated, the regulations set forth in Sections [33-26](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-3/33-26) (Minimum Required Rear Yards) through [33-30](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-3/33-30) (OTHER SPECIAL PROVISIONS FOR REAR YARDS), inclusive, relating to **_rear yard_** regulations, may be modified in accordance with the provisions of Section [73-68](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-3/73-68) (Height and Setback and Yard Modifications).

C1 C2 C3 C4 C5 C6 C7 C8

(b) In all districts, the regulations set forth in Section [33-303](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-3/33-303) (For zoning lots with multiple rear lot lines) may be modified in accordance with the provisions of Section [73-69](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-3/73-69) (Rear Yard Modifications).
