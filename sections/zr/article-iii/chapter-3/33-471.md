# 33-471 Commercial and community facility uses

C5-5 C6-8 C6-9 C6-11 C6-12 C8

In the districts indicated, for **_commercial_** or **_community facility_** **_uses_** in specified situations, the Board of Standards and Appeals may modify the regulations set forth in Sections [33-41](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-3/33-41) (Definitions) to [33-45](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-3/33-45) (Tower Regulations), inclusive, relating to Height and Setback Regulations, in accordance with the provisions of Section [73-68](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-3/73-68) (Height and Setback and Yard Modifications).
