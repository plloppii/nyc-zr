# 33-457 Tower setbacks on narrow blocks

C5-3 C5-5 C6-6 C6-7 C6-9 C6-11 C6-12

In the districts indicated, if a **_zoning lot_** is bounded by at least three **_street lines_**, and any two of the **_street lines_** are opposite to each other and parallel or within 45 degrees of being parallel to each other, and their average distance apart is 150 feet or less, the minimum distance a tower is required to be set back from such opposite **_street lines_** under the provisions of Section [33-455](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-3/33-455) (Alternate regulations for towers on lots bounded by two or more streets) or Section [33-456](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-3/33-456) (Alternate setback regulations on lots bounded by two or more streets), is reduced in accordance with the following table:

TOWER SETBACKS ON NARROW BLOCKS

|  | Reduction of Required Tower Setback | Minimum Setback for Tower Built under Provisions of this Section |
| --- | --- | --- |
| On **_narrow street_** | 30 percent or 10 feet, whichever is less | 15 feet |
| On **_wide street_** | 40 percent or 10 feet, whichever is less | 10 feet |
