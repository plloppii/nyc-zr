However, in accordance with the provisions of Section [32-42](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-2/32-42) (Location Within Buildings), in C1, C2 or C3 Districts, no **_commercial building_** or portion thereof occupied by **_uses_** listed under Use Groups VI through X shall exceed in height 30 feet or two **_stories_**, whichever is less.

In C4-1 or C8-1 Districts, for **_community facility buildings_** or **_buildings_** used for both **_community facility_** **_use_** and **_commercial_** **_use_**, the maximum height above **_street line_** shall be 35 feet or three **_stories_**, whichever is less.
