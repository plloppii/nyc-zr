# 32-32 Regulations for Tier B Street Frontages

The provisions of this Section, inclusive, shall apply to the **_ground floor level_** **_street_** frontage of a **_building_** along a **_Tier B street frontage_**. Such provisions include **_ground floor level_**:

(a) **_use_** and transparency requirements, as set forth in Section [32-321](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-2/32-321);
(b) alternative regulations, for certain **_block_** frontages, as set forth in Section [32-322](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-2/32-322); and
(c) curb cut restrictions for all frontages, as set forth in Section 32-323.
