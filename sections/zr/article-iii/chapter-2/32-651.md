# 32-651 Permitted projection in C6-5 or C6-7 Districts

C6-5 C6-7

In the districts indicated, except as otherwise provided in Section [32-653](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-2/32-653) (Additional regulations for projecting signs), no permitted **_sign_** shall project across a **_street line_** more than eight feet.
