# 32-645 Illuminated or flashing signs in C8 Districts

C8

In the district indicated, **_illuminated_** or **_flashing signs_**, other than **_advertising signs_**, and **_advertising signs_** with indirect illumination, are permitted, provided that the total **_surface area_** of all such **_signs_** (in square feet) shall not exceed five times the **_street_** frontage of the **_zoning lot_** (in feet) and that the **_surface area_** of each **_sign_** shall not exceed 500 square feet.
