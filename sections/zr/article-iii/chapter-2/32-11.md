# 32-11 Use Group I – Agriculture and Open Uses

C1 C2 C3 C4 C5 C6 C7 C8

Use Group I consists of various primarily open **_uses_** of land. The provisions regulating **_uses_** classified in this Use Group are set forth as follows:

(a) Section [32-111](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-2/32-111) (Use Group I – general use allowances) which includes the compilation of **_uses_** in the Use Group table;
(b) Section [32-112](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-2/32-112) (Use Group I – uses subject to size limitations) for size restrictions that apply to certain **_uses_**, as denoted with an “S” in the Use Group table;
(c) Section [32-113](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-2/32-113) (Use Group I – uses subject to additional conditions) for additional conditions that apply to certain **_uses_**, as denoted with a “P” in the Use Group table;
(d) Section [32-114](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-2/32-114) (Use Group I – uses subject to open use allowances) for open **_use_** allowances that apply to certain **_uses_**, as denoted with a “U” in the Use Group table;
(e) Section [32-115](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-2/32-115) (Use Group I – uses permitted by special permit) for **_uses_** only permitted by special permit by the Board of Standards and Appeals, as denoted with “○” in the Use Group table; and
(f) Section [32-116](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-2/32-116) (Use Group I – additional provisions for parking requirement category) for **_uses_** with more than one parking requirement category or other applicable parking provisions, as denoted with “\*” in the Use Group table.
