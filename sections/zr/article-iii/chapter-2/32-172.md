# 32-172 Use Group VII – uses permitted with limited applicability

For **_uses_** denoted with a “♦” in Section [32-171](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-2/32-171) (Use Group VII – general use allowances), the provisions of this Section shall apply.

In C1 Districts outside of the Borough of Manhattan that are **_select commercial overlays_**, laboratories shall not be permitted.
