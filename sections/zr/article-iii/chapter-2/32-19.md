# 32-19 Use Group IX – Storage

C1 C2 C3 C4 C5 C6 C7 C8

Use Group IX consists of **_uses_** that provide storage for materials, goods, and vehicles. The provisions regulating **_uses_** classified in this Use Group are set forth as follows:

(a) Section [32-191](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-2/32-191) (Use Group IX – general use allowances) which includes the compilation of **_uses_** in the Use Group tables;
(b) Section [32-192](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-2/32-192) (Use Group IX – uses permitted with limited applicability) for additional limitations on applicability for certain **_uses_**, as denoted with “♦” in the Use Group tables;
(c) Section [32-193](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-2/32-193) (Use Group IX – uses subject to size limitations) for size restrictions that apply to certain **_uses_**, as denoted with an “S” in the Use Group tables;
(d) Section [32-194](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-2/32-194) (Use Group IX – uses subject to additional conditions) for additional conditions that apply to certain **_uses_**, as denoted with a “P” in the Use Group tables;
(e) Section [32-195](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-2/32-195) (Use Group IX – uses subject to open use allowances) for open **_use_** allowances that apply to certain **_uses_**, as denoted with a “U” in the Use Group tables;
(f) Section [32-196](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-2/32-196) (Use Group IX – uses permitted by special permit) for **_uses_** permitted by special permit of the City Planning Commission, as denoted with “○” in the Use Group tables; and
(g) Section [32-197](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-2/32-197) (Use Group IX – additional provisions for parking requirement category) for **_uses_** with more than one parking requirement category or other applicable parking provisions, as denoted with “\*” in the Use Group tables.
