# 32-352 Ground floor use requirements in the Borough of Staten Island

For all C1, C2 or C4 Districts in the Borough of Staten Island, the applicable **_ground floor level_** streetscape provisions of this Chapter shall apply, except that **_ground floor level_** **_street_** frontages on **_wide streets_**, or along **_narrow streets_** where such a C1, C2 or C4 District is mapped along the entire **_block_** frontage, shall be considered **_Tier C street frontages_**.
