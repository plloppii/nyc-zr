# 32-123 Use Group II – uses subject to additional conditions

For **_uses_** denoted with a “P” in Section [32-121](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-2/32-121) (Use Group II – general use allowances), the provisions of this Section shall apply. In C4 Districts, all **_residential uses_** shall be permitted except that in **_lower density growth management areas_** within the Borough of Staten Island, no **_residences_** shall be allowed on the following **_zoning lots_** in C4-1 Districts where such zoning district occupies at least four acres within a **_block_**, except by special permit of the City Planning Commission in accordance with Section [74-121](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-4/74-121) (Residential use in C4-1 Districts in Staten Island).
