# 32-641 Total surface area of signs

C1 C2 C3 C4 C5 C6 C7 C8

In all districts, as indicated, the total **_surface area_** of all permitted **_signs_**, including non-**_illuminated_** or **_illuminated signs_**, shall not exceed the limitation established for non-**_illuminated signs_**, as set forth in Section 32-642.
