# 32-122 Use Group II – uses permitted with limited applicability

For **_uses_** denoted with “♦” in Section [32-121](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-2/32-121) (Use Group II – general use allowances), the provisions of this Section shall apply. In C3A Districts, only **_single-_** or **_two-family detached_** or **_zero lot line residences_** shall be permitted.
