# 32-30 STREETSCAPE REGULATIONS

The provisions of Section [32-30](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-2/32-30), inclusive, set forth streetscape regulations on designated **_street_** frontages. Such provisions shall apply as follows:

(a) The provisions of Sections [32-31](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-2/32-31), [32-32](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-2/32-32) and [32-33](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-2/32-33), inclusive, apply to **_developments_** or **_ground floor level_** **_enlargements_** of **_buildings_** along **_Tier A_**, **_Tier B_** and **_Tier C street frontages_**, respectively.
(b) The provisions of Section [32-34](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-2/32-34) apply to changes of **_use_** to existing **_buildings_** along **_Tier A_**, **_Tier B_** and **_Tier C street frontages_**.
(c) The provisions of Section [32-35](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-2/32-35) set forth special streetscape rules for certain areas.

For **_zoning lots_** with multiple **_street_** frontages, each frontage shall be considered separately as a **_Tier A_**, **_Tier B_** or **_Tier C_** frontage, as applicable.

Special Purpose Districts or other provisions of this Resolution may modify or supplement the provisions of this Section, inclusive.

The Board of Standards and Appeals may permit modifications to the provisions of this Section, inclusive, pursuant to Section [73-32](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-3/73-32).

For **_ground floor levels_** of **_buildings_** that are designated as a landmark or considered a contributing **_building_** in an Historic District designated by the Landmarks Preservation Commission the Chairperson of the City Planning Commission shall modify the provisions of this Section, by certification, to the extent necessary to facilitate the **_ground floor level_** design of a **_building_** that has received a Certificate of Appropriateness from the Landmarks Preservation Commission.
