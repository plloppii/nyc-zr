# 32-191 Use Group IX – general use allowances

The following table includes **_uses_** classified as Use Group IX and sets forth their allowances by **_Commercial District_**. Such **_uses_** are categorized as general storage, specialized storage, or vehicle storage, as provided in paragraphs A, B and C of this Section. Notations found in the table are further described in Section [32-10](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-2/32-10) (USE ALLOWANCES).
