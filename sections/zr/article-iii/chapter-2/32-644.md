# 32-644 Illuminated or flashing signs in C4, C5-4, C6 or C7 Districts

C4 C5-4 C6 C7

In the districts indicated, **_illuminated_** or **_flashing signs_** with total **_surface areas_** not exceeding those shown in the following table are permitted:

| District |  | Maximum Surface Area (in square feet) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| C4 C5-4 C6-1 C6-2 C6-3 C6-4 C6-6 C6-8 C6-9 C6-11 C6-12 C7 |  | Five times the **_street_** frontage of the **_zoning lot_** (in feet), but in no event more than 500 square feet for **_interior_** or **_through lots_** or 500 square feet on each frontage for **_corner lots_**. |
| C6-5 C6-7 |  | No restrictions as to size |

However, in a C6-1A District, **_flashing signs_** are not permitted.
