# 32-196 Use Group IX – uses permitted by special permit

For **_uses_** denoted with “○” in Section [32-191](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-2/32-191) (Use Group IX – general use allowances), the provisions of this Section shall apply. **_Public parking garages_** may be permitted in C5 or C6 Districts by special permit by the City Planning Commission in accordance with Section [74-195](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-4/74-195) (Public parking garages or public parking lots in high density central areas). In the **_Manhattan Core_**, these **_uses_** are subject to the provisions of Article I, Chapter 3, and in the **_Long Island City area_**, as defined in Section [16-02](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-i/chapter-6/16-02) (Definitions), such **_uses_** are subject to the provisions of Article I, Chapter 6.
