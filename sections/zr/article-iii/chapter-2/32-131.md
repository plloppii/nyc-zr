# 32-131 Use Group III – general use allowances

The following tables include **_uses_** classified as Use Group III and set forth their allowances by **_Commercial District_**. Such **_uses_** are categorized as **_community facilities_** with and without sleeping accommodations, as provided in paragraphs A and B of this Section. Notations found in the tables are further described in Section [32-10](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-2/32-10) (USE ALLOWANCES).
