# 32-62 Permitted Signs

C1 C2 C3 C4 C5 C6 C7 C8

In all districts, as indicated, **_signs_** other than **_advertising signs_** are permitted subject to the provisions of the following Sections:

Section [32-64](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-2/32-64) (Surface Area and Illumination Provisions)

Section [32-65](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-2/32-65) (Permitted Projection or Height of Signs)

Section [32-66](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-2/32-66) (Additional Regulations for Signs Near Certain Parks and Designated Arterial Highways)

Section [32-67](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-2/32-67) (Special Provisions Applying along District Boundaries)

Section [32-68](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-2/32-68) (Permitted Signs on Residential or Mixed Buildings)

Section [32-69](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-2/32-69) (Additional Sign Regulations for Adult Establishments)

However, notwithstanding any provision of this Section, flags, banners or pennants other than those that are **_advertising signs_**, located on any **_zoning lot_** used primarily for **_community facility_** **_uses_** of a civic, philanthropic, educational or religious nature, are permitted in all districts, as indicated, without limitation.
