# 32-311 Blank wall provisions

Along the **_Tier A street frontage_** of a **_ground floor level_**, for any **_blank walls_**, at least 70 percent of the surface area of such **_blank wall_** shall be mitigated with wall treatment, in the form of permitted **_signs_**, subject to the provisions of Section [32-60](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-2/32-60), murals or other visual artwork, or living plant material. Any portion of a mural or other artwork that incorporates addresses, text or logos related to the **_building_** or tenants therein, shall be considered a **_sign_**.
