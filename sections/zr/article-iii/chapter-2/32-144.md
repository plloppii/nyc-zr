# 32-144 Use Group IV – uses subject to open use allowances

For **_uses_** denoted with a “U” in Section [32-141](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-2/32-141) (Use Group IV – general use allowances), a **_use_** may be open or enclosed without restriction.
