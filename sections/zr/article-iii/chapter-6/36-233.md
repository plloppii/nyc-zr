# 36-233 Exceptions to application of waiver provisions

C1 C2 C3 C4 C5 C6 C7 C8

In all districts, as indicated, the waiver provisions of Section [36-23](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-6/36-23) (Waiver of Requirements for Spaces below Minimum Number) shall not apply to the following types of **_uses_**:

(a) **_Uses_** within parking requirement category (PRC) D.
(b) The following **_commercial_** **_uses_** in PRC -F or G:
    Camps, overnight or day
    ​​​​​​​**_Motels_** or #tourist cabins
(c) The following **_community facility_** **_uses_** in PRC-G:
    Agricultural **_uses_**, including greenhouses, nurseries, or truck gardens
    Outdoor racket courts.
