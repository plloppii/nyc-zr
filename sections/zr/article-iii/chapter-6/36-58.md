# 36-58 Cross Access Connections in the Borough of Staten Island

C4-1 C8

In the Borough of Staten Island, in the districts indicated, existing or new open parking lots adjacent to one another on the same or separate **_zoning lots_** shall be required to provide vehicular passageways between such open parking lots. Such vehicular passageways are hereinafter referred to as “cross access connections” and shall be provided in accordance with the requirements of this Section, inclusive.
