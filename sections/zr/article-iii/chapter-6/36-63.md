# 36-63 Waiver of Requirements for All Zoning Lots Where Access Would Be Forbidden

C1 C2 C3 C4 C5 C6 C7 C8

In all districts, as indicated, the requirements set forth in Section [36-62](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-6/36-62) (Required Accessory Off-street Loading Berths) shall not apply to any **_building_** or **_zoning lot_** as to which the Commissioner of Buildings has certified that there is no way to arrange the required berths with access to the **_street_** to conform to the provisions of Section [36-662](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-6/36-662) (Location of access to the street). The Commissioner of Buildings may refer such matter to the Department of Transportation for report and may base a determination on such report.
