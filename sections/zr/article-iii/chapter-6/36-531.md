# 36-531 Location of curb cuts in C1 or C2 Districts mapped within R5D Districts

In C1 or C2 Districts mapped within R5D Districts, a minimum distance of 34 feet of uninterrupted curb space shall be provided between all curb cuts constructed after June 29, 2006.

Furthermore, no curb cuts shall be permitted on the  frontage of any **_zoning lot_** existing on June 29, 2006, with access to a **_narrow street_**.
