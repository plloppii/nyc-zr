# 35-03 Applicability to Specific Districts

In C3A Districts, the **_bulk_** regulations of this Chapter shall not apply. In lieu thereof, the **_bulk_** regulations for R3A Districts in Article II, Chapter 3 (Residential Bulk Regulations in Residence Districts), shall apply.
