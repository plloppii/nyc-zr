# 35-04 Applicability of Certain Bulk Regulations

Special rules governing optional **_bulk regulations_** affecting one or more **_bulk_** parameters are set forth in Section [35-80](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-5/35-80) (ALTERNATIVE BULK REGULATIONS FOR CERTAIN AREAS), inclusive.

These optional **_bulk_** provisions include alternative rules for **_sky exposure plane buildings_**, as set forth in Section [35-81](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-5/35-81), inclusive. Where any of such optional provisions are utilized, all the other associated **_bulk_** provisions shall apply.
