# 35-32 Maximum Floor Area for Mixed Buildings on Qualifying Residential Sites

On **_qualifying residential sites_**, subject to the individual maximum **_floor area ratios_** for **_commercial_**, **_community facility_** and **_residential uses_**, the maximum **_floor area ratio_** for a **_zoning lot_** with **_buildings_** containing **_residential_** and non-**_residential uses_**, shall be as set forth in this Section.

Within the **_Greater Transit Zone_**, the maximum **_floor area ratio_** shall be 2.50.

Outside the **_Greater Transit Zone_**, the maximum **_floor area ratio_** shall be as set forth for the applicable **_Residence District_** the **_Commercial District_** is mapped within, or the **_residential equivalent_** of such **_Commercial District_**.

MAXIMUM FLOOR AREA RATIO FOR MIXED BUILDINGS
OUTSIDE THE GREATER TRANSIT ZONE

| District | Maximum **_floor area ratio_** for **_zoning lot_** |
| --- | --- |
| R1 R2 R3-1 R3A R3X | 1.50 |
| R3-2 | 1.60 |
| R4 | 2.00 |
| R5 | 2.50 |
