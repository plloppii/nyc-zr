# 35-813 Floor area provisions in certain towers

In C1 or C2 Districts mapped within R9 and R10 Districts, or in **_Commercial Districts_** with a **_residential equivalent_** of an R9 or R10 District, for **_zoning lots_** containing a **_building_** that is **_developed_** or **_enlarged_** pursuant to the applicable tower regulations of Section [35-815](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-5/35-815), the **_floor area_** provisions of Section [35-361](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-5/35-361) (Special tower provisions) shall apply.
