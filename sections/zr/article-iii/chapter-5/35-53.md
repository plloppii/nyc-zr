# 35-53 Modification of Rear Yard Requirements

C1 C2 C3 C4 C5 C6

In the districts indicated, for a **_residential_** portion of a **_mixed building_**, the required **_residential_** **_rear yard_** shall be provided at the floor level of the lowest **_story_** used for **_dwelling units_** or **_rooming units_**, where any window of such **_dwelling units_** or **_rooming units_** faces onto such **_rear yard_**. If the level of such **_yard_** is at or higher than the first **_story_**, decks, parapet walls, roof thickness, solar energy systems up to four feet high, vegetated roofs, and weirs, shall be permitted, pursuant to Section [23-41](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-3/23-41) (Permitted Obstructions), inclusive.
