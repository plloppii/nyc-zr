# 35-641 Special tower provisions

C1 C2 C4 C5 C6

The tower provisions of Section [23-435](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-3/23-435) shall be modified in certain areas, as follows:

(a) In **_Commercial Districts_** mapped within, or with a **_residential equivalent_** of, an R9D or R10X District, the provisions of paragraph (a) of Section [23-441](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-3/23-441) shall apply.
(b) In C1 or C2 Districts mapped within R9 or R10 Districts without a letter suffix, or in C1-8, C1-9, C2-7 or C2-8 Districts, for **_mixed buildings_** that meet the criteria of paragraph (b) of Section [23-441](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-3/23-441), the tower-on-a-base provisions of such paragraph shall apply.
