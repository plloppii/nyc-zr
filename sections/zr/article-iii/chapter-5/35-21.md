# 35-21 General Provisions

C1 C2 C3 C4 C5 C6

In the districts indicated, the **_bulk_** regulations set forth in Article II, Chapter 3 shall apply to all **_residential_** portions of **_buildings_** in accordance with the provisions and modifications set forth in the remaining Sections of this Chapter. The purpose of these modifications is to make the regulations set forth in Article II, Chapter 3, applicable to **_Commercial Districts_**.
