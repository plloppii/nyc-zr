# 37-94 Refuse Storage

All site plans must show an area designated for refuse storage. Any container used for refuse storage must be enclosed and screened either within a **_building_** or an **_accessory_** **_structure_**. If refuse storage is located in a container or **_accessory_** **_structure_**, it must be located at least 50 feet from any **_street line_** and screened on all sides by a six foot high masonry wall, with one side consisting of an opaque, lockable gate.
