# 37-32 Ground Floor Depth Requirements for Certain Uses

The minimum depth for required ground floor non-**_residential uses_**, as applicable, shall be as set forth in this Section, except as set forth in Section [37-31](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-7/37-31) (Applicability).

Required **_ground floor level_** non-**_residential uses_** along a **_designated frontage_** shall extend to the **_minimum qualifying depth_**.
