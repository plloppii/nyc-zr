# 37-624 Kiosks and open air cafes

Kiosks and open air cafes may be placed within an existing **_plaza_**, **_residential plaza_** or **_urban plaza_** upon certification by the Chairperson of the City Planning Commission, pursuant to Section [37-73](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-7/37-73) (Kiosks and Open Air Cafes).
