# 37-745 Bicycle parking

All **_public plazas_** shall provide parking for at least two bicycles. **_Public plazas_** greater than 10,000 square feet in size must provide parking for at least four bicycles. Bike racks must be provided on the sidewalk directly adjacent to the **_public plaza_** in accordance with Department of Transportation standards, unless the Department of Transportation has determined that the sidewalk area adjacent to the **_public plaza_** cannot accommodate the required bicycle parking.
