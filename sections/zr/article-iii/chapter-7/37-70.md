# 37-70 PUBLIC PLAZAS

**_Public plazas_** are open areas on a **_zoning lot_** intended for public use and enjoyment. The standards contained within Sections [37-70](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-7/37-70) through [37-78](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-7/37-78), inclusive, are intended to serve the following specific purposes:

(a) to serve a variety of users of the **_public plaza_** area;

(b) to provide spaces for solitary users while at the same time providing opportunities for social interaction for small groups; and

(c) to provide safe spaces, with maximum visibility from the **_street_** and adjacent **_buildings_** and with multiple avenues for ingress and egress.

All **_public plazas_** shall comply with the provisions of Section [37-70](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-7/37-70) through [37-78](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-7/37-78), inclusive. These provisions may be modified pursuant to Section [74-91](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-4/74-91) (Modification of Public Plazas).
