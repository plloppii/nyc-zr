# 104-334 Type 4 street wall location

Type 4 **_street walls_**, as shown on Map 4 in Appendix A of this Chapter, shall comply with the provisions of this Section.

(a) The **_upper street wall_** shall be located within five feet of the **_street line_** or **_mandatory widened sidewalk line_**, as applicable. Such **_street wall_** shall extend along at least 50 percent of the length of the **_street line_** or **_mandatory widened sidewalk line_** of the Parcel, as applicable, and shall rise without setback to a minimum height of 60 feet above **_curb level_**, or the height of the **_building_**, whichever is less.

(b) The **_lower street wall_** shall be set back at least two feet but no more than 10 feet from the **_upper street wall_** required pursuant to paragraph (a) of this Section and shall extend along at least 50 percent of the **_street line_** or **_mandatory widened sidewalk line_** of the Parcel, as applicable.

(c) No **_street wall_** location regulation shall apply to the remaining 50 percent of the **_street_** frontage of a Parcel.
