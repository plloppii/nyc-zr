# 104-331 Type 1 street wall location

Type 1 **_street walls_**, as shown on Map 4 in Appendix A of this Chapter, shall comply with the provisions of this Section.

(a) The **_upper street wall_** shall be located anywhere within five feet of the **_street line_** for at least 70 percent of the **_street frontage_** of the Parcel and shall rise without setback to a minimum height of 85 feet above **_curb level_**, or the height of the **_building_**, whichever is less.

(b) The **_lower street wall_** shall be set back at least two feet but no more than 10 feet from the **_upper street wall_** required pursuant to paragraph (a) of this Section and shall extend along at least 70 percent of the **_street_** frontage of the Parcel. For Parcels D1, E1, F1, G1, G2 and H, the height of the **_lower street wall_** shall be not less than twice the depth of the setback of the **_lower street wall_** from the **_upper street wall_**, but not less than 15 feet.

(c) No **_street wall_** location regulation shall apply to the remaining 30 percent of the **_street_** frontage of a Parcel.

(d) At least 20 percent of the area of an **_upper street wall_** facing Broadway shall be recessed to a minimum depth of 10 feet.

(e) The **_street wall_** provisions of this Section may apply along a **_narrow street_** within 100 feet of its intersection of a **_wide street_**.
