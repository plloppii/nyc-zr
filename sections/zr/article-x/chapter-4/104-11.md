# 104-11 Residential Use Modifications

The **_residential use_** regulations of the underlying C6-1 District are modified, as follows.

In Subdistrict A, a **_residential use_** may locate in the same **_building_**, or **_abut_** a **_building_** containing a **_use_** listed in Section [104-13](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-x/chapter-4/104-13) (Commercial and Manufacturing Use Modifications), inclusive, only in accordance with the certification provisions of Section [104-14](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-x/chapter-4/104-14).
