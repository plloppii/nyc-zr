# 104-24 Maximum Floor Area Ratio for Multiple Uses on a Zoning Lot

When more than one **_use_** is located on a **_zoning lot_**, the maximum **_floor area ratio_** permitted for any **_use_** on such **_zoning lot_** shall not exceed the maximum permitted for such **_use_**, as set forth in Sections [104-21](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-x/chapter-4/104-21) through [104-24](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-x/chapter-4/104-24), inclusive, provided that the total of all such **_floor area ratios_** does not exceed the greatest **_floor area ratio_** permitted for any such **_use_** on the **_zoning lot_**.
