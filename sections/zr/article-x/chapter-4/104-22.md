# 104-22 Maximum Floor Area Ratio and Lot Coverage for Community Facility Uses

In Subdistricts A and C, the maximum **_floor area ratio_** permitted for **_community facility uses_** shall be 6.0.

In Subdistrict B, the maximum **_floor area ratio_** permitted for **_community facility uses_** shall be 2.0.
