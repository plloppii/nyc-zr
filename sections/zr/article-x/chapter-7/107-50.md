# 107-50 CERTIFICATIONS

Administrative certifications from the City Planning Commission are required, as set forth in various sections of this Chapter, in any one of the following circumstances:

(a) when certain **_plan review sites_** are subdivided, as set forth in Section [107-08](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-x/chapter-7/107-08) (Future Subdivision of Certain Plan Review Sites);
(b) when certain **_plan review sites_** meet the requirements set forth in Section [107-51](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-x/chapter-7/107-51) (Certification of Certain Plan Review Sites) and do not require an authorization pursuant to Section [107-60](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-x/chapter-7/107-60), inclusive;
(c) when a **_zoning lot_** contains **_designated open space_**, as set forth in Section [107-22](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-x/chapter-7/107-22) (Designated Open Space);
(d) where required **_yards_** or equivalents are to be modified as set forth in Section [107-465](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-x/chapter-7/107-465) (Modifications of special yard regulations for certain zoning lots);
(e) where plant material is substituted for required trees as set forth in Section [107-33](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-x/chapter-7/107-33) (Substitution of Other Plant Materials); or
(f) when a **_zoning lot_** contains a portion of the proposed **_waterfront esplanade_**, as set forth in Section [107-23](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-x/chapter-7/107-23) (Waterfront Esplanade).
(g) Any **_development_** or **_enlargement_** may modify applicable regulations governing the location of required parking spaces, driveways and curb cuts, as set forth in Section [107-34](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-x/chapter-7/107-34) (Preservation of Natural Features).
