# 107-11 Special Requirements for Application

For a **_site alteration_**, or a **_development_**, or **_enlargement_**, no certificate of occupancy or sign-off of a permit, as applicable, shall be issued by the Department of Buildings until the planting requirements of the following provisions, as applicable, have been satisfied, and reflected on an as-built survey, tree schedule, or any such information as may be required:

Section [107-32](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-x/chapter-7/107-32) (Tree Requirements)

Section [107-48](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-x/chapter-7/107-48) (Special Landscaping and Buffering Provisions).
