# 107-61 General Provisions

On application, the City Planning Commission may grant authorizations for modifications of specified regulations of this Chapter or of the underlying districts in accordance with the provisions of Sections [107-62](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-x/chapter-7/107-62) to [107-69](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-x/chapter-7/107-69), inclusive, relating to authorizations.

The Commission may prescribe appropriate conditions and safeguards in connection with the grant of such authorizations.
