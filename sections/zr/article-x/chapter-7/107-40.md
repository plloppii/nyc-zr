# 107-40 SPECIAL USE, BULK AND PARKING REGULATIONS

The underlying **_use_**, **_bulk_** and parking regulations shall apply. However, for **_zoning lots_** that are not **_qualifying residential sites_**, such provisions are modified by the provisions of this Section, inclusive.
