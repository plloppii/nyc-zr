# 107-20 DISTRICT PLAN ELEMENTS

All land in the **_open space network_** as shown on the District Plan (Map 3 in Appendix A), except **_public parks_**, and all **_zoning lots_** along an **_arterial_** as shown on the District Plan (Map 2 in Appendix A), are subject to the provisions of this Section, inclusive.
