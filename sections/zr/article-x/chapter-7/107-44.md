# 107-44 Maximum Floor Area Ratio for Community Facility Uses

The provisions of Sections [24-13](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-4/24-13) (Floor Area Bonus for Deep Front and Wide Side Yards) and [33-15](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-3/33-15) (Floor Area Bonus for Front Yards) shall not apply to any **_community facility uses_** located in the Special District.
