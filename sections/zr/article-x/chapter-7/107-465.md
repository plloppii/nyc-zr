# 107-465 Modifications of yard and court regulations

The **_yard_** and **_court_** regulations as set forth in Section [107-46](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-x/chapter-7/107-46) may be modified by authorization of the City Planning Commission in accordance with the provisions of Section [107-62](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-x/chapter-7/107-62) (Yard, Court and Parking Regulations).
