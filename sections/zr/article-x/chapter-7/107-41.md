# 107-41 Type of Residence

**_Semi-detached_** or **_attached_** **_single-family residences_** in R2 Districts and **_attached_** **_single-_** or **_two-family residences_** in R3-1 Districts may be permitted by special permit of the City Planning Commission in accordance with the provisions of Section [107-74](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-x/chapter-7/107-74) (Modification of Permitted Use Regulations). Such **_residences_** shall comply with the minimum **_lot area_** and **_lot width_** requirements as set forth in the table in Section [107-42](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-x/chapter-7/107-42).
