# 107-30 NATURAL FEATURE REGULATIONS

Any **_development_**, **_enlargement_** resulting in changes to **_lot coverage_**, or **_site alteration_** shall comply with the provisions of this Section, inclusive.

All **_zoning lots_** shall comply with the provisions of Sections [107-31](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-x/chapter-7/107-31) (General Regulations for Natural Features), inclusive, and [107-32](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-x/chapter-7/107-32) (Tree Requirements).

In addition, regulations within areas of **_designated open space_** are set forth in Section [107-311](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-x/chapter-7/107-311) (Areas within designated open space).

Any **_plan review site_** shall also comply with the provisions of Section [107-312](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-x/chapter-7/107-312) (Regulations within plan review sites).

Any application to the Department of Buildings for a **_site alteration_**, **_development_**, or **_enlargement_** shall include a survey map in accordance with the requirements set forth in Section [107-03](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-x/chapter-7/107-03) (Requirements for Certification, Authorization or Special Permit Application).

A site plan for such application shall also include the location of any **_area of no disturbance_**, proposed paving, and shall identify those trees proposed to be removed and proposed to be preserved.
