# 107-311 Areas within designated open space

Within **_designated open space_** on a **_zoning lot_**, any **_site alteration_** shall be permitted only by authorization of the City Planning Commission pursuant to Section [107-65](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-x/chapter-7/107-65) (Authorization for Modifications of Natural Features).
