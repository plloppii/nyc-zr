# 107-473 Location of required parking

For a **_residential_** **_building_** on a **_zoning lot_** containing an **_area of no land alteration_** or **_designated open space_**, the provisions of Section [25-622](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-5/25-622) (Location of parking spaces in lower density growth management areas) that do not permit open off-street parking between the **_street line_** and the **_street wall_** or prolongation thereof of a **_building_** shall not apply.
