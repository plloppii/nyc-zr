# 107-76 Boundary Adjustments in Designated Open Space

The City Planning Commission may grant special permits to allow adjustments in the boundaries of the **_designated open space_** on a **_zoning lot_** provided that such adjustment will not place the new boundary closer than 60 feet to a watercourse. As a condition for such adjustment in the boundaries, the Commission shall find that such adjustment will:

(a) result in a substantial improvement in the quality and usefulness of the **_designated open space_**; or

(b) permit **_development_** which better satisfies the purposes of this Chapter and that the new features which will be added to the **_designated open space_** will be at least equal in quality to those which are displaced from it; and

(c) provide an equivalent area replacement for the area removed from the **_designated open space_**.
