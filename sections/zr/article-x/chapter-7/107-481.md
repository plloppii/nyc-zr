# 107-481 Planting provisions along Residence District boundaries

For any **_commercial_** or **_manufacturing_** **_development_** on a **_zoning lot_** adjoining a **_Residence District_** boundary, there shall be in the open area required by the provisions of Sections [33-29](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-3/33-29) and [43-30](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iv/chapter-3/43-30) (SPECIAL PROVISIONS APPLYING ALONG DISTRICT BOUNDARIES) along the **_lot line_** adjoining the **_Residence District_**, a strip at least four feet wide, densely planted with evergreen shrubs at least four feet high at the time of planting and complying with the provisions applicable to screening for parking areas as set forth in Section [107-483](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-x/chapter-7/107-483) (Planting and screening for parking areas).
