# 107-492 Special bulk regulations

The maximum **_floor area ratio_** for two or more **_uses_** on a **_zoning lot_** shall be determined by the **_use_** that is permitted the greatest **_floor area ratio_** in Area M (Map 4 in Appendix A), provided that the **_floor area_** occupied by each **_use_** does not exceed the amount permitted by the **_floor area ratio_** for that **_use_** in Area M.
