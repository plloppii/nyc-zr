# 102-50 SPECIAL PROVISIONS FOR CITY-OWNED LANDS

For any **_development_** or site improvement on a city-owned **_zoning lot_** located within a **_Special Scenic View District_**, the provisions of this Chapter shall apply except that modifications permitted under Section [102-30](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-x/chapter-2/102-30) (SPECIAL PERMIT PROVISIONS) may be approved by authorization of the City Planning Commission.
