# 102-021 Requirement for application

An application to the City Planning Commission for a special permit pursuant to Section [102-30](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-x/chapter-2/102-30), shall include maps, plans or other documents showing topography, elevations, and site plans showing arrangement and spacing of **_buildings or other structures_**, and other information necessary to determine the impact of the proposal on the **_scenic view_** to be protected.
