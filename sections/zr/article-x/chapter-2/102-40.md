# 102-40 SPECIAL DISTRICT DESIGNATION ON PUBLIC PARKS

When a **_Special Scenic View District_** is designated on a **_public park_** or portion thereof, any future landscaping, erection of new **_signs_** or **_buildings or other structures_**, thereon, shall not penetrate a **_view plane_** unless authorized by the City Planning Commission. As a condition for such authorization, the Commission shall find that any penetration of a **_view plane_** shall not significantly obstruct the **_scenic view_** which is to be protected by the provisions of this Chapter.
