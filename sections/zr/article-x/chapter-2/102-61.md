# 102-61 SV-1 Brooklyn Heights Scenic View District

The SV-1 "Brooklyn Heights Scenic View District" is hereby established. The regulations of Sections [102-00](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-x/chapter-2/102-00) to [102-50](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-x/chapter-2/102-50), inclusive, as well as the regulations of this Section, inclusive, shall be applicable in this Special District.
