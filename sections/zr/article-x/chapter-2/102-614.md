# 102-614 View plane

The **_view plane_** of the SV-1 Special District is an imaginary plane formed by joining the elevation of the **_view reference line_** with the elevation of the **_view framing line_** as shown in Diagram 2. The horizontal distance between the **_view reference line_** and the **_view framing line_** is 2,300 feet. Those portions of the plane formed by points A, B3, B4 and A1, B1, B2 are conical surfaces.
