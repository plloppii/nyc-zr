# 108-20 MODIFICATION OF PARKING REQUIREMENTS IN THE RESIDENTIAL BUFFER SUBDISTRICT

In the Residential Buffer Subdistrict, parking shall be provided at the rate of one space per 300 square feet of **_floor area_** for grocery and convenience retailers and specialty food retailers listed under Use Group VI. **_Cellar_** space used for retailing shall be included for the purpose of calculating requirements for **_accessory_** off-street parking spaces and **_accessory_** off-street loading berths.
