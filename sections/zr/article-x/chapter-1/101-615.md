# 101-615 Temporary signs

Temporary **_signs_** relating to sales or events of limited duration not to exceed 45 days may be displayed in a **_show window_** of a **_building_**. Such **_signs_** must be removed after the last day of the sale or event to which they pertain. Temporary **_signs_** shall not occupy more than 20 percent of the surface area of the window in which they are displayed.
