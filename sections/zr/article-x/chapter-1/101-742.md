# 101-742 Screening

Any permitted **_accessory_** off-street parking area, regardless of size or location, shall be screened from all adjoining **_zoning lots_** and **_streets_** by screening that complies with the provisions of Section [36-55](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-6/36-55) (Screening) and which, in the case of a wall or barrier or uniformly painted fence, is finished in an approved color as indicated in Appendix D of this Chapter.
