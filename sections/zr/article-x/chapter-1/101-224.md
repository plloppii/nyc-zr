# 101-224 Special provision for certain sites

For **_large qualifying sites_** that have below-grade transit infrastructure which occupies at least 30 percent of the **_lot area_**, the underlying setback requirements shall be modified to exempt up to 30 percent of the total **_street wall_** width of the **_development_** from such setback requirements.
