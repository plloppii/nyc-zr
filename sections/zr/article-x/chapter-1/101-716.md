# 101-716 Sign materials and colors

**_Signs_** may be fabricated of any opaque material that does not have a glossy or reflective surface, except that glossy translucent material may be used:

(a) in areas where the intent is to imitate or replace glass, as in transoms; or

(b) for individual letters in internally lit **_illuminated signs_**.

Background colors for **_signs_** or common **_sign_** boards shall be limited to black, navy blue, dark green, maroon or bronze.
