# 101-223 Tower regulations

C5-4 C6-1 C6-4 C6-6 C6-9 C6-12

In the districts indicated, except C6-1A Districts, above the maximum base heights specified pursuant to Section [101-222](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-x/chapter-1/101-222) (Standard height and setback regulations), tower provided in accordance with the provisions of Section [23-435](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-3/23-435) (Tower regulations) shall be permitted as an alternative to the maximum **_building_** heights specified in Section [101-222](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-x/chapter-1/101-222). For **_buildings or other structures_** utilizing such provisions, no height limit shall apply, except that, in C6-1 Districts, the maximum height shall be 495 feet.
