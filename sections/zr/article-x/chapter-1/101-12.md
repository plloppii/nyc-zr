# 101-12 Sign Regulations

In the **_Special Downtown Brooklyn District_**, except as modified by the provisions of Section [101-131](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-x/chapter-1/101-131) (Permitted projection), the regulations of Section [32-60](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-2/32-60), inclusive, pertaining to **_signs_**, shall apply. Further regulations for the Fulton Mall Subdistrict and the Atlantic Avenue Subdistrict are set forth in Section [101-60](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-x/chapter-1/101-60), inclusive, and Section [101-70](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-x/chapter-1/101-70), inclusive.
