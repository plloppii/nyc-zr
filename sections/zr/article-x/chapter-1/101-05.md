# 101-05 Applicability of Special Permits by the Board of Standards and Appeals

Within the **_Special Downtown Brooklyn District_**, Section [73-68](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-3/73-68) (Height and Setback and Yard Modifications) shall not be applicable.
