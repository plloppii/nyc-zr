# 101-713 Area of illuminated signs

The total **_surface area_** (in square feet) of **_illuminated signs_** on a **_zoning lot_** shall not exceed two times the **_street_** frontage of the **_zoning lot_** (in feet), and in no event shall exceed 50 square feet for **_interior_** or **_through lots_** or 50 square feet on each frontage for **_corner lots_**. The vertical dimension on any **_illuminated sign_** shall not exceed 2 feet, 6 inches, nor shall **_illuminated signs_** project more than 10 inches from the surface to which they are affixed.
