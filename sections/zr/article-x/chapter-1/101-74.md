# 101-74 Modification of Accessory Off-street Parking and Loading Requirements

The provisions of Section [101-50](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-x/chapter-1/101-50) (OFF-STREET PARKING AND OFF-STREET LOADING REGULATIONS), inclusive, shall not apply within the Atlantic Avenue Subdistrict.
