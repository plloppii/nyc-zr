# 101-532 Off-site accessory parking spaces in public garages

Section [36-56](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-6/36-56) (Accessory Off-street Parking Spaces in Public Garages) shall be modified to allow **_accessory_** off-street parking spaces in any **_public parking garage_** **_developed_** after December 10, 2012, provided such off-site spaces comply with the provisions of Section [101-55](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-x/chapter-1/101-55) (Location of Off-site Parking Spaces).
