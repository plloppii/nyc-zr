# 106-42 Modification of Yard Regulations

No **_front_** or **_side yards_** are required for **_buildings_** containing both **_residential_** and **_commercial_** **_uses_**. For the **_residential_** portion of such **_buildings_**, the **_rear yard_** may be provided at any level not higher than the floor level of the lowest **_story_** used for **_residential use_**.
