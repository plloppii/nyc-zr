# 105-70 SPECIAL REGULATIONS FOR RESIDENTIAL DEVELOPMENT

In order to carry out the purposes of this Chapter, all **_zoning lots_** **_developed_** predominantly for **_residential use_** may be subject to the provisions of this Section.
