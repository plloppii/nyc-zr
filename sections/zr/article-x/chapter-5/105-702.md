# 105-702 Applicability of lower density growth management area regulations

The regulations for **_developments_** or **_enlargements_** within **_lower density growth management areas_** are modified as follows:

(a) Parking location regulations

**_Accessory_** parking spaces shall be permitted within a **_front yard_**.

(b) Private road regulations

The provisions of paragraph (b) of Section [105-35](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-x/chapter-5/105-35) (Tier II Requirements for Driveways and Private Roads) shall apply to **_Tier II sites_** accessed by **_private roads_**.
