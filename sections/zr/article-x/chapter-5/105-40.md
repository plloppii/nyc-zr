# 105-40 SPECIAL REVIEW PROVISIONS

The provisions of this Section shall apply to all **_developments_**, **_enlargements_** or **_site alterations_** located within a **_Special Natural Area District_**.

Prior to the issuance by the Department of Buildings of any permit for a **_development_**, **_enlargement_** or **_site alteration_** within a **_Special Natural Area District_**, the City Planning Commission shall certify to the Department of Buildings that the **_development_**, **_enlargement_** or **_site alteration_** is approved pursuant to Sections [105-41](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-x/chapter-5/105-41) (Certification), [105-42](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-x/chapter-5/105-42) (Authorizations to Alter Natural Features), [105-43](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-x/chapter-5/105-43) (Authorizations to Modify Bulk, Parking, Grading and Private Roads Regulations) or [105-44](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-x/chapter-5/105-44) (Special Permit), inclusive.

The provisions of Article VII, Chapter 8 (Special Regulations Applying to Large-scale Residential Developments), shall apply to a **_large-scale residential development_**, as modified by the provisions of Section [105-701](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-x/chapter-5/105-701) (Applicability of large-scale residential development regulations).
