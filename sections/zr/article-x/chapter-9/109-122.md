# 109-122 Height and setback regulations

The underlying height and setback provisions applicable to an R8B District shall apply to any **_building or other structure_** on an **_interior lot_** or **_through lot_**, and the underlying height and setback provisions applicable to an R7A District shall apply to **_corner lots_**.
