# 109-212 Sign Regulations

In addition to the underlying district **_sign_** regulations, the following regulations shall apply to all **_signs_**:

(a) **_signs_** may not occupy more than 25 percent of the total area of the storefront measured from **_curb level_** to 10 feet above **_curb level_**;

(b) all permitted **_signs_** which project from the front **_building_** wall shall be located not less than 10 feet above **_curb level_**; and

(c) **_signs_** may not cover columns, cornices or sills.
