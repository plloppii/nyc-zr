# 103-02 Special Planned Community Preservation District Areas

The **_Special Planned Community Preservation District_** areas are as follows, and are each indicated by the letters "PC" on the **_zoning maps_**:

Fresh Meadows in the Borough of Queens

The Harlem River Houses in the Borough of Manhattan

Parkchester in the Borough of the Bronx

Sunnyside Gardens in the Borough of Queens.
