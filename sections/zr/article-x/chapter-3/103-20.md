# 103-20 SPECIAL REGULATIONS FOR SUNNYSIDE GARDENS

In order to further protect and enhance the character of the Sunnyside Gardens area within the **_Special Planned Community Preservation District_**, the regulations of this Section, inclusive, shall supersede the regulations of the **_Special Planned Community Preservation District_** and the underlying zoning districts, as applicable.

The special permit provisions of Sections [103-11](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-x/chapter-3/103-11) (Special Permit for Bulk and Parking Modifications) and [103-12](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-x/chapter-3/103-12) (Special Permit for Landscaping and Topography Modifications) shall not apply within the Sunnyside Gardens area of the Special District.
