# 44-024 Applicability of regulations in an M1-1 District in Community District 12 in the Borough of Queens

In the M1-1 District bounded by 95th Avenue, 148th Street, 97th Avenue and 147th Place in Community District 12 in the Borough of Queens, the **_accessory_** off-street parking regulations of an M1 District shall apply, except that the **_accessory_** off-street parking regulations for an R5 District set forth in Article II, Chapter 5, shall apply to **_residential uses_**.
