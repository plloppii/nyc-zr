# 44-24 Waiver of Requirements for All Zoning Lots Where Access Would be Forbidden

M1 M2 M3

In all districts, as indicated, the requirements set forth in Sections [44-21](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iv/chapter-4/44-21) (General Provisions) or [44-22](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iv/chapter-4/44-22) (Special Provisions for a Single Zoning Lot With Uses Subject to Different Parking Requirements) shall not apply to any **_building_** or **_zoning lot_** as to which the Commissioner of Buildings has certified that there is no way to arrange the required spaces with access to the **_street_** to conform to the provisions of Section [44-43](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iv/chapter-4/44-43) (Location of Access to the Street).

The Commissioner of Buildings may refer such matter to the Department of Transportation for a report, and may base a determination on such report.
