# 44-11 General Provisions

M1 M2 M3

In all districts, as indicated, **_accessory_** off-street parking spaces may be provided for all permitted **_uses_** subject to the applicable provisions set forth in Section [44-12](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iv/chapter-4/44-12) (Maximum Size of Accessory Group Parking Facilities).

Such **_accessory_** off-street parking spaces may be open or enclosed. However, except as otherwise provided in Section [73-47](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-3/73-47) (Roof Parking), no spaces shall be located on any roof which is immediately above a **_story_** other than a **_basement_**.
