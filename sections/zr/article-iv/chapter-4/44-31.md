# 44-31 General Provisions

M1 M2 M3

In all districts, as indicated, all permitted or required off-street parking spaces, open or enclosed, **_accessory_** to any permitted **_use_** shall be provided on the same **_zoning lot_** as the **_building_** or **_use_** to which such spaces are **_accessory_**, except as provided in the following Sections:

Section [44-32](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iv/chapter-4/44-32) (Off-site Spaces for All Permitted Uses)

Section [44-33](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iv/chapter-4/44-33) (Joint and Shared Facilities)

Section [44-34](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iv/chapter-4/44-34) (Additional Regulations for Required Spaces When Provided Off-site)

Section [73-45](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-3/73-45) (Modification of Off-site Parking Provisions)

Such exceptions to the requirement that the spaces be provided on the same **_zoning lot_** as the **_building_** or **_use_** to which they are **_accessory_** shall not apply in the case of spaces provided in a permitted **_public parking garage_** in accordance with the provisions of Section [44-46](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iv/chapter-4/44-46) (Accessory Off-street Parking Spaces in Public Parking Garages).
