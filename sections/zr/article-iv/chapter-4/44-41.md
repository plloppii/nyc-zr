# 44-41 General Provisions

M1 M2 M3

In all districts, as indicated, all permitted or required off-street parking spaces shall conform to the provisions of Section [44-40](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iv/chapter-4/44-40), inclusive.

Special regulations applying to **_large-scale community facility developments_** are set forth in Article VII, Chapter 8.
