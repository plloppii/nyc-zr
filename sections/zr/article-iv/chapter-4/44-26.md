# 44-26 Special Provisions for Expansion of Existing Manufacturing Buildings

M1 M2 M3

In all districts, as indicated, whenever an existing **_manufacturing_** building is expanded pursuant to the provisions of Section [43-121](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iv/chapter-3/43-121) (Expansion of existing manufacturing buildings), the City Planning Commission may reduce, up to a maximum of 40 spaces, the parking requirements of Sections [44-21](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iv/chapter-4/44-21) (General Provisions) or [44-22](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iv/chapter-4/44-22) (Special Provisions for a Single Zoning Lot With Uses Subject to Different Parking Regulations), provided the Commission certifies:

(a) that because of site limitations such a reduction is necessary for the proper design and operation of the **_manufacturing_** building; and

(b) that off-site parking and mass transit facilities are adequate to satisfy the additional parking demand generated by the expansion.
