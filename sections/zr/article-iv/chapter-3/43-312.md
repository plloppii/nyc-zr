# 43-312 Along short dimension of block

M1 M2 M3

In all districts, as indicated, whenever a **_front lot line_** of a **_zoning lot_** coincides with the **_short dimension of a block_**, no **_rear yard_** shall be required within 100 feet of such **_front lot line_**.
