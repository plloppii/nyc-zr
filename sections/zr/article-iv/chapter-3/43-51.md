M1

Section [24-62](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-4/24-62) (Minimum Dimensions of Courts)

Section [24-63](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-4/24-63) (Outer Court Regulations)

Section [24-64](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-4/24-64) (Inner Court Regulations)

Section [24-65](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-4/24-65) (Minimum Distance Between Required Windows and Walls or Lot Lines)

Section [24-66](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-4/24-66) (Modifications of Court Regulations or Distance Requirements)

Section [24-67](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-4/24-67) (Permitted Obstructions in Courts).
