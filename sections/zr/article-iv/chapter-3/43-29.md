# 43-29 Special Provisions Applying Along Railroad Right-of-way

M1 M2 M3

In all districts, as indicated, along such portion of a **_rear lot line_** which coincides with a boundary of a **_railroad right-of-way_**, no **_rear yard_** shall be required.
