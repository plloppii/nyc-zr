# 42-19 Use Group IX – Storage

Use Group IX consists of **_uses_** that provide storage for materials, goods, and vehicles. The provisions regulating **_uses_** classified in this Use Group are set forth as follows:

(a) Section [42-191](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iv/chapter-2/42-191) (Use Group IX – general use allowances) which includes the compilation of **_uses_** in the Use Group tables;
(b) Section [42-192](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iv/chapter-2/42-192) (Use Group IX – uses permitted with limited applicability) for additional limitations on applicability for certain **_uses_**, as denoted with “♦” in the Use Group tables;
(c) Section [42-193](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iv/chapter-2/42-193) (Use Group IX – uses subject to additional conditions) for additional conditions that apply to certain **_uses_**, as denoted with a “P” in the Use Group tables; and
(d) Section [42-194](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iv/chapter-2/42-194) (Use Group IX – uses subject to open use allowances) for open **_use_** allowances that apply to certain **_uses_**, as denoted with a “U” in the Use Group tables.
