# 42-643 Height of signs

M1 M2 M3

In all districts, as indicated, permitted **_signs_** shall not extend to a height greater than 40 feet above **_curb level_**, provided that non-**_illuminated signs_** or **_signs with indirect illumination_** may extend to a maximum height of 75 feet.
