# 42-69 Sign Regulations in M1-6D Districts

In M1-6D Districts, **_signs_** are permitted subject to the **_sign_** regulations applicable in C6-4 Districts, as set forth in Section [32-60](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-2/32-60), inclusive.
