# 42-142 Use Group IV – uses subject to size limitations

For **_uses_** denoted with an “S” in Section [42-141](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iv/chapter-2/42-141) (Use Group IV – general use allowances), the provisions of this Section shall apply. Gas utility substations in all **_Manufacturing Districts_** shall be limited to a site of not more than 10,000 square feet.
