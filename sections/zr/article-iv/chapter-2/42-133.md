# 42-133 Use Group III – uses permitted by special permit

For **_uses_** denoted with “○” in Section [42-131](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iv/chapter-2/42-131) (Use Group III – general use allowances), the following provisions shall apply:

(a) **_Schools_** may be permitted in M1 Districts by special permit of the Board of Standards and Appeals, in accordance with Section [73-133](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-3/73-133) (Schools).
(b) **_Uses_** listed in Use Group III(B), except for educational institutions, may be permitted in M1 Districts by special permit of the City Planning Commission, in accordance with Section [74-134](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-4/74-134) (Other community facility uses in M1 Districts).
