# 42-661 Restrictions along district boundary located in a street

M1 M2 M3

In all districts, as indicated, and within 100 feet of the **_street line_** of any **_street_** or portion thereof in which the boundary of an adjoining **_Residence District_** is located, or which adjoins a **_public park_** of one-half acre or more, **_advertising signs_** that face at an angle of less than 165 degrees away from such **_Residence District_** or park boundary shall not be permitted and all other **_signs_** facing at less than such an angle shall conform with all the **_sign_** regulations applicable in C1 Districts as set forth in Sections [32-61](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-2/32-61) to [32-68](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-2/32-68), inclusive, relating to Sign Regulations.
