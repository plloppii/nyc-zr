# 42-114 Use Group I – uses permitted by special permit

For **_uses_** denoted with “○” in Section [42-111](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iv/chapter-2/42-111) (Use Group I – general use allowances), the provisions of this Section shall apply. Sand, gravel or clay pits, may be permitted in all **_Manufacturing Districts_**, by special permit of the Board of Standards and Appeals, in accordance with the provisions of Section [73-112](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-3/73-112) (Sand, gravel or clay pits).
