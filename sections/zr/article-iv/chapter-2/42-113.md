# 42-113 Use Group I – uses subject to open use allowances

For **_uses_** denoted with a “U” in Section [42-111](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iv/chapter-2/42-111) (Use Group I – general use allowances), a **_use_** may be open or enclosed without restriction.
