# 42-463 Maximum permitted quantities of fissionable materials

In M1 or M2 Districts, no one of the following fissionable materials shall be assembled at any one point, place, or work area on a **_zoning lot_** in a quantity equal to or in excess of the amount set forth herein:

| Material | Quantity |
| --- | --- |
| Uranium-233 | 200 grams |
| Plutonium-239 | 200 grams |
| Uranium-235 | 350 grams |

In addition, any establishment which provides radiation waste disposal services in the nature of collection or storage of radioactive waste from other **_manufacturing uses_** shall be prohibited in M1 or M2 Districts.
