# 42-513 Drive-through facilities

In all districts, as indicated, **_accessory_** drive-through facilities shall be permitted for any **_use_** listed in Use Group VI, as set forth in Section [42-16](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iv/chapter-2/42-16) (Use Group VI – Retail and Services).
