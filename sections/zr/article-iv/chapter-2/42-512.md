# 42-512 In M2 or M3 Districts

M2 M3

In the districts indicated, all such activities within 300 feet of a **_Residence District_** boundary shall be located within **_completely enclosed buildings_**, provided, however, that **_commercial_** **_uses_** may be located within **_buildings_** which are **_completely enclosed_** except for store fronts or store windows which may be opened to serve customers outside the **_building_**. All such activities beyond 300 feet of a **_Residence District_** boundary may be conducted outside a **_completely enclosed building_**, and any enclosure requirements set forth in Sections [42-11](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iv/chapter-2/42-11) through [42-20](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iv/chapter-2/42-20) need not apply.
