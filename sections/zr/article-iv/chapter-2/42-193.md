# 42-193 Use Group IX – uses subject to additional conditions

For **_uses_** denoted with a “P” in Section [42-191](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iv/chapter-2/42-191) (Use Group IX – general use allowances), the following provisions shall apply:

(a) Boat storage shall be restricted to boats less than 100 feet in length.
(b) **_Public parking garages_** and **_public parking lots_** shall be subject to the provisions set forth in Section [44-025](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iv/chapter-4/44-025) (Applicability of regulations to public parking garages and public parking lots).
(c) All **_uses_** denoted with a “P” in Section [42-191](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iv/chapter-2/42-191) (Use Group IX – general use allowances), except boat storage, **_public parking garages_** and **_public parking lots_**, shall conform to the performance standards set forth in Sections [42-40](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iv/chapter-2/42-40) (PERFORMANCE STANDARDS) through [42-48](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iv/chapter-2/42-48) (Performance Standards Regulating Humidity, Heat or Glare), inclusive.
