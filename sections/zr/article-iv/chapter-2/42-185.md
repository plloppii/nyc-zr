# 42-185 Use Group VIII – uses permitted by special permit

For **_uses_** denoted with “○” in Section [42-181](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iv/chapter-2/42-181) (Use Group VIII – general use allowances), the following provisions shall apply:

(a) Drive-in theaters may be permitted in all **_Manufacturing Districts_** by special permit of the City Planning Commission as set forth in Section [74-183](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-4/74-183) (Drive-in theaters).
(b) Racetracks may be permitted in all **_Manufacturing Districts_** by special permit of the City Planning Commission, in accordance with Section [74-184](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-4/74-184) (Racetracks).
