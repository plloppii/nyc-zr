# 66-52 Additional Modifications

In conjunction with any application pursuant to Section [66-21](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-6/66-21) (Determination and Certification for Transit Volumes), [66-31](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-6/66-31) (Determination and Certification for Optional Transit Volumes) and [66-51](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-6/66-51) (Additional Floor Area for Mass Transit Station Improvements), modifications may be granted pursuant to Section [66-521](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-6/66-521) (Authorization for transit-adjacent sites or qualifying transit improvement sites) or Section [66-522](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-6/66-522) (Special permit for transit-adjacent sites or qualifying transit improvement sites).
