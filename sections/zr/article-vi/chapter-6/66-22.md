# 66-22 Special Use Regulations

Where an **_easement volume_** is provided, the applicable **_use_** regulations of this Resolution shall be modified in accordance with the provisions of Sections [62-221](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-2/62-221) (Temporary uses) and [62-222](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-2/62-222) (Special use allowances around easement volumes).
