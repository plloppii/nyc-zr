# 66-43 Annual Reporting

The **_transit agency_** shall submit annually to the Chairperson of the City Planning Commission and to the City Council a report containing an inventory of all **_easement volumes_** established through the provisions of this Chapter and describing the status of improvements within all such **_easement volume_**.
