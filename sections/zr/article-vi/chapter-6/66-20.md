# 66-20 SPECIAL PROVISIONS FOR PRIMARY TRANSIT-ADJACENT SITES

For all **_developments_** or **_enlargements_** on the lowest **_story_** of a **_building_** on **_primary transit-adjacent sites_**, a determination, or certification, where applicable, for a **_transit volume_** shall be obtained pursuant to Section [66-21](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-6/66-21) (Determination and Certification for Transit Volumes). Where a **_transit volume_** is needed, special **_use_**, **_bulk_**, parking, and streetscape regulations are set forth in Sections [66-22](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-6/66-22) (Special Use Regulations), [66-23](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-6/66-23) (Special Bulk Regulations), [66-24](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-6/66-24) (Special Regulations for Accessory Off-street Parking), and [66-25](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-6/66-25) (Special Streetscape Regulations) respectively. Separate applicability is set forth within such Sections for **_primary transit-adjacent sites_** with **_easement volumes_** and for those with **_clear paths_**.
