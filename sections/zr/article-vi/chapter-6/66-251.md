# 66-251 Ground floor level requirements

An **_easement volume_** shall be excluded from any ground floor level requirements of this Resolution, including, but not limited to, the location of such ground floor in relation to the adjoining sidewalk level, restrictions of types of **_use_**, the minimum depth for certain **_uses_**, maximum width for certain **_uses_**, minimum transparency requirement, and parking wrap and screening requirements.
