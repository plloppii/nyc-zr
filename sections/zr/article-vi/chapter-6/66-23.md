# 66-23 Special Bulk Regulations

Where an **_easement volume_** is provided, the applicable **_bulk_** regulations of this Resolution shall be modified in accordance with the provisions of this Section. Where a **_clear path_** is provided, only the **_street wall_** provisions of Section [66-233](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-6/66-233) (Special street wall modifications) shall apply.
