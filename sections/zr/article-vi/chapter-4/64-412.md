# 64-412 Surfacing

R1 R2 R3 R4 R5

In the districts indicated, Section [25-64](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-5/25-64) (Surfacing) may be modified to allow dustless gravel on all open off-street parking spaces and on portions of driveways beyond the **_front lot line_** that access **_single-_** or **_two-family residences_** on a **_zoning lot_**.
