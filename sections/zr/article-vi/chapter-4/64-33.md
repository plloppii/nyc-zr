# 64-33 Special Bulk Regulations for Cottage Envelope Buildings

The provisions of this Section, inclusive, are optional, and are only applicable to **_zoning lots_** located wholly or partially within **_flood zones_**. The provisions of this Section, inclusive, may be applied to **_zoning lots_** with **_cottage envelope buildings_**. For such **_zoning lots_**, the underlying **_bulk_** regulations shall apply, except where permitted to be modified by this Section, inclusive.
