# 65-20 SPECIAL DAMAGE AND DESTRUCTION PROVISIONS

The following modifications to damage and destruction provisions associated with this Resolution may be applied to **_zoning lots_** within **_designated recovery areas_**, as set forth in Section [65-01](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-5/65-01) (Applicability of Article VI, Chapter 5).
