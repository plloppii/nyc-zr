# 65-13 Discontinuance Provisions

In all districts, where a **_non-conforming_** **_use_** has been discontinued, the time limits associated with restoring active operations of such **_use_** to retain its **_non-conforming_** status shall be suspended. Subsequent to the expiration of the applicable time duration as set forth in Section [65-01](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-5/65-01) (Applicability of Article VI, Chapter 5), the time period established by the applicable provisions of this Resolution shall be resumed, and the remaining time associated with restoring active operations shall be the amount existing prior to the **_severe disaster_**.
