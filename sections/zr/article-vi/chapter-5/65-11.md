# 65-11 Vesting Provisions

In all districts, the provisions of Section [11-30](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-i/chapter-1/11-30) (BUILDING PERMITS ISSUED BEFORE EFFECTIVE DATE OF AMENDMENT), inclusive, and any other provision that requires a building permit to be submitted, or a particular amount of construction to be completed within a certain timeframe, shall be suspended. Subsequent to the expiration of the applicable time duration as set forth in Section [65-01](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-5/65-01) (Applicability of Article VI, Chapter 5), the time period established by the applicable provisions of this Resolution shall be resumed, and the remaining time associated with submitting a building permit, or completing a particular amount of construction shall be the amount existing prior to the **_severe disaster_**.
