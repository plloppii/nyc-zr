# 62-422 Accessory non-residential off-site parking

For **_developments_** on **_waterfront blocks_**, the provisions of Sections [36-43](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-6/36-43) (Off-site Spaces for Commercial or Community Facility Uses) and [44-32](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iv/chapter-4/44-32) (Off-site Spaces for All Permitted Uses) shall be modified to permit such spaces to be within 1,000 feet of the **_zoning lot_** to which they are **_accessory_**.
