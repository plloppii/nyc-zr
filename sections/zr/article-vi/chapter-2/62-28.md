# 62-28 Special Sign Regulations

Within a **_waterfront block_**, no **_flashing sign_** permitted in accordance with the applicable district regulations shall exceed 50 square feet in **_surface area_** and no more than one such **_sign_** shall be permitted for each establishment located on a **_zoning lot_**, except that no **_flashing sign_** shall be permitted on any **_pier_** or **_platform_**.
