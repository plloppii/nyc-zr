# 62-911 Establishment of Waterfront Access Plans

The City Planning Commission and City Council may adopt a Waterfront Access Plan as an amendment to this Resolution pursuant to Section 200 or 201 of the City Charter and in accordance with the provisions of Sections [62-912](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-2/62-912) (Elements of a Waterfront Access Plan), [62-913](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-2/62-913) (Conditions for adoption of a Waterfront Access Plan) and this Section in order to adjust the **_waterfront public access area_** and **_visual corridor_** requirements of Sections [62-50](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-2/62-50) and [62-60](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-2/62-60), inclusive, retain the **_waterfront block bulk_** regulations of Section [62-30](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-2/62-30) on newly-created non-**_waterfront blocks_** within a specifically defined portion of the **_waterfront area_**, or establish **_waterfront yard_** requirements for **_developments_** otherwise exempt from the requirements of Section [62-33](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-2/62-33) (Special Yard and Lot Coverage Regulations on Waterfront Blocks).

To be considered for a Waterfront Access Plan, an area shall:

(a) be entirely in the **_waterfront area_**;

(b) not include any portions within R1 or R2 Districts;

(c) comprise either entire **_blocks_** or a minimum of four acres, all portions of which are contiguous tracts of land except for intervening **_streets_**; and

(d) have at least 600 feet of **_shoreline_**.
