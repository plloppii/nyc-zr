# 62-36 Special Bulk Regulations in Certain Areas Within Community District 1, Brooklyn

On **_waterfront blocks_** in Waterfront Access Plan BK-1 in Community District 1, Borough of Brooklyn, the special **_bulk_** regulations of this Chapter are further modified as set forth in this Section, inclusive.
