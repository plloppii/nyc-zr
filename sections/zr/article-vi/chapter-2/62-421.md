# 62-421 Accessory non-residential roof parking

On **_waterfront blocks_**, parking spaces **_accessory_** to non-**_residential uses_** may be located on the roof of a **_non-residential building_** or a non-**_residential_** portion of a **_building_** containing **_residential use_**, provided that they comply with Section [62-411](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-2/62-411) (Accessory residential roof parking), paragraphs (a) through (e).
