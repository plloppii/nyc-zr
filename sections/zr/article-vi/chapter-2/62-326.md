# 62-326 Maximum floor area ratio for a mixed use building in a Special Mixed Use District

The maximum **_floor area ratio_** for **_zoning lots_** containing a **_mixed use building_** in a **_Special Mixed Use District_** shall be in accordance with the provisions of Section [123-62](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-xii/chapter-3/123-62) (Special Floor Area Regulations).
