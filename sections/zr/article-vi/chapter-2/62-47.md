# 62-47 Special Parking and Loading Regulations for Waterfront Access Plan BK-1

Within Waterfront Access Plan BK-1, the special parking and loading regulations of this Section are further modified as follows:

(a) The provisions of Sections [62-411](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-2/62-411) and [62-421](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-2/62-421) shall not be applicable.

(b) **_Accessory_** off-street parking spaces for **_uses_** permitted pursuant to Section [62-29](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-2/62-29) shall be provided in conformity with the regulations of Sections [36-21](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-6/36-21), [36-22](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-6/36-22) and [36-232](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-6/36-232) for C2-4 Districts.

(c) Any required **_accessory_** off-street parking spaces provided for **_uses_** located on a parcel identified in Waterfront Access Plan BK-1 may be located anywhere within such parcel.
