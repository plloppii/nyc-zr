# 62-22 Commercial Docking Facilities

Commercial docking facilities are listed in Use Group IV(B) in Sections [32-14](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-2/32-14) and [42-14](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iv/chapter-2/42-14). Such **_uses_** are permitted as-of-right in all districts set forth in the Use Groups and are subject to the **_accessory_** off-street parking and passenger loading requirements of Sections [62-43](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-2/62-43) and [62-462](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-2/62-462) of this Chapter.
