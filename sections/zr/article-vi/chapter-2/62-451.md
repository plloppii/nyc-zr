# 62-451 Parking spaces on floating structures

No **_accessory_** parking spaces shall be located on a **_floating structure_** except in conjunction with a special permit pursuant to Section [62-834](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-2/62-834) (Uses on floating structures).
