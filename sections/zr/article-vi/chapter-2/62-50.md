# 62-50 GENERAL REQUIREMENTS FOR VISUAL CORRIDORS AND WATERFRONT PUBLIC ACCESS AREAS

All **_zoning lots_** **_developed_** within **_waterfront blocks_** shall be subject to the provisions of this Section and Section [62-81](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-2/62-81) (Certifications by the Chairperson of the City Planning Commission).

For the purpose of determining requirements for **_waterfront public access areas_**, the **_lot area_** of **_waterfront zoning lots_** shall be deemed to be the area of the **_upland lot_** and **_water coverage_** of structurally sound **_piers_** and **_platforms_** within the **_seaward lot_**.

All **_waterfront public access areas_**, including those required pursuant to paragraph (b) of Section [62-52](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-2/62-52) (Applicability of Waterfront Public Access Area Requirements), shall comply with the provisions of Section [62-60](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-2/62-60) (DESIGN REQUIREMENTS FOR WATERFRONT PUBLIC ACCESS AREAS), except as modified by:

(a) authorization of the City Planning Commission, pursuant to Sections [62-822](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-2/62-822) (Modification of waterfront public access area and visual corridor requirements) or [75-24](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-5/75-24) (Bulk Modifications Associated With a Transfer of Development Rights From Landmark Sites);
(b) special permit of the City Planning Commission, pursuant to Sections [62-834](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-2/62-834) (Uses on floating structures), [62-835](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-2/62-835) (Developments on piers or platforms) or [74-79](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-4/74-79) (Transfer of Development Rights From Landmark Sites); or
(c) the establishment of a Waterfront Access Plan, pursuant to Section [62-90](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-2/62-90).

However, the design of portions of **_waterfront public access areas_** located within New York State-designated wetlands or their adjacent regulated areas, shall be in accordance with an approval from the New York State Department of Environmental Conservation.

In the event of a conflict between the provisions of this Section and a Waterfront Access Plan, the Plan shall control.
