# 62-60 DESIGN REQUIREMENTS FOR WATERFRONT PUBLIC ACCESS AREAS

**_Waterfront public access areas_** required pursuant to Section [62-52](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-2/62-52) (Applicability of Waterfront Public Access Area Requirements) shall comply with the provisions of this Section, inclusive.
