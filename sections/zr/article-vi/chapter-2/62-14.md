# 62-14 Integration of Waterfront Access Plans

Waterfront Access Plans shall be set forth in Section [62-90](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-2/62-90) of this Chapter. Such plans shall supersede, supplement or modify certain provisions of this Chapter. Except as expressly stated otherwise in the plan, all provisions of this Chapter remain in effect in the area subject to such plan.
