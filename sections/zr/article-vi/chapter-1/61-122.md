# 61-122 Airport Circling District

The Airport Circling District comprises those parts of the **_flight obstruction area_** of any major airport which lie generally below the flight path of aircraft circling such airport and, more specifically, below the following **_airport referenced imaginary surfaces_**: those parts of the **_horizontal surface_** and the **_conical surface_** which do not coincide with the **_approach surfaces_** and the **_transitional surfaces_**.
