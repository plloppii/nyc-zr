# 61-12 Establishment of Flight Obstruction Area; Division into Districts

A **_flight obstruction area_** is hereby established in the vicinity of any such major airport.

Each **_flight obstruction area_** shall be divided into two parts, the Airport Approach District and the Airport Circling District, as described in this Section (and as shown, for convenient reference only, in the **_flight obstruction area_** maps available on request at the offices of the City Planning Commission).
