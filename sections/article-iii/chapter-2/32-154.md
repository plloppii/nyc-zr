# 32-154 Use Group V – uses subject to open use allowances

For **_uses_** denoted with a “U” in Section [32-151](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-2/32-151) (Use Group V – general use allowances), a **_use_** may be open or enclosed without restriction.
