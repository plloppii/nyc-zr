# 32-31 Regulations for Tier A Street Frontages

The provisions of this Section, inclusive, shall apply to the **_ground floor level_** **_street_** frontage of a **_building_** along a **_Tier A street frontage_**. Such provisions include **_ground floor level_**:

(a) **_blank wall_** restrictions, as set forth in Section [32-311](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-2/32-311); and
(b) parking restrictions, as set forth in Section 32-312.
