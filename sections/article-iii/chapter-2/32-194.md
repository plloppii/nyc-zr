# 32-194 Use Group IX – uses subject to additional conditions

For **_uses_** denoted with a “P” in Section [32-191](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-2/32-191) (Use Group IX – general use allowances), the following provisions shall apply:

(a) Wholesale establishment in C1 or C2 Districts, or C4 through C8 Districts, shall not include produce or meat markets.
(b) Boat storage in all **_Commercial Districts_** shall be restricted to boats less than 100 feet in length.
(c) **_Public parking garages_** and **_public parking lots_** in all applicable districts, shall be subject to the provisions set forth in Section [36-026](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-6/36-026) (Applicability of regulations to public parking garages and public parking lots).
