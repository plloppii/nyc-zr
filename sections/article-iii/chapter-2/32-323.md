# 32-323 Ground floor level curb cut restrictions

Along the **_Tier B street frontage_** of a **_ground floor level_**, the following curb cut restrictions shall apply:

For **_zoning lots_** with frontage along a standard **_Tier B street frontage_** subject to the provisions of Section [32-321](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-2/32-321) and frontage along another **_street_** that is neither a standard **_Tier B_** nor a **_Tier C street frontage_**, no curb cut accessing off-street parking spaces or loading spaces shall be permitted along such standard **_Tier B street frontage_**.
