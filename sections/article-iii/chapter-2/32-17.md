# 32-17 Use Group VII – Offices and Laboratories

C1 C2 C3 C4 C5 C6 C7 C8

Use Group VII consists of **_uses_** that provide administrative and research workspaces for business, professional or governmental purposes. The provisions regulating **_uses_** classified in this Use Group, are set forth as follows:

(a) Section [32-171](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-2/32-171) (Use Group VII – general use allowances) which includes the compilation of **_uses_** in the Use Group table;
(b) Section [32-172](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-2/32-172) (Use Group VII – use permitted with limited applicability) for additional limitations on applicability for certain **_uses_**, as denoted with a “♦” in the Use Group table; and
(c) Section [32-173](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-2/32-173) (Use Group VII – uses subject to additional conditions) for additional conditions that apply to certain **_uses_**, as denoted with a “P” in the Use Group table.
