# 32-12 Use Group II – Residences

C1 C2 C3 C4 C5 C6 C7 C8

Use Group II consists of **_residences_** of various types. The provisions regulating **_uses_** classified in this Use Group are set forth as follows:

(a) Section [32-121](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-2/32-121) (Use Group II – general use allowances) which includes the compilation of **_uses_** in the Use Group table;
(b) Section [32-122](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-2/32-122) (Use Group II – uses permitted with limited applicability) for additional limitations on applicability for certain **_uses_**, as denoted with “♦” in the Use Group table; and
(c) Section [32-123](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-2/32-123) (Use Group II – uses subject to additional conditions) for additional conditions that apply to certain **_uses_**, as denoted with a “P” in the Use Group table.
