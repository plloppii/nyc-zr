# 32-204 Use Group X – additional provisions for parking requirement category

In C1 or C2 Districts, or C4 through C8 Districts, all permitted **_uses_** are classified in parking requirement category B except that **_uses_** located above the ground floor shall be classified in parking requirement category C.
