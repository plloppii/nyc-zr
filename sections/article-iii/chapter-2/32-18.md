# 32-18 Use Group VIII – Recreation, Entertainment and Assembly Spaces

C1 C2 C3 C4 C5 C6 C7 C8

Use Group VIII consists of **_uses_** that provide recreation and entertainment opportunities, as well as other places of assembly. The provisions regulating **_uses_** classified in this Use Group are set forth as follows:

(a) Section [32-181](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-2/32-181) (Use Group VIII – general use allowances) which includes the compilation of **_uses_** in the Use Group table;
(b) Section [32-182](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-2/32-182) (Use Group VIII – uses subject to size restrictions) for size restrictions that apply to certain **_uses_**, as denoted with an “S” in the Use Group table;
(c) Section [32-183](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-2/32-183) (Use Group VIII – uses subject to additional conditions) for additional conditions that apply to certain **_uses_**, as denoted with a “P” in the Use Group table;
(d) Section [32-184](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-2/32-184) (Use Group VIII – uses subject to open use allowances) for open **_use_** allowances that apply to certain **_uses_**, as denoted with a “U” in the Use Group table;
(e) Section [32-185](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-2/32-185) (Use Group VIII – uses permitted by special permit) for **_uses_** permitted by special permit of the Board of Standards and Appeals or the City Planning Commission, as denoted with “○” in the Use Group table; and
(f) Section [32-186](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-2/32-186) (Use Group VIII – additional provisions for parking requirement category) for **_uses_** with more than one parking requirement category or other applicable parking provisions, as denoted with “\*” in the Use Group table.

The provisions of Sections [32-182](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-2/32-182), [32-183](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-2/32-183) and [32-184](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-2/32-184), except as otherwise specified, may be modified by special permit of the Board of Standards and Appeals, in accordance with Section [73-181](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-3/73-181) (Recreation, entertainment and assembly space uses), or by special permit of the City Planning Commission, in accordance with Section [74-181](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-4/74-181) (Recreation, entertainment and assembly space uses).
