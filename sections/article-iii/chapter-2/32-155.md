# 32-155 Use Group V – uses permitted by special permit

For **_uses_** denoted with a “○” in Section [32-151](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-2/32-151) (Use Group V – general use allowances), the provisions of this Section shall apply. In C1 through C7 Districts, overnight camps may only be permitted by special permit of the Board of Standards and Appeals, in accordance with Section [73-151](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-3/73-151) (Overnight camps).
