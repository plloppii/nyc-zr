# 32-413 Drive-through facilities

In C1 through C7 Districts, **_accessory_** drive-through facilities for any **_use_** listed in Use Group VI, as set forth in Section [32-16](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-2/32-16) (Use Group VI – Retail and Services), may be permitted by special permit of the Board of Standards and Appeals, in accordance with Section [73-311](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-3/73-311) (Drive-through facilities).
