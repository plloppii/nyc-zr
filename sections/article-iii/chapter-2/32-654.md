# 32-654 Height of signs in C8 Districts

C8

In the district indicated, permitted **_signs_** shall not extend to a height greater than 40 feet above **_curb level_**, provided that non-**_illuminated signs_** or **_signs with indirect illumination_** may extend to a maximum height of 58 feet.
