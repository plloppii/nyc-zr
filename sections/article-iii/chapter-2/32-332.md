# 32-332 Ground floor level curb cut restrictions

Along the **_Tier C street frontage_** of a **_ground floor level_**, the following curb cut restrictions shall apply: for **_zoning lots_** with frontage along a **_Tier C street frontage_** and frontage along another **_street_** that is not a **_Tier C street frontage_**, no curb cut accessing off-street parking spaces or loading spaces shall be permitted along such **_Tier C street frontage_**.
