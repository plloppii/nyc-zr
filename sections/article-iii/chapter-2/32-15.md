# 32-15 Use Group V – Transient Accommodations

Use Group V consists of **_uses_** for transient occupancy of various types. The provisions regulating **_uses_** classified in this Use Group are set forth as follows:

(a) Section [32-151](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-2/32-151) (Use Group V – general use allowances) which includes the compilation of **_uses_** in the Use Group table;
(b) Section [32-152](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-2/32-152) (Use Group V – uses permitted with limited applicability) for additional limitations on applicability for certain **_uses_**, as denoted with a “♦” in the Use Group table;
(c) Section [32-153](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-2/32-153) (Use Group V – uses subject to additional conditions) for additional conditions that apply to certain **_uses_**, as denoted with a “P” in the Use Group table;
(d) Section [32-154](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-2/32-154) (Use Group V – uses subject to open use allowances) for open **_use_** allowances that apply to certain **_uses_**, as denoted with a “U” in the Use Group table;
(e) Section [32-155](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-2/32-155) (Use Group V – uses permitted by special permit) for **_uses_** permitted by special permit of the Board of Standards and Appeals, as denoted with a “○” in the Use Group table; and
(f) Section [32-156](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-2/32-156) (Use Group V – additional provisions for parking requirement category) for **_uses_** with more than one parking requirement category or other applicable parking provisions, as denoted with an “\*” in the Use Group table.
