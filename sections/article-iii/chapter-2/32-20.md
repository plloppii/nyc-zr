# 32-20 Use Group X – Production Uses

C1 C2 C3 C4 C5 C6 C7 C8

Use Group X consists of **_uses_** engaged in the mechanical, physical, or chemical transformation of materials into new goods. The provisions regulating **_uses_** classified in this Use Group are set forth as follows:

(a) Section [32-201](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-2/32-201) (Use Group X – general use allowances) which includes the compilation of **_uses_** in the Use Group table;
(b) Section [32-202](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-2/32-202) (Use Group X – uses subject to size limitations) for size restrictions that apply to certain **_uses_**, as denoted with an “S” in the Use Group table;
(c) Section [32-203](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-2/32-203) (Use Group X – uses subject to additional conditions) for additional conditions that apply to certain **_uses_**, as denoted with a “P” in the Use Group table; and
(d) Section [32-204](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-2/32-204) (Use Group X – additional provisions for parking requirement category) for **_uses_** with more than one parking requirement category or other applicable parking provisions, as denoted with “\*” in the Use Group table.

The provisions of Sections [32-202](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-2/32-202) and [32-203](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-2/32-203), except as otherwise specified in such Sections, may be modified by special permit of the Board of Standards and Appeals, in accordance with Section [73-211](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-3/73-211) (Production uses), or by special permit of the City Planning Commission, in accordance with Section [74-211](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-4/74-211) (Production uses).
