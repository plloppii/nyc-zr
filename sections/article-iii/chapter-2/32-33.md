# 32-33 Regulations for Tier C Street Frontages

The provisions of this Section, inclusive, shall apply to the **_ground floor level_** **_street_** frontage of a **_building_** along a **_Tier C street frontage_**.

The provisions for **_Tier B street frontages_**, as set forth in Section [32-32](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-2/32-32), inclusive, shall apply, except that the regulations of Section [32-322](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-2/32-322) shall not be permitted as an alternative to those of Section 32-321. Such provisions of Section [32-32](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-2/32-32), inclusive, are further modified by the provisions of this Section, inclusive
