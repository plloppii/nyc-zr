# 37-77 Maintenance

The **_building_** owner shall be responsible for the maintenance of the **_public plaza_** including, but not limited to, the location of permitted obstructions pursuant to Section [37-726](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-7/37-726), litter control, management of pigeons and rodents, maintenance of required lighting levels, and the care and replacement of furnishings and vegetation within the **_zoning lot_**.
