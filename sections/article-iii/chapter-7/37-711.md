# 37-711 Definitions

Corner public plaza

A “corner public plaza” is a **_public plaza_** that is located on an intersection of two or more **_streets_**.

Through block public plaza

A “through block public plaza” is a **_public plaza_** or portion of a **_public plaza_** that is not a **_corner public plaza_** and that connects two **_streets_** that are parallel or within 45 degrees of being parallel to each other.
