# 37-621 Elimination or reduction in size of non-bonused open area

Any existing open area for which a **_floor area_** bonus has not been utilized that occupies the same **_zoning lot_** as an existing **_plaza_**, **_residential plaza_** or **_urban plaza_**, for which a **_floor area_** bonus has been utilized, may be reduced in size or eliminated only upon certification of the Chairperson of the City Planning Commission that all bonused amenities comply with the standards under which such **_floor area_** bonus was granted.
