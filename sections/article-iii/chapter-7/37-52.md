# 37-52 Types of Pedestrian Circulation Space

The pedestrian circulation space provided shall be of one or more of the following types: an arcade, **_building_** entrance recess area, corner arcade, corner circulation space, relocation or renovation of a subway stair, sidewalk widening, **_transit volumes_** and improvements to **_mass transit stations_**, through **_block_** connection or **_public plaza_**. For the purposes of this Section, defined terms additionally include those in Section [66-11](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-6/66-11) (Definitions).

Each **_zoning lot_** shall be categorized as either a **_corner lot_**, **_through lot_** or **_interior lot_**, and pedestrian circulation space shall be provided on each **_zoning lot_** in at least one of the applicable types, or combinations of types, specified in the following table:

| Arcade | x | x | x |

| Relocation or renovation of subway stair | x | x | x |
| Sidewalk widening | x | x | x |
| **_Transit volumes_** and improvements to **_mass transit stations_** | x | x | x |
| Through **_block_** connection | x | x |  |
| **_Public plaza_** | x | x | x |

Minimum design standards for each type of pedestrian circulation space and, where applicable, the maximum amount of each type of pedestrian circulation space that may be counted toward meeting the requirements of Section [37-51](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-7/37-51) (Amount of Pedestrian Circulation Space) are set forth in Section [37-53](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-7/37-53) (Design Standards for Pedestrian Circulation Spaces).
