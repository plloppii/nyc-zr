# 37-724 Subway entrances

Where an entry to a subway station exists in the sidewalk area of a **_street_** on which a **_public plaza_** fronts and such entry is not replaced within the **_public plaza_** itself, the **_public plaza_** shall be at the same elevation as the adjacent sidewalk for a distance of at least 15 feet in all directions from the entry superstructure. Such **_public plaza_** area around a subway entry shall be free of all obstructions and may count towards the required clear area requirements as specified in Section [37-721](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-7/37-721) (Sidewalk frontage).
