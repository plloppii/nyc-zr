# 37-51 Amount of Pedestrian Circulation Space

The minimum amount of pedestrian circulation space to be provided for **_developments_** or **_enlargements_** shall be determined by the following table:

MINIMUM PEDESTRIAN CIRCULATION SPACE REQUIREMENTS

| Size of **_zoning lot_** | Required area of pedestrian circulation space |
| --- | --- |
| 5,000 to 20,000 sq. ft. | 1 sq. ft. per 350 sq. ft. of new **_floor area_** |
| Above 20,000 sq. ft. | 1 sq. ft. per 300 sq. ft. of new **_floor area_** |
