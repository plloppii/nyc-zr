# 37-623 Nighttime closings

The City Planning Commission may, upon application, authorize the closing during certain nighttime hours of an existing **_plaza_**, **_residential plaza_** or **_urban plaza_** for which a **_floor area_** bonus has been received, pursuant to Section [37-727](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-7/37-727) (Hours of access).
