# 37-43 Modification of Requirements for a Relocated or Renovated Subway Stair

The Chairperson of the City Planning Commission may, by certification to the Commissioner of Buildings, allow modifications of the requirements of Sections [32-351](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-2/32-351) (Ground floor use in high-density areas) and [37-41](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-7/37-41) (Standards for Location, Design and Hours of Public Accessibility) or [37-70](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-7/37-70) (PUBLIC PLAZAS) if the relocated subway stair cannot be accommodated without modification to these provisions.
