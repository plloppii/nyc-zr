# 36-02 Applicability of District Regulations

Except as otherwise provided in this Section, the regulations of this Chapter on permitted and required **_accessory_** off-street parking spaces and **_accessory_** bicycle parking spaces apply to **_residences_**, **_community facility_** **_uses_** or **_commercial_** **_uses_**, as set forth in the provisions of the various Sections. In addition, the regulations of this Chapter, or of specified Sections thereof, also apply in other provisions of this Resolution where they are incorporated by cross reference.
