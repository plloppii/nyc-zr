# 36-421 Maximum distance from zoning lot

C1 C2 C3 C4 C5 C6

In the districts indicated, all such spaces shall not be further than the distance set forth in the following table from the nearest boundary of the **_zoning lot_** occupied by the **_residences_** to which they are **_accessory_**.

| District | Maximum Distance from the **_Zoning Lot_** |
| --- | --- |
| C1-1 C1-2 C1-3 C2-1 C2-2 C2-3 C3 C4-1 C4-2 C4-3 | 600 feet |
| C1-4 C1-5 C1-6 C1-7 C1-8 C1-9 C2-4 C2-5 C2-6 C2-7 C2-8 C4-4 C4-5 C4-6 C4-7 C5 C6 | 1,000 feet |
