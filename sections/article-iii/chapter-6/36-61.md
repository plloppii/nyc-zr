# 36-61 Permitted Accessory Off-street Loading Berths

C1 C2 C3 C4 C5 C6 C7 C8

In all districts, as indicated, **_accessory_** off-street loading berths, open or enclosed, may be provided for all permitted **_uses_**, under rules and regulations promulgated by the Commissioner of Buildings, and subject to the provisions of Sections [36-662](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-6/36-662) (Location of access to the street), [36-663](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-6/36-663) (Restrictions on location of berths near Residence Districts), [36-664](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-6/36-664) (Surfacing) and [36-665](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-6/36-665) (Screening).
