# 36-11 General Provisions

C1 C2 C3 C4 C5 C6 C7 C8

In all districts, as indicated, except as otherwise provided in Section [85-03](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-viii/chapter-5/85-03) (Modifications of Use Regulations), **_accessory_** off-street parking spaces may be provided for all permitted **_uses_** subject to the applicable provisions set forth in Section [36-12](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-6/36-12) (Maximum Size of Accessory Group Parking Facilities). Such **_accessory_** off-street parking spaces may be open or enclosed. However, except as otherwise provided in Sections [73-47](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-3/73-47) (Roof Parking) or [74-531](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-4/74-531) (Additional parking spaces or roof parking for accessory group parking facilities), no spaces shall be located on any roof which is immediately above a **_story_** other than a **_basement_**.
