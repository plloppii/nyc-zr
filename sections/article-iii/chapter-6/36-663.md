# 36-663 Restrictions on location of berths near Residence Districts

C1 C2 C3 C4 C5 C6 C7 C8

In all districts, as indicated, where **_accessory_** off-street loading berths are located within 60 feet of a **_Residence District_** boundary, such berths shall be enclosed within a **_building_**, and no entrance to or exit from the berths onto the **_street_** shall be less than 30 feet from the district boundary.
