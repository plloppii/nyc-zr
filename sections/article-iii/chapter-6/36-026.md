# 36-026 Applicability of regulations to public parking garages and public parking lots

The following provisions of this Chapter shall apply to **_public parking garages_** and **_public parking lots_** in addition to **_accessory_** off-street parking facilities:

Section [36-462](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-6/36-462) (Restrictions on the use of parking spaces in public parking garages and public parking lots)

Section [36-524](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-6/36-524) (Calculating floor area in parking facilities with lift systems, or in automated parking facilities)

Section [36-53](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-6/36-53) (Width of Curb Cuts and Location of Access to the Street)

Section [36-54](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-6/36-54) (Surfacing)

Section [36-55](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-6/36-55) (Screening)

Section [36-57](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-6/36-57) (Parking Lot Maneuverability and Curb Cut Regulations)
