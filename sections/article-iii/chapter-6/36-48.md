# 36-48 Electric Vehicle Charging

C1 C2 C3 C4 C5 C6 C7 C8

In all districts, as indicated, electric vehicle charging facilities shall be permitted at all **_accessory_** off-street parking spaces. Such charging shall be for the owners, occupants, employees, customers, residents or visitors using such **_accessory_** parking spaces.
