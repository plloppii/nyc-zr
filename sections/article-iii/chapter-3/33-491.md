# 33-491 Limited Height Districts

C1 C2 C3 C4 C5 C6 C7 C8

In all districts, as indicated, wherever such districts are located within a **_Limited Height District_**, the maximum height of a **_building or other structure_**, or portion thereof, shall be as shown in the following table:

| **_Limited Height District_** | Maximum Height Above **_Curb Level_** or **_Base Plane_**, as Applicable |
| --- | --- |
| LH-1 | 50 feet |
| LH-1A | 60 feet |
| LH-2 | 70 feet |
| LH-3 | 100 feet |
