# 33-261 Beyond one hundred feet of a street line

C1 C2 C3 C4 C5 C6 C7 C8

In all districts, as indicated, for **_corner lots_**, and for **_zoning lots_** that are bounded by two or more **_streets_** that are neither **_corner lots_** or **_through lots_**, the portion of a **_side lot line_** beyond 100 feet of the **_street line_** that it intersects shall be considered a **_rear lot line_** and a **_rear yard_** with a minimum depth of 20 feet shall be provided where such **_rear lot line_** coincides with a **_rear lot line_** of an adjoining **_zoning lot_**.
