# 33-291 Required yards along district boundary coincident with side lot line of zoning lot in an R1, R2, R3, R4 or R5 District

C1 C2 C3 C4 C5 C6 C7 C8

In all districts, as indicated, along such portion of the boundary of a **_Commercial District_** that coincides with a **_side lot line_** of a **_zoning lot_** in an R1, R2, R3, R4 or R5 District an open area not higher than **_curb level_** shall be provided within the **_Commercial District_** with at least the width or depth set forth in the following table. Such an open area shall not be used for **_accessory_** off-street loading, or for storage or processing of any kind.

| Districts | Required Width or Depth of **_Yard_** (in feet) |
| --- | --- |
| C1 C2 C3 C4 C5 C6 C7 | 8 |
| C8 | 15 |
