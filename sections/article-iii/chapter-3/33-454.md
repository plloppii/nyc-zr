# 33-454 Towers on small lots

C1 C2 C4-4 C4-5 C4-6 C4-7 C4-8 C4-9 C4-11 C-4-12 C5 C6 C8-3 C8-4

In the districts indicated, a tower permitted under the provisions of Sections [33-451](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-3/33-451) (In certain specified Commercial Districts), [33-452](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-3/33-452) (Community facility buildings in C1 or C2 Districts when mapped within R7-2 through R12 Districts) or [33-453](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-3/33-453) (Community facility buildings in certain specified Commercial Districts) may occupy the percent of the **_lot area_** of a **_zoning lot_** set forth in the following table:

LOT COVERAGE OF TOWERS ON SMALL ZONING LOTS

| Area of **_Zoning Lot_** (in square feet) | Maximum Percent of **_Lot Coverage_** |
| --- | --- |
| 10,500 or less | 50 |
| 10,501 to 11,500 | 49 |
| 11,501 to 12,500 | 48 |
| 12,501 to 13,500 | 47 |
| 13,501 to 14,500 | 46 |
| 14,501 to 15,500 | 45 |
| 15,501 to 16,500 | 44 |
| 16,501 to 17,500 | 43 |
| 17,501 to 18,500 | 42 |
| 18,501 to 19,999 | 41 |
