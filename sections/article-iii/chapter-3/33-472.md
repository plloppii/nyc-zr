# 33-472 Community facility uses

C1 C2 C3 C4 C5 C6 C7 C8

In all districts, as indicated, for certain **_community facility_** **_uses_** in specified situations, the Board of Standards and Appeals may modify the regulations set forth in Sections [33-40](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-3/33-40) through [33-45](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-3/33-45), inclusive, relating to Height and Setback Regulations in accordance with the provisions of Section [73-64](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-3/73-64) (Modifications for community facility uses).
