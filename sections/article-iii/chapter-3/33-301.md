# 33-301 Within one hundred feet of corners

C1 C2 C3 C4 C5 C6 C7 C8

In all districts, as indicated, no **_rear yard_** shall be required within 100 feet of the point of intersection of two **_street lines_** intersecting at an angle of 135 degrees or less.
