# 33-40 HEIGHT AND SETBACK REGULATIONS

All **_buildings_** in **_Commercial Districts_** shall comply with the height and setback regulations set forth in this Section, inclusive. However, the height and setback regulations of this Section, inclusive, shall not apply in **_Commercial Districts_** mapped within, or with a **_residential equivalent_** of an R6 through R12 District with a letter suffix. In lieu thereof, the height and setback regulations of Section [23-43](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-3/23-43) (Height and Setback Requirements in R6 Through R12 Districts), inclusive, shall be applied to all **_buildings_** in accordance with the applicable **_Residence District_** regulations, pursuant to Section [33-03](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-3/33-03) (Applicability of Article II, Chapter 3 Regulations).
