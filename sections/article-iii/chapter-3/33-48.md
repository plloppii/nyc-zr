# 33-48 Special Provisions for Zoning Lots Divided by District Boundaries

C1 C2 C3 C4 C5 C6 C7 C8

In all districts, as indicated, whenever a **_zoning lot_** is divided by a boundary between districts, or is subject to other regulations resulting in different height and setback regulations, or whenever a **_zoning lot_** is divided by a boundary between a district to which the provisions of Section [33-45](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-3/33-45) (Tower Regulations) apply and a district to which such provisions do not apply, the provisions set forth in Article VII, Chapter 7, shall apply.
