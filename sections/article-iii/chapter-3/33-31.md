# 33-31 Special Provisions for Zoning Lots Divided by District Boundaries

C1 C2 C3 C4 C5 C6 C7 C8

In all districts, as indicated, whenever a **_zoning lot_** is divided by a boundary between districts with different **_yard_** regulations, the provisions set forth in Article VII, Chapter 7 shall apply.
