# 33-282 Excepted through lots

C1 C2 C3 C4-1 C8-1 C8-2 C8-3

In the districts indicated, no **_rear yard_** regulations shall apply to any **_through lots_** which extend less than 110 feet in maximum depth from **_street_** to **_street_**.
