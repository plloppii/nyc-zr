# 33-492 Height limitations for narrow buildings or enlargements

In C1 or C2 Districts mapped within, or with a **_residential equivalent_** of, R7-2, R8, R9 or R10 Districts, the provisions of Section [23-738](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-3/23-738) (Height limitations for narrow buildings) shall apply to portions of **_buildings_** with **_street walls_** less than 45 feet in width.
