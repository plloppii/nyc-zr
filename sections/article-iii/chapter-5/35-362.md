# 35-362 Special provisions for certain Community Districts

Borough of the Bronx

In C6-3 Districts without a letter suffix in Mandatory Inclusionary Housing Program Area 4, as of February 14, 2018, in Community District 1, in the Borough of the Bronx, for **_zoning lots_** containing **_qualifying affordable housing_** or **_qualifying senior housing_**, the **_floor area ratio_** shall be that for an R9-1 District set forth in Section [23-22](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-3/23-22) (Floor Area Regulations for R6 Through R12 Districts).
