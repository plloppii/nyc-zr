# 35-60 MODIFICATION OF HEIGHT AND SETBACK REGULATIONS

Height and setback regulations are set forth in Section [35-62](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-5/35-62) for **_Commercial Districts_** mapped within, or with a **_residential equivalent_** of, an R1 through R5 District and Section [35-63](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-5/35-63) inclusive, for **_Commercial Districts_** mapped within, or with a **_residential equivalent_** of, an R6 through R12 District. Special regulations governing certain areas are set forth in Section [35-64](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-5/35-64), inclusive. Permitted obstructions to maximum heights are set forth in Section [35-61](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-5/35-61).
