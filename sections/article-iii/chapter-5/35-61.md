# 35-61 Permitted Obstructions

In all **_Commercial Districts_**, the provisions of Section [33-42](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-3/33-42) (Permitted Obstructions) shall apply to any **_building or other structure_**. In addition, a dormer shall be allowed as a permitted obstruction pursuant to paragraph (b) of Section [23-413](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-3/23-413) (Permitted obstructions in certain districts).
