# 35-361 Special tower provisions

In C1 or C2 Districts mapped within R9 and R10 Districts, or in **_Commercial Districts_** with a **_residential equivalent_** of an R9 or R10 District, for **_zoning lots_** containing a **_building_** that is **_developed_** or **_enlarged_** pursuant to the applicable tower regulations of paragraph (c) of Section [35-632](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-5/35-632), the provisions of Section [23-241](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-3/23-241) (Special tower provisions) shall apply:

(a) to only the **_residential_** portion of a **_building_** where less than 75 percent of the total **_floor area_** of such **_building_** is allocated to **_residential use_**; and

(b) to the entire **_building_** where 75 percent or more of the total **_floor area_** of such **_building_** is allocated to **_residential use_**.
