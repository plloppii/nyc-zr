# 35-71 Planting

The provisions of Section [23-613](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-3/23-613) (Front yard planting requirements) shall apply, except that plantings shall additionally not be required in the area of the **_zoning lot_** between the **_street line_** and any portion of **_ground floor level_** **_street walls_** allocated to non-residential **_uses_** with no sleeping accommodations.
