# 35-31 Maximum Floor Area Ratio

C1 C2 C3 C4 C5 C6

In the districts indicated, the provisions of this Section shall apply to any **_zoning lot_** subject to the provisions of this Chapter.

The maximum **_floor area ratio_** permitted for a **_commercial_** or **_community facility_** **_use_** shall be as set forth in Article III, Chapter 3, and the maximum **_floor area ratio_** permitted for a **_residential use_** shall be as set forth in Article II, Chapter 3, except as set forth in Section [35-30](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-5/35-30) (APPLICABILITY OF FLOOR AREA AND OPEN SPACE REGULATIONS), inclusive. The total of all such **_floor area ratios_** shall not exceed the greatest **_floor area ratio_** permitted for any such **_use_** on the **_zoning lot_**, except where explicitly stated otherwise. Special rules for certain areas are set forth in Section [35-36](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-5/35-36) (Special Floor Area Ratio and Related Bulk Provisions for Certain Areas).

Where **_floor area_** in a **_building_** is shared by multiple **_uses_**, the **_floor area_** for such shared portion shall be attributed to each **_use_** proportionately, based on the percentage each **_use_** occupies of the total **_floor area_** of the **_zoning lot_** less any shared **_floor area_**.

A non-**_residential use_** occupying a portion of a **_building_** that was in existence on December 15, 1961, may be changed to a **_residential use_** and the regulations on maximum **_floor area ratio_** shall not apply to such change of **_use_**.
