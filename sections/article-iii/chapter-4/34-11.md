# 34-11 General Provisions

C1 C2 C3 C4 C5 C6

In the districts indicated, the **_bulk_** regulations of Article II, Chapter 3, shall apply to all **_residential buildings_** in accordance with the provisions of this Section, except as modified by the provisions of Sections [34-21](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-4/34-21) through [34-24](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-4/34-24), relating to exceptions to applicability of **_Residence District_** controls.
