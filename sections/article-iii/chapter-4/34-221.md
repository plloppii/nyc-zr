# 34-221 Maximum floor area ratio

C1 C2 C3 C4 C5 C6

In the districts indicated, the maximum **_floor area ratio_** on a **_zoning lot_** shall be the applicable maximum **_floor area ratio_** permitted pursuant to the provisions of Article II, Chapter 3, except as provided for in the following Sections:

Section [34-223](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-4/34-223) (Floor area bonus for a public plaza)

Section [34-224](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-4/34-224) (Floor area bonus for an arcade)

However, for **_Commercial Districts_** with a **_residential equivalent_** of an R10 or R11 District with a letter suffix, no **_floor area_** bonuses for **_public plazas_** or **_arcades_** shall be permitted.
