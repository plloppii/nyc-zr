# 34-21 General Provisions

C1 C2 C3 C4 C5 C6

In the districts indicated, the **_bulk_** regulations applicable to **_residential buildings_** as set forth in Section [34-11](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-4/34-11) (General Provisions) are modified by the provisions of Sections [34-22](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-4/34-22) (Modification of Floor Area Regulations), [34-23](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-4/34-23) (Modification of Yard Regulations) and [34-24](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-4/34-24) (Modification of Height and Setback Regulations). The purpose of these modifications is to make the regulations set forth in Article II, Chapter 3, applicable to **_Commercial Districts_**.
