# 34-24 Modification of Height and Setback Regulations

C1 C2 C3 C4 C5 C6

In the districts indicated, the height and setback regulations set forth in Article II, Chapter 3, and made applicable to such districts in Section [34-11](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-4/34-11) (General Provisions), are modified as set forth in this Section.

(a) In Commercial Districts with R1 through R5 equivalency
    In **_Commercial Districts_** mapped within, or with a **_residential equivalent_** of an R1 through R5 District, the modifications to **_residential_** height and setback regulations set forth in Section [35-62](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-5/35-62) shall be applied.
(b) In Commercial Districts with R6 through R12 equivalency
    In **_Commercial Districts_** mapped within, or with a **_residential equivalent_** of R6 through R12 Districts:
    (1) the modifications to **_residential_** height and setback regulations set forth in Section [35-63](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-5/35-63), inclusive, shall be applied;
    (2) the special height and setback provisions for certain areas set forth in Section [36-64](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-6/36-64) shall be applied; and
    (3) where the optional **_bulk_** regulations for **_sky exposure plane buildings_** are utilized, the provisions set forth in Section [35-71](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-5/35-71), inclusive, shall be applied. Where any of such optional provisions are utilized, all the other associated **_bulk_** provisions shall apply.
