# 34-224 Floor area bonus for an arcade

C4-6 C4-7 C4-11 C4-12 C5-1 C5-2 C5-4 C6-4 C6-5 C6-8 C6-11 C6-12

In the districts indicated, for each square foot of **_arcade_** provided in accordance with the provisions of Section [37-80](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-7/37-80) (ARCADES), the total **_floor area_** permitted on that **_zoning lot_** under the provisions of Section [23-22](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-3/23-22) (Floor Area Regulations for R6 Through R12 Districts) may be increased by three square feet.
