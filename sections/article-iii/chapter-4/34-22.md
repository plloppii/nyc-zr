# 34-22 Modification of Floor Area Regulations

C1 C2 C3 C4 C5 C6

In the districts indicated, the **_floor area_** and **_open space_** regulations as set forth in Section [23-20](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-3/23-20) (FLOOR AREA REGULATIONS), inclusive, and made applicable to such districts in Section [34-11](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-4/34-11) (General Provisions), are modified as set forth in this Section.
