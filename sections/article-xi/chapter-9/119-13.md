# 119-13 Administration of Tier I Requirements

No permanent certificate of occupancy shall be issued by the Department of Buildings unless an inspection report, verifying that the requirements of Section [119-10](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-xi/chapter-9/119-10) (PROVISIONS REGULATING TIER I SITES) have been met, is filed with the Department of Buildings by a registered landscape architect, licensed surveyor, registered architect or professional engineer.
