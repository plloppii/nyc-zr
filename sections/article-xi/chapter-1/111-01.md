# 111-01 Definitions

For purposes of this Chapter, matter in italics is defined in Section [12-10](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-i/chapter-2/12-10) (DEFINITIONS) and in this Section.

Special Tribeca Mixed Use District

(repeated from Section [12-10](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-i/chapter-2/12-10))

The "Special Tribeca Mixed Use District" is a Special Purpose District designated by the letters "TMU" in which special regulations set forth in Article XI, Chapter 1, apply. The **_Special Tribeca Mixed Use District_** and its regulations supplement or supersede those of the districts on which it is superimposed.
