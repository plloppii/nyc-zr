# 111-22 Bulk Regulations for Area A2

The underlying regulations applicable to a C6-3 District shall apply to **_developments_** and **_enlargements_**, except as set forth herein.

(a) Maximum **_floor area ratio_**
    In no case shall the **_floor area ratio_** of the **_commercial_** or **_community facility_** portion of the **_building_** be more than 6.0.
    For **_developments_** or **_enlargements_** on **_qualifying transit improvement sites_**, a **_floor area_** bonus for **_mass transit station_** improvements may be granted by the City Planning Commission pursuant to the provisions of Section [66-51](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-6/66-51) (Additional Floor Area for Mass Transit Station Improvements). For the purposes of this paragraph, defined terms additionally include those in Section [66-11](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-6/66-11) (Definitions). No other **_floor area_** bonuses shall be permitted in Area A2.
(b) Height and setback regulations
    The underlying height and setback regulations applicable to a C6-3A District shall apply.
(c) Curb cuts
    Curb cuts shall not be permitted on Greenwich Street, Murray Street and Chambers Street.
