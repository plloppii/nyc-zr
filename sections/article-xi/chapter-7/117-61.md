# 117-61 General Provisions

In the Dutch Kills Subdistrict, the **_use_**, #bulk, and parking regulations of the underlying districts shall apply, except as modified by the provisions of Section [117-60](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-xi/chapter-7/117-60), inclusive.
