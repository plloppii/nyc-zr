# 117-522 Floor area regulations

The maximum **_floor area ratio_** permitted for **_commercial_**, **_community facility_**, **_manufacturing_** or **_residential uses_**, separately or in combination, is specified in the following table:

MAXIMUM FLOOR AREA RATIO FOR ALL USES

| Area | Maximum **_Floor Area Ratio_** |
| --- | --- |
| A-1 A-2 | 12.0 C, M, CF or R |
| B | 8.0 C, M, CF or R |
| C | 5.0 C, M, CF or R |

C=Commercial

M=Manufacturing

CF=Community Facility

R=Residential

However, for **_qualifying affordable housing_** or **_qualifying senior housing_**, the maximum **_residential_** **_floor area ratio_** shall be 9.6 in Area B, and 6.0 in Area C.
