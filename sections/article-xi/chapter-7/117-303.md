# 117-303 Applicability of Article VI, Chapter 2

In the Northern Hunters Point Waterfront Subdistrict, all **_zoning lots_** in M1 Districts paired with a **_Residence District_** without a letter suffix shall be considered **_waterfront zoning lots_** for the purposes of applying the height and setback regulations of Section [62-343](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-2/62-343) (Height and setback regulations in other medium- and high-density districts), as modified by the provisions of Section [117-34](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-xi/chapter-7/117-34) (Special Height and Setback Regulations), inclusive. Such height and setback provisions, along with other applicable **_bulk_** regulations, may be modified by special permit of the City Planning Commission on such **_waterfront zoning lots_** pursuant to Section [62-837](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-2/62-837) (Bulk modifications on waterfront blocks).

In addition, all **_zoning lots_** under common ownership that are contiguous or would be contiguous but for a **_street_** established after November 12, 2025, shall be considered **_waterfront zoning lots_** for the purposes of applying the provisions of Section [62-50](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-2/62-50) (GENERAL REQUIREMENTS FOR VISUAL CORRIDORS AND WATERFRONT PUBLIC ACCESS AREAS), inclusive, as modified by Section [117-36](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-xi/chapter-7/117-36) (Northern Hunters Point Waterfront Access Plan), inclusive.
