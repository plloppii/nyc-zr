# 117-213 Sign regulations

In M1 Districts without a letter suffix paired with an R9 or R10 District, the **_sign_** regulations applicable to the Queens Plaza Subdistrict set forth in Section [117-513](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-xi/chapter-7/117-513) may be applied.
