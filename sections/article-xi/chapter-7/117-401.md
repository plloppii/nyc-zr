# 117-401 General provisions

In the Court Square Subdistrict, the **_use_**, #bulk, and parking regulations of the underlying districts shall apply, except as modified by the provisions of Section [117-40](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-xi/chapter-7/117-40) (COURT SQUARE SUBDISTRICT), inclusive.

Mandatory subway improvements are elements of the Subdistrict Plan, which shall be built by the developer of the **_zoning lot_** to which they apply.
