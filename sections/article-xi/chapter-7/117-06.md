# 117-06 Applicability of Article XII, Chapter 3

In the **_Special Long Island City Mixed Use District_**, M1 Districts are paired with a **_Residence District_**. In paired districts, the special **_use_**, **_bulk_** and parking and loading provisions of Article XII, Chapter 3 (Special Mixed Use District) shall apply, except where modified by the provisions of this Chapter. For the purposes of applying the provisions of this Chapter, such provisions shall be considered the underlying district regulations.

Notwithstanding the provisions of Section [123-10](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-xii/chapter-3/123-10) (GENERAL PROVISIONS), in the event of a conflict between the provisions of this Chapter and the provisions of Article XII, Chapter 3, the provisions of this Chapter shall control.
