# 117-63 Special Bulk Regulations in the Designated Districts

Maximum **_floor area ratio_** and **_street wall_** provisions in the designated districts are modified as set forth in Sections [117-631](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-xi/chapter-7/117-631) and [117-632](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-xi/chapter-7/117-632), respectively.
