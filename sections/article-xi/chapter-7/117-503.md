# 117-503 Definitions

Definitions specifically applicable to the Queens Plaza Subdistrict of the **_Special Long Island City Mixed Use District_** are set forth in this Section. Other defined terms are set forth in Section [117-01](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-xi/chapter-7/117-01) (Definitions) of the **_Special Long Island City Mixed Use District_** and Section [12-10](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-i/chapter-2/12-10) (DEFINITIONS).

Accessory use

In addition to those **_accessory uses_** listed in Section [12-10](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-i/chapter-2/12-10), for the purposes of this Section, an **_accessory use_** shall also include a **_dwelling unit_** in connection with any **_commercial_**, **_manufacturing_** or **_community facility_** establishment permitted in the Queens Plaza Subdistrict of the **_Special Long Island City Mixed Use District_**, pursuant to Section [117-51](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-xi/chapter-7/117-51) (Queens Plaza Subdistrict Special Use Regulations), provided that no more than one such unit shall be permitted in connection with any establishment, and provided further that each such unit shall not exceed a gross area of 1,200 square feet or contain more than one kitchen.

Home occupation

Within the Queens Plaza Subdistrict, the **_home occupation_** provisions of Section [12-10](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-i/chapter-2/12-10) shall apply, except that a **_home occupation_** may occupy more than 1,000 square feet of **_floor area_** and up to three persons not residing in the **_dwelling unit_** or **_rooming unit_** may be employed.
