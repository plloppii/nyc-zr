# 117-50 QUEENS PLAZA SUBDISTRICT

In the **_Special Long Island City Mixed Use District_**, the special regulations of Section [117-50](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-xi/chapter-7/117-50), inclusive, shall apply within the Queens Plaza Subdistrict.
