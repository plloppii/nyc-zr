# 117-34 Special Height and Setback Regulations

In **_Manufacturing Districts_**, the underlying height and setback regulations shall apply.

In M1 Districts paired with a **_Residence District_** with a letter suffix, the provisions of Section [123-65](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-xii/chapter-3/123-65) (Special Height and Setback Regulations in Special Mixed Use Districts With R6 Through R12 District Designations), inclusive, shall apply, except as modified by the provisions of this Section, inclusive.

In other districts, the underlying height and setback regulations shall apply, except as modified by the provisions of this Section, inclusive.
