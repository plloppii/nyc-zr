# 117-32 Special Floor Area Regulations

The **_floor area_** regulations of the underlying districts shall apply, except as modified by the provisions of this Section, inclusive.

For the purpose of applying the special **_floor area ratio_** provisions of this Subdistrict, six Subareas are designated. The locations of these subareas are shown on Map 1 (Subdistrict Plan Map and Designated Subareas) in Appendix C to this Chapter. Outside of a Subarea, the underlying **_floor area_** regulations shall apply.
