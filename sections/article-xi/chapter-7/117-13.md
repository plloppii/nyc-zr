# 117-13 Street Wall Location Modifications

R6B M1-4/R6A M1-4/R7A M1-5/R8A M1-4/R6B M1-5/R7X

(a) In the districts indicated, the **_street wall_** location provisions of paragraph (a) Section [23-431](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-3/23-431) shall apply.
(b) In C1 and C2 Districts
    For C1 or C2 Districts, the **_street wall_** location provisions of paragraph (a) Section [35-631](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-5/35-631) shall apply.
