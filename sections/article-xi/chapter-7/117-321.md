# 117-321 Maximum floor area ratio

The maximum **_floor area ratio_** permitted for **_commercial_**, **_community facility_**, **_manufacturing_**, or **_residential uses_** in Subareas A through F is specified in the following table:

MAXIMUM FLOOR AREA RATIO FOR ALL USES

| Subarea | **_Residential_** **_Floor Area Ratio_** for Standard **_Residences_** | **_Residential_** **_Floor Area Ratio_** for **_Qualifying Affordable Housing_** or **_Qualifying Senior Housing_** | **_Community Facility_** **_Floor Area Ratio_** | **_Commercial_** or **_Manufacturing_** **_Floor Area Ratio_** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A | 8.42 | 10.10 | 10.0 | 8.0 |
| B | 6.00 | 7.20 | 7.2 | 6.5 |
| C1 | 6.45 | 7.74 | 7.2 | 4.0 |
| C2 | 6.50 | 7.80 | 7.2 | 4.0 |
| D | 7.02 | 8.42 | 8.0 | 8.0 |
| E | 6.00 | 7.20 | 4.0 | 6.5 |
| F | 5.00 | 6.00 | 4.0 | 4.0 |
