# 117-322 Floor area bonus for active recreation space

In R9 Districts and in M1 Districts paired with an R9 District, for **_zoning lots_** with a minimum **_lot area_** of 40,000 square feet or more, for each square foot of publicly accessible, supplemental open area provided in accordance with Section [117-365](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-xi/chapter-7/117-365) (Requirements for supplemental open spaces and amenities), inclusive, the maximum **_floor area_** permitted on the **_zoning lot_** may be increased by six square feet, provided that the resulting bonus **_floor area ratio_** shall not exceed 0.6.
