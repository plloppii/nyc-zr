# 117-523 Floor area bonuses

Any **_floor area_** bonus for a **_public plaza_** or **_arcade_** permitted under the regulations of the designated **_Residence_** or M1 District shall not apply in the Queens Plaza Subdistrict.

For **_developments_** or **_enlargements_** on **_qualifying transit improvement sites_**, a **_floor area_** bonus for **_mass transit station_** improvements may be granted by the City Planning Commission pursuant to the provisions of Section [66-51](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-6/66-51) (Additional Floor Area for Mass Transit Station Improvements). For the purposes of this paragraph, defined terms additionally include those in Section [66-11](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-6/66-11) (Definitions).
