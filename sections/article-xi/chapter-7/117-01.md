# 117-01 Definitions

Definitions specifically applicable to this Chapter are set forth in this Section. Other defined terms are set forth in Section [12-10](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-i/chapter-2/12-10) (DEFINITIONS), Section [32-301](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-2/32-301) (Definitions) and Section [66-11](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-6/66-11) (Definitions). Additional defined terms specific to certain Subdistricts are set forth in Sections [117-361](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-xi/chapter-7/117-361) and 117-503.

Mixed use building or development

For the purposes of this Chapter, a "mixed use building" or a “mixed use development” shall be any **_building_** or **_development_** used partly for **_residential use_** and partly for **_community facility_**, **_commercial_** or **_manufacturing_** **_use_**.
