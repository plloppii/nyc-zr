# 117-301 General provisions

In the Northern Hunters Point Waterfront Subdistrict, the **_use_**, **_bulk_**, and parking regulations of the underlying districts shall apply, except as modified by the provisions of Section [117-30](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-xi/chapter-7/117-30) (NORTHERN HUNTERS POINT WATERFRONT SUBDISTRICT), inclusive.
