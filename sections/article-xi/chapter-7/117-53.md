# 117-53 Height and Setback and Street Wall Location Regulations

The height and setback regulations of the designated **_Residence_** and M1 Districts shall not apply, except for permitted obstructions pursuant to Sections [23-41](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-3/23-41) or [43-42](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iv/chapter-3/43-42), as applicable. In lieu thereof, all **_buildings or other structures_** shall comply with the regulations set forth in Sections [117-531](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-xi/chapter-7/117-531) (Street wall location) and [117-532](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-xi/chapter-7/117-532) (Setback regulations for buildings that exceed the maximum base height). Such regulations, however, shall not apply along the **_street frontage_** of that portion of a **_zoning lot_** occupied by existing **_buildings_**, unless the **_street walls_** of such existing **_buildings_** are vertically extended by more than 15 feet. The height of all **_buildings or other structures_** shall be measured from the **_base plane_**.
