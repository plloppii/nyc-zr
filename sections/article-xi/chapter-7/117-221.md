# 117-221 Special floor area regulations in certain paired districts

In M1 Districts paired with an R9 or R10 District, the following maximum **_floor area ratios_** shall apply:

MAXIMUM FLOOR AREA RATIO FOR ALL USES

|  | **_Residential_** **_Floor Area Ratio_** for Standard **_Residences_** | **_Residential Floor Area Ratio_** for **_Qualifying Affordable Housing_** or **_Qualifying Senior Housing_** | **_Community Facility_** **_Floor Area Ratio_** | **_Commercial_** or **_Manufacturing_** **_Floor Area Ratio_** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| M1-6/ R9 | 8.0 | 9.6 | 10.0 | 15.0 |
| M1-6/ R10 | 10.0 | 12.0 | 12.0 | 12.0 |

Such maximum **_floor area_** may be increased where a **_public plaza_** is provided pursuant to 117-223.
