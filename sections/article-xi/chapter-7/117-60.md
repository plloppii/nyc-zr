# 117-60 DUTCH KILLS SUBDISTRICT

In the **_Special Long Island City Mixed Use District_**, the special regulations of Section [117-60](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-xi/chapter-7/117-60), inclusive, shall apply within the Dutch Kills Subdistrict.
