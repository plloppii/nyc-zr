# 116-52 Waterfront Public Access Signage

The New York City Waterfront Symbol Plaque shall be used to direct the public to waterfront public access areas including the **_Esplanade_** and **_upland connections_** within Subarea B, **_Pier Place_** and the **_Cove_**, and to identify the entry points of these areas. Such signage shall be provided in accordance with requirements of Section 62-654.
