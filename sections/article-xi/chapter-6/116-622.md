# 116-622 Required yards

The special **_yard_** provisions of [62-332](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-2/62-332) (Rear yards and waterfront yards) shall apply, except the 40 foot minimum depth requirement for a **_waterfront yard_** may be reduced by up to five feet, to a minimum depth of 35 feet, along those portions of the landward edge of the stabilized shore, bulkhead or natural **_shoreline_** where the depth of the landward portions of the **_zoning lot_** is less than 150 feet, as measured perpendicular and landward from such edge.
