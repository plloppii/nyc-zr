# 116-60 SPECIAL REGULATIONS IN SUBAREA E

The special **_use_**, **_bulk_**, **_visual corridor_** and **_waterfront public access area_** requirements of Section [116-60](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-xi/chapter-6/116-60), inclusive, shall apply to Subarea E.
