# 116-20 SPECIAL BULK REGULATIONS FOR SUBAREAS A, B AND C, THE ESPLANADE, PIER PLACE AND THE COVE

The special **_bulk_** regulations of Section [116-20](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-xi/chapter-6/116-20), inclusive, shall apply to Subareas A, B and C, the **_Esplanade_**, **_Pier Place_** and the **_Cove_**.
