# 116-62 Special Bulk Regulations

The **_bulk_** regulations of Article VI, Chapter 2 (Special Regulations Applying in the Waterfront Area) shall apply, except as modified in Section [116-62](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-xi/chapter-6/116-62), inclusive.
