# 116-633 Phased development of Waterfront Public Access Area

For the purposes of applying for an authorization for phased **_development_** of a **_waterfront public access area_** in paragraph (c)(1) of Section [62-822](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-2/62-822) (Modification of waterfront public access area and visual corridor requirements), the **_lot area_** shall be the portion of the **_zoning lot_** above water.
