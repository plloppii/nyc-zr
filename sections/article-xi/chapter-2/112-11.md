# 112-11 Special Height and Setback Regulations

The underlying height and setback regulations shall apply, except that no **_building or other structure_** shall exceed a height limit of 35 feet, and the height shall be measured from the **_base plane_**. Such height and setback regulations may be modified only by authorization or special permit of the City Planning Commission, as applicable, pursuant to Sections [112-12](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-xi/chapter-2/112-12) or [112-13](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-xi/chapter-2/112-13).
