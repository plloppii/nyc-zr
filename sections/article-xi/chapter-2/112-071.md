# 112-071 Uses permitted in C1 and C2 Districts

Within C1 and C2 Districts, the underlying **_use_** provisions shall apply, except that:

(a) **_uses_** listed under Use Group IV(B) shall not be permitted; and
(b) **_uses_** listed under Use Group X with a size limitation, as denoted with an “S” in the Use Group table set forth in Section [32-20](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-2/32-20) (Use Group X – Production Uses), inclusive, shall not exceed 10,000 square feet of **_floor area_** per establishment.
