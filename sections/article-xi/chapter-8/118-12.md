# 118-12 Sign Regulations

On **_street walls_** fronting on 14th Street, no **_sign_** may be located more than 25 feet above **_curb level_**.

**_Signs_** on **_street walls_** fronting on all other **_streets_** within the Special District shall be subject to the provisions of Section [32-672](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-2/32-672) (Special provisions for high density areas)

**_Flashing signs_** are not permitted within the Special District.
