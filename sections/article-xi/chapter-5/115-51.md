# 115-51 Special Permit to Modify Use or Bulk Regulations

For any **_zoning lot_** within the **_Special Downtown Jamaica District_**, the City Planning Commission may permit modification of the **_use_** or **_bulk_** regulations, except **_floor area ratio_** provisions, provided the Commission shall find that such:

(a) **_use_** or **_bulk_** modification will aid in achieving the general purposes and intent of the Special District;

(b) **_use_** modification will encourage a lively pedestrian environment along the **_street_** or mandatory sidewalk widening, or is necessary for, and the only practicable way to achieve, the programmatic requirements of the development;

(c) **_bulk_** modifications will enhance the distribution of **_bulk_** on the **_zoning lot_**;

(d) **_bulk_** modifications will permit adequate access of light and air to surrounding **_streets_** and properties; and

(e) **_use_** or **_bulk_** modification will relate harmoniously to the character of the surrounding area.

The Commission may prescribe appropriate conditions and safeguards to minimize adverse effects on the character of the surrounding area.
