# 115-20 SPECIAL BULK REGULATIONS

The underlying **_bulk_** provisions shall apply except as modified in this Section, inclusive.

For the purposes of applying the provisions of this Section, the **_residential equivalent_** for a C6-3 District shall be an R9-1 District in the Core Subdistrict.
