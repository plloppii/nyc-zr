# 63-022 Special Purpose Districts where regulations for FRESH food stores are not applicable

The provisions of this Chapter shall not apply to the following Special Purpose Districts:

**_Special Madison Avenue Preservation District_**;

**_Special Manhattanville Mixed Use District_**; and

**_Special Park Improvement District_**.
