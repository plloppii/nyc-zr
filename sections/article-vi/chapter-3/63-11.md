# 63-11 Special Use Regulations for FRESH Food Stores in M1 Districts

In M1 Districts, the regulations of Section [42-162](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iv/chapter-2/42-162) (Use Group VI – uses subject to size limitations) are modified to permit **_FRESH food stores_** with up to 30,000 square feet of **_floor area_**. The provisions of this Section shall not apply where the regulations of the underlying district permit grocery and convenience retailer or specialty food retailer, listed under Use Group VI, with **_floor area_** greater than 30,000 square feet.
