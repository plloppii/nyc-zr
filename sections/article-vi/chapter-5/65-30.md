# 65-30 SPECIAL USE REGULATIONS

The following modifications to **_use_** regulations set forth in this Section, inclusive, may be applied to **_zoning lots_** within **_designated recovery areas_**, as set forth in Section [65-01](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-5/65-01) (Applicability of Article VI, Chapter 5).
