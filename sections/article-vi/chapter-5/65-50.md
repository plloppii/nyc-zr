# 65-50 SPECIAL DOCUMENTATION PROVISIONS

The following modifications to documentation procedures may be applied to **_zoning lots_** within **_designated recovery areas_**, as set forth in Section [65-01](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-5/65-01) (Applicability of Article VI, Chapter 5).
