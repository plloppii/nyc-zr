# 62-131 Applicability of Article VII, Chapter 3

The following special permits by the Board of Standards and Appeals shall not be applicable within **_waterfront blocks_**:

Section [73-64](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-3/73-64) (Modifications for Community Facility Uses)

Section [73-68](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-3/73-68) (Height and Setback and Yard Modifications).

The following special permits by the Board of Standards and Appeals shall be applicable on **_waterfront blocks_** only as modified in the following Sections:

Section [73-13](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-3/73-13) (Community Facilities) shall be applicable, except that:

(a) all references to **_floor area ratio_** shall be modified in accordance with the provisions of Sections [62-32](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-2/62-32) and [62-333](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-2/62-333) pertaining to **_floor area ratio_** and **_lot coverage_**, which provisions the Board may modify pursuant to Section [73-12](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-3/73-12); and
(b) all findings involving the amount and distribution of **_open space_** shall be made using the open area of the lot resulting from the maximum **_lot coverage_** set forth in Section [62-333](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-2/62-333); and

Section [73-45](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-3/73-45) (Modification of Off-site Parking Provisions); and

Section [73-47](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-3/73-47) (Roof Parking) shall be applicable, except for those provisions expressly modified by Section [62-40](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-2/62-40) (SPECIAL PARKING AND LOADING REGULATIONS).
