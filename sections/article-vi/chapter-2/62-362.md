# 62-362 Special floor area, lot coverage and residential density distribution regulations

Within any parcel identified in Section [62-931](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-2/62-931) (Waterfront Access Plan BK1: Greenpoint-Williamsburg), and with respect to any such parcels that are adjacent to each other and that are under single-fee ownership and with respect to which each party having any interest therein is a party in interest (as defined in paragraph (e) of the definition of a **_zoning lot_** in Section [12-10](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-i/chapter-2/12-10)) or with respect to which each party in interest (as defined in paragraph (f)(4) of the definition of a **_zoning lot_** in Section [12-10](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-i/chapter-2/12-10)) has executed a declaration declaring that the properties are to be developed as a single parcel or has waived its right to execute such declaration, the total **_lot coverage_** permitted pursuant to Section [62-333](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-2/62-333), the total **_floor area_** permitted pursuant to Sections [62-361](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-2/62-361) or [62-362](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-2/62-362) and the **_residential_** density permitted pursuant to Section [23-50](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-3/23-50), may be located anywhere within such parcel or between such parcels without regard to **_zoning lot_** **_lines_** or district boundaries provided that such location of **_floor area_**, **_lot coverage_** or **_residential_** density complies with Sections [62-31](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-2/62-31) (Bulk Computations on Waterfront Lots) and [62-34](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-2/62-34) (Height and Setback Regulations on Waterfront Blocks), as modified by Sections [62-363](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-2/62-363) and [32-364](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-2/32-364) (Special height and setback regulations)
