# 62-334 Maximum lot coverage for residences in Commercial Districts

In **_Commercial Districts_**, for **_residential_** **_buildings_**, or the **_residential_** portion of **_mixed buildings_**, the maximum **_lot coverage_** regulations of Section [62-333](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-2/62-333) (Maximum lot coverage in Residence Districts) shall apply.
