**_Floor area_** regulations for **_zoning lots_** within **_waterfront blocks_** are set forth in:

Section [62-321](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-2/62-321) for R1 through R5 Districts

Section [62-322](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-2/62-322) for R6 through R12 Districts

Section [62-323](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-2/62-323) for Community Facility and Commercial uses in Residence Districts

Section [62-324](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-2/62-324) for Commercial Districts

Section [62-325](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-2/62-325) for Manufacturing Districts

Section [62-326](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-2/62-326) for **_mixed use buildings_** in a **_Special Mixed Use District_**
