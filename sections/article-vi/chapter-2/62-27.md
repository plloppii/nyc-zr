# 62-27 Special Use Regulations for Playgrounds or Private Parks

Playgrounds and private parks shall be a permitted **_use_** in M2 and M3 Districts within the **_waterfront area_** in Community Districts 1, 2 and 4 in the Borough of Manhattan.
