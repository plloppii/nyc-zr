# 62-42 Special Regulations for Accessory Non-residential Parking Facilities

The applicable district regulations pertaining to permitted or required off-street parking facilities **_accessory_** to non-**_residential uses_** shall apply to all **_developments_** on **_waterfront blocks_** except as modified in this Section or in Section [62-45](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-2/62-45).
