# 62-65 Public Access Design Reference Standards

The standards of this Section, inclusive, shall be applicable to all **_waterfront public access areas_** and **_visual corridors_**.

No hollow plastic material, such as PVC (polyvinyl chloride) or similar material shall be permitted in guardrails, fences, seating, trash receptacles or other similar furniture within a **_waterfront public access area_**. However, high-density polyethylene shall be permitted.
