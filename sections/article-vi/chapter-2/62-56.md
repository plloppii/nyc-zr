# 62-56 Requirements for Upland Connections

All **_waterfront public access areas_** provided pursuant to Sections [62-53](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-2/62-53) (Requirements for Shore Public Walkways), [62-54](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-2/62-54) (Requirements for Public Access on Piers) or [62-55](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-2/62-55) (Requirements for Public Access on Floating Structures) shall be accessible from an open **_street_**, **_public park_** or other public place at intervals along the **_shore public walkway_** not to exceed 600 feet by means of either a direct connection or, where no direct connection exists, an **_upland connection_** between the **_shore public walkway_** and an adjoining public sidewalk within an open **_street_**, **_public park_** or other public place.

Where there is an intervening **_zoning lot_** that would prevent compliance with the maximum interval, such interval may be increased to the minimum necessary to clear the intervening **_zoning lot_**. In the event there is no way to provide the **_upland connection_** without encroaching on an intervening **_zoning lot_**, the **_upland connection_** shall not be required.
