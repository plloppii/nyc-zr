# 62-30 SPECIAL BULK REGULATIONS

All **_zoning lots_** within **_waterfront blocks_** shall comply with the **_bulk_** regulations of this Section. For the purposes of this Section, non-**_waterfront blocks_** included in Waterfront Access Plan BK-1 shall be considered to be **_waterfront blocks_**. Existing **_non-complying_** **_buildings or other structures_** shall be subject to the provisions of Article V (Non-conforming Uses and Non-complying Buildings).

A change of **_use_** involving a **_building or other structure_** lawfully existing on October 25, 1993, shall be permitted in accordance with the applicable district **_use_** regulations, as modified by the provisions of this Chapter. Any **_non-compliances_** created with respect to the provisions of this Section due solely to the change of **_use_** shall be deemed to be existing **_non-compliances_**. However, no **_enlargement_** or other alteration of such **_buildings or other structures_** may be made which would either create a new **_non-compliance_** or increase the degree of **_non-compliance_** with respect to the provisions of this Section.

Modification of the **_bulk_** regulations of Sections [62-31](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-2/62-31), [62-32](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-2/62-32), [62-33](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-2/62-33), [62-341](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-2/62-341), [62-342](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-2/62-342), [62-343](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-2/62-343) or [62-35](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-2/62-35) shall only be allowed by authorization or special permit of the City Planning Commission pursuant to Sections [62-837](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-2/62-837), [74-711](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-4/74-711), [74-74](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-4/74-74), [74-79](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-4/74-79), [75-24](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-5/75-24), [78-00](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-8/78-00) or [79-00](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-9/79-00), as applicable.

Provisions for modification of the **_bulk_** regulations on **_piers_** and **_floating structures_** are set forth in Sections [62-344](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-2/62-344) and 62-345.

In no event shall any **_bulk_** modification include modification of the provisions of paragraph (a) of Section [62-31](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-2/62-31) or the maximum **_floor area ratio_** for the applicable district set forth in Sections [62-321](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-2/62-321) and 62-322.
