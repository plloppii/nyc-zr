# 64-221 Measurement of height

In all districts, as an alternative to measuring heights from **_base plane_**, **_curb level_**, or other applicable datum, all height measurements in **_flood zones_**, including the number of **_stories_** permitted, as applicable, may be measured from the **_reference plane_**. However, this provision shall not apply to **_signs_** not affixed to **_buildings or other structures_**.
