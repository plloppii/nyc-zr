# 64-42 Special Parking Regulations for Flood-resistant Buildings

The provisions of this Section, inclusive, are optional, and are only applicable to **_zoning lots_** located wholly or partially within **_flood zones_**. The provisions of this Section, inclusive, may apply to **_zoning lots_** containing **_flood-resistant buildings_**.
