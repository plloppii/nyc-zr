# 64-12 Applicability

The optional provisions of this Chapter shall apply to **_zoning lots_** located wholly or partially within **_flood zones_**, as follows:

(a) For all **_zoning lots_** in the **_flood zone_**
    The provisions of Sections [64-31](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-4/64-31) (Special Bulk Regulations for All Buildings) and [64-41](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-4/64-41) (Special Parking Regulations for All Buildings), inclusive, may be applied to all **_zoning lots_**, regardless of whether **_buildings or other structures_** on such **_zoning lots_** comply with **_flood-resistant construction standards_**.
(b) For **_zoning lots_** containing **_flood-resistant buildings_**
    The provisions of Sections [64-20](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-4/64-20) (SPECIAL USE REGULATIONS), [64-32](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-4/64-32) (Special Bulk Regulations for Flood-resistant Buildings), [64-42](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-4/64-42) (Special Parking Regulations for Flood-resistant Buildings), and [64-60](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-4/64-60) (SPECIAL REGULATIONS FOR NON-CONFORMING USES AND NON-COMPLYING BUILDINGS), inclusive, may be applied only to **_zoning lots_** containing **_flood-resistant buildings_**, including **_cottage envelope buildings_**, as applicable, and Section [64-33](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-4/64-33) (Special Bulk Regulations for Cottage Envelope Buildings) may additionally be applied exclusively to **_zoning lots_** containing **_cottage envelope buildings_**. Where such provisions are utilized, the provisions of Section [64-50](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-4/64-50) (STREETSCAPE REGULATIONS), inclusive, shall apply.
(c) For portions of **_buildings_**
    The following provisions may be applied to portions of **_buildings_** as follows:
    (1) the provisions of Section [64-311](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-4/64-311) (Special floor area modifications for all buildings) may be applied to portions of **_buildings_**, regardless of whether such portions comply with **_flood-resistant construction standards_**;
    (2) the provisions of Section [64-32](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-4/64-32) (Special Bulk Regulations for Flood-resistant Buildings), inclusive, may be applied to portions of **_buildings_**, provided that such portions comply with **_flood-resistant construction standards_** for the entirety of its vertically contiguous segments. Where such provisions are utilized within portions of **_buildings_**, the provisions of Section [64-50](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-4/64-50), inclusive, shall apply.
