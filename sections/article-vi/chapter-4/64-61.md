# 64-61 Special Provisions for Non-conforming Uses

For all **_buildings_** with **_non-conforming_** **_uses_**, the provisions of Sections [52-20](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-v/chapter-2/52-20) (REPAIRS OR ALTERATIONS), [52-40](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-v/chapter-2/52-40) (ENLARGEMENTS OR EXTENSIONS), and [52-50](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-v/chapter-2/52-50) (DAMAGE OR DESTRUCTION), inclusive, shall be modified to allow a **_non-conforming_** **_use_** to be continued, and a **_building_** with **_non-conforming_** **_uses_** to be altered, **_enlarged_**, relocated or reconstructed to comply with **_flood-resistant construction standards_**, pursuant to the provisions of this Section, inclusive.

Where a **_building_** with **_non-conforming_** **_uses_** is also **_non-complying_** with the applicable **_bulk_** regulations, **_non-compliances_** may be continued, increased or newly created only in accordance with the provisions of Section [64-612](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-4/64-612) (Special floor area regulations for buildings with non-conforming uses), Section [64-613](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-4/64-613) (Special height regulations for buildings with non-conforming uses), and Section [64-614](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-4/64-614) (Process for establishing non-conforming uses), except that Section [64-622](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-4/64-622) (Special open area regulations for non-complying buildings) and Section [64-624](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-4/64-624) (Process for establishing non-compliances) may also apply.

In addition, damage and destruction provisions set forth in Section [64-611](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-4/64-611) (Special regulations for damage or destruction provisions for buildings with non-conforming uses) shall apply to such **_buildings_**.
