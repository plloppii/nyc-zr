# 64-612 Special floor area regulations for buildings with non-conforming uses

The maximum amount of **_non-conforming_** **_floor area_** in the altered, **_enlarged_**, relocated or reconstructed **_building_** shall not exceed the amount of **_non-conforming_** **_floor area_** existing prior to the alteration or reconstruction work.
