# 64-621 Special floor area regulations for non-complying buildings

For **_buildings_** with **_non-complying_** **_floor area_**, the maximum amount of **_floor area_** in the altered, **_enlarged_**, relocated or reconstructed **_building_** shall not exceed the amount of pre-existing **_floor area_**, nor shall it exceed the maximum **_floor area_** permitted by the applicable district regulations by more than 20 percent. In addition, subsequent to such alteration, **_enlargement_**, relocation or reconstruction, no **_extension_** or change of **_use_** may create a new **_non-compliance_** or increase the degree of existing **_non-compliance_** with **_floor area_**.
