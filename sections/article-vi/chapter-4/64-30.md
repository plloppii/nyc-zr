# 64-30 SPECIAL BULK REGULATIONS

The provisions of this Section, inclusive, are optional, and are only applicable to **_zoning lots_** located wholly or partially within **_flood zones_**.

The provisions of Section [64-31](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-4/64-31) (Special Bulk Regulations for All Buildings), inclusive, may be applied to **_zoning lots_** regardless of whether **_buildings or other structures_** on such **_zoning lots_** comply with **_flood-resistant construction standards_**.

The provisions of Section [64-32](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-4/64-32) (Special Bulk Regulations for Flood-resistant Buildings), inclusive, may be applied to **_zoning lots_** containing **_flood-resistant buildings_**, including **_cottage envelope buildings_**.

The provisions of Section [64-33](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-4/64-33) (Special Bulk Regulations for Cottage Envelope Buildings), inclusive, may be applied to **_zoning lots_** with **_cottage envelope buildings_**.
