# 61-21 Restriction on Highest Projection of Building or Structure

Notwithstanding any other provisions of this Resolution, except as provided in Section [61-22](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-1/61-22) (Permitted Projection within any Flight Obstruction Area), the highest projection of any **_building or other structure_** hereafter constructed or of any existing **_building or other structure_** hereafter relocated, **_enlarged_** or reconstructed shall not penetrate:

(a) the **_approach surfaces_**, the **_transitional surfaces_**, the **_horizontal surface_**, or the **_conical surface_**, whichever is more restrictive, within the Airport Approach District of the **_flight obstruction area_**; and

(b) the **_horizontal surface_** or the **_conical surface_** within the Airport Circling District of the **_flight obstruction area_**.
