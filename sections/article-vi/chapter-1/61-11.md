# 61-11 Designation of Major Airports

The major airports are hereby designated to include John F. Kennedy International, LaGuardia, and U.S. Naval Air Station (Floyd Bennett Field). For the purposes of this Chapter, the runways for such airports shall be the runways as shown in the diagrams in Section [61-42](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-1/61-42) (Runways for Major Airports).
