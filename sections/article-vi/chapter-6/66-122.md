# 66-122 Limitations on applicability in certain areas

The provisions of this Chapter shall not apply as follows:

(a) The provisions of Section [66-20](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-6/66-20) (SPECIAL PROVISIONS FOR PRIMARY TRANSIT-ADJACENT SITES) and Section [66-30](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-6/66-30) (SPECIAL PROVISIONS FOR SECONDARY TRANSIT-ADJACENT SITES), shall not apply to the following areas within Special Purpose District:

(1) all subdistricts except 34th Street Corridor Subdistrict C and South of Port Authority Subdistrict E within **_Special Hudson Yards District_**;

(2) **_qualifying sites_** in the East Midtown Subdistrict within **_Special Midtown District_**, as defined in Section [81-613](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-viii/chapter-1/81-613) (Definitions); or

(3) the **_Special Transit Land Use District_**.

(b) The provisions of Section [66-51](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-6/66-51) (Additional Floor Area for Mass Transit Station Improvements) shall not apply:

(1) to the following areas within Special Purpose Districts:

(i) all subdistricts except 34th Street Corridor Subdistrict C and South of Port Authority Subdistrict E within **_Special Hudson Yards District_**;

(ii) the Court Square Subdistrict within **_Special Long Island Mixed Use District_**; or

(iii) **_qualifying sites_** in the East Midtown Subdistrict within the **_Special Midtown District_**, as defined in Section [81-613](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-viii/chapter-1/81-613) (Definitions); or

(2) to the 34th Street - Hudson Yards Station.
