# 66-51 Additional Floor Area for Mass Transit Station Improvements

For **_developments_** or **_enlargements_** located on **_qualifying transit improvement sites_**, the City Planning Commission may grant a **_floor area_** bonus, by authorization, pursuant to Section [66-511](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-6/66-511) (Additional floor area for mass transit station improvements by authorization), or by special permit, pursuant to Section [66-512](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-6/66-512) (Additional floor area for mass transit station improvements by special permit). All such **_floor area_** bonuses shall comply with the conditions, findings and additional requirements set forth in Section [66-513](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-6/66-513) (Additional rules and limitations, conditions, findings and requirements).
