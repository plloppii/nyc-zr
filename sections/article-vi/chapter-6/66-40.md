# 66-40 CONSTRUCTION, MAINTENANCE, AND ADDITIONAL PROVISIONS

Where an **_easement volume_** is provided pursuant to Section [66-20](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-6/66-20) (SPECIAL PROVISIONS FOR PRIMARY TRANSIT-ADJACENT SITES) or Section [66-30](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-6/66-30) (SPECIAL PROVISIONS FORSECONDARY TRANSIT-ADJACENT SITES) the provisions of this Section, inclusive, shall apply. Where a **_clear path_** is provided pursuant to the aforementioned Sections, the provisions of Section [66-42](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-6/66-42) (Termination of a Transit Volume) shall also apply.
