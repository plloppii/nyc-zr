# 66-121 General applicability

The provisions of this Chapter shall apply to **_transit-adjacent sites_** or **_qualifying transit improvement sites_**, as follows:

(a) For **_transit-adjacent sites_**

(1) The provisions of Section [66-20](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-6/66-20) (SPECIAL PROVISIONS FOR PRIMARY TRANSIT-ADJACENT SITES), inclusive, shall apply to all **_developments_** or **_enlargements_** on the lowest **_story_** of a **_building_** on **_primary transit-adjacent sites_**.

(2) The provisions of Section [66-30](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-6/66-30) (SPECIAL PROVISIONS FOR SECONDARY TRANSIT-ADJACENT SITES), inclusive, include optional provisions available to **_developments_** and **_enlargements_** on **_secondary transit-adjacent sites_**, **_conversions_** on **_transit-adjacent sites_**.

(3) The provisions of Section [66-40](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-6/66-40) (CONSTRUCTION, MAINTENANCE AND ADDITIONAL PROVISIONS), inclusive, shall apply to all **_transit-adjacent sites_** providing an **_easement volume_** pursuant to Section [66-20](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-6/66-20) or Section [66-30](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-6/66-30).

(b) For **_qualifying transit improvement sites_**

The provisions of Section [66-51](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-6/66-51) (Additional Floor Area for Mass Transit Station Improvements) shall be optional for **_qualifying transit improvement sites_**.
