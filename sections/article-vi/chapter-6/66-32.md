# 66-32 Special Modifications for Secondary Transit-adjacent Sites

Where a **_transit volume_** is provided pursuant to Section [66-31](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-6/66-31) (Determination and Certification for Optional Transit Volumes), special **_use_**, **_bulk_**, parking, and streetscape regulations may be applied as follows:

(a) For all **_developments_** or **_enlargements_** on **_secondary transit-adjacent sites_**, the modifications set forth in Sections [66-22](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-6/66-22) through [66-25](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-6/66-25) shall apply as if such **_development_** or **_enlargement_** was on a **_primary transit-adjacent site_**.
(b) For **_conversions_** on all **_transit-adjacent sites_**, the following modifications shall apply as if such **_conversion_** was on a **_primary transit-adjacent site_**:
    (1) **_Use_** modifications pursuant to Section [66-221](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-6/66-221) (Temporary uses);
    (2) **_Bulk_** modifications pursuant to Section [66-231](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-6/66-231) (Special floor area modifications), paragraph (a)(1) of Section [66-232](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-6/66-232) (Special open space, lot coverage and yard modifications), Section [66-233](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-6/66-233) (Special street wall modifications), and paragraph (a) of Section [66-234](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-6/66-234) (Special height and setback modifications);
    (3) Waiver provisions for required parking pursuant to paragraph (c) of Section [66-24](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-6/66-24) (Special Regulations for Accessory Off-street Parking); and
    (4) Streetscape modifications pursuant to Section [66-25](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-6/66-25) (Special Streetscape Regulations).
