# 66-33 Additional Modifications

Where a **_transit volume_** is provided pursuant to Section [66-31](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-6/66-31) (Determination and Certification for Optional Transit Volumes), the City Planning Commission may authorize or grant, by special permit, the modification of applicable regulations of this Resolution, other than **_floor area ratio_**, including the provisions set forth in this Section, pursuant to Section [66-52](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-6/66-52) (Additional Modifications).
