# 42-67 Additional Sign Regulations for Adult Establishments

M1 M2 M3

In all districts, as indicated, all permitted **_signs_**, other than **_advertising signs_**, for **_adult establishments_** shall conform with the provisions of this Chapter, except that the maximum **_surface area_** of all **_signs_**, other than **_advertising signs_**, for **_adult establishments_** shall not exceed, in the aggregate, three times the **_street_** frontage of the **_zoning lot_**, but in no event more than 150 square feet per establishment, of which no more than 50 square feet may be **_illuminated_** and no portion thereof may be **_flashing_**.

No **_signs_** for **_adult establishments_** shall be permitted on the roof of any **_building_**, nor shall such **_signs_** extend above **_curb level_** at a height greater than 25 feet.
