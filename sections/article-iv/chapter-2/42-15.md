# 42-15 Use Group V – Transient Accommodations

M1 M2 M3

Use Group V consists of **_uses_** for transient occupancy of various types. The provisions regulating **_uses_** classified in this Use Group are set forth as follows:

(a) Section [42-151](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iv/chapter-2/42-151) (Use Group V – general use allowances) which includes the compilation of **_uses_** in the Use Group table;
(b) Section [42-152](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iv/chapter-2/42-152) (Use Group V – uses subject to additional conditions) for additional conditions that apply to certain **_uses_**, as denoted with a “P” in the Use Group table;
(c) Section [42-153](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iv/chapter-2/42-153) (Use Group V – uses subject to open use allowances), for open **_use_** allowances that apply to certain **_uses_**, as denoted with a “U” in the Use Group table; and
(d) Section [42-154](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iv/chapter-2/42-154) (Use Group V – additional provisions for parking requirement category) for **_uses_** with more than one parking requirement category or other applicable parking provisions, as denoted with “\*” in the Use Group table.
