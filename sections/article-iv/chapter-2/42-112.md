# 42-112 Use Group I – uses subject to additional conditions

For **_uses_** denoted with a “P” in Section [42-111](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iv/chapter-2/42-111) (Use Group I – general use allowances), the following provisions shall apply:

(a) Agricultural **_uses_** in all **_Manufacturing Districts_** shall conform with the applicable performance standards set forth in Sections [42-40](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iv/chapter-2/42-40) (PERFORMANCE STANDARDS) through [42-48](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iv/chapter-2/42-48) (Performance Standards Regulating Humidity, Heat or Glare), inclusive.
(b) Outdoor racket courts are permitted in M1 Districts provided that all lighting shall be directed away from nearby **_residences_**.
