# 42-662 Restriction on angle and height above curb level

M1 M2 M3

In all districts, as indicated, and within 500 feet of the boundary of a **_Residence District_** or **_Commercial District_**, except C7 or C8 Districts, any illuminated portion of any **_sign_** shall face at an angle of more than 90 degrees away from such boundary line and a **_sign with indirect illumination_** may extend only to a height of 58 feet above **_curb level_**.
