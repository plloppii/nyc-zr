# 42-17 Use Group VII – Offices and Laboratories

M1 M2 M3

Use Group VII consists of **_uses_** that provide administrative and research workspaces for business, professional or governmental purposes. The provisions regulating **_uses_** classified in this Use Group, by **_Manufacturing District_** are set forth in the table below. Notations found in the table are further described in Section [42-10](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iv/chapter-2/42-10) (USE ALLOWANCES).
