# 42-145 Use Group IV – uses permitted by special permit

For **_uses_** denoted with “○” in Section [42-141](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iv/chapter-2/42-141) (Use Group IV – general use allowances), the provisions of this Section shall apply.

(a) The following **_uses_** may be permitted by the Board of Standards and Appeals:
    (1) Radio or television towers may be permitted in all **_Manufacturing Districts_**, in accordance with Section [73-141](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-3/73-141) (Radio or television towers).
(b) The following **_uses_** may be permitted by special permit of the City Planning Commission:
    (1) Sewage disposal plants may be permitted in M1 or M2 Districts, in accordance with Section [74-143](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-4/74-143) (Sewage pumping stations and sewage disposal plants).
    (2) Airports may be permitted in all **_Manufacturing Districts_**, in accordance with Section [74-144](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-4/74-144) (Airports).
    (3) Bus stations may be permitted in all **_Manufacturing Districts_**, in accordance with Section [74-145](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-4/74-145) (Bus stations).
    (4) Heliports may be permitted in all **_Manufacturing Districts_**, in accordance with Section [74-146](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-4/74-146) (Heliports).
    (5) Railroad passenger stations may be permitted in all **_Manufacturing Districts_**, in accordance with Section [74-148](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-4/74-148) (Railroad passenger stations).
    (6) Seaplane bases may be permitted in all **_Manufacturing Districts_**, in accordance with Section [74-149](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-4/74-149) (Seaplane).
