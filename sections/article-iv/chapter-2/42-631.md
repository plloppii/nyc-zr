# 42-631 Total surface area of signs

M1 M2 M3

In all districts, as indicated, the total **_surface area_** of all permitted **_signs_**, including non-**_illuminated_** or **_illuminated signs_**, shall not exceed the limitation established for non-**_illuminated signs_**, as set forth in Section 42-632.
