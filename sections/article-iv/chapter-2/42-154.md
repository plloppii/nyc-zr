# 42-154 Use Group V – additional provisions for parking requirement category

For **_uses_** denoted with “\*” for parking requirement category (PRC) in Section [42-151](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iv/chapter-2/42-151) (Use Group V – general use allowances), the provisions of this Section shall apply. For **_transient hotels_**, **_floor area_** used for sleeping accommodations shall be classified as PRC F2. **_Floor area_** used for meeting halls, auditoriums, eating or drinking places, wedding chapels or banquet halls or radio or television studios shall be classified as PRC B1.
