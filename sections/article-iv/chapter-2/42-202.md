# 42-202 Use Group X – uses subject to additional conditions

For **_uses_** denoted with a “P” in Section [42-201](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iv/chapter-2/42-201) (Use Group X – general use allowances), the provisions of this Section shall apply. Permitted **_uses_** in all **_Manufacturing Districts_** shall conform to the performance standards set forth in Sections [42-40](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iv/chapter-2/42-40) (PERFORMANCE STANDARDS) through [42-48](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iv/chapter-2/42-48) (Performance Standards Regulating Humidity, Heat or Glare), inclusive. However, beverage manufacturing establishments shall not be subject to the provisions of Section [42-47](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iv/chapter-2/42-47) (Performance Standards Regulating Fire and Explosive Hazards).
