# 42-642 Additional regulations for projecting signs

M1 M2 M3

In all districts, as indicated, permitted **_signs_** other than **_advertising signs_** may be displayed as follows:

(a) Non-**_illuminated signs_** may be displayed on awnings or canopies permitted by the Administrative Code, with a **_surface area_** not exceeding 12 square feet and with the height of letters not exceeding 12 inches. Any commercial copy on such **_signs_** shall be limited to identification of the name or address of the **_building_** or an establishment contained therein.

(b) **_Signs_** may be displayed on marquees permitted by the Administrative Code, provided that no such **_sign_** shall project more than 48 inches above nor more than 12 inches below such marquee.
