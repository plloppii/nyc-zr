# 42-511 In M1 Districts

M1

In the district indicated, all such activities shall be located within **_completely enclosed buildings_**, provided, however, that **_commercial_** **_uses_** may be located within **_buildings_** which are **_completely enclosed_** except for store fronts or store windows which may be opened to serve customers outside the **_building_**.
