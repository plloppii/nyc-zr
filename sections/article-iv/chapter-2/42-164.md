# 42-164 Use Group VI – uses subject to open use allowances

For **_uses_** denoted with a “U” in Section [42-161](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iv/chapter-2/42-161) (Use Group VI – general use allowances), a **_use_** may be open or enclosed without restriction, except that for eating or drinking establishments, such open **_use_** shall be limited to outdoor table service.
