# 42-452 Regulation of toxic or noxious matter

In all **_Manufacturing Districts_**, the emission of **_toxic or noxious matter_** into the atmosphere shall be in accordance with limits established by the Department of Environmental Protection. In addition to such emission limits, the emission of such matter shall be so controlled that no concentration at or beyond **_lot lines_** shall be detrimental to or endanger the public health, safety, comfort, and other aspects of the general welfare, or cause damage or injury to property.
