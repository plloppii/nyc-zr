# 42-434 General control over smoke and other particulate matter

In addition to the performance standards of regulating **_smoke_** and other **_particulate matter_**, the emission of such matter shall be so controlled in manner and quantity of emission as not to be detrimental to or endanger the public health, safety, comfort, or other aspects of the general welfare, or cause damage or injury to property.
