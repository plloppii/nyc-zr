# 42-414 Special provisions applying along district boundaries

Whenever a **_Manufacturing District_** adjoins a **_Residence District_**, at any point at the district boundary or within the **_Residence District_**, the maximum permitted **_decibel_** levels in all **_octave bands_** shall be reduced by six **_decibels_** from the maximum levels set forth in the table in Section [42-413](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iv/chapter-2/42-413) (Maximum permitted decibel levels).
