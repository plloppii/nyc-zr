# 42-441 In M1 or M2 Districts

In M1 or M2 Districts, the emission of odorous matter shall be in accordance with limits established by the Department of Environmental Protection. In addition to such limits, the emission of odorous matter in such quantities as to be readily detectable at any point along **_lot lines_** or to produce a public nuisance or hazard beyond **_lot lines_** is prohibited.
