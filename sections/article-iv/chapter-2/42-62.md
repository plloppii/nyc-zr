# 42-62 Permitted Signs

M1 M2 M3

In all districts, as indicated, **_signs_** are permitted subject to the provisions of the following Sections:

Section [42-63](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iv/chapter-2/42-63) (Surface Area and Illumination Provisions)

Section [42-64](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iv/chapter-2/42-64) (Permitted Projection or Height of Signs)

Section [42-65](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iv/chapter-2/42-65) (Additional Regulations for Signs Near Certain Parks and Designated Arterial Highways)

Section [42-66](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iv/chapter-2/42-66) (Special Provisions Applying Along District Boundaries)

Section [42-67](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iv/chapter-2/42-67) (Additional Sign Regulations for Adult Establishments)

Section [42-68](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iv/chapter-2/42-68) (Signs Erected Prior to December 13, 2000).

However, notwithstanding any provision of this Section, flags, banners or pennants other than those that are **_advertising signs_**, located on any **_zoning lot_** used primarily for **_community facility_** **_uses_** of a civic, philanthropic, educational or religious nature, are permitted in all districts, as indicated, without limitation.
