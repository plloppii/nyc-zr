# 44-022 Applicability of regulations in M1-1D through M1-5D Districts

In M1-1D, M1-2D, M1-3D, M1-4D and M1-5D Districts, the parking regulations governing M1 Districts shall apply to **_manufacturing_**, **_commercial_** or **_community facility_** **_uses_**, and the parking regulations applicable to R5 Districts, as set forth in Article II, Chapter 5, shall apply to **_residences_**.
