# 44-563 Restrictions on location of berths near Residence Districts

M1 M2 M3

In all districts, as indicated, where **_accessory_** off-street loading berths are located within 60 feet of a **_Residence District_** boundary, such berths shall be enclosed within a **_building_**, and no entrance to or exit from the berths on to the **_street_** shall be less than 30 feet from the district boundary.
