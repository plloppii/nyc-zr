# 44-566 Regulations for permitted or required loading berths for zoning lots containing self-service storage facilities in designated areas

M1-1 M1-2 M1-4 M2-1 M2-2 M3-1 M3-2

In the Districts indicated, in designated areas within **_Manufacturing Districts_** in Subarea 1, as shown on the maps in APPENDIX J (Designated Areas Within Manufacturing Districts) of this Resolution, the provisions of Sections [44-52](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iv/chapter-4/44-52) (Required Accessory Off-street Loading Berths) and [44-581](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iv/chapter-4/44-581) (Size of required loading berths) are modified as set forth in this Section.

For any **_zoning lot_** containing a **_self-service storage facility_** that meets the requirements of paragraphs (d)(1) or (d)(2)(i) of Section [42-193](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iv/chapter-2/42-193) (Use Group IX – use subject to additional conditions), all required **_accessory_** off-street loading berths for a **_self-service storage facility_** shall have a minimum length of 37 feet. The dimensions of off-street loading berths shall not include driveways, or entrances to or exits from such off-street loading berths.

The number of **_accessory_** off-street loading berths required for **_uses_** occupying **_industrial floor space_** shall be as set forth in the following table:

| **_Floor Area_** (in square feet) | Required Loading Berths |
| --- | --- |
| First 15,000 | None |
| Next 25,000 | 1 |
| Next 40,000 | 1 |
| Each additional 80,000 or fraction thereof | 1 |
