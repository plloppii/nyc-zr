# 44-025 Applicability of regulations to public parking garages and public parking lots

The following provisions of this Chapter shall apply to **_public parking garages_** and **_public parking lots_** in addition to **_accessory_** off-street parking facilities:

Section [44-352](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iv/chapter-4/44-352) (Restrictions on the use of parking spaces in public parking garages and public parking lots)

Section [44-423](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iv/chapter-4/44-423) (Calculating floor area in parking facilities with lift systems, or in automated parking facilities)

Section [44-43](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iv/chapter-4/44-43) (Location of Access to the Street)

Section [44-44](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iv/chapter-4/44-44) (Surfacing)

Section [44-45](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iv/chapter-4/44-45) (Screening)

Section [44-47](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iv/chapter-4/44-47) (Parking Lot Maneuverability and Curb Cut Regulations)
