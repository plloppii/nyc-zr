# 44-561 Size of required loading berths

M1 M2 M3

In all districts, as indicated, all required off-street loading berths, open or enclosed, shall conform to the regulations on minimum dimensions set forth in the following table. The dimensions of off-street berths shall not include driveways, or entrances to or exits from such off-street berths. Loading requirement categories (LRC) shall be as set forth in Section [44-52](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iv/chapter-4/44-52) (Required Accessory Off-street Loading Berths).

|  |  | Length | Width | Vertical Clearance |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| LRC-A | with less than 10,000 sq. ft. of **_floor area_** | 37 | 12 | 14 |
| LRC-A | with 10,000 sq. ft. or more of **_floor area_** | 50 | 12 | 14 |
| LRC-B |  | 37 | 12 | 14 |
| LRC-C |  | 37 | 12 | 12 |
| LRC-D |  | 37 | 12 | 12 |
| LRC-E |  | 25 | 10 | 8 |
