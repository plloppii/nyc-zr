# 43-451 Towers on small lots

M1-3 M1-4 M1-5 M1-6

In the districts indicated, a tower may occupy the percent of the **_lot area_** of a **_zoning lot_** set forth in the following table:

LOT COVERAGE OF TOWERS ON SMALL ZONING LOTS

| Area of **_Zoning Lot_** (in square feet) | Maximum Percent of **_Lot Coverage_** |
| --- | --- |
| 10,500 or less | 50 |
| 10,501 to 11,500 | 49 |
| 11,501 to 12,500 | 48 |
| 12,501 to 13,500 | 47 |
| 13,501 to 14,500 | 46 |
| 14,501 to 15,500 | 45 |
| 15,501 to 16,500 | 44 |
| 16,501 to 17,500 | 43 |
| 17,501 to 18,500 | 42 |
| 18,501 to 19,999 | 41 |
