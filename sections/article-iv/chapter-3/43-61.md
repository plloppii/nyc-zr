# 43-61 Bulk Regulations for Residential Uses in M1-1D Through M1-5D Districts

In M1-1D through M1-5D Districts, where **_residential_** **_uses_** are permitted pursuant to Article IV, Chapter 2, the **_bulk_** regulations of a C2 District mapped within an R5 District shall apply.
