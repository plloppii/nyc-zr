# 43-30 SPECIAL PROVISIONS APPLYING ALONG DISTRICT BOUNDARIES

M1 M2 M3

In all districts, as indicated, open areas shall be provided in accordance with the provisions of this Section along the boundaries of **_Residence Districts_**, except where such district boundaries are also the boundaries of **_railroad rights-of-way_** or cemeteries.
