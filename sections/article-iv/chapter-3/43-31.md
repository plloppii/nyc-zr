# 43-31 Other Special Provisions for Rear Yards

M1 M2 M3

In all districts, as indicated, the **_rear yard_** requirements set forth in Section [43-26](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iv/chapter-3/43-26) (Minimum Required Rear Yards) shall be modified as set forth in this Section.
