# 43-301 Required yards along district boundary coincident with side lot line of zoning lot in an R1, R2, R3, R4 or R5 District

M1 M2 M3

In all districts, as indicated, along such portion of the boundary of a **_Manufacturing District_** which coincides with a **_side lot line_** of a **_zoning lot_** in an R1, R2, R3, R4 or R5 District, an open area not higher than **_curb level_** and at least 15 feet wide shall be provided within the **_Manufacturing District_**. Such an open area shall not be used for **_accessory_** off-street parking, **_accessory_** off-street loading, or for storage or processing of any kind.
