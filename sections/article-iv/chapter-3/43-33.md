# 43-33 Modifications of Rear Yard Regulations

M1 M2 M3

In all districts, as indicated, the regulations set forth in Section [43-313](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iv/chapter-3/43-313) (For zoning lots with multiple rear lot lines) may be modified in accordance with the provisions of Section [73-69](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-3/73-69) (Rear Yard Modifications).
