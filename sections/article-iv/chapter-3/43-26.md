# 43-26 Minimum Required Rear Yards

M1 M2 M3

In all districts, other than districts with an A suffix, a **_rear yard_** with a depth of not less than 20 feet shall be provided at every **_rear lot line_** on any **_zoning lot_** except as otherwise provided in Sections [43-27](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iv/chapter-3/43-27) (Special Provisions for Shallow Interior Lots), [43-28](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iv/chapter-3/43-28) (Special Provisions for Through Lots) or [43-31](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iv/chapter-3/43-31) (Other Special Provisions for Rear Yards). **_Rear yards_** shall also be provided along portions of **_side lot lines_** as set forth in Section [43-261](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iv/chapter-3/43-261) (Beyond one hundred feet of a street line).

For districts with an A suffix, the provisions of Section [43-262](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iv/chapter-3/43-262) shall apply. However, such provisions shall be modified by the provisions of [43-27](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iv/chapter-3/43-27) (Special Provisions for Shallow Interior Lots), [43-28](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iv/chapter-3/43-28) or [43-31](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iv/chapter-3/43-31).
