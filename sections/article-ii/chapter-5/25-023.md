# 25-023 Applicability of regulations to certain community facility uses in lower density growth management areas

(a) In **_lower density growth management areas_** other than R6 and R7 Districts in Community District 10, Borough of the Bronx, all **_zoning lots_** containing **_buildings_** with the following **_uses_** shall be subject to the provisions of paragraph (b) of this Section:

(1) ambulatory diagnostic or treatment health care facilities listed under Use Group III(B), except where such **_zoning lot_** contains **_buildings_** used for hospitals, as defined in the New York State Hospital Code, or **_long-term care facilities_**; or

(2) child care services as listed under the definition of **_school_** in Section [12-10](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-i/chapter-2/12-10) (DEFINITIONS), except where such **_zoning lot_** contains **_buildings_** used for houses of worship or, for **_zoning lots_** that do not contain **_buildings_** used for houses of worship, where the amount of **_floor area_** used for child care services is equal to 25 percent or less of the amount of **_floor area_** permitted for **_community facility_** **_use_** on the **_zoning lot_**.

(b) All **_zoning lots_** that meet the conditions of paragraph (a) of this Section shall comply with the provisions of Section [25-624](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-5/25-624) (Special parking regulations for certain community facility uses in lower density growth management areas) in lieu of the following provisions:

(1) the parking location provisions of Sections [25-622](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-5/25-622) (Location of parking spaces in lower density growth management areas) and [25-623](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-5/25-623) (Maneuverability standards);

(2) the driveway and curb cut provisions of Sections [25-632](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-5/25-632) (Driveway and curb cut regulations in lower density growth management areas) and [25-634](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-5/25-634) (Curb cut regulations for community facilities); and

(3) the screening provisions of Section [25-65](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-5/25-65) (Screening).

In addition, where the **_uses_** listed in paragraphs (a)(1) and (a)(2) of this Section result from a change of **_use_**, the provisions of Section [25-31](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-5/25-31) (General Provisions) shall be modified to require **_accessory_** off-street parking spaces for such **_uses_**. However, the requirements of Sections [25-31](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-5/25-31) and [25-624](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-5/25-624) may be modified for **_zoning lots_** containing **_buildings_** with such changes of **_use_** where the Chairperson of the City Planning Commission certifies to the Commissioner of Buildings that such modifications are necessary due to the location of existing **_buildings_** on the **_zoning lot_**, and such requirements have been complied with to the maximum extent feasible.
