# 25-142 For accessory uses in large-scale residential developments

R1 R2 R3 R4 R5 R6 R7 R8 R9 R10 R11 R12

In all districts, as indicated, the City Planning Commission may permit **_group parking facilities_** **_accessory_** to **_uses_** in **_large-scale residential developments_** with more than the prescribed maximum of Section [25-12](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-5/25-12) (Maximum Size of Accessory Group Parking Facilities), in accordance with the provisions of Section [74-531](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-4/74-531) (Additional parking spaces or roof parking for accessory group parking facilities).
