# 25-022 Applicability of regulations to non-profit hospital staff dwellings

In all districts, the regulations of this Chapter applicable to **_community facility_** **_uses_** shall not apply to **_non-profit hospital staff dwellings_**. In lieu thereof, the regulations applicable to **_residences_** shall apply, as follows:

(a) the regulations of an R5 District shall apply to **_non-profit hospital staff dwellings_** located in R1, R2 and R3 Districts;
(b) the regulations of an R6 District shall apply to **_non-profit hospital staff dwellings_** located in R4 and R5 Districts; and
(c) the regulations of an R10 District shall apply to **_non-profit hospital staff dwellings_** located in R6 through R10 Districts.
