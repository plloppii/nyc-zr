# 25-431 Limited repairs permitted in specified districts

R3 R4 R5 R6 R7 R8 R9 R10 R11 R12

In the districts indicated, within a **_completely enclosed_** garage, **_detached_** from a **_building_** containing **_residences_** and containing not less than 150 **_accessory_** off-street parking spaces, minor automotive repairs (not including body work) are permitted.
