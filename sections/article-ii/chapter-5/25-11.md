# 25-11 General Provisions

R1 R2 R3 R4 R5 R6 R7 R8 R9 R10 R11 R12

In all districts, as indicated, **_accessory_** off-street parking spaces may be provided for **_residences_**, for permitted **_community facility_** **_uses_**, for **_commercial_** **_uses_** permitted as **_accessory_** **_uses_** in **_large-scale residential developments_**, or for **_uses_** permitted by special permit, subject to the provisions set forth in the following Sections:

Section [25-12](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-5/25-12) (Maximum Size of Accessory Group Parking Facilities)

Section [25-15](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-5/25-15) (Maximum Spaces for Single-Family Detached Residences)

Section [25-16](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-5/25-16) (Maximum Spaces for Other Residences)

Section [25-18](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-5/25-18) (Maximum Spaces for Permitted Community Facility or Commercial Uses).

Such **_accessory_** off-street parking spaces may be open or enclosed. However, except as otherwise provided in Sections [73-47](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-3/73-47) (Roof Parking) or [74-531](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-4/74-531) (Additional parking spaces or roof parking for accessory group parking facilities), no spaces shall be located on any roof which is immediately above a **_story_** other than a **_basement_**.
