# 25-15 Maximum Spaces for Single-Family Detached Residences

R1 R2 R3 R4 R5 R6 R7 R8 R9 R10 R11 R12

In all districts, as indicated, not more than three off-street parking spaces shall be provided for a **_single-family_** **_detached_** **_residence_**, except on **_zoning lots_** with a **_lot area_** of 10,000 square feet or more. For the purposes of this Section, a driveway shall not be considered as off-street parking space.
