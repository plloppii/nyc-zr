# 25-141 For hospitals

R1 R2 R3 R4 R5 R6 R7 R8 R9 R10 R11 R12

In all districts, as indicated, the Board of Standards and Appeals may permit **_group parking facilities_** **_accessory_** to hospitals, with more than 150 spaces, in accordance with the provisions of Section [73-46](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-3/73-46) (Exceptions to Maximum Size of Accessory Group Parking Facilities).
