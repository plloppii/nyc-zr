# 25-36 Waiver of Requirements for Certain Small Zoning Lots

R1 R2 R3 R4 R5 R6 R7 R8 R9 R10 R11 R12

In all districts, as indicated, the parking requirements of Section [25-31](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-5/25-31) (General Provisions) for camps, overnight or day, shall only apply to **_developments_** or **_enlargements_** with a minimum of either 10,000 square feet of **_lot area_** or 10 employees.
