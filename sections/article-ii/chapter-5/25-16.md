# 25-16 Maximum Spaces for Other Than Single-Family Detached Residences

R1 R2 R3 R4 R5 R6 R7 R8 R9 R10 R11 R12

In all districts, as indicated, for **_zoning lots_** occupied by **_residential_** **_uses_** other than **_single-family_** **_residences_**, no more than two off-street parking spaces **_accessory_** to such **_residences_** shall be provided for each **_dwelling unit_** located on the **_zoning lot_**.
