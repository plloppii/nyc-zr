# 23-36 Maximum Lot Coverage

For **_developments_** or **_enlargements_** of **_residences_**, the maximum **_lot coverage_** regulations are set forth in Section [23-361](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-3/23-361) for R1 through R5 Districts and Section [23-362](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-3/23-362) for R6 through R12 Districts. Special rules for certain **_interior_** or **_through lots_** are set forth in Section 23-363.
