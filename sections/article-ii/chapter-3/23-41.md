# 23-41 Permitted Obstructions

In all **_Residence Districts_**, the obstructions set forth in this Section, inclusive, shall be permitted to penetrate a maximum height limit set forth in Sections [23-42](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-3/23-42) (Height and Setback Requirements in R1 Through R5 Districts), [23-43](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-3/23-43) (Height and Setback Requirements for R6 Through R12 Districts) or [23-44](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-3/23-44) (Special Provisions for Certain Areas).
