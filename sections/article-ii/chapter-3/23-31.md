# 23-31 General Provisions

In R6 through R12 Districts, the obstructions permitted within a **_front yard_** pursuant to Section [23-31](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-3/23-31), inclusive, may also be permitted in the open area between the **_street wall_** and the **_street line_**.
