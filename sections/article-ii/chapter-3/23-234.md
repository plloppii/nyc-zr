# 23-234 Elevated Ground Floor Units

For **_buildings_** with entryways at **_curb level_** that accommodate ramps, stairs or lifts to **_dwelling units_** that are elevated above **_curb level_** on the first **_story_** of the **_building_**, up to 100 square feet of such entryways may be exempted from the definition of **_floor area_** for each foot of difference between the floor level of such **_dwelling units_** and **_curb level_**. However, no more than a maximum of 500 square feet of floor space may be exempted from the definition of **_floor area_** for each **_building_**.
