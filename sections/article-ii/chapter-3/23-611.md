# 23-611 Street tree planting

R1 R2 R3 R4 R5 R6 R7 R8 R9 R10 R11 R12

In all districts, as indicated, the following shall provide **_street_** trees in accordance with Section [26-41](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-6/26-41) (Street Tree Planting):

(a) **_developments_**, or **_enlargements_** that increase the **_floor area_** on a **_zoning lot_** by 20 percent or more. However, **_street_** trees shall not be required for **_enlargements_** of **_single-_** or **_two-family residences_**;
(b) **_conversions_** of 20 percent or more of the **_floor area_** of a **_building_** to a **_residential use_**; or
(c) construction of a **_detached_** garage that is 400 square feet or greater.
