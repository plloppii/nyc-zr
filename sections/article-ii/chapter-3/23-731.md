# 23-731 Applicability

The optional provisions for **_sky exposure plane buildings_** set forth in Section [23-73](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-3/23-73), inclusive, may be applied to **_zoning lots_** in R6 through R10 Districts without a letter suffix, except that such alternative provisions shall not apply to:

(a) **_zoning lots_** in R6-1, R6-2, R7-3 and R9-1 Districts;
(b) **_zoning lots_** in R8 Districts without a letter suffix in the portion of Community District 9 in the Borough of Manhattan located north of West 125th Street; or
(c) **_zoning lots_** located in the **_Limited Height Districts_**.
