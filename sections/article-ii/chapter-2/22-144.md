# 22-144 Use Group IV – uses permitted by special permit

For **_uses_** denoted with “○” in Section [22-141](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-2/22-141) (Use Group IV – general use allowances), the provisions of this Section shall apply.

(a) The following **_uses_** may be permitted in all **_Residence Districts_** by special permit of the Board of Standards and Appeals:
    (1) Radio or television towers may be permitted in accordance with Section [73-141](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-3/73-141) (Radio or television towers).
    (2) Telephone exchanges or other communications equipment structures may be permitted in accordance with Section [73-142](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-3/73-142) (Telephone exchanges or other communications equipment structures).
    (3) Electric utility substations on a site no greater than 40,000 square feet may be permitted in accordance with Section [73-143](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-3/73-143) (Electric or gas utility substations).
    (4) Gas utility substations may be permitted in accordance with Section [73-143](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-3/73-143) (Electric or gas utility substations).
    (5) Public utility stations for oil or gas metering or regulating may be permitted in accordance with Section [73-144](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-3/73-144) (Public utility stations or terminal facilities).
    (6) Terminal facilities at river crossings for access to electric, gas or steam lines may be permitted in accordance with Section [73-144](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-3/73-144) (Public utility stations or terminal facilities).
    (7) Water pumping stations may be permitted in accordance with Section [73-145](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-3/73-145) (Water pumping stations).
    (8) Public transit or railroad electric substations on a site no greater than 40,000 square feet may be permitted in accordance with Section [73-146](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-3/73-146) (Public transit or railroad electric substations);
(b) The following **_uses_** may be permitted in all **_Residence Districts_** by special permit of the City Planning Commission, except as otherwise specified:
    (1) Fire or police stations may be permitted in accordance with Section [74-141](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-4/74-141) (Fire or police stations).
    (2) Electric utility substations on a site greater than 40,000 square feet may be permitted in accordance with Section [74-142](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-4/74-142) (Electric utility substations).
    (3) Sewage disposal plants may be permitted in accordance with Section [74-143](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-4/74-143) (Sewage pumping stations and sewage disposal plants).
    (4) Sewage pumping stations may be permitted in accordance with Section [74-143](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-4/74-143) (Sewage pumping stations and sewage disposal plants).
    (5) Docks for ferries or water taxis may be permitted in all **_Residence Districts_**, except R1 and R2 Districts, in accordance with Section [62-833](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-2/62-833) (Docks for ferries or water taxis in Residence Districts). However, in R6 through R12 Districts, when located within Community District 1 in the Borough of Brooklyn, such docks are permitted by certification by the Chairperson of the City Planning Commission, pursuant to Section [62-813](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-2/62-813) (Docking facilities for ferries or water taxis in certain waterfront areas).
    (6) Public transit or railroad electric substations on a site greater than 40,000 square feet may be permitted in accordance with Section [74-147](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-4/74-147) (Public transit or railroad electric substations).
    (7) Railroad passenger stations may be permitted in accordance with Section [74-148](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-4/74-148) (Railroad passenger stations).
    (8) Seaplane bases may be permitted in accordance with Section [74-149](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-4/74-149) (Seaplane Bases).
