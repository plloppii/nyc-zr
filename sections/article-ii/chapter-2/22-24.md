# 22-24 Additional Regulations

R1 R2 R3 R4 R5 R6 R7 R8 R9 R10 R11 R12

In all districts as indicated, any **_sign_** permitted under the provisions of Sections [22-22](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-2/22-22) (Permitted Non-illuminated Accessory Signs) and [22-231](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-2/22-231) (Permitted illuminated accessory signs for hospitals and related facilities) shall conform to the regulations set forth in this Section.
