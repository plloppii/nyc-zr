# 22-222 "For sale" or "for rent" signs

R1 R2 R3 R4 R5 R6 R7 R8 R9 R10 R11 R12

"For sale" or "for rent" **_signs_**, with an area not exceeding 12 square feet, are permitted. If located on vacant land, such a **_sign_** shall not be within 15 feet of the **_street line_**, nor within six feet of any other **_lot line_**.
