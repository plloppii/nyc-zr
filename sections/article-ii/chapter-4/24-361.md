# 24-361 Beyond one hundred feet of a street line

R1 R2 R3 R4 R5 R6 R7 R8 R9 R10 R11 R12

In all districts, as indicated, for **_corner lots_**, and for **_zoning lots_** that are bounded by two or more **_streets_** that are neither **_corner lots_** nor **_through lots_**, the portion of a **_side lot line_** beyond 100 feet of the **_street line_** that it intersects shall be considered a **_rear lot line_** and the following rules shall apply along such **_rear lot line_**:

(a) In all districts, a **_rear yard_** with a minimum depth of 30 feet shall be provided where such **_rear lot line_** coincides with a **_rear lot line_** of an adjoining **_zoning lot_**.

[![Zoning Resolutions 24-361.0](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/sites/default/files/doc_images/Zoning Resolutions 24–361.0.png)](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/sites/default/files/doc_images/Zoning Resolutions 24–361.0.png)

CORNER LOT

(b) In R1 through R5 Districts, a **_rear yard_** with a minimum depth of eight feet shall be provided where such **_rear lot line_** coincides with a **_side lot line_** of an adjoining **_zoning lot_**.

[![Zoning Resolutions 24-361.1](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/sites/default/files/doc_images/Zoning Resolutions 24–361.1.png)](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/sites/default/files/doc_images/Zoning Resolutions 24–361.1.png)

ZONING LOT BOUNDED BY TWO OR MORE STREETS (NEITHER A CORNER LOT NOR A THROUGH LOT)

(c) In R6 through R12 Districts, no **_rear yard_** shall be required where such **_rear lot line_** coincides with a **_side lot line_** of an adjoining **_zoning lot_**.
