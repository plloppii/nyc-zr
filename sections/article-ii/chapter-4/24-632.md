# 24-632 Wide outer courts

R1 R2 R3 R4 R5 R6 R7 R8 R9 R10 R11 R12

In all districts, as indicated, if an **_outer court_** is 20 feet or more in width, the width of such **_outer court_** must be at least equal to the depth of such **_outer court_**, except that such width need not exceed 40 feet.
