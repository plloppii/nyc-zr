# 24-111 Maximum floor area ratio for certain community facility uses

R1 R2

(a) In the districts indicated, for any **_zoning lot_** containing **_community facility_** **_uses_** other than those **_uses_** for which a permit is required pursuant to Sections [73-11](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-3/73-11) (Agriculture and Open Uses), [73-13](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-3/73-13) (Community Facilities) or [73-18](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-3/73-18) (Recreation, Entertainment and Assembly Spaces), or where **_bulk_** modification is permitted pursuant to Section [74-902](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-4/74-902) (Certain community facility uses in R1 and R2 Districts and certain Commercial Districts), the maximum **_floor area ratio_** shall not exceed the **_floor area_** permitted for **_residential uses_** by the applicable district regulations. The provisions of this paragraph shall not apply to **_buildings_** for which plans were filed with the Department of Buildings prior to November 15, 1972, including any subsequent amendments thereof.

R3 R4 R5 R6 R7 R8 R9

(a) In R3 through R5 Districts, and in R6 through R9 Districts without a letter suffix, the maximum **_floor area ratio_** on a **_zoning lot_** for philanthropic or non-profit institutions with sleeping accommodations, and in R3-1, R3A, R3X, R4-1, R4A, R4B, R5A, R5B and R5D Districts, the maximum **_floor area ratio_** on a **_zoning lot_** for **_long-term care facilities_** shall be as set forth in the table in this Section, except that such maximum **_floor area ratio_** shall not apply to **_qualifying affordable housing_**. Such maximum **_floor area ratio_** may be modified by special permit of the City Planning Commission pursuant to Section [74-903](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-4/74-903) (Certain community facility uses in R3 to R9 Districts and certain Commercial Districts).

MAXIMUM FLOOR AREA RATIO FOR CERTAIN COMMUNITY FACILITY USES

| District | Maximum **_Floor Area Ratio_** Permitted |
| --- | --- |
| R3 | 0.50 |
| R4 | 0.75 |
| R5 R5A R5B | 1.27 |
| R5D | 2.00 |
| R6 | 2.43 |
| R7 | 3.44 |
| R8 | 6.02 |
| R9 | 7.52 |

(c) In R6 through R12 Districts with a letter suffix, the maximum **_floor area ratio_** on a **_zoning lot_** for philanthropic or non-profit institutions with sleeping accommodations shall be as set forth in Section [24-11](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-4/24-11) (Maximum Floor Area Ratio and Percentage of Lot Coverage).
