# 24-39 Other Special Provisions for Rear Yards

R1 R2 R3 R4 R5 R6 R7 R8 R9 R10 R11 R12

In all districts, as indicated, the **_rear yard_** requirements set forth in Section [24-36](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-4/24-36) (Minimum Required Rear Yards) shall be modified, as set forth in this Section, inclusive.
