# 24-59 Special Height Limitations

The provisions of this Section, inclusive, shall apply to **_buildings_** utilizing the **_sky exposure plane_** provisions of this Chapter. In no event shall such provisions apply to **_buildings_** that utilize the **_residential_** height and setback regulations of Section [23-40](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-3/23-40), inclusive.
