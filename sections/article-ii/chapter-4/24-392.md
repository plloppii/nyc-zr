# 24-392 Along short dimension of block

R6 R7 R8 R9 R10 R11 R12

In the districts indicated, whenever a **_front lot line_** of a **_zoning lot_** coincides with the **_street line_** of the **_short dimension of a block_**, no **_rear yard_** shall be required within 100 feet of such **_front lot line_**.
