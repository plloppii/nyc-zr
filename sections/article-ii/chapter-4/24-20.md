# 24-20 APPLICABILITY OF DENSITY REGULATIONS TO ZONING LOTS CONTAINING BOTH RESIDENTIAL AND COMMUNITY FACILITY USES

In all districts, the maximum number of **_dwelling units_** on a **_zoning lot_** containing both **_community facility_** and **_residential uses_** shall

be as set forth in Section [23-50](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-3/23-50) (DENSITY REGULATIONS).
