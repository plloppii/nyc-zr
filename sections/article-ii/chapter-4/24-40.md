# 24-40 SPECIAL PROVISIONS FOR ZONING LOTS DIVIDED BY DISTRICT BOUNDARIES

R1 R2 R3 R4 R5 R6 R7 R8 R9 R10 R11 R12

In all districts, as indicated, whenever a **_zoning lot_** is divided by a boundary between districts or is subject to other regulations resulting in different **_yard_** regulations on portions of the **_zoning lot_**, the provisions set forth in Article VII, Chapter 7, shall apply.
