# 24-391 Within one hundred feet of corners

R6 R7 R8 R9 R10 R11 R12

In the districts indicated, no **_rear yard_** shall be required within 100 feet of the point of intersection of two **_street lines_** intersecting at an angle of 135 degrees or less.
