# 24-52 Maximum Height of Walls and Required Setbacks

R1 R2 R3 R4 R5 R6 R7 R8 R9 R10 R11 R12

In all districts, as indicated, the maximum height of a front wall or of any other portion of a **_building or other structure_** shall be as set forth in this Section, except as otherwise provided in Section [24-51](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-4/24-51) (Permitted Obstructions), [24-53](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-4/24-53) (Alternate Front Setbacks) or [24-54](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-4/24-54) (Tower Regulations).
