# 26-20 SPECIAL REQUIREMENTS FOR LOTS WITH PRIVATE ROADS

To provide for the orderly development of **_residences_** that are distant from **_streets_**, site planning requirements are established in Sections [26-20](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-6/26-20) through [26-27](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-6/26-27), inclusive. The regulations of this Section are intended to:

(a) optimize vehicular access within and among **_zoning lots_** containing **_private roads_**;

(b) regulate the size of and distance between curb cuts to minimize undue conflict between pedestrian and vehicular movement;

(c) provide for sidewalks to facilitate social interaction and enhance pedestrian safety; and

(d) provide for tree planting along **_private roads_** in order to enhance the visual and environmental character of the neighborhood.
