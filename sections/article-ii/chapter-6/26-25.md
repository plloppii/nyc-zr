# 26-25 Parking Location and Curb Cuts Accessing Driveways

For the purposes of this Section, a **_private road_** shall be considered to be a **_street_**, and the applicable parking location and curb cut provisions of Section [25-62](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-5/25-62), inclusive, and Section [25-63](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-5/25-63), inclusive, shall be applied accordingly.
