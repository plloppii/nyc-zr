# 27-20 ANTI-HARASSMENT

Within the Greenpoint-Williamsburg **_anti-harassment areas_** in Community District 1, Borough of Brooklyn, as shown in the diagrams in this Section, the provisions of paragraphs (a) through (d), inclusive, of Section [93-90](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ix/chapter-3/93-90) (HARASSMENT) shall apply as modified in this Section.

For the purposes of this Section, the following definitions in paragraph (a) of Section [93-90](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ix/chapter-3/93-90), shall be modified:

Anti-harassment area

“Anti-harassment area” shall mean the Greenpoint-Williamsburg **_anti-harassment areas_** as shown in the diagrams:

![Section 27-20, Greenpoint-Williamsburg Anti-Harassment Areas, map 1](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/sites/default/files/23-013_1%20Anti-Hrassmnt_no%20strikeout-01.jpg)

![Section 27-20, Greenpoint-Williamsburg Anti-Harassment Areas, map 2](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/sites/default/files/27-20_2%20Anti-Hrassmnt_no%20strikeout-01.jpg)

Greenpoint-Williamsburg Anti-Harassment Areas

Referral date

“Referral date” shall mean October 4, 2004.

In addition, paragraph (d)(3) of Section [93-90](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ix/chapter-3/93-90) is modified as follows:

(3) No portion of the **_low income housing_** required under this Section shall qualify to:

(i) increase the **_floor area ratio_** pursuant to the provisions of Section [27-10](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-7/27-10) (ADMINISTRATION OF AFFORDABLE HOUSING) or [62-361](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-2/62-361) (Special floor area regulations); or

(ii) increase the maximum height of a **_building_** or the height above which the gross area per **_residential_** **_story_** of a **_building_** is limited pursuant to the provisions of paragraphs (b)(2) and (d) of Section [62-363](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-2/62-363) (Special height and setback regulations); or

(iii) satisfy an eligibility requirement of any real property tax abatement or exemption program with respect to any **_multiple dwelling_** that does not contain such **_low income housing_**.
