# 27-01 Applicability of This Chapter

The regulations of this Chapter shall apply:

(a) to the provision of affordable housing, as set forth in [27-10](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-7/27-10) (ADMINISTRATION OF AFFORDABLE HOUSING); and
(b) to anti-harassment areas, as set forth in [27-20](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-7/27-20) (ANTI-HARASSMENT).
