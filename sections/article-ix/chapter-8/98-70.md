# 98-70 SUPPLEMENTAL REGULATIONS

(a) In the **_Special West Chelsea District_**, the provisions of paragraphs (a) through (d), inclusive, of Section [93-90](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ix/chapter-3/93-90) (HARASSMENT) shall apply as modified in this Section.

(b) In the **_Special West Chelsea District_**, the provisions of Section [93-91](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ix/chapter-3/93-91) (Demolition) shall apply.

For the purposes of this Section, the following definitions in Section [93-90](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ix/chapter-3/93-90) shall be modified:

Anti-harassment area

“Anti-harassment area” shall mean the **_Special West Chelsea District_**.

Referral date

“Referral date” shall mean December 20, 2004.

In addition, Section [93-90](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ix/chapter-3/93-90), paragraph (d)(3), is modified as follows:

No portion of the **_low income housing_** required under this Section shall qualify to:

(a) increase the **_floor area ratio_** pursuant to the provisions of Section [27-10](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-7/27-10) (ADMINISTRATION OF AFFORDABLE HOUSING), inclusive, as modified by the provisions of the **_Special West Chelsea District_**, **_Special Hudson Yards District_** or **_Special Clinton District_**; or

(b) satisfy an eligibility requirement of any real property tax abatement or exemption program with respect to any **_multiple dwelling_** that does not contain such **_low income housing_**.
