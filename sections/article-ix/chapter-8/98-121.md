# 98-121 In Subarea H

In Subarea H, the provisions of Section [32-19](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-2/32-19) (Use Group IX - Storage), inclusive, are modified to permit, in C6 Districts, warehouse **_uses_** only in **_cellars_** located wholly below **_curb level_**.
