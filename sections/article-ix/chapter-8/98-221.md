# 98-221 Additional regulations for Subdistrict A

In Subdistrict A, for **_zoning lots_** containing a **_building_** that is **_developed_** or **_enlarged_** pursuant to the applicable tower regulations of Section [98-423](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ix/chapter-8/98-423) (Street wall location, minimum and maximum base heights and maximum building heights), the provisions of Section [23-241](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-3/23-241) (Special tower provisions) shall apply:

(a) to only the **_residential_** portion of a **_building_** where less than 75 percent of the total **_floor area_** of such **_building_** is allocated to **_residential use_**; and
(b) to the entire **_building_** where 75 percent or more of the total **_floor area_** of such **_building_** is allocated to **_residential use_**.
