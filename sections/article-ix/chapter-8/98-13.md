# 98-13 Modification of Use Regulations in M1 Districts

In the **_Special West Chelsea District_**, the provisions of Section [42-10](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iv/chapter-2/42-10) (USE ALLOWANCES), inclusive, are modified to permit, as-of-right, without limitation, in M1 Districts, museums listed under Use Group III.
