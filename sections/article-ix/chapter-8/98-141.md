# 98-141 Transparency requirements within Subareas H and I

The transparency requirements of this Section shall apply to all portions of **_buildings_** **_developed_** or **_enlarged_** after June 23, 2005, within the **_High Line frontage_** of Subareas H and I, except for such portions that contain **_dwelling units_**. At least 70 percent of the area of such frontage, to be measured from a point not lower than four feet and not higher than eight feet above the level of the **_High Line bed_** shall be glazed and transparent and at least 75 percent of such glazed surface shall be fully transparent.
