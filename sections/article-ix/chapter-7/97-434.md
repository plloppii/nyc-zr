# 97-434 Height and setback regulations in Subdistrict A

Within Subdistrict A, as shown on Map 1 in Appendix A of this Chapter, the underlying height and setback regulations shall apply, except that the **_street wall_** provisions of paragraph (a) of Section [35-631](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-5/35-631) (Street wall location) shall apply on **_wide street_** frontages in C4-7 Districts. The minimum and maximum base heights and the overall maximum **_building_** height provisions of Section [35-632](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-5/35-632), inclusive, shall be modified in accordance with the following table:

| **MINIMUM BASE HEIGHT, MAXIMUM BASE HEIGHT AND MAXIMUM BUILDING HEIGHT** |  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **District** | **_Street Wall_** Height (in feet) |  | Maximum Height of **_Building or Other Structure_** (in feet) |
| **District** | **Minimum Base Height** | **Maximum Base Height** | Maximum Height of **_Building or Other Structure_** (in feet) |
| C4-7 | 60 | 85 | 245 |

Above the maximum base height, a setback shall be provided in accordance with the provisions of Section 23-433.
