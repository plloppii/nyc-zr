# 97-50 SPECIAL OFF-STREET PARKING AND OFF-STREET LOADING REGULATIONS

The underlying provisions of Article II, Chapter 5, Article III, Chapter 6 and Article IV, Chapter 4 (Accessory Off-street Parking and Off-street Loading Regulations) shall apply within the **_Special 125th Street District_**, subject to modification by the regulations of this Section, inclusive.

Enclosed parking spaces, or parking spaces covered by a **_building_**, including such spaces **_accessory_** to **_residences_** shall be permitted to occupy the ground floor provided they are located beyond 30 feet of the **_street wall_** of the **_building_**.

The applicable district regulations for the location of **_accessory_** off-street parking spaces along 125th Street within the Special District may be modified, so that such facilities may be provided off-site, within a **_Commercial District_**, but at a distance no greater than 1,200 feet from the **_zoning lot_**.
