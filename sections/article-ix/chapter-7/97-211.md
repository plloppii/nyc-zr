# 97-211 Location of and Access to Arts and Entertainment Uses

Any arts and entertainment **_uses_** listed in Section [97-11](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ix/chapter-7/97-11) that are provided in order to comply with the requirements of Section [97-12](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ix/chapter-7/97-12) (Arts and Entertainment Use Requirement) or Section [97-422](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ix/chapter-7/97-422) (Floor area bonus for visual or performing arts uses) shall be subject to the following location and access requirements:

The designated **_uses_** listed in Section [97-11](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ix/chapter-7/97-11) may be located anywhere throughout a **_building_** that fronts on 125th Street, provided that:

(a) any such designated **_uses_** within the Core Subdistrict required pursuant to Section [97-12](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ix/chapter-7/97-12) shall be accessed from 125th Street; and
(b) in **_mixed buildings_**, the provisions of Section [32-422](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-2/32-422) (Location of floors occupied by commercial uses) shall apply. However, such provisions shall be modified where **_commercial uses_** are located on the same **_story_** as a **_dwelling unit_** such that the limitations set forth in paragraphs (a) and (b) of such Section need not apply.
