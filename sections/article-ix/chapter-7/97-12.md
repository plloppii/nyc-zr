# 97-12 Arts and Entertainment Use Requirement

Within the Core Subdistrict, as shown on Map 1 in Appendix A of this Chapter, or for that portion of a **_zoning lot_** located within the Core Subdistrict, for **_buildings_** or portions of **_buildings_** **_developed_** or **_enlarged_** after April 30, 2008, that contain at least 60,000 square feet of **_floor area_** and are located on **_zoning lots_** with frontage on 125th Street, an amount of space equivalent to a minimum of five percent of the **_floor area_** of the **_development_** or **_enlargement_** shall be occupied by one or more of the **_uses_** designated in Section [97-11](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ix/chapter-7/97-11) (Special Arts and Entertainment Uses).
