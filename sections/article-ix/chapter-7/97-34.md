# 97-34 Accessory Signs for Visual or Performing Arts Uses

Notwithstanding the regulations of paragraph (b) of Section [32-653](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-2/32-653) (Additional regulations for projecting signs) and the relevant provisions of the Administrative Code, only the following visual or performing arts **_uses_** fronting on 125th Street or Fifth Avenue within the **_Special 125th Street District_** shall be permitted to erect a **_marquee sign_** on or above a **_marquee_**:

From Use Group III

Museums

From Use Group VIII

Art, music, dancing or theatrical studios

Theaters.

**_Flashing signs_** shall not be permitted as **_accessory_** **_signs_** for arts **_uses_**.
