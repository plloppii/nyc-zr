# 97-53 Parking Access Through Zoning Lots in Residence Districts

For a **_zoning lot_** within a **_Residence District_**, which **_zoning lot_** fronts upon either 124th or 126th Street and the **_rear lot line_** **_abuts_** a **_zoning lot_** that fronts only on 125th Street, and such **_zoning lot_** has been vacant since April 30, 2008, access for parking and loading purposes may be provided through such **_zoning lot_**.
