# 93-124 Restrictions on conversions of residential use

In **_Commercial Districts_** mapped within R8A Districts, a **_residential use_** existing on December 21, 2005, within a **_story_** that has a floor level within five feet of **_curb level_**, may not be **_converted_** to a **_commercial use_**.
