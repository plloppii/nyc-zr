# 93-134 C6-4M Districts in Subdistrict H

In the C6-4M District located within Subdistrict H, for **_buildings_** existing on January 19, 2005, the **_use_** regulations of the underlying district shall be modified as follows:

The following **_uses_** shall be allowed:

From Use Group IX

All **_uses_** listed under Use Group IX(A) that are permitted in a C8 District

From Use Group X

All **_uses_** listed under Use Group X that are permitted in a C8 District.
