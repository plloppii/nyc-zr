# 93-054 Applicability of Article VII, Chapter 4

(a) The following special permits by the City Planning Commission shall not be applicable:
    Section [74-142](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-4/74-142) (Electric utility substations) shall not apply. In lieu thereof, such **_uses_** shall be allowed within the **_Special Hudson Yards District_** upon authorization of the City Planning Commission pursuant to Section [93-18](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ix/chapter-3/93-18) (Authorization for Electrical Utility Substations
    Section [74-61](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-4/74-61) (Developments on Lots that Include Railroad Right-of-Ways)
    Section [74-72](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-4/74-72) (Bulk Modification)
    Section [74-74](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-4/74-74) (Large-scale General Development) shall be inapplicable in the Large-scale Plan Subdistrict A
    Section [74-821](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-4/74-821) (Court houses)
    Section [74-831](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-4/74-831) (Development in certain Commercial Districts)
    Section [74-85](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-4/74-85) (Covered Pedestrian Space)
    Section [74-91](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-4/74-91) (Modification of Public Plazas).
(b) The following provisions regarding special permits by the City Planning Commission shall be applicable as modified:
    Section [74-194](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-4/74-194) (Public parking garages or public parking lots in high density central areas) shall be applicable to the renewal of City Planning Commission special permits for **_public parking lots_** and **_public parking garages_** granted prior to April 14, 2010.
    Section [74-81](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-4/74-81) (Through Block Arcades) shall apply to any **_development_** or **_enlargement_** for which a **_through block arcade_** would not otherwise be permitted pursuant to this Chapter, except that no **_floor area_** bonus shall be permitted.
