# 93-224 Maximum floor area ratio in the South of Port Authority Subdistrict E

In the South of Port Authority Subdistrict E, **_residential use_** shall only be permitted as part of a **_development_** or **_enlargement_** on a **_zoning lot_** with a **_commercial_** **_floor area ratio_** of 15.0 or more, or as provided for phased **_developments_** in Section [93-122](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ix/chapter-3/93-122) (Certification for residential use in Subdistricts A, B and E). The maximum **_residential_** **_floor area ratio_** for **_zoning lots_** containing standard **_residences shall be as set forth in Row C. For_** zoning lots **_containing_** qualifying affordable housing **_or_** qualifying senior housing**_, the maximum_** residential floor area ratio# shall be 3.6.

For **_developments_** or **_enlargements_** on **_qualifying transit improvement sites_**, a **_floor area_** bonus for **_mass transit station_** improvements may be granted by the City Planning Commission pursuant to the provisions of Section [66-51](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-6/66-51) (Additional Floor Area for Mass Transit Station Improvements). As a pre-condition to applying for such **_floor area_** bonus, the applicant shall demonstrate that a **_floor area ratio_** of no less than 0.1 of the maximum **_floor area ratio_** set forth in Row C of Section [93-22](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ix/chapter-3/93-22) (Floor Area Regulations in Subdistricts B, C, D, E, F, G and H), has been achieved, prior to, or in conjunction with, the application. For the purposes of this paragraph, defined terms additionally include those in Section [66-11](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-6/66-11) (Definitions).
