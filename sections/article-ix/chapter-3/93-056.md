# 93-056 Modification of use and bulk regulations for zoning lots bounding Hudson Boulevard Park

Where the **_lot line_** of a **_zoning lot_** coincides with the boundary of the **_public parks_** located between West 35th Street, Hudson Boulevard East, West 33rd Street and Eleventh Avenue, such **_lot line_** shall be considered to be the **_street line_** of Hudson Boulevard West for the purposes of applying all **_use_** and **_bulk_** regulations of this Resolution.

Where the **_lot line_** of a **_zoning lot_** coincides with the boundary of the **_public park_** located between West 39th Street, Tenth Avenue, West 38th Street and Eleventh Avenue, such **_lot line_** shall be considered to be the **_street line_** of Hudson Boulevard East and West, as applicable, for the purposes of applying all **_use_** and **_bulk_** regulations of this Resolution.
