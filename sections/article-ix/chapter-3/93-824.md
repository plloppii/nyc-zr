# 93-824 Publication of data

The Department of City Planning shall make available, in a form easily accessed by the public, regularly updated calculations of the current **_Hudson Yards development parking supply_**, **_reservoir parking supply_**, spaces described in paragraphs (e)(2)(i) and (e)(2)(ii) of Section [93-821](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ix/chapter-3/93-821), and **_reservoir surplus_** or **_reservoir deficit_**, as applicable.
