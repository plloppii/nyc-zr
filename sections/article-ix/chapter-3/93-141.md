# 93-141 Certification to modify ground floor level requirements in Subdistrict F

Within Subdistrict F, the Chairperson of the City Planning Commission may modify the ground floor level requirements of Section [93-14](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ix/chapter-3/93-14) (Ground Floor Level Requirements), provided that the Chairperson certifies to the Commissioner of Buildings that such a change is the minimum necessary to accommodate the ventilation requirements of the below-grade rail operations. Any application for such change shall include a mechanical plan that conveys the extent of the needs and required modifications, as well as a letter from the Metropolitan Transportation Authority describing the needs for such modifications.
