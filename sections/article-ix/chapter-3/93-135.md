# 93-135 Vehicle storage establishments

Within Subdistrict G, commercial or public vehicle storage, including **_accessory_** motor fuel pumps listed under Use Group IX(C) shall be permitted as-of-right, applicable to a C8 District. The **_floor area_** of a **_building_** shall not include floor space used for public utility vehicle storage provided in any **_story_** located not more than 56 feet above **_curb level_**.
