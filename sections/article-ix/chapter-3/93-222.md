# 93-222 Maximum floor area ratio in the 34th Street Corridor Subdistrict C

In the 34th Street Corridor Subdistrict C, the basic maximum **_floor area ratios_** of non-**_residential buildings_** are set forth in Row A in the table in Section [93-22](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ix/chapter-3/93-22) and may be increased to the amount specified in Row C pursuant to Section [93-31](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ix/chapter-3/93-31) (District Improvement Fund Bonus). The basic maximum **_floor area ratios_** of any **_building_** containing **_residences_** are set forth in Row B.

The **_floor area ratio_** of any **_building_** containing **_residences_** may be increased from 6.5, as follows:

(a) the **_residential_** **_floor area ratio_** may be increased to a maximum of 12.0 where the following are met:
    (1) an amount of **_floor area_** equal to at least 20 percent of the total **_residential_** **_floor area_** is allocated to **_qualifying affordable housing_** or **_qualifying senior housing_**;
    (2) a **_floor area_** increase equal to a **_floor area ratio_** of 2.5 has been earned pursuant to Section [93-31](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ix/chapter-3/93-31) (District Improvement Fund Bonus); and
(b) any **_floor area_** increase above a **_floor area ratio_** of 12.0 shall be only pursuant to Section [93-31](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ix/chapter-3/93-31).

For **_developments_** or **_enlargements_** on **_qualifying transit improvement sites_**, a **_floor area_** bonus for **_mass transit station_** improvements may be granted by the City Planning Commission pursuant to the provisions of Section [66-51](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-6/66-51) (Additional Floor Area for Mass Transit Station Improvements). As a pre-condition to applying for such **_floor area_** bonus, the applicant shall demonstrate that a **_floor area ratio_** of no less than 0.1 of the maximum **_floor area ratio_** set forth in Row C of Section [93-22](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ix/chapter-3/93-22) (Floor Area Regulations in Subdistricts B, C, D, E, F, G and H), has been achieved prior to, or in conjunction with, the application. For the purposes of this paragraph, defined terms additionally include those in Section [66-11](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-6/66-11) (Definitions).
