# 94-082 Special parking bonus

In Areas C, D or E, any **_development_** on a **_zoning lot_** with a minimum area of 20,000 square feet shall be eligible for a **_floor area_** bonus at the rate of one square foot of **_floor area_** for every square foot of **_accessory_** **_commercial_** parking space above the minimum amount required by the underlying district regulations and made available for daily long-term parking.

To be eligible for a **_floor area_** bonus under the provisions of this Section, there shall be at least five additional parking spaces provided and the size of each parking space shall be at least 300 square feet. In no event shall the dimension of any parking stall be less than 18 feet long and 8 feet, 6 inches wide.
