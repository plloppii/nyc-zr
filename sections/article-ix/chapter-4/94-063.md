# 94-063 Additional sign regulations

Where **_illuminated signs_** are permitted by the underlying district regulations, such **_signs_** shall have only indirect illumination. Where **_signs_**, other than **_advertising signs_**, are permitted by the underlying district regulations, such **_signs_** shall not extend above the roof level of any **_building or other structure_** in the Special District.
