# 96-106 Alterations of buildings

Prior to the issuance of an alteration permit by the Department of Buildings for a **_material alteration_** of a **_multiple dwelling_** within the Preservation Area, the Department of Housing Preservation and Development shall certify to the Department of Buildings that:

(a) prior to evicting or otherwise terminating the occupancy of any tenant preparatory to alteration, the owner shall have notified the Commissioner of his or her intention to alter the **_building_**; and

(b) the Department of Housing Preservation and Development has issued a **_certification of no harassment_** or that the owner has complied with paragraph (d) of Section 96-107.

However, a permit for alterations may be exempted from the provisions of this Section by written determination of the Department of Housing Preservation and Development that such alterations are to be performed solely for the purpose of either:

(1) making the public areas of a **_multiple dwelling_** accessible to persons with disabilities without altering the configuration of any **_dwelling unit_** or **_rooming unit_**; or

(2) making a **_dwelling unit_** or a **_rooming unit_** accessible to persons with disabilities.

For the purposes of this Section, **_dwelling unit_** and **_rooming unit_** shall be defined as in Section 96-107.
