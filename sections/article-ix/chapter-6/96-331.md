# 96-331 Adult establishments

The provisions of Section [52-77](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-v/chapter-2/52-77) (Termination of Adult Establishments) shall not apply to any **_adult establishment_** that located within the **_Special Clinton District_** after October 25, 1995, and prior to May 11, 2011, and which, as of May 11, 2011, and June 14, 2011, was an existing **_use_** and conformed to all provisions of Section [42-01](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iv/chapter-2/42-01) (Special Provisions for Adult Establishments) applicable to M2-4 Districts.
