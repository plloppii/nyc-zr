# 96-102 Yard regulations

The underlying **_yard_** regulations shall apply except that on a **_through lot_**, the alternative location allowances of paragraph (c)(2) of Section [23-343](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-3/23-343) (Rear yard equivalent requirements) shall not apply
