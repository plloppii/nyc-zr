# 96-34 Special Regulations in Northern Subarea C1

Within Northern Subarea C1, Special Use Regulations Areas C1-1 and C1-2, as shown on the map in Appendix A of this Chapter, the following **_uses_** shall be permitted below the level of the lowest floor occupied by **_dwelling units_**:

(a) automobile dealers listed under Use Group VI with preparation of automobiles for delivery; and
(b) automobile repair and maintenance listed under Use Group VI.
