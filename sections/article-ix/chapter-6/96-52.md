# 96-52 Bulk Modifications for Public Parking Garages

Except within the Eighth Avenue Perimeter Area set forth in Section [96-22](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ix/chapter-6/96-22) (Special Regulations for Eighth Avenue Perimeter Area), in all other C6 Districts, the City Planning Commission, by special permit, may permit, for **_public parking garages_**, modification of the applicable **_lot coverage_**, **_yard_** and height and setback regulations. As a condition of permitting such modifications, the Commission shall make the following findings:

(a) that, because of site limitations, such modifications are necessary for the proper design and operation of the **_public parking garage_**; and

(b) that, such modifications will not unduly obstruct access to light and air in the **_street_** or on adjacent **_zoning lots_**.

The Commission shall consider the characteristics of surrounding development and may prescribe appropriate conditions and safeguards to minimize adverse effects on the character of adjacent areas.
