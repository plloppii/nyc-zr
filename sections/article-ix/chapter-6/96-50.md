# 96-50 REGULATIONS APPLICABLE TO ALL AREAS

The provisions of Sections [96-51](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ix/chapter-6/96-51) (Mandatory Tree Planting Provisions) and [96-52](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ix/chapter-6/96-52) (Bulk Modifications for Public Parking Garages) shall apply to all areas within the Special District.
