# 96-108 Off-street parking regulations

**_Accessory_** off-street parking spaces, **_public parking lots_** or **_public parking garages_** are not permitted within the Preservation Area except by the applicable special permit in Section [13-45](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-i/chapter-3/13-45) (Special Permits for Additional Parking Spaces), inclusive.

In addition, the Commission shall find that:

(a) the property has been or will be vacated pursuant to the provisions of Section [96-108](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ix/chapter-6/96-108); and
(b) the applicant has followed the relocation procedures set forth in Section [96-24](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ix/chapter-6/96-24).
