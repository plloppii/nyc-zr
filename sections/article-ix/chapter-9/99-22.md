# 99-22 Special Height and Setback Regulations

The underlying height and setback regulations applicable to a C5-1A District shall apply except as modified in this Section. The **_street wall_** location provisions of paragraph (a) of Section [35-631](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-5/35-631) shall apply to all **_street_** frontages. For **_zoning lots_** that do not contain **_qualifying affordable housing_** or **_qualifying senior housing_**, the gross area of each **_story_** located completely above a height of 170 feet shall not exceed 80 percent of the gross area of the **_story_** directly below it.
