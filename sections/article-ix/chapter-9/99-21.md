# 99-21 Special Floor Area Regulations

The underlying **_floor area_** regulations shall apply except as modified in this Section.

For **_developments_** or **_enlargements_** on **_qualifying transit improvement sites_**, a **_floor area_** bonus for **_mass transit station_** improvements may be granted by the City Planning Commission pursuant to the provisions of Section [66-51](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-6/66-51) (Additional Floor Area for Mass Transit Station Improvements). For the purposes of this paragraph, defined terms additionally include those in Section [66-11](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-6/66-11) (Definitions). No other **_floor area_** bonuses shall be permitted.
