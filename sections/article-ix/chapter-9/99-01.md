# 99-01 Definitions

For purposes of this Chapter, matter in italics is defined in Sections [12-10](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-i/chapter-2/12-10), [32-301](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-2/32-301) or within this Section.

Development

For purposes of this Chapter, a "development" includes the construction of a new **_building or other structure_** on a **_zoning lot_**, the relocation of an existing **_building_** on another **_zoning lot_**, and an **_enlargement_**.

Landmark building

A "landmark building" is any **_building_** designated as a landmark by the Landmarks Preservation Commission, pursuant to procedures set forth in Section 3020 of the New York City Charter and other applicable laws.

Style building

A "style building" is a **_building_** possessing an architectural style, as described in the Upper East Side Historic District Designation Report prepared by the New York City Landmarks Preservation Commission in 1981.
