# 91-837 Subsequent design changes

Design changes to any **_publicly accessible open area_**, required open area or indoor public space previously improved pursuant to the provisions of Sections [91-832](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ix/chapter-1/91-832) (Plaza improvements) or [91-834](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ix/chapter-1/91-834) (Indoor public spaces) may only be made upon certification by the Chairperson of the City Planning Commission that such changes would result in a **_publicly accessible open area_** or indoor public space that is compliant with the Section under which it was previously approved. As part of the certification, a horizontal **_enlargement_** on the ground floor level may be permitted along existing **_building_** walls that face the **_publicly accessible open area_** and do not face an **_arcade_**, but such **_enlargement_** shall not occupy any **_publicly accessible open area_**. The locational requirements of paragraph (a)(1) of Section [91-831](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ix/chapter-1/91-831) (Ground floor requirements) and the **_use_** and transparency requirements of Section [91-831](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ix/chapter-1/91-831) for new **_building_** walls facing a **_publicly accessible open area_** shall apply. The legal requirements of paragraph (a) of Section [91-836](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ix/chapter-1/91-836) shall apply.
