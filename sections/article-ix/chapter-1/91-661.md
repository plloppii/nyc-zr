# 91-661 Bulk modifications in C6-2A Districts

Within the South Street Seaport Subdistrict, for any **_zoning lot_** located in a C6-2A District, the underlying height and setback regulations shall apply, except that no minimum base height shall apply, and the depth of a required setback along a **_narrow street_** shall be at least 10 feet.
