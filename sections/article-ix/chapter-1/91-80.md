# 91-80 PUBLIC ACCESS AREAS

The following regulations shall apply to **_arcades_** and **_publicly accessible open areas_** existing on June 21, 2016, located within the Water Street Subdistrict as shown on Map 8 in Appendix A of this Chapter except for the **_plaza_** that was the subject of special permit application CP-20518, approved by the City Planning Commission on November 27, 1968.

For the purposes of this Section, inclusive, “arcade” shall refer to an **_arcade_** or **_through block arcade_** provided in accordance with the provisions of Sections [12-10](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-i/chapter-2/12-10) (DEFINITIONS) and [37-80](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-7/37-80) (ARCADES), or any other **_arcade_** that generated a **_floor area_** bonus as evidenced by plans approved by the Department of Buildings.

A horizontal **_enlargement_** permitted by Sections [91-83](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ix/chapter-1/91-83) (Retail Uses Within Existing Arcades), inclusive, or [91-841](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ix/chapter-1/91-841) (Authorization for retail uses within existing arcades) shall not be included as **_floor area_**, and such additional area shall not result in a reduction of the permitted **_floor area_**.

No **_arcade_** may be eliminated or reduced in size pursuant to paragraphs (a) or (d) of Section [33-124](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-3/33-124) (Existing public amenities for which floor area bonuses have been received). In lieu thereof the following provisions shall apply: Sections [91-83](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ix/chapter-1/91-83), [91-841](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ix/chapter-1/91-841) and, as applicable, Section [91-85](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ix/chapter-1/91-85) (Special Permit for Enlargements of 7,500 Square Feet or Greater).

For any **_zoning lot_** that was the subject of application C810325ZSM, C810506ZSM or C841070ZSM, a certification pursuant to Section [91-83](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ix/chapter-1/91-83) or an authorization pursuant to Section [91-841](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ix/chapter-1/91-841) shall not result in a departure from the findings and conditions specified in the applicable special permit, and such certification or authorization shall not require modification of the applicable special permit unless such a modification is required pursuant to a related restrictive declaration. For the **_zoning lot_** that was the subject of application C810325ZSM, the existing **_through block arcade_** shall not be eliminated, but may be modified in size and configuration provided that the standards for **_through block arcades_** set forth in Section [12-10](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-i/chapter-2/12-10) are met.

Public events may take place within a **_publicly accessible open area_** or **_arcade_** pursuant to the provisions of Section [91-81](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ix/chapter-1/91-81) (Events Within Public Access Areas). Publicly accessible tables, chairs, shade umbrellas and heating lamps may be located within a **_publicly accessible open area_** or **_arcade_** pursuant to the provisions of Section [91-82](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ix/chapter-1/91-82) (Amenities Within Public Access Areas). An outdoor cafe may be located within an **_arcade_** pursuant to Section [91-821](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ix/chapter-1/91-821) (Certification for outdoor cafes within arcades).

A horizontal **_enlargement_** of the ground floor and second floor levels may be permitted within **_arcades_**, or portions thereof, located within Area A in Map 9 of Appendix A of this Chapter pursuant to the provisions of Section [91-83](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ix/chapter-1/91-83), and within Area B pursuant to the provisions of Section [91-841](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ix/chapter-1/91-841). In addition, a horizontal **_enlargement_** of 7,500 square feet or greater shall also require a special permit pursuant to Section [91-85](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ix/chapter-1/91-85). For the purposes of calculating the total area of the horizontal **_enlargement_** that is subject to the special permit, the aggregate area of the horizontal **_enlargement_** permitted by prior certifications pursuant to Sections [91-83](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ix/chapter-1/91-83) and [91-837](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ix/chapter-1/91-837) (Subsequent design changes) and prior authorizations pursuant to Section [91-841](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ix/chapter-1/91-841) shall be included in such calculation, except the area of an indoor public space shall be excluded from such calculation. In no event shall an **_enlargement_** be permitted within **_arcades_**, or portions thereof, located within Area C on Map 9 in Appendix A of this Chapter.
