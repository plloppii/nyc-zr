# 91-61 General Provisions

The regulations of Section [91-60](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ix/chapter-1/91-60), et seq., relating to special regulations for the South Street Seaport Subdistrict are applicable only in the South Street Seaport Subdistrict. The boundaries of the South Street Seaport Subdistrict are shown on Map 1 (Special Lower Manhattan District) and Map 6 (South Street Seaport Subdistrict) in Appendix A. The regulations of Section [91-60](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ix/chapter-1/91-60), et seq., supplement or modify the regulations of this Chapter applying in general to the South Street Seaport Subdistrict area of the **_Special Lower Manhattan District_**.

In order to preserve and protect the character of the South Street Seaport Subdistrict and to implement the provisions of the Brooklyn Bridge Southeast Urban Renewal Plan, as amended, special controls and incentives are provided.

The provisions of Article VI, Chapter 2 (Special Regulations in the Waterfront Area), shall apply to **_waterfront zoning lots_** within the South Street Seaport Subdistrict. The provisions of paragraph (a) of Section [75-422](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-5/75-422) (Certification to transfer development rights from landmarks) concerning the transfer of development rights from landmark sites in C5-3, C5-5, C6-6, C6-7 or C6-9 Districts shall not apply in the South Street Seaport Subdistrict.
