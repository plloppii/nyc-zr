# 91-11 Sign Regulations

In the **_Special Lower Manhattan District_**, except as modified by the provisions of this Section, inclusive, the regulations of Section [32-60](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-2/32-60), et seq., pertaining to **_signs_**, shall apply.
