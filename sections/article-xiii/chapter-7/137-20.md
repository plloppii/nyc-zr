# 137-20 SPECIAL USE REGULATIONS

The special **_use_** regulations of this Section [137-20](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-xiii/chapter-7/137-20), inclusive, shall apply in the **_Special Coastal Risk Districts_** as set forth in the table in Section [137-12](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-xiii/chapter-7/137-12) (Applicability of Special Regulations).
