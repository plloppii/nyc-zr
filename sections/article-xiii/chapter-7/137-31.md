# 137-31 Minimum Lot Width

In **_Special Coastal Risk District_** 2, the regulations of Section [23-11](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-3/23-11) (Lot Area and Lot Width in R1 Through R5 Districts) are modified such that the minimum **_lot width_** for a **_two-family detached residence_** in an R3A District shall be 40 feet.
