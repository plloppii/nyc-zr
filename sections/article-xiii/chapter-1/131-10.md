# 131-10 SPECIAL USE REGULATIONS

The special **_use_** regulations set forth in this Section, inclusive, shall modify the underlying **_Commercial Districts_**, as applicable.

For the purposes of this Chapter, “ground floor level” shall mean the finished floor level within five feet of an adjacent public sidewalk or any other publicly accessible open space.

In C7 Districts, **_outdoor amusement parks_** listed under Use Group VIII, of any size, shall be permitted.
