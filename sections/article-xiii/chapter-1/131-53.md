# 131-53 Curb Cuts

No curb cuts shall be permitted on Surf Avenue, Wonder Wheel Way or Bowery except on a **_zoning lot_** with no frontage on any other **_street_**. The curb cut provisions of paragraph (c) of Section [36-57](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-6/36-57) shall apply.
