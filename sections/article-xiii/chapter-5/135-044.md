# 135-044 Applicability of Article VI, Chapter 6

Notwithstanding the general provisions of Section [135-01](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-xiii/chapter-5/135-01), for **_transit-adjacent sites_**, as defined in Section [66-11](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-6/66-11) (Definitions), in the event of a conflict between the provisions of this Chapter and the provisions of Article VI, Chapter 6 (Special Regulations Applying Around Mass Transit Stations), the provisions of Article VI, Chapter 6 shall control.
