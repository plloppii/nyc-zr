# 134-15 Authorization for Certain Commercial Uses

The City Planning Commission may authorize any **_commercial_** **_use_** not allowed pursuant to Section [134-11](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-xiii/chapter-4/134-11), inclusive, to locate within the Northern Subdistrict, Eastern Subarea or the Western Subarea of the **_Special Governors Island District_**, provided that such **_commercial_** **_use_**:

(a) will promote the goals of the **_Special Governors Island District_**;

(b) will complement existing **_uses_** within the special district; and

(c) is compatible with the nature, scale and character of other **_uses_** within its subdistrict.

Any application to authorize a **_commercial_** **_use_** in the Eastern Subarea or Western Subarea pursuant to this Section shall be referred to the local community board. The City Planning Commission shall not grant such authorization prior to forty-five (45) days after the referral.
