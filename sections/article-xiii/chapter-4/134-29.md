# 134-29 Authorization for Bulk Modifications in the Southern Subdistrict

The City Planning Commission may authorize modification of the provisions of Sections [134-25](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-xiii/chapter-4/134-25) through [134-28](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-xiii/chapter-4/134-28), provided that the Commission finds that such modification:

(a) will promote the goals of the **_Special Governors Island District_**; and

(b) will result in a superior urban design relationship with surrounding ways, **_buildings_** and public open areas or provide an equivalent or better distribution of **_bulk_** on the **_building parcel_**.

Any application pursuant to this Section shall be referred to the local Community Board. The Commission shall not grant such authorization prior to forty-five (45) days following the referral.
