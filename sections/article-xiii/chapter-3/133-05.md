# 133-05 Applicability of Special Regulations Applying in the Waterfront Area

The provisions of Article VI, Chapter 2 (Special Regulations Applying in the Waterfront Area), shall not apply in the **_Special Southern Roosevelt Island District_**. In lieu thereof, the area between the **_shoreline_** and the western **_street line_** of the **_West Loop Road_** and the area between the **_shoreline_** and the eastern **_street line_** of the **_East Loop Road_** shall be used exclusively for open recreational uses, and shall be accessible to the public at all times.
