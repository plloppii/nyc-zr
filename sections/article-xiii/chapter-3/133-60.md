# 133-60 MODIFICATION OF PUBLIC ACCESS AREAS

Any public access area may be modified, eliminated or reconfigured over time, provided that such modification, elimination or reconfiguration does not reduce the amount of public access area required under Section [133-60](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-xiii/chapter-3/133-60) (PHASING) for the amount of **_floor area_** located on the **_development parcel_** at the time of such activity. Any modified or reconfigured public access area shall comply with the applicable provisions of Section [133-30](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-xiii/chapter-3/133-30), inclusive.
