# 133-234 Permitted obstructions

The underlying permitted obstructions to height and setback allowances as set forth in Sections [23-41](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-3/23-41), [24-51](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-4/24-51) and [33-42](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-3/33-42) (Permitted Obstructions) shall be modified to allow **_energy infrastructure equipment_** and **_accessory_** mechanical equipment on the roof of a **_building_**, or any other structures supporting such systems, as permitted obstructions, without limitations.
