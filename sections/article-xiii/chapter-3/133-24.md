# 133-24 Distance Between Buildings

The requirements of Section [23-371](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-3/23-371) (Distance between buildings) shall not apply, provided that if two or more **_buildings_** or portions of **_buildings_** are detached from one another at any level, such **_buildings_**, or such detached portions of **_buildings_**, shall at no point be less than eight feet apart at or below a height of 180 feet, and shall at no point be less than 60 feet apart above a height of 180 feet.
