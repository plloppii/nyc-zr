# 136-23 Maximum Building Height

R6 R7-1

In the districts indicated, and in C2 Districts mapped within these districts, the height of a **_building or other structure_** shall not exceed the maximum height shown on Map 4 (Maximum Building Height) in the Appendix to this Chapter.
