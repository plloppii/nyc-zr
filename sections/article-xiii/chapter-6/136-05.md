# 136-05 Applicability of the Mandatory Inclusionary Housing Program

R6 R7-1

In the districts indicated, and in C2 Districts mapped within these districts, the regulations for a **_Mandatory Inclusionary Housing area_** shall apply. The locations of **_Mandatory Inclusionary Housing areas_** are shown on the maps in APPENDIX F of this Resolution.
