# 136-11 Location Within Buildings

Within the locations shown on Map 2 (Commercial Core) in the Appendix to this Chapter, the provisions of Section [32-421](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-2/32-421) (Limitation on floors occupied by commercial uses) shall not apply. In lieu thereof, the provisions of Section [32-422](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-2/32-422) (Location of floors occupied by commercial uses) shall apply.
