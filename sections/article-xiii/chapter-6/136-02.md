# 136-02 Definitions

For purposes of this Chapter, matter in italics is defined in Sections [12-10](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-i/chapter-2/12-10) (DEFINITIONS), [32-301](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-2/32-301) (Definitions), or in this Section.

Open Space A

“Open Space A” shall be a publicly accessible open space designed and constructed pursuant to the provisions of Section [136-324](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-xiii/chapter-6/136-324) (Publicly accessible open space requirements) and located within the area designated as “Flexible Open Space A Location” on Map 7 (Mandatory Street Walls and Flexible Public Open Space Locations) in the Appendix to this Chapter.

Open Space B

“Open Space B” shall be a publicly accessible open space designed and constructed pursuant to the provisions of Section [136-324](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-xiii/chapter-6/136-324) (Publicly accessible open space requirements) and located within the area designated as “Flexible Open Space B Location” on Map 7 (Mandatory Street Walls and Flexible Public Open Space Locations) in the Appendix to this Chapter.
