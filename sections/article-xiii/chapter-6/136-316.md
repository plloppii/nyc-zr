# 136-316 Maximum length of buildings

The outermost walls of each **_story_** of a **_building_** located entirely above a height of 95 feet shall be measured in plan view and inscribed within a rectangle. The maximum length of any side of such rectangle shall be 170 feet. For the purposes of this Section, **_abutting_** portions of **_buildings_** above a height of 95 feet shall be considered a single **_building_**.
