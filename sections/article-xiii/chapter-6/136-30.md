# 136-30 SPECIAL REGULATIONS WITHIN SUBDISTRICT A

The regulations of Section [136-30](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-xiii/chapter-6/136-30), inclusive, shall apply within Subdistrict A, as shown on Map 1 (Special Downtown Far Rockaway District and Subdistrict) in the Appendix to this Chapter. The regulations of the **_Special Downtown Far Rockaway District_** shall apply, except as modified by the regulations of Section [136-30](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-xiii/chapter-6/136-30), inclusive.
