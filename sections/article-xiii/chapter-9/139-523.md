# 139-523 Parcel 12

On Parcel 12, where a **_supplemental public access area_** adjoining an **_upland connection_** or **_street_** is provided, such **_supplemental public access area_** shall be permitted to be provided with a maximum width to depth ratio of 3:1, and the longest side shall be permitted to adjoin the **_street_**.
