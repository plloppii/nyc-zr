# 139-215 Special floor area provisions for zoning lots containing comfort stations

For **_zoning lots_** containing a comfort station provided in accordance with the provisions of paragraph (c)(2) of Section [139-51](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-xiii/chapter-9/139-51) (Area-wide Modifications), an area equal to 200 percent of the floor space within such comfort station may be exempted from the definition of **_floor area_**.
