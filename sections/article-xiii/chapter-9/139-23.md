# 139-23 Special Height and Setback Regulations

The height and setback regulations of the applicable underlying districts are modified as follows:

(a) In **_Commercial Districts_**, the height and setback regulations of Section [35-60](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-5/35-60) (MODIFICATION OF HEIGHT AND SETBACK REGULATIONS) shall apply to all **_buildings_**, as modified by the provisions of this Section, inclusive.

(b) In **_Mixed Use Districts_**, the height and setback regulations of Section [123-60](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-xii/chapter-3/123-60) (SPECIAL BULK REGULATIONS) shall apply, as modified by the provisions of this Section, inclusive.

(c) In **_Manufacturing Districts_**, the underlying height and setback regulations of Sections [43-43](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iv/chapter-3/43-43) (Maximum Height of Front Wall and Required Front Setbacks), [43-44](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iv/chapter-3/43-44) (Alternate Front Setbacks), and [43-45](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iv/chapter-3/43-45) (Tower Regulations) shall not apply. In lieu thereof, minimum and maximum base heights and maximum heights for **_buildings or other structures_** shall be as set forth in this Section, inclusive. The other underlying regulations of Article IV, Chapter 3 (Bulk Regulations) shall apply, as modified by the provisions of this Section, inclusive.

(d) The special **_bulk_** regulations applicable in the **_waterfront area_** of Section [62-30](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-2/62-30) (SPECIAL BULK REGULATIONS) shall not apply. In lieu thereof, the height and setback regulations of this Section, inclusive, shall control.

The height of all **_buildings or other structures_** shall be measured from the **_base plane_**.
