# 139-025 Applicability of Article XII, Chapter 3

In **_Mixed Use Districts_**, the special **_use_**, **_bulk_**, and parking and loading provisions of Article XII, Chapter 3 (Special Mixed Use Districts) shall apply, except where modified by the provisions of this Chapter, and shall supplement or supersede the provisions of the designated **_Residence_** or M1 District, as applicable.

Notwithstanding the provisions of Section [123-10](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-xii/chapter-3/123-10), in the event of a conflict between the provisions of this Chapter and the provisions of Article XII, Chapter 3, the provisions of this Chapter shall control.
