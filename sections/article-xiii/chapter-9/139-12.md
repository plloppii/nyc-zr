# 139-12 Gowanus Mix Uses

For the purposes of applying the special **_bulk_** regulations of Section [139-212](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-xiii/chapter-9/139-212) (Gowanus mix), **_Gowanus mix uses_** shall include **_referenced commercial and manufacturing uses_**, as well as libraries, museums, community centers, non-commercial art galleries and philanthropic or non-profit institutions without sleeping accommodations listed under Use Group III. However, automotive repair and maintenance establishments listed under Use Group VI shall not be included.
