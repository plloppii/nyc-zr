# 139-30 SPECIAL PARKING REGULATIONS

In Subdistricts A, B, C, and D, the underlying parking and loading regulations shall be modified by the provisions of this Section. On **_waterfront blocks_**, the provisions of Section [62-40](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-2/62-40) shall not apply. In Subdistrict E, the underlying regulations shall apply.
