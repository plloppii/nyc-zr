# 138-24 Height and Setback Regulations in M1 Districts Paired With an R9 or R10 District

In M1 Districts paired with an R9 or R10 District, the applicable **_street wall_** location and minimum base height provisions of paragraph (b) of Section [138-22](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-xiii/chapter-8/138-22) (Street Wall Regulations) shall apply, and the applicable maximum height of **_buildings or other structures_** and setback provisions set forth in Section [123-65](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-xii/chapter-3/123-65) (Special Height and Setback Regulations in Special Mixed Use Districts with R6 Through R12 District Designations), inclusive, shall apply as modified in this Section.

(a) In M1 Districts paired with an R9 District, at a height not lower than the minimum base height set forth in Section [138-22](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-xiii/chapter-8/138-22), nor higher than a maximum base height of 105 feet, a setback shall be provided in accordance with Section [23-433](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-3/23-433) (Standard setback regulations). The maximum **_building_** height shall be 215 feet;

(b) in M1 Districts paired with an R10 District whose maximum **_floor area ratio_** is 10.0, at a height not lower than the minimum base height set forth in Section [138-22](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-xiii/chapter-8/138-22), nor higher than a maximum base height of 155 feet, a setback shall be provided in accordance with Section [23-433](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-3/23-433). The maximum **_building_** height shall be 275 feet; and

(c) in M1 Districts paired with an R10 District whose maximum **_floor area ratio_** is 12.0, at a height not lower than the minimum base height set forth in Section [138-22](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-xiii/chapter-8/138-22), nor higher than a maximum base height of 155 feet, a setback shall be provided in accordance with Section [23-433](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-3/23-433). The maximum **_building_** height shall be 295 feet.
