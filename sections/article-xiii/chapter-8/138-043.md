# 138-043 Applicability of the Mandatory Inclusionary Housing Program

For the purposes of applying the Mandatory Inclusionary Housing Program set forth in Section [27-10](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-7/27-10) (ADMINISTRATION OF AFFORDABLE HOUSING), the **_Special East Harlem Corridors District_** shall be a **_Mandatory Inclusionary Housing area_**.
