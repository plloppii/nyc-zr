# BC 2614 Reflective Plastic Core Insulation

### 2614.1 General

The provisions of this section shall govern the requirements and uses of reflective plastic core insulation in buildings and structures. Reflective plastic core insulation shall comply with the requirements of Section

2614.2 and of one of the following: Section 2614.3 or 2614.4.

### 2614.2 Identification

Packages and containers of reflective plastic core insulation delivered to the job site shall show the manufacturer’s or supplier’s name, product identification and information sufficient to determine that the end use will comply with the code requirements.

### 2614.3 Surface-burning characteristics

Reflective plastic core insulation shall have a flame spread index of not more than 25 and a smoke-developed index of not more than 450 when tested in accordance with ASTM E 84 or UL

723. The reflective plastic core insulation shall be tested at the maximum thickness intended for use. Test specimen preparation and mounting shall be in accordance with ASTM E 2599.

### 2614.4 Room corner test heat release

Reflective plastic core insulation shall comply with the acceptance criteria of Section 803.1.2.1 of this code when tested in accordance with NFPA 286 or UL 1715 in the manner intended for use and at the maximum thickness intended for use.
