# BC 2105 Quality Assurance

### 2105.1 General

A quality assurance program shall be used to ensure that the constructed masonry is in compliance with the approved construction documents. The quality assurance program shall comply with the inspection and testing requirements of Chapter 17 and TMS 602.

### 2105.2 Submittals

Submittals required by Article 1.5 of TMS 602 shall be sent to the applicant of record for review prior to use of the materials or methods of construction. In addition the contractor shall submit a Material Storage and Protection Plan.
