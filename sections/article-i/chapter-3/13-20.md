# 13-20 SPECIAL RULES FOR MANHATTAN CORE PARKING FACILITIES

All **_accessory_** off-street parking facilities, automobile rental establishments, and **_public parking lots_** **_developed_**, **_enlarged_** or **_extended_** in the **_Manhattan Core_** after May 8, 2013, shall comply with the applicable provisions of this Section, inclusive.
