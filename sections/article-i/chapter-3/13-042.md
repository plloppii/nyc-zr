# 13-042 Applicability of special permits within the Manhattan Core

The following special permits shall not be applicable within the **_Manhattan Core_**:

Section [73-46](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-3/73-46) (Exceptions to Maximum Size of Accessory Group Parking Facilities);

Section [74-193](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-4/74-193) (Public parking garages or public parking lots outside high density areas);

Section [74-194](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-4/74-194) (Public parking garages or public parking lots in high density central areas), except as set forth in Section [13-06](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-i/chapter-3/13-06) (Previously Filed or Approved Special Permits or Authorizations); and

Section [74-53](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-4/74-53) (Accessory Group Parking Facilities for Uses in Large-Scale Residential Developments or Large-Scale Community Facility Developments or Large-Scale General Developments), inclusive.
