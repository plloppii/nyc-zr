# 13-05 Exceptions

The provisions of this Chapter shall not apply to Roosevelt Island, in Community District 8, or to Governors Island, in Community District 1, in the Borough of Manhattan. In the **_Hudson Yards parking regulations applicability area_**, as defined in Section [93-81](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ix/chapter-3/93-81), the provisions of this Chapter shall apply as specified in Section [93-80](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ix/chapter-3/93-80) (OFF-STREET PARKING REGULATIONS).

Additional modifications to the provisions of this Chapter are found in the following Special Purpose Districts:

(a) the **_Special Midtown District_**, as set forth in Section [81-30](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-viii/chapter-1/81-30) (OFF-STREET PARKING AND OFF-STREET LOADING REGULATIONS), inclusive, Section [81-44](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-viii/chapter-1/81-44) (Curb Cut Restrictions) and paragraph (c) of Section [81-84](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-viii/chapter-1/81-84) (Mandatory Regulations and Prohibitions);

(b) the **_Special Lincoln Square District_**, as set forth in Section [82-50](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-viii/chapter-2/82-50) (OFF-STREET PARKING AND OFF-STREET LOADING REGULATIONS);

(c) the **_Special Battery Park City District_**, as set forth in Section [84-14](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-viii/chapter-4/84-14) (Parking Regulations and Curb Cuts), inclusive;

(d) the **_Special United Nations Development District_**, as set forth in Section [85-03](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-viii/chapter-5/85-03) (Modifications of Use Regulations);

(e) the **_Special Lower Manhattan District_**, as set forth in Section [91-50](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ix/chapter-1/91-50) (OFF-STREET PARKING, LOADING AND CURB CUT REGULATIONS), inclusive;

(f) the **_Special Park Improvement District_**, as set forth in Section [92-05](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ix/chapter-2/92-05) (Maximum Number of Accessory Off-street Parking Spaces);

(g) the **_Special Transit Land Use District_**, as set forth in Section [95-09](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ix/chapter-5/95-09) (Special Regulations for Accessory Off-street Parking and Curb Cuts);

(h) the **_Special Clinton District_**, as set forth in Section [96-111](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ix/chapter-6/96-111) (Off-street parking regulations);

(i) the **_Special Madison Avenue Preservation District_**, as set forth in Section [99-06](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ix/chapter-9/99-06) (Off-street Parking Regulations);

(j) the **_Special Little Italy District_**, as set forth in Sections [109-16](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-x/chapter-9/109-16) (Parking Regulations), [109-351](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-x/chapter-9/109-351) (Parking regulations), [109-352](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-x/chapter-9/109-352) (Curb cut regulations) and [109-521](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-x/chapter-9/109-521) (Modification of accessory off-street parking facilities); and

(k) the **_Special Hudson River Park District_**, as set forth in Section [89-21](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-viii/chapter-9/89-21) (Transfer of Floor Area From Hudson River Park).
