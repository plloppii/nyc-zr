# 13-11 Permitted Parking for Residences

**_Accessory_** off-street parking spaces are permitted for **_residences_** in **_developments_** or **_enlargements_**, as follows:

(a) for Community Districts 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, and 6, **_accessory_** off-street parking spaces may be provided for not more than 20 percent of the total number of new **_dwelling units_** contained in the **_development_** or **_enlargement_**, or 200 spaces, whichever is less;

(b) for Community Districts 7 and 8, **_accessory_** off-street parking spaces may be provided for not more than 35 percent of the total number of new **_dwelling units_** contained in the **_development_** or **_enlargement_**, or 200 spaces, whichever is less.
