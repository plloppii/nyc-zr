# 13-32 Location of Access to the Street

In addition to the provisions of Sections [25-75](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-5/25-75), [36-662](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-6/36-662) and [44-562](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iv/chapter-4/44-562) (Location of access to the street), no entrance or exit to an **_accessory_** off-street loading berth shall be located on a **_street_** with a roadbed width that is less than 20 feet, as measured curb to curb.
