# 13-10 PERMITTED OFF-STREET PARKING IN THE MANHATTAN CORE

No parking shall be required within the **_Manhattan Core_**. As-of-right off-street parking spaces located within **_accessory_** off-street parking facilities, automobile rental establishments and **_public parking lots_** in the **_Manhattan Core_** shall be permitted only as set forth in this Section, inclusive.
