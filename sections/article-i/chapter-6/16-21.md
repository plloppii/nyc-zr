# 16-21 Public Use and Off-site Parking

All **_accessory_** off-street parking spaces may be made available for public use. However, any such space shall be made available to the occupant of a **_residence_** to which it is **_accessory_** within 30 days after a written request therefor is made to the landlord.

No **_accessory_** off-street parking spaces shall be located on a **_zoning lot_** other than the same **_zoning lot_** as the **_use_** to which they are **_accessory_**.
