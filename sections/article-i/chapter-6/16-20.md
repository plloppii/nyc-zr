# 16-20 SPECIAL RULES FOR PARKING FACILITIES WITHIN THE LONG ISLAND CITY AREA

All **_accessory_** off-street parking facilities, **_public parking lots_** and **_public parking garages_** **_developed_** or **_enlarged_** after October 25, 1995, in the **_Long Island City area_** shall comply with the applicable provisions of this Section, inclusive.
