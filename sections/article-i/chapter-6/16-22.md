# 16-22 Enclosure, Surfacing and Screening Requirements

All **_accessory_** off-street parking spaces shall be located within a **_completely enclosed_** **_building_**, with the exception of:

(a) parking spaces **_accessory_** to non-profit, voluntary or proprietary hospitals and related facilities, listed under Use Group III(B); and

(b) up to 15 off-street parking spaces **_accessory_** to **_commercial_** **_uses_** other than a **_transient hotel_**, listed under Use Group V, **_community facility_** **_uses_** other than hospitals, or **_manufacturing_** **_uses_**.
