# 16-11 Permitted Parking for Residences

**_Accessory_** off-street parking spaces are permitted for **_residences_** in **_developments_** or **_enlargements_**, as follows:

(a) within Area A, as shown on the map in Section [16-03](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-i/chapter-6/16-03), **_accessory_** off-street parking spaces may be provided for not more than 50 percent of the total number of new **_dwelling units_** contained in the **_development_** or **_enlargement_**, or 200 spaces, whichever is less; and
(b) within Areas B and C, as shown on the map, **_accessory_** off-street parking spaces may be provided for not more than 100 percent of the total number of new **_dwelling units_** contained in the **_development_** or **_enlargement_**.
