# 16-052 Applicability of parking regulations for large-scale residential developments within the Long Island City area

The provisions of this Chapter shall not apply to **_large-scale residential developments_** utilizing Sections [78-41](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-8/78-41) (Location of Accessory Parking Spaces) or [78-42](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-8/78-42) (Parking Regulations for Commercial and Community Facility Uses).
