# 16-13 Permitted Parking for Zoning Lots With Multiple Uses

Where a **_development_** or **_enlargement_** contains a combination of **_uses_** for which parking regulations are set forth in Sections [16-11](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-i/chapter-6/16-11) (Permitted Parking for Residences) and [16-12](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-i/chapter-6/16-12) (Permitted Parking for Non-residential Uses), the number of **_accessory_** off-street parking spaces for all such **_uses_** shall not exceed the number of spaces permitted for each **_use_** in accordance with the provisions of such Sections. However, in no event shall the maximum number exceed 225 **_accessory_** off-street parking spaces. The exclusive or primary **_use_** provisions of Sections [16-11](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-i/chapter-6/16-11) and [16-12](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-i/chapter-6/16-12) shall be applicable to the number of spaces provided for each **_use_**.
