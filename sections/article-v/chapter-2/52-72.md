# 52-72 Land with Minor Improvements

In all **_Residence Districts_**, a **_non-conforming_** **_use_** of **_land with minor improvements_** that is listed in Use Groups IV, VI, VII, VIII, IX or X and not permitted as-of-right within C7 Districts, may be continued for three years after December 15, 1961, or such later date that the **_use_** becomes **_non-conforming_**, provided that after the expiration of that period such **_non-conforming_** **_use_** shall terminate, and thereafter such land shall be used only for a conforming **_use_**.
