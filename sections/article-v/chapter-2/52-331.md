# 52-331 Buildings designed for residential use

In all **_Residence Districts_**, a **_non-conforming_** **_use_** that is:

(a) listed in Use Groups IV, VI, VII, VIII, IX or X and not permitted as-of-right within C7 Districts; and
(b) which is located in a **_building designed for residential use_**,

may be changed only to a **_use_** permitted in **_Residence Districts_**.
