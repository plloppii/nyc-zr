# 52-55 Lesser Damage or Destruction

In the event that a **_building or other structure_** substantially occupied or utilized by a **_non-conforming_** **_use_** is damaged or destroyed to a lesser extent than that specified in Sections [52-51](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-v/chapter-2/52-51) to [52-54](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-v/chapter-2/52-54), inclusive, relating to Damage, Destruction or Demolition, the **_building or other structure_** may be restored and the **_non-conforming_** **_use_** of such **_building or other structure_** or **_zoning lot_** may be continued, provided that such restoration shall not create a **_non-compliance_** or increase the degree of **_non-compliance_**, if any, existing prior to such damage, destruction or demolition.
