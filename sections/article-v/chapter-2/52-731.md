# 52-731 Advertising signs

In all **_Residence Districts_**, a **_non-conforming_** **_advertising sign_** may be continued for 10 years after December 15, 1961, or such later date that such **_sign_** becomes **_non-conforming_**, providing that after the expiration of that period such **_non-conforming_** **_advertising sign_** shall terminate.
