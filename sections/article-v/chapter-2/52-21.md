# 52-21 Repairs and Incidental Alterations

Repairs to both structural and non-structural parts or **_incidental alterations_** may be made in a **_building or other structure_** substantially occupied by a **_non-conforming_** **_use_**, or in connection with a permitted change or **_extension_** of a **_non-conforming_** **_use_**.
