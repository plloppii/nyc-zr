# 52-37 Non-Conforming Commercial Uses in Manufacturing Districts

In all **_Manufacturing Districts_**, any **_non-conforming_** **_commercial_** **_use_** may be changed, initially or in any subsequent change, only to a conforming **_use_** or to any **_use_** listed in Use Groups VI or VIII, other than an **_outdoor amusement park_**.
