# 54-40 DAMAGE OR DESTRUCTION IN NON-COMPLYING BUILDINGS

For the purposes of this Section, **_buildings_** that **_abutted_** one another on a single **_zoning lot_** on the date of such damage or destruction shall be considered a single **_building_**.
