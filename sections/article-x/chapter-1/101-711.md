# 101-711 Total surface area of signs

The total **_surface area_** and number of all permitted **_signs_**, including non-**_illuminated_** and **_illuminated signs_**, shall not exceed the limitations set forth for non-**_illuminated signs_** in Section [101-712](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-x/chapter-1/101-712) (Area of non-illuminated signs).
