# 101-632 Waiver of off-street parking and loading requirements

The Commissioner of Buildings may waive any requirement in this Chapter that **_accessory_** off-street parking or loading berths be provided where the Commissioner finds that the required parking or loading cannot be provided consistent with Section [101-631](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-x/chapter-1/101-631) (Restricted access and prohibition on curb cuts).
