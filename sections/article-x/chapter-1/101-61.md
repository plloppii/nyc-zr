# 101-61 Special Sign Regulations for the Fulton Mall Subdistrict

Any erection of a new **_sign_**, or alteration (including a change in graphic representation on an existing **_sign_**), reconstruction or replacement of an existing **_sign_**, shall be subject to the special **_sign_** regulations of this Section, inclusive.
