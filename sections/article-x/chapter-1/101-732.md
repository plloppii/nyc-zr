# 101-732 Special provisions for other existing buildings

Any alteration, **_incidental alteration_** or minor work done to the front wall of any **_building_** not of the **_specified building type_**, shall comply with the following standards:

(a) alteration or replacement of storefronts shall comply with the provisions of Section [101-734](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-x/chapter-1/101-734); and

(b) alteration or replacement of front walls shall comply with the provisions of Sections [101-721](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-x/chapter-1/101-721) (Height and setback) and [101-733](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-x/chapter-1/101-733) (Special provisions for development and enlargement).
