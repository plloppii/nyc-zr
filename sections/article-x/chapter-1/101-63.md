# 101-63 Modification of Accessory Off-street Parking and Loading Requirements

The parking regulations of Section [101-50](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-x/chapter-1/101-50) (OFF-STREET PARKING AND OFF-STREET LOADING REGULATIONS) shall apply, except as set forth in this Section, inclusive.
