# 104-02 General Provisions

In harmony with the general purposes and content of this Resolution and the general purposes of the **_Special Manhattanville Mixed Use District_**, the regulations of this Chapter shall apply within the Special District. The regulations of all other Chapters of this Resolution are applicable, except as superseded, supplemented or modified by the provisions of this Chapter. In the event of a conflict between the provisions of this Chapter and other regulations of this Resolution, the provisions of this Chapter shall control. However, in **_flood zones_**, or for **_transit-adjacent sites_**, as defined in Section [66-11](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vi/chapter-6/66-11) (Definitions), in the event of a conflict between the provisions of this Chapter and the provisions of Article VI, Chapter 4 (Special Regulations Applying in Flood Hazard Areas), or Article VI, Chapter 6 (Special Regulations Applying Around Mass Transit Stations), the provisions of Article VI shall control.
