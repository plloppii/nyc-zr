# 104-336 Type 6 street wall location

Type 6 **_street walls_**, as shown on Map 4 in Appendix A of this Chapter, shall be located at or within 10 feet of the **_street line_** and shall extend along at least 70 percent of the length of the **_street line_** of the Parcel, and may rise to a maximum height of 120 feet. No **_street wall_** location regulations shall apply to the remaining 30 percent of the **_street_** frontage of a Parcel.
