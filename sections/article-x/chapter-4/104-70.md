# 104-70 PARKING AND LOADING REGULATIONS AND CURB CUT LOCATIONS

In the **_Special Manhattanville Mixed Use District_**, the **_accessory_** off-street parking and loading regulations of the underlying zoning districts shall apply, except as set forth in this Section, inclusive.
