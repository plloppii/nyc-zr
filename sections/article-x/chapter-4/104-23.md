# 104-23 Maximum Floor Area Ratio for Certain Uses

In Subdistricts A and C, the maximum **_floor area ratio_** permitted for **_commercial_** **_uses_** shall be 6.0, except that the maximum **_floor area ratio_** for **_uses_** listed in Section [104-13](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-x/chapter-4/104-13) (Commercial and Manufacturing Use Modifications) shall be 2.0.
