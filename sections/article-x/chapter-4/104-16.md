# 104-16 Modification of Article VII, Chapter 4 (Special Permits by the City Planning Commission)

The provisions of Section [74-171](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-4/74-171) (Laboratories) shall not apply in the **_Special Manhattanville Mixed Use District_**.
