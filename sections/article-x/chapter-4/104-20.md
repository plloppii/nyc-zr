# 104-20 SPECIAL BULK REGULATIONS

In Subdistricts A, B and C, the **_bulk_** regulations of the underlying C6 and M1 Districts, as modified in this Chapter, shall apply.

The **_floor area ratio_**, **_open space ratio_** and **_lot coverage_** regulations applicable in the underlying C6 Districts are modified as set forth in Sections [104-21](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-x/chapter-4/104-21) through [104-25](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-x/chapter-4/104-25).

The **_floor area ratio_** regulations applicable in the underlying M1 District are modified as set forth in Section [104-22](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-x/chapter-4/104-22). No **_floor area_** bonuses shall be permitted.

Special provisions regulating change of **_use_** in **_non-complying_** **_buildings_** are set forth in Section [104-26](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-x/chapter-4/104-26).

The height and setback regulations of the underlying C6 Districts are superseded as set forth in Sections [104-30](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-x/chapter-4/104-30) through [104-34](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-x/chapter-4/104-34), inclusive.

The special maximum **_building_** height regulations for the M1-2 District are set forth in Section [104-31](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-x/chapter-4/104-31).

Notwithstanding the special **_bulk_** regulations of this Chapter, any **_development_** containing **_dwelling units_**, or college or school student dormitories as listed in Use Group 3, on Parcel H, as shown on Map 5 (Parcel Designation and Maximum Building Heights) in Appendix A of this Chapter, shall comply with the height and setback regulations for an R8A District as set forth in Article II of this Resolution.
