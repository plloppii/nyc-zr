# 108-15 Applicability of Article V, Chapter 2 (Non-conforming Uses)

In the **_Special Hunts Point District_**, a **_non-conforming_** **_use_** may be changed only to a **_conforming_** **_use_**.

The following sections pertaining to **_non-conforming_** **_uses_** in the **_Special Hunts Point District_** shall not apply:

Section [52-32](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-v/chapter-2/52-32) (Land With Minor Improvements)

Section [52-33](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-v/chapter-2/52-33) (Manufacturing or Related Uses in Residence Districts), inclusive

Section [52-34](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-v/chapter-2/52-34) (Commercial Uses in Residence Districts)

Section [52-35](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-v/chapter-2/52-35) (Manufacturing or Related Uses in Commercial Districts)

Section [52-36](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-v/chapter-2/52-36) (Non-conforming Commercial Uses in Commercial Districts)

Section [52-37](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-v/chapter-2/52-37) (Non-conforming Commercial Uses in Manufacturing Districts)

Section [52-43](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-v/chapter-2/52-43) (Residence Districts Except R1 and R2 Districts)

Section [52-44](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-v/chapter-2/52-44) (Non-conforming Residential Uses in M1 Districts)

Section [52-45](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-v/chapter-2/52-45) (Conforming and Non-conforming Residential Uses in M1-1D Through M1-5D Districts)

Section [52-54](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-v/chapter-2/52-54) (Buildings Designed for Residential Use in Residence Districts)

Section [52-56](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-v/chapter-2/52-56) (Multiple Dwellings in M1-1D Through M1-5D Districts)

Section [52-62](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-v/chapter-2/52-62) (Buildings Containing Residences in M1-1D Through M1-5D Districts)

Section [52-72](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-v/chapter-2/52-72) (Land with Minor Improvements)

Section [52-731](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-v/chapter-2/52-731) (Advertising signs)

Section [52-732](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-v/chapter-2/52-732) (Signs on awnings or canopies)

Section [52-74](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-v/chapter-2/52-74) (Uses Objectionable in Residence Districts)

Section [52-75](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-v/chapter-2/52-75) (Certain Types of Uses Involving Open Storage or Salvage).
