# 108-13 Use Modifications in the Food Industry Subdistrict

In the **_Special Hunts Point District_**, in the underlying M1-2 District within the Food Industry Subdistrict, **_uses_** listed under Use Groups IV(B) and IX shall be limited to those permitted within M1 Districts in **_Special Mixed Use Districts_**, as set forth in Section [123-20](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-xii/chapter-3/123-20) (SPECIAL USE REGULATIONS), inclusive, except that refrigerating plants listed under Use Group IX, shall be permitted.
