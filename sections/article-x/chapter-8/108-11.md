# 108-11 Use Modifications in the Special Hunts Point District

In the **_Special Hunts Point District_**, **_transient hotels_** or **_motels_** listed under Use Group V shall not be permitted within the areas designated on the **_Special Hunts Point District_** Map in Appendix A. Within such areas, Section [74-153](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-vii/chapter-4/74-153) (In M1 Districts) shall not be applicable.
