# 108-14 Enclosure Regulations

In the **_Special Hunts Point District_**, all **_uses_** listed under Use Groups IV, VI, VII, VIII, IX or X and are not permitted as-of-right within C7 Districts, shall be located within completely enclosed **_buildings_**, except that building materials or contractors’ yards listed under Use Group IX(A) may be open or enclosed.
