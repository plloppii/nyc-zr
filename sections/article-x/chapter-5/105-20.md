# 105-20 PROTECTION OF NATURAL FEATURES

All **_natural features_** within a **_Special Natural Area District_** shall be protected by the regulations of this Chapter in accordance with the provisions set forth in Sections [105-02](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-x/chapter-5/105-02) (General Provisions), [105-30](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-x/chapter-5/105-30) (PRESERVATION OF NATURAL FEATURES) and [105-50](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-x/chapter-5/105-50) (REGULATIONS FOR PROTECTION OF NATURAL FEATURES).

Except for any existing **_natural feature_** that is unsafe and the removal of which is required by the Department of Buildings to eliminate hazardous conditions, no **_natural features_** described in Section [105-11](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-x/chapter-5/105-11) (Description of Natural Features) shall be removed, destroyed or altered unless permitted by certification, authorization or special permit of the City Planning Commission, pursuant to Section [105-40](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-x/chapter-5/105-40) (SPECIAL REVIEW PROVISIONS), or allowed pursuant to Section [105-021](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-x/chapter-5/105-021) (Actions not requiring special review).
