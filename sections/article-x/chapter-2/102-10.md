# 102-10 HEIGHT REGULATIONS FOR BUILDINGS, SIGNS OR STRUCTURES

Notwithstanding any other provisions of this Resolution, no portion of any **_building or other structure_** hereafter constructed, or of any **_sign_** hereafter erected, or of any existing **_building or other structure_** hereafter relocated, **_enlarged_** or reconstructed, shall penetrate a **_view plane_** unless authorized by the City Planning Commission, pursuant to Section [102-30](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-x/chapter-2/102-30) (SPECIAL PERMIT PROVISIONS).
