# 102-11 Damage, Destruction or Demolition

If an existing **_building or other structure_** which penetrates the **_view plane_** of a **_Special Scenic View District_** is damaged, destroyed or demolished by any means, such **_building or other structure_** may be reconstructed provided that such reconstruction shall not create a new **_non-compliance_** nor increase the pre-existing degree of **_non-compliance_**.
