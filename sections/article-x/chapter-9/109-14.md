# 109-14 Parking Regulations

No **_accessory_** off-street parking is permitted for any **_development_** or **_enlargement_** in Area A, except as set forth herein.

The City Planning Commission may allow **_accessory_** off-street parking facilities for any **_development_** or **_enlargement_** on a **_zoning lot_** pursuant to the applicable authorization or special permit in Article I, Chapter 3 (Comprehensive Off-street Parking and Loading Regulations in the Manhattan Core).
