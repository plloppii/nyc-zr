# 109-42 Additional Requirements

Any **_zoning lots_** partially located within 100 feet of the **_street line_** of Mulberry Street (Area A1) shall comply with the requirements set forth in Section [109-21](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-x/chapter-9/109-21) (Use Regulations), inclusive.
