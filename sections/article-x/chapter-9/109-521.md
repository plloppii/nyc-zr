# 109-521 Modification of accessory off-street parking facilities

The City Planning Commission may, by special permit, modify **_accessory_** off-street parking facilities for the **_residential_** portion of any **_development_** on a **_zoning lot_** as set forth in Section [109-14](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-x/chapter-9/109-14) or Section 109-341.
