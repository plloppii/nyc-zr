# 109-312 Special use regulations for new development

For any **_building_** or portion of a **_building_** **_developed_** or **_enlarged_** within Area B, **_uses_** listed in the underlying district regulations are permitted, except for the following **_uses_**:

From Use Group IV(B)

All **_uses_**

From Use Group IX

Vehicle storage **_uses_**.
