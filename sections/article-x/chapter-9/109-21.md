# 109-21 Use Regulations

The provisions of Section [109-11](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-x/chapter-9/109-11) (Special Use Regulations) shall apply, except that all **_uses_** under Use Group VI that are subject to a size limitation of 10,000 square feet of **_floor area_** per establishment, shall be further limited to a maximum size of 5,000 square feet of **_floor area_** per establishment.
