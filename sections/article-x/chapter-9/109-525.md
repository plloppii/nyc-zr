# 109-525 Relationship to public improvement projects

In all cases, the City Planning Commission shall deny an application for a special permit whenever the **_development_** or **_enlargement_** will interfere with a public improvement project (including housing, highways, public **_buildings_** or facilities) which is approved by the Board of Estimate or City Planning Commission, or Site Selection Board, as determined from the calendar of each such agency issued prior to the date of the public hearing on the application for a special permit.
