# 107-464 Court regulations

The **_court_** regulations as set forth in Section [23-35](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-3/23-35) shall apply except as modified by this Section.

For any **_building_** containing **_residences_** not more than one **_story_** in height, the area of an **_inner court_** shall not be less than 225 square feet and the minimum dimension of such **_inner court_** shall not be less than 15 feet.

For any **_building_** containing **_residences_** more than one **_story_** in height, the area of an **_inner court_** shall not be less than 400 square feet and the minimum dimension of such **_inner court_** shall not be less than 20 feet.

No court regulations shall apply to **_single-_** and **_two-family_** **_detached_** **_residences_**.
