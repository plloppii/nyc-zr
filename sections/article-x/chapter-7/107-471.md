# 107-471 Modification of waiver requirements for spaces below minimum number

For the purposes of this Chapter, the waiver provision set forth in Section [36-231](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-iii/chapter-6/36-231) (In districts with high, medium or low parking requirements) shall not apply to any **_development_** in the Special District.
