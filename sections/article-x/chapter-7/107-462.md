# 107-462 Side yards for permitted non-residential use

For **_community facility_** **_buildings_** or other **_buildings_** used for permitted non-**_residential uses_** in **_Residence Districts_**, the provisions of Section [24-35](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-4/24-35) (Minimum Required Side Yards) shall apply to such **_community facility_** **_buildings_**, except that no **_side yard_** shall have a width less than 10 feet and, in the case of **_buildings_** more than three **_stories_** in height, the required total width of both **_side yards_** shall not be less than 25 feet.

Where greater widths of **_side yards_** are required by the provisions of Section [24-35](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-ii/chapter-4/24-35) than by the provisions of this Section, such requirement of greater width shall apply.
