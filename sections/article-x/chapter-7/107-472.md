# 107-472 Group parking facilities within plan review sites

For the purposes of this Chapter, no **_accessory_** **_group parking facility_** for non-**_residential uses_** shall be permitted anywhere on a **_plan review site_**, or any **_zoning lot_** that contained one or more acres on November 2, 2023, except as set forth in Sections [107-51](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-x/chapter-7/107-51) (Certification of Certain Plan Review Sites) or [107-68](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/article-x/chapter-7/107-68) (Modification of Group Parking Regulations).
